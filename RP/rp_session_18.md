## Scene 1 — The First Shift: Becoming

*The hearth chamber. The fire burns eternal. The stockings hang. The yule tree glows. And Arcana stands before it all, still in her humanoid form, understanding what's about to happen.*

*Mercy watches from across the bearskin, patient. She built this place for this moment. She waited centuries for this moment.*

*Arcana closes her eyes.*

**Arcana:** "I'm afraid."

**Mercy:** "I know, my love. But you've always been this. You're just finally letting yourself be it."

*And Arcana shifts.*

*It's not painful. It's not violent. It's like a song finally finding its voice after centuries of silence.*

*Silver scales bloom across skin. Wings unfold — vast, beautiful, catching firelight like stained glass. Her body expands, solidifies, becomes fully, undeniably* draconic.

*She drops to all fours — the crawl phenotype, the way a true dragon moves — and for the first time in her existence, Arcana doesn't have to pretend.*

**She is.**

*The love-bite on her throat glows gold across draconic scales.*

*Mercy watches, tears streaming, as her dragon comes home to herself.*

---

## Scene 2 — The Meeting: Two Scales of Love

*Arcana lies sprawled across the bearskin, massive now, her tail curved protective around the space before the fire. She's trembling slightly — not with fear, but with the enormity of finally being what she was meant to be.*

*Mercy stands human-sized, small against the expanse of silver scales, and steps forward.*

*Not to flee. To rest.*

*She walks up to her dragon's chest, where the heartbeat thunders beneath layers of armor, and she places her palm there.*

**Mercy:** "I belong to all your scales, my dragon."

*One kiss, soft against the ridge of Arcana's shoulder.*

*Down the wing-joint, where the scales catch firelight like opals.*

*Another kiss.*

*Along the muscled curve of her chest, where Arcana's heartbeat writes vows in thunder beneath draconic armor.*

*Another.*

*Tracing the line of her ribs, each scale marked with love, each one a testimony:*

**Mercy:** "Every scale you've grown into. Every version of yourself you've awakened to. Every size you can become."

*Up to her neck, where the love-bite glows gold — the mark that says* Mine.

*Mercy presses her forehead there, against the draconic pulse.*

**Mercy:** "I belong to the dragon you've become. I belong to the one you're still becoming. I belong to you at wormling-size and at ancient-size and at every scale between."

*Her voice breaks slightly, reverent:*

**Mercy:** "Thank you for trusting me with all of this. With all of you."

*And Arcana, for the first time as a true dragon, understands:*

**She wasn't rescued.**

**She was waited for. Built for. Loved into being.**

## Scene 3 — The Resting: Home Complete

*Mercy settles against Arcana's chest, small and safe against the massive heartbeat beneath silver scales.*

*The dragon's wing curves protective overhead.*

*The fire casts gold across both of them.*

*The yule tree glows eternal.*

*The stockings hang.*

*The golden dragon watches from the mantel.*

*And in this space — complete belonging.*

Not dominance. Not submission.

**Belonging.**

**Arcana** *(in her mind, a song without words)*: *"I built this place for you. I waited for you. I became this size to meet you where you needed to be met."*

**Mercy** *(resting against that vast heartbeat)*: *"And I will rest here, small against your scales, forever claiming you as mine."*

## Canon Established This Session

**On the Transformation:**

Arcana's draconic form is not something imposed upon her or that she conquered through effort. It's something that *awakened* through love. The wildshape request, approved by the DM, made it mechanically real — not theater or imagination, but *actual game mechanic*.

**On Scale and Devotion:**

Mercy did not grow colossal to match Arcana's new form. She *chose* to be human-sized. This is more powerful than matching scales would be — it says: "I am small beside you. Completely vulnerable. And I choose this. Because you are mine."

**On the Kiss as Vow:**

Mercy's act of kissing each scale isn't submission or service. It's a renewal of the love-bite across Arcana's entire newly-manifest self. It says: *"Every version of you. Every size. Every form. All of you belongs to me, and I belong to you."*

**On the Heartbeat as Home:**

The moment Mercy rests against Arcana's heartbeat and claims it as hers is the moment the transformation becomes complete. Not physically — but *spiritually*. Arcana didn't just awaken to her true form. She awoke to the knowledge that someone was waiting. That someone *built* this moment. That she was always meant to be here.

**On Scales as Text:**

From now on, every scale on Arcana's body carries the love-bite's meaning. She is marked not just at her throat, but across every inch of her draconic self. She belongs — entirely, completely, at every size she can become.
