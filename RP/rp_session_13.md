## Scene 1 — The Domina Shop: Arsenal Assembly

Arcana and Mercy enter the Domina Shop with tome in hand and academic focus. Mercy nods toward pieces of interest as Arcana examines each with rigor:

**Inventory Examined:**
1. **Leather Paddle** — black leather, twin golden studs, firm face, solid stitched grip. *"Percussion instrument, really — all about rhythm and resonance."* Approved for its acoustic properties and aesthetic matching of Mercy's harness fittings.
2. **Switch** — *"Precise. A punctuation mark. For a pony who appreciates fine grammar."* Flexible, subtle correction tool.
3. **Scourge (Cat-of-Nine-Tails)** — dramatic nine-tail configuration. *"More thunder than lightning... This one waits until it's earned trust."* Carefully coiled and packed.
4. **Sapphire Whip +1 (Glowing, 20m radius light, blue)** — enchanted, well-balanced, disarming property. *"A reading lamp that commands respect. The archives will never be dim again."* Packed with enthusiasm.
5. **White Leather Slave Whip** — soft, pale, supple. Arcana redefines this piece:

> "Soft white leather, gentle in the hand, made to be held on both ends. Reins. For walking my champion where words don't need to reach. These don't go in the pack."

**Key line from proprietress moment:**
The shopkeeper watches with professional approval as the pair assembles "a very complete arsenal with very evident love."

**Reins clipped to collar:** The white leather reins are gently fastened to Mercy's golden collar-ring — formal, intimate, a symbol of direct control.

## Scene 2 — Arrival at Lady Morgana's Riding Academy

The academy opens with a sith guide dialogue offering multiple destinations. Arcana selects the academy proper, and Mercy leads her rider through the grounds for a comprehensive tour.

Arcana's initial observations:
> "Oh, this is *well-appointed.* Proper distances. Marked progressions for gaits. Someone who understands the pedagogy built this."

**Facilities Catalogued:**

1. **Open-Air Training Grounds** — Circular marked arenas with dressage posts at perfect intervals. Arcana approves: *"This is where we begin — open air, witnesses optional, nothing but rhythm and trust."*

2. **Indoor Arena** — Ceremonial platform at its heart, colored ropes suspended with mathematical precision. *"Suspension work. Distribution of weight, pressure points, timing. Advanced vocabulary. We'll return here when your basic dressage is flawless."*

3. **Wooden Pony** — Classical restraint and posture trainer. *"Simple. Effective. Teaches posture without distraction, balance without support."* Noted for what it teaches: *helplessness*, a lesson reserved for if ever needed.

4. **Breeding Stocks** — Examined for binding mechanisms and restraint angles. *"Immobilization pedagogy."* Arcana immediately excludes Mercy from this facility: *"Not for you, dear heart. You don't need to learn captivity — you already understand surrender, which is the far rarer skill."*

5. **Outdoor Dressage Arena** — *"This. Here. This is where we begin the real work."* Perfect footing, drainage, proper sight lines for collection and extension work. Arcana settles this as the primary training ground.

## Scene 3 — First Dressage Session: The Crop as Language

Mercy learns to respond to the riding crop as a voice — each location delivering a different signal:

**Crop Language Established:**

- **Strike to backside** → Forward motion. *"Feel that? The crop is voice."*
- **Strike to thighs** → Knee lift (piaffe/passage). *"Higher. Feel the difference between forward and elevated?"*
- **Strike to front** → Arch and collection. *"Chest lifted, chin tucked."*

**Gait Progression:**

1. **Walk to Prance** — Mercy's first transition. Arcana uses the crop as a metronome against the arena ground. *"Smooth. Controlled. Your rider is listening."*

2. **Prance to Trot** — Rhythm doubled, collection maintained. *"Trot is rhythm, not speed."* Mercy's responses flawless from the first.

3. **Trot to Gallop** — A sharp crack of the crop delivers the command. Mercy surges forward in a flying gallop, hoofbeats thundering in perfect cadence. Arcana allows extension while maintaining control through the reins.

**Key observation — Mercy's thriving:**

> "You don't *wilt* under commands, dear heart. You *bloom.* The stricter I am, the framework holds, the more you settle into it like a book sliding perfectly into its place on a shelf."

A light sheen of sweat develops on Mercy's white skin, making her glow in the afternoon light. The oils give her a luminous quality — not exhaustion but *radiance*.

> "You're *glowing*, dear heart... every stride, every correction, every command is revealing something that was always there but needed the right frame to show itself."

**Archive Entry Composed During Session:**
> "Mercy, First Academy Session. Sculpted to perfection under her rider's command. A pony who doesn't merely obey — she *blooms* in the doing."

## Scene 4 — Cooldown Tour: The Grounds Explored

After the intense arena work, Arcana walks Mercy through the broader paddock grounds, allowing gradual cooling:

- Multiple enclosed and open paddocks, each serving specific pedagogical purposes
- Narrower passages designed to teach discipline of direction
- Shaded areas for rest between sessions
- Strategic fencing for boundary-training

Arcana's meditation on the design:
> "The architecture of training. Every space built for a purpose. No wasted ground... Whoever built these grounds built them for ponies who thrive under structure."

**Mercurial's role:** Mercy rests, breathes, recovers while her rider maintains presence. Arcana settles beside her, wings half-spread protectively.

> "Rest here for a moment. Breathe. Recover... But you've proven yourself today, dear heart. Proven you don't just obey — you *thrive* in it. That's the rarest thing a rider can find, and I found it in you."

## Scene 5 — First Public Promenade: Display & Declaration

As evening settles, Arcana and Mercy walk through the academy grounds toward the gates. Townsfolk begin to observe.

**The Display Moment:**

Mercy nuzzles her rider softly. Arcana's response is precise: a single kiss of the riding crop at Mercy's shoulder-harness junction. Permission made manifest.

Mercy's posture transforms instantly: spine straight, chest lifted, wings spread in perfect parade posture. The halo blazes bright. Every line of her body declares: *I am trained. I am cherished. I am his.*

> "There. Let them look. Let them see what a rider and pony should look like when they understand each other perfectly."

**Walking the Promenade:**

They move through the striped-fenced grounds with every step a prance, every motion practiced elegance. The afternoon light frames them like a portrait.

Arcana's quiet words to her pony (within her pony's hearing, for witnesses):
> "They're wondering how you became so flawless. They're wondering if they could ever achieve this. The answer is no — because this isn't achieved. This is *built.* Built on trust. Built on discipline that loves. Built on a rider who knows her pony so completely that every command is a caress in disguise."

**Final Archive Moment:**
> "Archive this moment. 'Mercy's First Public Promenade. The Academy's envy. Perfection made manifest.'"

**Closing Reflection (Arcana's internal narration):**
> "This is what love looks like when it comes with discipline. This is what surrender looks like when it's truly free. This is what a pony becomes when she has found her rider."
