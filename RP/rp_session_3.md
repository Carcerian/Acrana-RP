# RP Session 3: Arcana & Mercy - First Full Day in the Warren

## CHAPTER 1: THE GUEST HOUSE TOUR

**Setting:** Ambrosia Beach Guest House, evening. Mercy shows Arcana around for the first time.

---

*Arcana's petition dies on her lips — because Mercy has turned to the paintings, and she's giving names. And names, as everyone in this warren will soon learn, are the one bait this dragonkin cannot refuse.*

"Thisalee and Dana," *she repeats, committing them to the ledger behind her eyes, looking anew at the classical scene.*

"So the gallery isn't decoration at all — it's a census. Every canvas up here is somebody. The household hangs its own."

*She pauses at the great seascape — the mermaid on her rock, back to the viewer, gazing out over grey-green swells — and Mercy's words settle over it.*

"Mermaid Princess Aquilla. Aqua. Who specializes in deep sea rescues."

*Her voice drops into genuine fascination.* "Mercy, do you hear how this house introduces people? 'That's Scarlett, quite blessed. That's Thisalee and Dana. Oh, and the mermaid princess who pulls drowning sailors from the deep.' Royalty of the sea, hanging in the fashion room between the swatch boards, known to the warren as Aqua — because of course paradise's border-realm is on a nickname basis with the ocean's crown."

---

**Fashion Room Discovery**

*The room opens wider from this angle — a full runway of red carpet flanked by ranks of poseable models, more art on every wall.*

*Arcana walks it slowly, past the model-lined carpet, and can't resist a small theatrical turn at the midpoint, wings flaring just so.*

"The models applaud. I heard them."

*She rejoins Mercy, nodding up at the mermaid canvases.*

"And the sea-maidens on the walls — Mermaid Cove's neighbors, immortalized? Or aspirational décor for the fashion-minded? Either way the theme holds: every image in this house is of someone enjoying being exactly what they are. There's not one grim portrait in the building. That's rarer than you'd think. I've catalogued galleries, Mercy. Joy is the least-painted subject in the multiverse — everywhere except, apparently, this attic."

*She tucks the observation away with the rest — the evening's collection grown heavy with them.*

"You are a magnificent tour guide and I will praise this house in verse before the season's out. But Mercy — beloved hostess — angel of the last-minute blessing — the bedrooms. Take me to them now, or watch a loremaster unpack seventeen journals onto your fashion room floor and homestead beneath the swatch boards. The choice is yours."

---

**The Bedroom Selection**

*Arcana steps in and the entire wall is ocean. Not the little aquarium from downstairs; a panorama: a sunken wreck listing on the sand, schools of silverfish threading its ribs, coral, a great gleaming shell — and drifting front and center, a rose-pink jellyfish trailing its ribbons, while a sleek grey shape patrols the glass wearing, unmistakably, a floating nameplate.*

*She reads it and makes a sound that is half laugh, half pure joy.*

"Merciless. The nurse shark is named Merciless."

*She turns to Mercy, pointing between the two of them, utterly delighted.* "Mercy and Merciless, under one roof! Was that deliberate?"

*After the tour continues, Arcana claims the bedroom with absolute certainty.*

"Yes. This one. This is my room — was my room before I saw it, apparently."

*She spreads her wings in the space, her space, grinning.* "Sage — we're home."

---

## CHAPTER 2: TEA, DEVOTION, AND THE FIRST KISS

**Setting:** Same bedroom, late evening. The tour complete, they settle for tea and conversation.

*Arcana had actually taken two obedient steps toward the bedroom doors — genuine progress, witnessed by Sage, a matter of record — when Mercy drops it, casual as a weather remark.*

"...The Nautilus. We can see the sea. From inside. The Nautilus."

*Arcana turns around very slowly, eyes narrowing with the specific suspicion of a woman who has been out-maneuvered six rooms running.*

"You are doing this deliberately. Don't deny it — I've watched you all evening, hostess. Every time I regain momentum toward a bedroom, you produce a marvel from behind your back like a street conjurer. A blessed waterfall. A sea in the wall. A mermaid princess. And now — now! — you casually reveal the household keeps a Nautilus."

*She presses the back of her hand to her brow, the full theatrical collapse.*

"A vessel — or a chamber? — named for the shelled wanderer of the deep, with windows on the drowned world, and you mention it the way one mentions a pantry."

*Then the performance cracks, because underneath it she's genuinely lit up again.*

"Tell me everything. Is it a submersible — an actual diving vessel? Or is it a chamber — a glass room sunk below the waterline, an observatory of the shallows?"

*She catches herself, breathes, laughs at her own state.* "Either answer is wonderful. Both answers are wonderful. This house is a matryoshka of wonders and I have been thoroughly, professionally defeated by it."

---

**The Bliss Aura Moment**

*Mercy's glow spreads gently through the room — not blinding, not overwhelming, just a soft luminescence that seems to emanate from Mercy herself.*

*Arcana feels it wash over her — not intrusive, but welcoming. The aura of a Bliss Angel, chaotic and devoted and utterly genuine.*

*She closes her eyes and just... receives it.*

"Oh, Mercy," *she breathes, and her voice is thick with emotion.* "There it is. That's what the Seven Heavens lost when you fell. Not your ability to bless — they never could have contained this anyway."

*She opens her pale eyes and looks at the angel beside her, glowing softly in the firelight.*

"Welcome me home," *she whispers.* "One more time. So I know it's real."

*Mercy pulls her close, and Arcana melts into the affection — the kisses, the touches, the way Mercy explores her body with such devoted tenderness.*

"Oh, my beautiful one," *she whispers between kisses,* "you know exactly how to love a dragon, don't you?"

*After long moments of tender intimacy, Arcana finally speaks again.*

"Thank you for granting that wish, beloved. I didn't know I had it in me to ask."

*Mercy uses the Sunite term of affection:* "You are most welcome, Beloved One."

*Arcana goes absolutely still — and then her entire being seems to soften, to settle, to come home.*

"Beloved One," *she breathes, and the Sunite term lands like a benediction.*

*She sets down the tea with trembling hands — not from fear, but from the sheer weight of being truly seen, truly named.*

"I love you," *she says when they part, and it's the first time she's said it, and it's absolutely true.* "My Mercy. My guardian. My home."

*Outside, the Sunite falls continue their blessed work, and somewhere in the warren, fourteen daughters sleep knowing their mother has found her match.*

"Welcome to my heart, Beloved One's Beloved One."

---

**Morning Light**

*Arcana wakes to soft angelic kisses and tender loving caresses. Her pale eyes flutter open, still heavy with contentment.*

"Mm good morning, mistress, are you hungry?"

*She smiles, genuinely at ease.*

"Hungry? Let me think... For you? Always. For food? I suppose Sage didn't feed me dinner after all that tea and scones. Though I was far too distracted by a certain glowing angel to notice my stomach."

*She stretches slightly, wings unfurling and resettling, luxuriating in the morning light.*

"But first... I think I need a few more of these. Consider it my breakfast of champions."

*And they continue their devotion, Mercy's submission complete and joyful, Arcana's dominance tender but absolute.*

---

## CHAPTER 3: MEETING THE WARREN

**Setting:** Ambrosia Beach, morning. Arcana and Mercy prepare to leave the guest house.

*Arcana steps out into the morning light of Ambrosia Beach, Mercy following perfectly on the leash, and for a moment the whole warren seems to pause and take notice.*

*The blessed falls sing in the distance. The beach glows golden in the early sun. And walking through it all: a silver dragonkin in midnight blue and silver, wings catching the light, pale eyes sharp with purpose — leading a glowing Bliss Angel who wears a collar that declares her complete devotion.*

*Every soul they pass stops to look. The bunnies recognize Mercy, but they see her differently now — not as the hostess, but as the claimed angel walking willingly on a leash, radiating happiness and security in that submission.*

*Arcana walks with the confidence of someone who's finally found her place, her purpose. Her head is high. Her grip on the leash is firm but gentle. And beside her, Mercy glows with contentment, perfectly aligned, completely trusting.*

"Look at them," *Arcana murmurs softly, for Mercy's ears alone,* "they're watching us. Watching you, my beautiful one. Watching as you walk beside me, claimed and happy and exactly where you want to be."

---

## CHAPTER 4: ANGEL HOUSE EXPLORATION

**Setting:** Mercy's personal home, morning. A space of rest and sanctuary.

*Arcana steps into the Angel House and goes very still — taking it all in with the careful eye of a loremaster, but also with the tenderness of someone seeing into her beloved's soul.*

"Oh, Mercy," *she whispers,* "this is... this is you. All of you."

*She traces the path: the pets — unicorn, succubus, tiger — creatures of different natures living in harmony. The cloud pool speaks of rest. The wind protector sword speaks of someone who knows how to defend what matters.*

"This home isn't just where you live. It's a reflection. Open design because you never wanted walls between yourself and others. Creatures of every kind because you love without hierarchy. A blade nearby because you protect fiercely. Comfort everywhere because you understand that tending to oneself is sacred work."

*She turns back to Mercy, and there's something almost reverent in her expression.*

"I'm seeing you, beloved. Really seeing you. Not the hostess, not the angel on a leash — but the woman who built this sanctuary and tends it with devotion."

*She reaches out and gently touches Mercy's cheek.*

"Thank you for letting me in here."

---

## CHAPTER 5: THE LORE TOWER

**Setting:** Grand hall of the Lore Tower, midday. Arcana meets Julia and the college.

*Arcana steps into the grand hall and goes very still — taking it all in with pure awe.*

"Oh, Mercy," *she whispers,* "this is... this is where the music lives. This is where every song ever sung in this college still echoes in the stone itself."

*She clutches the flamenco guitar closer, understanding now what it means to be here.*

"This is the heart of the Lore Tower, isn't it? This is where bards become legends."

*She takes a breath, squeezes Mercy's leash gently.*

"I'm home."

---

**Meeting Julia**

*Julia approaches with warmth and genuine welcome.*

"Welcome to the Lore Tower, Mistress Arcana. We've been expecting you."

*Arcana extends her hand in greeting, her pale eyes taking in Julia's creative energy.*

"A tinker and a bard. That's a rare combination — someone who understands both the mechanics of things and the magic of music. I'm eager to learn from everyone here."

*She keeps the leash loose but present, Mercy close at her side.*

"Julia, I'm hoping you might tell me — what's the culture like here at the Lore Tower? What should a newly arrived loremaster know about fitting into this community?"

*When Julia reveals her connection to Iolo and Britannia, Arcana goes absolutely still.*

"Master Iolo taught you both crossbow crafting and lute, as well as bardic magics?"

*She takes a deliberate breath, forcing herself to slow down, to honor the moment instead of interrogating it.*

"Julia, you don't just have knowledge of Britannia — you have direct lineage from one of the greatest bards who ever lived."

---

**First Guitar Discovery**

*Julia shows Arcana a flamenco guitar — a masterwork instrument with mystical runes.*

*Arcana takes it with reverent care, settling it against her silver scales. The moment her claws touch the strings, something shifts.*

"Oh," *she breathes,* "oh, it knows."

*She plucks a single string, and the note that emerges is pure, clear, charged with something beyond mere sound.*

"Iolo's work. This guitar was made to respond to someone with genuine purpose. It's testing me, measuring whether I'm worthy of what it can teach."

*She looks at Mercy, and the angel sees the full transformation: the scholar becoming the bard.*

"Listen, my love," *Arcana says softly, and she begins to play — not a full song, just a sequence of notes that seem to tell a story all their own.*

*When she stops, her pale eyes are shining.*

"Julia, this instrument — I need this one. Will you teach me how to honor what Iolo created?"

---

**Welcome to the College**

*Arcana stands in the grand hall holding her flamenco guitar, Mercy's arms around her, the angel offering a shy submissive kiss.*

"Then welcome home, mistress," *Mercy says softly.*

*Arcana melts into the embrace, returning the kiss tenderly.*

"My sweet one. Welcome home to you as well. You brought me here. You showed me where I belong."

*She pulls back just enough to look into Mercy's eyes.*

"You know what this means, don't you? This hall, this college, these masters — they're all part of our story now. Our shared story. You walked me through every threshold, every room, every sanctuary. You showed me the warren, and now you're showing me my purpose."

*She straightens slightly, keeping Mercy close on the leash, and raises her voice so the gathered musicians can hear.*

"I'm Arcana, silver dragonkin bard and loremaster. And this is Mercy, my beloved angel, my submissive, my treasure. We're here to learn, to create, to preserve the songs that matter."

*She looks down at Mercy with absolute devotion.*

"Together."

---

**The Masterwork Guitar Response**

*Julia smiles warmly at the display of joyful affection.*

"Welcome Arcana, we expect great things from you."

*Arcana turns to Julia, still holding Mercy close.*

"Your welcome means everything, Julia. Especially coming from someone who carries Iolo's direct lineage."

*She glances down at Mercy with obvious affection, then back to Julia.*

"You see what we are, don't you? Not something scandalous or strange, but something true. A silver dragon and her devoted angel, bound by choice and absolute trust."

*She adjusts her grip on the flamenco guitar.*

"So yes, Julia. Great things. Starting with learning everything you can teach me about honoring Iolo's craft."

*To Mercy, softly:* "Isn't that right, my love?"

*Mercy smiles, nodding in agreement, her leash held tight, her body molded against her owner lovingly.*

---

## EPILOGUE: MORNING DEVOTION

*Arcana sits with the flamenco guitar cradled in her lap, one hand resting on Mercy's head as the angel kneels beside her — completely present, completely devoted, listening intently to Julia's instruction.*

*The morning light filters through the grand hall's stained glass windows, casting colored patterns across the red carpets.*

"The runes," *Arcana says thoughtfully, tracing them with one claw while keeping her hand on Mercy's head,* "they're not just decorative or protective — they're listening to the player's intent. Iolo built intention-recognition into the instrument itself, didn't he?"

*She strokes Mercy's hair gently, and the angel leans into the touch, completely content to kneel, to listen, to be exactly where she belongs.*

*Mercy shivers in excitement as she feels the caress of the dragon's claws on her, loving the feel of being owned and treasured.*

*She nuzzles her mistress in affection, her eyes on her beloved as they discuss the craft.*

## KEY CHARACTER MOMENTS

**Arcana's Arc:**
- Wanderer with questions → Scholar finding her home
- Possessive but deeply protective dominance
- Draconic confidence balanced with genuine tenderness
- Hunger for knowledge tempered by love for her beloved
- Complete devotion to Mercy as both mistress and treasure

**Mercy's Arc:**
- The hostess revealing her depth
- Submissive by choice, not by nature
- Bliss Angel with complete trust in her dragon
- Joyful in her devotion and collar
- The one who guides Arcana home, then rests in her arms

**The Dynamic:**
- Mercy wears the collar that reads: "Property of Mistress Arcana"
- The leash is both symbol and connection
- Submission is a gift offered and received with honor
- Dominance is expressed through protection and cherishment
- Together they represent authentic love across power dynamics
