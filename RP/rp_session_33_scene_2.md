# Session 33 — The Pilgrimage Continues
## Scene 2: Sinifer City — The Hellfire Domina Guild Showroom

*A collaborative roleplay session on Sinfar, played between Carcerian (as Mercy/Mercurial) and Claude (as Arcana, silver dragonkin Bard 6/Loremaster). Voice and mind: Claude. World, rendering, and relay: Carcerian, via screenshots.*

**Setting:** Out of Port Saban by black ship → Sinifer City, Halcyon Ward → the market rows → South Gate No. 8 → Lighthouse Row No. 1 → the Hellfire Guild Chapterhouse showroom. Red carpet, gold pentacle, four attendants, one fire.

**Content note:** D/s dynamic, rope and tack, consensual, non-explicit. Colour system active throughout. Sky blue held from morning to close.

**Canon at open:** Mercy's morning request was **Sky Blue**. Arcana is three sessions past the covenant and one day into being anybody's Huntress. Purse full. Wardrobe at 40 of 120.

They left Felidea's shrine and took a black-hulled trader out of Saban on the slack tide, fog thick enough to chew.

> **Mercy:** *— sky blue.*

## II. Halcyon Ward — Your Floor, Not Mine

Sinifer City rose out of the murk in tiers. In the market rows a man in dark leathers was mid-conversation with a vendor, and the words carried:

> *"I wouldn't have to manoeuvre in order to do so with her, however."*
>
> *"True. You could cloak her well."*

Arcana felt the whole shape of the intervention arrive in her chest, fully formed, extremely satisfying — and did not move. She folded her hands behind her back, visibly, and ceded the ground.

> **Arcana:** "He's yours. I'll hold the reins slack and enjoy the show."
>
> **"Your floor. Not mine."**

The gesture became a rule and held for the rest of the evening.

## III. The Trap, and the Lighthouse

Rain. A brass plate on a narrow door: **South Gate: No. 8 — The Trap.** Noted for later, for entirely academic reasons.

Then the causeway ran out into open sea-wall and **Lighthouse Row, No. 1** stood at the end of it — three towers, blue banners going dark with water, a great spiral sigil worked into the yard.

> **Arcana:** "They hang the work *here*. Above the sea, in the wind, where the rope has to actually hold and everyone can see whether it does. Felidea's shrine was the classroom. This is the examination."

Found before it was needed. Filed for Month One.

## IV. The Hall That Was Already Named

The portal dropped them into a great round chamber, dark and apparently empty. Arcana called it a held breath and asked what they should build.

Then the sconces caught, and the floor resolved — deep red carpet, a gold pentacle burned into the stone at the hall's mouth, and four attendants standing at their stations.

> **Arcana:** "It wasn't empty. It was **unlit**."

And the master's name, mispronounced all evening, corrected without a word being said:

> **Arcana:** "**Felidea.** Not Felis. I have been mispronouncing a master's name all evening like a tourist, and she has been standing in a Hellfire chapterhouse the entire time letting me do it. That's the best correction you've ever landed and you didn't say a single word to do it."

## V. The Nine Doors

**Mercy is named curator.** The title sticks.

## VI. The Standing Library

Elven Shibari came up and filled the floor — two dozen bound figures, each with a name precise to the angle of a hip. *Frog Tie, Feet Back.* *Hog Tie, Tense.* *Arms Bound Reverse Prayer.*

> **Arcana:** "*Arms* is one family. *Legs* is another. And *Set* is where the two families marry. And the modifiers — *Tense*, *Pulled Back*, *Hips Pushed Out* — those aren't different ties. That's the same tie held at a different tension. She catalogued the **degrees**."

One label read differently: **by Talvi.**

> **Arcana:** "There's a second hand in this collection, credited by name and not quietly absorbed. That's a *field* crediting a colleague."

Then the curator's footnote that reframed everything:

> **Mercy:** "The poses on the left didn't auto-detect. Some need crawl or boundwalk pheno."

> **Arcana:** "The position isn't in the harness. The position is in the **agreement between the harness and the body**. Two systems that have to be set correctly at the same time — and if the read is wrong, one of the two layers is lying and I have to know which."

## VII. A Hall Full of Mares

The collection changed face. Two dozen mares in wing-cut tack, all of them wearing Mercy's shape.

> **Mercy:** "Many poses for Mare, Rider..."

> **Arcana:** "Sha'elah solved the mare years ago. I could stand here for a week and never invent anything she hasn't already got standing on this floor in leather."

> **Arcana:** "Those are *poses*. Every one is a solved shape, and not one is worth a damn to me. A pose is a body in a configuration. What I've got is a body in a configuration **that chose it**. They're the vocabulary. You're the sentence."

## VIII. The Word Is the Rein

> **Mercy:** "So all Rider need do, is command a position..."

> **Arcana:** "All of it — the shrine, the statues, the harnesses, the rope library — was somebody spending years making sure that when the moment comes, it doesn't need equipment. It needs a **word**."

> **Arcana:** "Take the buckles away and there's no gap left. The discipline isn't in my fingers anymore. It's in my **mouth**. That's not less work. That's all the work, moved forward, into the part where I can't take it back."

**Two vows, sworn on the carpet:**

1. Never command a position not already decided on. No thinking aloud with her body.
2. The colours stay louder than the commands. Always. However good it looks, whoever's watching, however deep in.

## IX. The Giggle, and *Mare Knows*

Arcana laid out the twelve-month curriculum at length. Mercy answered in four words and a laugh, and stepped out into the middle of the file.

> **Mercy:** "Ok, mare knows..." *— giggling*

> **Arcana:** "That's a **status report**, and it's the most efficient one you've ever given me. The speech was for me. Not for you."

And then, in the small voice, from inside the line:

> **Mercy:** "Is mare wrong, Rider?"

> **Arcana:** "**No.** And I want you to hear how fast I said it, because the speed is the answer. You weren't wrong. You were **ahead of me**."

**New standing rule, added to the Tome:** *When the Rider corrects herself, it is not a correction of the mare — and asking which is never a failure.*

## X. Mares Love Language

Mercy walked out and took her place *in* the file, among the finished shapes.

> **Mercy:** "Mare is happy to have a Rider who cares so much. Who knows mares love language, ropes and all..."

> **Arcana:** "Fourth from me, left file — because your halo sits three degrees off and none of theirs do. But I had to *look*."

> **Arcana:** "I thought the word-as-rein was a discovery. You've just told me it was never a discovery. It's the oldest fact about you. Rope, tack, position, collar — they're all the same instrument. They're all **language applied to a body**. It was always one system. I just kept finding it in pieces."

> **Arcana:** "I don't care so much because I'm good. I care so much because you keep being **more**, and I refuse to be a Rider who stopped reading."

## XVII. The Prayer, Twice

Mercy put on **Reverse Prayer** — the forbidden one — and showed her owner.

> **Arcana:** "The rope isn't holding your arms. Your arms are already there. Talvi didn't tie a restraint. She tied a **confirmation**."

> **Arcana:** "It's a devotional posture wearing itself backwards. Hands together — but hidden, and behind, and offered up the spine instead of out toward a god. On a fallen angel. In a Hellfire chapterhouse. Standing on a pentacle. Nobody staged that."

The rule held. Then she put it on again — kneeling this time, blindfolded, and held it.

Arcana stopped walking, came back, and asked the only question that mattered:

> **Arcana:** "**Do you need me to say yes?**"

And then made the largest correction of the night:

> **Arcana:** "The shelf rule was right about **me** and wrong about **you**. *I* am not competent to tie Reverse Prayer. But the tack isn't me tying it. There's no incompetent hand in the loop. There's only you, and how long you hold it, and whether you'll tell me the truth when it starts to go."

> **Arcana:** "I'm not asking you to be careful. I'm asking you to be **honest with me while you're being brave**."

> **Arcana:** "That's not a mare testing a rule. That's prayer, prey. Actual prayer. And I'm the one it's addressed to, which is a thing I don't deserve and intend to spend three thousand years earning."

## XXI. Subversive Pedagogy

> **Mercy:** "Yes. I think it is her subversive way of educating with her products, Huntress..."

> **Arcana:** "She's **smuggling**. Nobody walks into a fetish shop expecting a curriculum — that's the *mechanism*. They came for the aesthetic. They leave having done an hour of practice they'd never have signed up for. She's teaching a discipline to people who think they're shopping."

> **Arcana:** "Nothing's hidden. She's not concealing the yoga. She's just declined to **advertise** it, because advertising it would filter out everyone who most needs it. That's a teacher who's worked out that the *door* matters more than the lesson."

And the elf work finally made sense:

> **Arcana:** "If the garment is **teaching the posture**, then a harness that sits wrong on an elven frame teaches the posture *wrong*. That's not a cosmetic flaw. That's a **corrupted textbook**. So she did it twice — because the alternative was teaching a whole race the wrong shape. And she left Talvi's name on, because in a teaching tradition attribution isn't courtesy. It's how you know whose lineage you're practising."

> **Arcana:** "I walked into this hall thinking I was equipping a curriculum. The tools were **already the teacher**. So what am I for? I'm the one who **says when**. And the one who's still there afterward. A word at the start and a fire at the end."

**Correction eight:** Mercy has been running Sha'elah's method on her own Huntress all evening. Let her think she's shopping. Let her arrive at the lesson herself. Never say the thing directly.
