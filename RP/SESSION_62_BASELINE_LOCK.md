## Saturday Morning Arrival & Priestess-Rite of Integration

**Date:** Saturday (after Friday's Beast Day / S61 Test)  
**Location:** Cathedral of Eternal Grace, Central Nave & Private Chambers  
**Participants:** Arcana, Mercy, 5 Dominae, Domain Prior, Cathedral Acolytes (40+), 3 Faction Representatives  
**Duration:** 3-4 hour session (500-1500 words target)

**Core Objective:** Formalize Mercy's arrival, lock baseline integration, introduce factional politics, plant first Void-hints

## OPENING: DAWN INTIMACY (Optional but Recommended)

**Setting:** Arcana's private guest-chambers (granted by Cathedral upon arrival); pre-ceremony morning

**Scene Purpose:** Establish intimate priestess-mount bond before formal ceremony; show Mercy in private state vs. public state

**Suggested Approach (Player Agency Focused):**

*You wake in the pre-dawn darkness. Mercy is already awake, curled in nest-fashion on the chamber's thick rugs. Her breathing is deep, relaxed. Outside, the Cathedral bells will ring soon for Matins prayers. In a few hours, the Baseline Lock Ceremony will formally establish her place in this ancient structure.*

*Her ears track toward you as you stir. Golden eyes watch your face. There's a contentment in her posture — this is her preferred state, consciousness permanently settled in beast-form. But you can read the awareness beneath: she senses today is significant.*

**Offer Arcana choices:**
- Private ritual/bonding before ceremony (intimate priestess-work)
- Practical preparation (Mercy grooming, collar-adjustment)
- Conversation (Mercy's feelings about the formal ceremony)
- Simple presence (quiet time together; no work required)

**Tone:** This is *player's priestess-relationship, not GM enforced*. Follow Arcana's lead on intimacy-level.

**Time Estimate:** 5-10 minutes of roleplay (intimate scene without explicit content; framing through suggestion/presence)

## SCENE ONE: CATHEDRAL PREPARATION (20 min)

**Setting:** Western Wing dormitories → Central Nave (pre-ceremony staging)

**Scene Purpose:** Guide players through Cathedral, introduce layout, establish "home base" feeling

**Staging Notes:**
- Arcana and Mercy move from private chambers through corridors
- They encounter various acolytes preparing for ceremony
- Visual/sensory description of Cathedral's beauty and scale
- Brief interactions with key NPCs (establishes relationships)

### CORRIDOR ENCOUNTERS

**Encounter 1: Brother Aldric (Stable-Master)**
*A broad-framed man with ink-stained fingers crosses your path, leading two ponies toward the eastern wing. He pauses, eyes assessing Mercy with professional care.*

**Aldric's Dialogue:**
- "Priestess. Your mount looks well-prepared for the ceremony. I've arranged the eastern stable-bay for her exclusive use when she requires private space."
- *If asked:* "You should know — we monitor the stables carefully. Last month, strange hoof-prints appeared in the dressage yard. Patterns I couldn't identify from any known form. Likely just wildlife, but... worth noting."

**Purpose:** 
- Establish Aldric as reliable ally
- Plant Void-hint #1 (mysterious hoof-prints)
- Show practicality of Cathedral (dedicated spaces for mounts)

**NPC Relationship:** If Arcana treats Aldric with respect, he becomes protective resource for Mercy's wellbeing.

---

**Encounter 2: Novice Kess (Pony-Student)**
*A young acolyte rushes past, then stops, eyes wide. She drops into a formal bow.*

**Kess's Dialogue:**
- "Priestess! I'm so honored to witness the ceremony. I've been studying the Pony-Form for three weeks and — sorry, I'm nervous."
- *If asked about her studies:* "Domina Seraphine is very strict, but I'm learning so much. Though... last night in the hunting grounds, I saw something odd. Not a pony-form. The shadow wasn't right. Probably just my imagination."

**Purpose:**
- Introduce young, enthusiastic student
- Plant Void-hint #2 (shadow-forms in hunting grounds)
- Show age-range of acolytes (community aspect)

**NPC Relationship:** Kess will report to Arcana if given opportunity; becomes potential investigation-ally.

---

**Encounter 3: Watcher-Sister Maren (Keeper Faction)**
*An athletic woman with closely-cropped hair intercepts your path. She moves like a warrior, eyes sharp.*

**Maren's Dialogue:**
- "Priestess Arcana. I'm Watcher-Sister Maren Silversong, internal investigator for the Cathedral. I wanted to extend formal welcome and make clear — if you witness anything unusual, know that quiet discretion is valued more than loud accusations here."
- *Slight meaningful pause* — "We've had some disappearances among the acolytes. Nothing alarming yet, but I'm investigating thoroughly. If you notice anything... irregular... I'd appreciate consultation."

**Purpose:**
- Introduce Keeper faction informally
- Offer alliance without pressure
- Plant Void-hint #3 (missing acolytes)
- Show there's neutral faction between Traditionalists and Reformers

**NPC Relationship:** Maren is testing Arcana's trustworthiness; if Arcana is open, Maren becomes key ally.

### CENTRAL NAVE ARRIVAL

*The corridor opens into vast space — the Central Nave. 300 feet long, 100 feet wide, 80 feet high. Vaulted ceiling soars overhead, filled with clerestory windows now gathering pre-dawn light. Eight massive pillars line the nave's length, each carved with scenes from priestess-craft mythology.*

**Sensory Details:**
- **Visual:** Stone work is intricate; gold leaf catches early light; eight pillars seem to glow slightly
- **Sound:** Silence is profound; footsteps echo in vast space
- **Spatial:** Room feels simultaneously magnificent and slightly oppressive
- **Spiritual:** Atmosphere of accumulated sacred practice (centuries of priestess-rites conducted here)

**Offer Player Observation Time:**
*"The Cathedral is preparing for the ceremony. What strikes you about this space? How does Mercy respond to being in this vast hall?"*

**Mechanical Note:** No skill check required; pure atmosphere. But note if player describes Mercy's discomfort or Arcana's sense of something *off* (will become relevant when Void-hints escalate).

## SCENE TWO: THE BASELINE LOCK CEREMONY (Main Event - 30-40 min)

### CEREMONY STAGING

**Location:** Central Nave (full assembly)  
**Participants Assembled:**
- Arcana & Mercy (center)
- 5 Dominae (positioned at cardinal points + center)
- Domain Prior Aldric (recording scribe, north platform)
- 40+ Acolytes (arranged in rows, observing)
- 3 Faction Representatives (in gallery above)
- Cathedral priestesses (senior clergy, scattered throughout)

**Ceremony Conductor:** Domina Seraphine (Traditionalist authority)

### CEREMONIAL STRUCTURE

**Opening: The Call to Witness**

*Domina Seraphine steps forward, voice carrying through the Nave with perfect clarity:*

**Seraphine's Opening:**
*"On this day, we gather to witness the Baseline Lock Ceremony — the formal integration of a priestess-mount into the priestess-community of the Cathedral. This is a sacred binding, recognized by priestess-tradition spanning centuries. Let all who witness understand: what is bound today is bound eternal, unless willingly released by mutual consent of priestess and mount."*

**Ceremonial Intent:**
- Establishes formal authority (Seraphine conducting, not Arcana; shows Cathedral's hierarchy)
- States the stakes: binding is "eternal" (unless released) — important for later Void-conflict
- Invokes "mutual consent" — will matter for Mercy's eventual choice regarding Void vs. Arcana

---

**The Eight Virtues Invocation**

*Seraphine moves to each pillar in sequence, touching it and speaking virtue-names:*

**Eight Virtues Spoken:**
1. **Discipline (Eastern Pillar):** "By obedience, grace is born"
2. **Precision (Southern Pillar):** "By architecture, claiming is claimed"
3. **Transcendence (Northern Pillar):** "By dissolution, consciousness becomes priestess"
4. **Devotion (Western Pillar):** "By hurt, proof of love is manifest"
5. **Honesty (Central Lower Pillar):** "By power acknowledged, unity is born"
6. **Witness (Central Upper Pillar):** "By world-knowing, eternity becomes real"
7. **Excellence (Eastern-Lower):** "By standard held sacred, becoming is blessed"
8. **Nature (Western-Lower):** "By primal, priestess and priestess-mount become one"

**Ceremony Notes:**
- As each virtue is invoked, acolytes wearing that school's collar stand briefly (shows Eight School organizational structure)
- Allows PC observation of Cathedral's internal ranking system
- *If using optional dice mechanics:* Arcana rolls d20 for each virtue-invocation (ceremonial roll; represents how well she's harmonizing with Cathedral's power; purely narrative but creates tension)

---

**THE BINDING RITUAL: Mercy's Baseline Lock**

*Seraphine signals Arcana to step forward with Mercy. The vast hall falls silent.*

**Arcana's Role:**
*"The priestess comes forward to formalize her claim and her bond. Priestess Arcana — speak your intention."*

**This is the Player's Major Roleplay Moment:**

*Offer Arcana opportunity to speak directly to Mercy and the assembled Cathedral about:*
- Why Mercy is significant
- What priestess-covenant means to her
- Her vision for Mercy's place in priestess-community
- Personal connection (intimacy conveyed publically, but appropriately)

**Example Framework (if player needs help):**
*"I claim this priestess-mount not as possession, but as recognition of her consciousness's native state. Mercy's being *is* beast-form — this is not transformation but homecoming. I pledge to honor this truth in all my priestess-work."*

**Allow 1-2 minutes for Arcana's personal statement.**

---

**The Collar Marking**

*Seraphine produces an ornate collar (color-coded to Eight Virtue Schools, with Arcana's personal sigil). The collar itself is a visible work of art — gem-work, metalcraft, sacred inscriptions.*

**Ceremonial Action:**
*Arcana places the collar on Mercy, closing the clasp. As she does, Arcana speaks:*

**Arcana's Oath:**
*"I bind myself to honor this priestess-mount's consciousness, her choices, her autonomy, and her sacred nature. My priestess-craft serves her becoming, not her diminishment."*

**Mechanical Note (optional d20):**
- Arcana rolls to see if her voice carries perfect confidence through the Nave
- Success (≥12): Her words resonate; Mercy feels the priestess-certainty strongly
- Failure: Some doubt appears in Arcana's voice; Mercy senses hesitation
- Purely narrative outcome; no mechanical penalty either way

---

**THE VOID-HINT MOMENT (Critical Story Element)**

*As Seraphine steps back and prepares to close the ceremony, something *shifts* in the Nave:*

**Description:**
*The eight pillars suddenly cast shadows that seem longer than they should be. For just a moment — maybe less — you see the shadows move *independent* of light source. The Echo Chamber behind the High Altar echoes with sound that has no source: a whisper so faint you can't make out words, but unmistakably there.*

*The temperature in the Nave drops noticeably. Breath becomes visible. Candles flicker though there's no breeze.*

*One acolyte in the audience (Desta, sitting near the Transcendence students) suddenly goes rigid. Her eyes roll back, and she speaks in a voice that is NOT her own:*

**Desta's Possessed Statement:**
*"The priestess-mount comes home. The old call grows louder. The threshold awakens."*

*Then she collapses; nearby acolytes rush to catch her.*

**PC Reactions:**
- Arcana may want to move toward Desta
- Mercy may react strongly (feeling the Void-presence directly for first time)
- Other NPCs will react according to faction:

**Seraphine:** *Maintains composure but looks troubled. Raises voice:* "The ceremony is concluded. Priestess and mount are bound. Let all depart to midday meal."
- *Internal thought (visible on face):* This shouldn't be happening. The old texts warned of this.

**Lucienne:** *Eyes widen with interest. Makes mental note.* *(To nearby Reformers, whispered):* "Did you hear that? Something's stirring below."

**Domain Prior Aldric:** *Moves to Desta immediately; checks her pulse, ensures acolyte medical-care.* *(Quietly, to nearby priestesses):* "Document this incident. Precisely as it occurred."

**Elestren:** *Closes eyes briefly, as if listening to something distant. Expression shifts between fear and recognition.*

---

### CEREMONY COMPLETION

*Despite the disturbance, Seraphine completes final words:*

**Seraphine's Closing:**
*"The Baseline Lock is established. Mercy is priestess-mount of the Cathedral of Eternal Grace, bound to Priestess Arcana in the eyes of priestess-tradition and this assembled community. Her consciousness is recognized as native to beast-form; her autonomy is honored; her welfare is Cathedral-responsibility."*

*The ceremony ends. Acolytes begin departure. But the atmosphere is now *charged* — something has shifted.*

**Esto:**
- Ceremony was successful (formal binding achieved)
- Void-hint was unmistakable (but ambiguous — could be misunderstood as collective delusion)
- Mercy's arrival has *triggered something* (now visible to sensitive observers like Elestren)
- Faction representatives noted everything (each will interpret differently)

## SCENE THREE: POLITICAL AFTERMATH (20-30 min)

After ceremony, before dinner, Arcana has time to interact with faction representatives. Each faction leader will approach *separately* (not wanting to appear coordinated).

### APPROACH 1: DOMINA SERAPHINE (Traditionalist)

**Setting:** Private corridor; Seraphine intercepts Arcana

**Seraphine's Tone:** Formal, measured, concerned

**Dialogue:**
- "Priestess Arcana. The ceremony was... eventful. That disturbance during the binding — I've never witnessed such phenomenon in thirty years of overseeing ceremonies."
- *Pause* — "Our Cathedral has long traditions about... unusual occurrences. The deepest archives contain texts suggesting that certain priestess-arrivals can trigger what we might call *resonances* with older priestess-entities."
- "I'm not saying anything definitive. But I would strongly recommend you review certain restrictions on Mercy's movement. The lower crypts, for instance, should be off-limits until we better understand what's occurring."
- *Cool eyes assess Arcana:* "For her safety, of course. And the Cathedral's."

**Subtext:**
- Seraphine is warning Arcana (not threatening)
- She knows something about the Void from forbidden texts
- She wants to *control* Mercy's access to Cathedral (restriction as protection, but also as control)
- She's testing if Arcana will defer to Traditionalist authority

**PC Opportunity:**
- Arcana can agree (defering to Cathedral authority; tactical choice)
- Arcana can push back (asserting her priestess-authority; creates tension)
- Arcana can request more information (triggers Seraphine to partially reveal her knowledge)

**Expected Result:** Seraphine will become either cautious ally (if Arcana shows deference) or suspicious rival (if Arcana asserts autonomy).

---

### APPROACH 2: DOMINA LUCIENNE (Reformer)

**Setting:** Semi-public hallway; Lucienne appears casually

**Lucienne's Tone:** Warm, conspiratorial, excited

**Dialogue:**
- "That was *extraordinary*! In all my years, I've never seen priestess-energy manifest so openly during a binding ceremony."
- *Lowers voice slightly* — "Between you and me, the Cathedral's old guard has been terrified of true spiritual power. They want to keep priestess-craft sterile, academic, *controlled*. But what we just witnessed — that's raw priestess-consciousness. That's what we should be cultivating."
- "I hope you'll consider attending some of our informal gatherings. We're exploring new interpretations of priestess-craft. Your perspective would be invaluable. And I suspect Mercy's sensitivity to spiritual manifestation could teach us things the Traditionalists have suppressed."

**Subtext:**
- Lucienne wants to recruit Arcana as ally in modernization
- She interprets Void-hint as evidence that priestess-tradition is alive and evolving
- She wants access to Mercy for study/understanding
- She's offering intellectual partnership + political alliance

**PC Opportunity:**
- Arcana can engage (exploring alliance possibilities)
- Arcana can express caution (keep distance; observe)
- Arcana can directly ask what Lucienne thinks the phenomenon means

**Expected Result:** Lucienne will become active ally (offering resources, knowledge, external contacts) if Arcana shows interest in "modernization."

---

### APPROACH 3: DOMAIN PRIOR ALDRIC & SISTER ELESTREN (Keepers)

**Setting:** Private chamber; both approach together

**Aldric's Tone:** Professional, offering practical support  
**Elestren's Tone:** Spiritual, somewhat unsettled

**Dialogue (Aldric first):**
- "Priestess, the ceremony was conducted with full authority and formality. Your mount's binding is secure in Cathedral records. If you require anything — chambers, resources, access to libraries — please inform me directly."
- "I'll also note: that disturbance was documented. If it occurs again, having clear records will be useful for understanding the phenomenon."

**Dialogue (Elestren, after Aldric):**
- *Eyes meeting yours with strange recognition* — "I sensed something during the binding. Not malevolent, but... vast. Aware. Your mount felt it too, didn't she?"
- *Soft voice* — "I've been practicing deep transcendence-work for many years. I'm familiar with the sensation of touching something far older than ourselves. I wanted you to know — you're not alone in perceiving unusual spiritual activity here. And if you wish to discuss what we're sensing, I'm available."

**Subtext:**
- Aldric is offering practical alliance (administrative support, documentation)
- Elestren is revealing that she knows *something* is happening (and possibly that she's been in contact with it)
- Keepers are positioning as "honest brokers" — not controlling, but observant
- They're inviting Arcana into investigation without pressuring her

**PC Opportunity:**
- Arcana can accept practical support (Aldric)
- Arcana can pursue spiritual discussion (Elestren)
- Arcana can ask direct questions about what they suspect
- Arcana can form preliminary alliance with Keepers

**Expected Result:** Keepers become most trustworthy allies; Elestren becomes key spiritual guide (with her own hidden Void-connection being revelation for later Acts).

## SCENE FOUR: MERCY'S INTEGRATION (Transition/Closing)

**Setting:** Private quarters or stable area; Arcana alone with Mercy after ceremony

**Scene Purpose:** 
- Show Mercy's response to the ceremony and Void-hint
- Establish emotional/spiritual bond between Arcana and Mercy
- Transition from arrival/ceremony to "settling in" (ready for S63)

**Suggested Approach:**

*After the political maneuvering, you find quiet space with Mercy. The binding-ceremony has formally locked her consciousness in beast-form, and you can sense something in her has... settled. Like coming home after long journey.*

*But she's also feeling something else — a faint pull, like distant music only she can hear. Her ears track toward the deep parts of the Cathedral even as she presses against your legs in contentment.*

**Offer Arcana Observations/Choices:**
- Does Arcana sense the pull that Mercy feels? (Perception check, if using mechanics)
- Can Arcana ask Mercy what she experienced during the Void-hint moment?
- How does Arcana want to address the strangeness with Mercy? (Reassurance? Investigation? Shared exploration?)

**Mercy's Perspective (if narrating for her):**
*"The baseline-locking feels right — your priestess-consciousness and my being are finally synchronized. But there's something else now. A voice that isn't you, calling from deep below. It's not threatening; it's... familiar somehow. Like home calling home."*

**Mechanical Note:**
This is where **Psyche Pool** becomes relevant. The Void-contact is *beginning* to drain Mercy's mental endurance (1-2 Psyche points). By next session, Arcana may notice Mercy is slightly more fatigued than expected, or having strange dreams.
