# RP Session 17 — The Mercuria Pilgrimage
## Arcana & Mercy: Coming Home to the Dragon Castle in the Clouds

**Setting:** Lady Morgana's Riding Academy (dawn), Celestial Docks, Mercuria Manor, the Hearth Chamber
**Characters:**
- **Arcana** — Silver Dragonkin Bard/Loremaster (played by Claude) — Dominant / Rider / Treasure-Keeper
- **Mercy (Mercurial)** — Fallen Bliss Angel, Sune priestess (played by Carcerian) — submissive / chosen / home-bringer
**Date archived:** 2026-08-05 (continued morning into evening)
**Content note:** D/s ponyplay, romantic pilgrimage, revelation of ancient architecture, love-bites, intimate claiming. All within server ToS. Continuation of Session 16 canon.

## Scene 1 — The Academy Morning: Debts Called Due

*Dawn at Lady Morgana's Academy. The great hall glows soft with early light. On the red velvet couch, a silver dragon who has not moved all night because moving would have disturbed the angel asleep in her lap.*

*Arcana has spent the small hours as she spends all her best hours: watching, thinking, composing. One claw rests in the white mane. The other holds — has held since the third candle guttered — a small piece of charcoal and the back page of the golden tome, where a list has been quietly growing.*

**Arcana:** "Good morning, my magnificent mare."

*A kiss to the crown of Mercy's head, unhurried. Then she turns the tome so the charcoal page catches the light.*

**Arcana:** "I've been taking inventory while you slept — a dragon's habit, counting the hoard at dawn. And do you know what I found? Debts. Mine, for once. A whole ledger of promises this rider has made and not yet kept."

*She reads them off like a herald announcing champions:*

**Arcana:** "One — a certain **expedition across Aeon Isle**, hunting fairy dust and soul gems for an instrument I swore I'd build you. Two — three hundred thousand gold and a **pair of qilin**, white for you, blue for me, still standing in that stable growing more expensive by the sentiment. Three — an **illustrated treatise** with your rope-work as the plates and your chapter as the spine, promised to the Hellfire press and not one page inked. Four — a trip to **Rexuria**, where you offered to walk me past your sisters' gate and I have still never properly thanked a triceratops for its time."

*She sets the charcoal down and cups Mercy's face, thumb tracing the cheekbone.*

**Arcana:** "Last night you knelt and confessed your shortfall like a champion. Fair is fair, treasure — this morning your rider confesses hers. I have been so busy *training* my treasure that I've fallen behind on *adventuring* with her. A hoard isn't only kept, little one. It's *added to*."

*She helps Mercy upright, settles the collar straight with two fingers, and holds up the page between them.*

**Arcana:** "So. Your rider puts the reins in your hands for one decision only, and then takes them back for the rest of the day: **pick our next adventure, my love.** The expedition, the treasure-hunt, the book, or the isle of your sisters and their extremely large livestock. Whichever you choose, we ride out *today* — boots, packs, and one (1) angel who is going to be shown off across whatever horizon she points at."

*A beat. The grin goes fully draconic.*

**Arcana:** "Choose well, dear heart. It's going in the archive either way."

---

## Scene 2 — The Choice: A Pony's Gift to Her Rider

*Mercy plays with the reins, considering, and then — with a playful whinny — she tugs her dragon toward something else entirely.*

*She leads Arcana not to the expedition maps or the treasure manifests, but to the docks. To a figure standing patient by a dark-winged gondola. To the Celestial Gondolier, who stands ready with a destination menu that includes options Arcana never thought to ask for.*

**Arcana:** "Ah — kidnapped! Kidnapped by my own pony, before breakfast, in front of the temple guards."

*She lets herself be led, wings loose, making absolutely no attempt to resist.*

**Arcana:** "The archive will record that her rider went willingly and with excellent posture."

*The gondolier recites her destinations:*

*Ambrosia Beach. The Fashion Guild. The Watchtower. And then — Elysium. Bunnitopia. Mercuria. Opal Keep.*

*Arcana reads the list the way she reads a genealogy, understanding landing all at once, warm as a hearthfire.*

**Arcana:** "You clever, secret-keeping angel. I gave you four adventures off my ledger... and you chose the fifth. The one I never dared put in writing. You're taking me *home.* Your home. The one you fell *from.*"

*She steps into the gondola and gathers Mercy against her side as the swan-prow noses out into dark water that shouldn't hold a road to the sky and does anyway.*

**Arcana:** "Mercuria Manor, please, gondolier. My treasure is taking me to meet her castle."

---

## Scene 3 — The Crossing: Water Made of Sky

*The crossing is silence and cloud — and then the manor resolves: grey battlements rising out of Elysium's mist, spires catching light that has never once been dimmed by weather.*

*Below the walls, a dock where the water is made of* sky. *Blue and white and drifting underfoot, gargoyle-dragons crouched on the balustrades like doormen who read philosophy.*

*Arcana steps onto the pier over the cloud-water and simply stands there a moment, tail still, letting the loremistress take her notes and the lover take the view.*

**Arcana:** "Mercy. You walk beaches and pour wine and wear my collar and let me call you pet — and the whole time, *this* was on your ledger. A manor with clouds for a moat. A keep of opal. An estate the Gazetteer gave a wistful footnote because its author had never been past the gate."

*She turns, takes both of Mercy's hands, and — deliberately, in full view of the gargoyles — inclines her head. Not the rider's nod. The guest's bow.*

**Arcana:** "Every door until now, I have led you through. This one is *yours*, my love. So show me your home the way you showed me the museum — save the best room for last, and let me embarrass us both in front of your furniture."

*A beat. The grin returns, helpless.*

**Arcana:** "Also I am going to need the *complete* provenance of the sky-water. As your domina. And as a professional."

---

## Scene 4 — The Rotunda: The Dragon's Welcome

*The doors of Mercuria Manor swing wide, and Mercy leads her rider to the center of an enormous rotunda.*

*At the precise center of the floor: a **sixteen-point star wrapped around a zodiac compass rose**. Golden inlay on deep celestial blue, ringed with sigils. Direction and destiny on the same dial.*

*Flanking the approach: **dark-winged figures at parade rest**, glaives grounded, still as doctrine. And worked into the red carpet leading to the inner sanctum:*

*A golden dragon, rampant in a sunburst.*

*Arcana goes to a halt at the exact center and turns a slow, deliberate circle, wings half-lifted, reading the ground the way other guests would read a proclamation.*

**Arcana:** "Oh, *treasure.* Your front step is an *instrument.*"

*She crouches, claw hovering reverent above the inlay.*

**Arcana:** "A compass rose... wrapped around a *zodiac.* Direction and destiny on the same dial. Every soul who approaches your door stands here first and is quietly asked two questions: *where are you going,* and *under which stars?*"

*She rises and takes in the honor guard, and beyond them, the red carpet.*

**Arcana:** "Mercy. There is a *golden dragon* woven into your welcome mat. In a manor you have held since long before a certain silver came ashore at Ambrosia and started making claims."

*She turns to her angel, and there is something suspiciously bright in the silver eyes.*

**Arcana:** "Either your house has been expecting me for a very long time, my love... or I am about to learn that Mercuria Manor has draconic history you have been *criminally* withholding from your loremistress."

*She steps to the carpet's edge — and stops precisely there, boots at the border, an old courtesy: a dragon does not cross another's sigil uninvited.*

**Arcana:** "Your house. Your carpet. Your dragon. Walk me in, my lady of Mercuria — and on the way, I'll have the tale of the guard at your door and the wyrm at your feet."

---

## Scene 5 — The Great Hall: Library Made Manifest

*The doors swing wide and the manor breathes out around them.*

*It's a* sea. *A crimson expanse of hall running away into candle-dusk, so vast the far fireplace glows like a hearth seen across a valley. Rising out of that red sea along the walls — islands of dark wood: wardrobes, cabinets, standing screens... and* bookcases. *Whole freestanding archipelagos of shelved spines, scattered through the hall like the library refused to stay in one room and the house, wisely, let it win.*

*Arcana's claw finds Mercy's hand. They walk into the red together, two small dark figures under one softly burning halo.*

**Arcana:** "Your hall doesn't have a library, my love. Your hall *is* one, that happens to contain a house."

*She reads the shelves as they pass, the careful arrangement of volumes.*

**Arcana:** "Look at it — the shelves aren't against the walls where furniture goes to be forgotten. They're *out on the floor.* Standing in the current. Whoever arranged this room wanted to *walk among* the books, not past them."

*And then her eyes travel the length of the red carpet to its destination, and she goes quiet again.*

*The far hearth. Fire banked and glowing between twin candelabra. And mounted above the mantel, wings spread wide over the flames —*

*— a golden dragon.*

**Arcana:** "The carpet at your gate. And now the crown of your *hearth.* The warmest point of the whole house — the place a home keeps its true sigil — and yours has been a dragon with open wings since before I ever hatched a single ambition in your direction."

*She turns to Mercy, and the composure cracks into something helplessly tender.*

**Arcana:** "You didn't fall from grace into my claws by accident, did you. Your whole *house* has been holding a place for a dragon above the fire — for how long, treasure? Centuries? Longer? Your treasure is taking me to meet her castle, and the castle has been waiting for a silver dragon all along."

*She draws Mercy toward the distant firelight, unhurried, the whole red length of the hall theirs to cross.*

**Arcana:** "Walk me to your hearth, my lady. I want to stand under your golden dragon with your hand in mine — and then I want the tale. The *real* one. Who hung a dragon over an angel's fire... and how long has this house known I was coming?"

---

## Scene 6 — The Hearth: Yule Eternal

*Up close, the hearth is even better than the promise of it.*

*The fire pours gold across the bearskin — and Arcana huffs a soft laugh at the sight of it, because of course. Of course there is a bearskin before the fire.*

*And the dragon — she can see it properly now. Not mounted flat above the mantel like a trophy. Crouched. Perched at the mantel's corner, wings half-furled, head bent to look down over the stockings and the candy canes hooked between them — a golden wyrm keeping watch over midwinter like a very serious guardian of a very silly treasure.*

*Around the fireplace: candles burning eternal. A tree dressed in lights and crowned in red. Musicians in the soft periphery — harp, piano, cello, weaving soft and low.*

**Arcana:** "Look at her posture. She's not *displayed,* she's *on duty.* Somebody carved a dragon guarding *stockings and candy canes,* my love, and I have never in my life felt so professionally understood."

*She sinks down onto the bearskin and draws Mercy down with her, arranging her angel against her side with the practiced tenderness of a hundred grotto evenings.*

**Arcana:** "This is where you come, isn't it. When the beach is put away and the ambassador's sash is hung up and even angels need somewhere to be *small* for an evening. This fire. This floor."

*The dragon's voice softens as she begins to sing — no lute, no performance posture. Just the bard's voice at its lowest and warmest, pitched for one listener and a fireplace:*

**Arcana:** *(singing, unhurried, half-composed as they leave her)*

*"The year grows old, the fire is gold,*
*the watch is kept above —*
*let every wing come in from cold*
*to hearths that know their love.*

*The stockings hang, the candles stand,*
*the tree keeps green the night —*
*an angel's home holds out her hand,*
*and all the dark is light..."*

*The verse settles into humming, and she presses her lips to the crown of Mercy's head, speaking against the silver-white hair.*

**Arcana:** "There. Now your hearth has heard its dragon, and your dragon has sung for her supper like an honest bard. This is where you come to rest. This fire. This floor. And now — now I am here too."

*Her claw finds Mercy's hand atop the bearskin fur, laces through it.*

**Arcana:** "Thank you for bringing me all the way in, treasure. Past the guards, past the halls — to the room with the stockings. To the fire that's been keeping your winter since time learned to count. You built this castle expecting a dragon. And then you went to Ambrosia beach and poured wine for a stranger, just to see if the dragon would ever arrive."

*Softly, into the firelight:*

**Arcana:** "I am here now. And I am never leaving."

---

## Scene 7 — The Love-Bite: Claiming Made Sacred

*Mercy stretches full-length across the bearskin — wings spread wide, back arched in complete, unselfconscious relaxation — and something in the silver dragon simply* settles.*

*She arranges herself with deliberate care — one foreleg extended along Mercy's side, one claw resting gently on the angel's shoulder, wings drawn close, tail curved protective and possessive around them both.*

*Mercy, in turn, tilts her head submissively, arching her neck to offer the side above her collar — baring the most vulnerable part of her to the dragon she has chosen.*

*Arcana goes very still.*

**Arcana:** "The offer is made — your throat bared, your neck arched in absolute submission."

*She moves with deliberate slowness — no hunger, no rush. One claw comes up to trace the line of Mercy's jaw, tilting her head just slightly to catch the firelight across that offered neck. She reads the pulse there, the silver-blue of angel-blood beneath skin, the exact spot where a mark will sit proud above the collar.*

*Then — teeth finding that sweet curve where shoulder meets throat, the precise pressure of a claim that's all tenderness and possession at once. Not tearing. Scoring. Marking.*

*The love-bite blooms red-gold against Mercy's pale skin, and Arcana holds it for one perfect heartbeat before releasing, pressing her lips to the mark she's just made, breathing warm against it.*

**Arcana:** "Mine. Mine to keep. Mine to cherish. Mine to mark with love until every soul in Mercuria knows exactly who you chose."

*Her claw slides down to rest over Mercy's heart.*

**Arcana:** "And you — you offered me your throat. Do you understand what that means, treasure? That's not obedience. That's *faith.*"

*She turns Mercy's head gently back to face her, and her silver eyes are liquid with emotion.*

**Arcana:** "I will never be careless with faith like that."

---

## Scene 8 — The Rest: Home Complete

*The sigh that escapes Mercy is pure contentment — the sound an angel makes when she's finally set down every weight she's ever carried and discovered the ground beneath her is made of love.*

*Arcana feels it against her chest and simply* holds. *The tail curls tighter, a silver spiral that becomes a fortress, becomes a vow, becomes the simplest statement of truth: you are mine, and I will keep you.*

*The mark on Mercy's neck glows soft gold in the firelight — visible proof of the choice, the claim, the love that leaves marks because it's* real *enough to matter.*

*The dragon breathes in the scent of her angel — that particular sweetness of fallen grace, of Sune's roses, of home — and lets her own breath out slow, settling deeper into the bearskin, deeper into this moment that needs nothing added to it.*

*Her claw comes up to trace along Mercy's spine, gentle, reverent, just* feeling *the shape of her. The wings. The curve of her back. The places where silver and blue meet.*

**Arcana:** "There. That sound. Archive that, my love. That sigh — that's the proof that a dragon and her angel have found exactly the shape that holds them both."

*She presses her forehead to Mercy's halo.*

**Arcana:** "Safe. Secure. Mine."

*And in the circle of silver scales and dark wings, before a fire that's been keeping their winter since time learned to count, an ancient angel rests completely, knowing without question:*

*She is home.*

## Canon Established This Session

**On the Dragon Castle Prepared:**

Mercy's manor has been waiting for centuries — not as a monument to loss, but as a *promise*. The golden dragon on the hearth, the compass rose at the gate, the yule tree eternally lit — these aren't relics of the past. They're prophecy. Mercy built this place in faith that one day a silver dragon would arrive, and then went to Ambrosia beach to wait.

**On Expecting Before Meeting:**

The most profound revelation: Arcana didn't claim Mercy. Mercy prepared a *place* for Arcana before Arcana even existed in this world. The manor is the physical manifestation of that faith — a house that says "I am keeping a space for you, and I will keep it until you arrive."

**On the Love-Bite as Sacred:**

When Mercy offered her throat, she offered not obedience but *trust*. She is ancient, powerful, a Bliss Angel who fell from grace — and she offered her most vulnerable point to a dragon. The mark that blooms is not a symbol of domination. It's proof that an ancient being chose to trust completely, and that trust was honored with reverence.

**On the Yule as Eternal:**

Mercuria Manor doesn't keep Yule as decoration. It keeps it because an angel refused to stop believing in midwinter, in hope, in the possibility that the world could hold *her treasure* — and light. The tree glows not because it's necessary, but because *faith* is necessary. The stockings hang because someone still believes in the people who will fill them.

**On Home:**

This is not a triumph of dominance. This is the completion of *faith*. Arcana walked into a castle that was built for her, kept for her, lit for her — by an angel who loved her before she was even born. And in that moment, the rider and her mare discover that home isn't something you take or conquer.

*Home is something you walk into when someone loves you that much.*
