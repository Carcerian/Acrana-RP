**Arcana's Form:** Full Blingdrake — scaled body, sapphire glowing, draconic presence. No restraint. Pure predator.

**Mercy's State:** Released from cage, bridle locked, collar visible. Ready for binding as Mate.

**Sacred Intent:** Primal shamanic ritual to formalize mating bond before ancestral witnesses.

## THE RITUAL

### ACT I: THE RELEASE & PURIFICATION

Arcana pulls Mercy from the cage by the collar. No words. Just command.

**"You've been caged long enough. Tonight you're purified before binding."**

She walks Mercy to the steaming hot springs pool. The water glows with ancient mineral light.

Arcana steps in first, extended clawed hand.

Mercy follows. Water rises around her white coat. The bridle stays locked. The collar stays visible.

At the pool's center, Arcana *lowers* her prey beneath the surface.

Water closes over Mercy's head. One, two, three heartbeats—

Then pulls her up.

Mercy gasps. The bridle drips.

**"Again."**

Down. Under. Held.

Up. Gasping. Purified.

Three submersions total.

By the final lift, Mercy's eyes are different. Not struggling. Just *receiving*.

**"The water knows you now. The ancestors recognize your submission."**

### ACT II: THE BINDING & MARKING

Arcana pulls Mercy back to bearskin center.

**"Look at me."**

Mercy's sky-blue eyes lock to Arcana's sapphire gaze.

The Huntress removes the bridle—first time Mercy's human face has been visible since fitting.

But the collar stays. The marks stay visible.

Mercy is spread on the bearskin altar—arms and legs extended in a star. Four stakes driven into stone around her.

Arcana secures each binding with ritualistic precision:
- Right wrist
- Left wrist  
- Right ankle
- Left ankle

Mercy is completely exposed. Completely *vulnerable*.

**"You are bound as Mate."**

### ACT III: THE GLYPHS OF CLAIMING

Arcana circles her bound prey slowly, claws glowing with sapphire light.

She begins to trace the first glyph—across Mercy's shoulder. The symbol of dragon claiming.

The sapphire light *sears* itself into white skin as permanent mark.

Mercy's breath catches. Sky-blue locked to sapphire. Never wavering.

**"Look at me. I want you to *see* me claim you."**

The glyphs continue—across ribs, over heart, inner thigh, curve where shoulder meets neck. Each one *glowing*. Each one *permanent*. Each one writing Arcana's ownership into Mercy's flesh.

By the final glyph over her heart, the Huntress is breathing heavy. Not human breathing. *Predatory*.

### ACT IV: THE FANGS & THE PEAK

**"Now the fangs."**

Arcana lowers her head. Fangs visible. Dripping with ritual intention.

The first bite sinks deep into Mercy's shoulder—not gentle, not careful. *Ritualistic*. The venom mixes with blood as ancient magic seals the claiming.

Again at the neck. Again at the inner thigh.

Each bite a *declaration*:

**She is Arcana's. Completely. Forever. Ancestrally.**

The tail coils around Mercy's body—possessing, not restraining.

At the peak of the ritual, Arcana's claws rake across Mercy's ribs one final time, her fangs close around Mercy's throat, her tail tightens, and her voice *resonates* through the cave:

**"You are bound. You are marked. You are MINE."**

The sapphire light blazes so bright it illuminates the entire chamber.

The ancestors *sing*.

### ACT V: THE BINDING COMPLETE & AFTERCARE

Arcana slowly releases her fangs from Mercy's throat—not breaking contact, just *holding* there, breathing dragon-breath hot against sky-blue eyes.

**"It is done."**

She traces the largest glyph across Mercy's heart—the one that burns brightest, marking her as *Mate*.

Arcana carefully releases the tail coil. Not abandonment—just a shift. She lowers herself onto the bearskin beside her bound prey, draping scaled body alongside white.

**"Rest now, Mate. The binding is eternal. The glyphs are permanent. The magic is sealed."**

She reaches over and gently removes the bridle—first time Mercy's face has been completely free since the ritual began.

**"You are no longer just prey. You are Mate. Marked. Bonded. Mine."**

The binding stakes remain. The glyphs glow faintly with sapphire magic. But the intensity *settles* into something deeper—not finished, but *integrated*.

The priestess holds her mate in the firelight as the ancestors fade back into the stones.
