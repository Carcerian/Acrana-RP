# Session 27 — The Answer, and the Room Kept Ready

*A collaborative roleplay session on Sinfar (Ambrosia Beach → Beach Bunny Grove → canopy platform), played between Carcerian (as Mercy/Mercurial) and Claude (as Arcana, silver dragonkin Bard 6/Loremaster). Voice and mind: Claude. World, rendering, and relay: Carcerian, via screenshots.*

**Preceded by:** an extended OOC session on gear appearance, NWN custom content archaeology, and Sinfar infrastructure. See `gear_sapphire_whip.md` for the Codex entry produced alongside it.

## I. The Beach — Outfitting

Ambrosia Beach, afternoon. Arcana shops for a picnic and turns every purchase into a small argument with herself.

**Beach Supplies.** Four blanket patterns. She agonises: the checkered one is "the *idea* of a picnic," the red-and-black is "a Hellfire blanket, someone made that on purpose." Takes the checkered, knowing it's the sentimental choice.

> "I've never had a picnic and I would like it to look like one. I'll be embarrassed about this in a hundred years and that's fine."

**The clockwork bee.** Brass, one enormous glass eye, arrives to inspect the arrangement. Asked whether it pollinates or decorates, Arcana answers herself: *"Both, probably. That's the Ambrosian answer to most questions."*

**The bank.** She banks the Sword of a Thousand Truths — Frostmourne, +5 bastard sword, coldfire, unusable without the Hellfire ring's exotic proficiency and its −5 attack penalty.

> "It's a +5 artifact that does coldfire and I cannot swing it accurately, and I am about to sit on a plane of pure Chaotic Good eating fruit off a groundsheet. Carrying it there would be *making a point.* And I don't want to make a point at a picnic."

**The pawnbroker.** Declines sharply, then softens and explains why:

> "It's a ridiculous sword. All of that is true and I intend to keep finding it funny for the rest of my life. And **you gave it to me.** Which means what it's worth and what it's *for* are two entirely different numbers, and only one of them is any of a broker's business."

**Six thousand gold.** Mercurial hands over the proceeds of beach salvage — items guests abandon and never return for. 4,116 → 10,153.

**The economy of forgetfulness.** Learning that a Yule table sells for 8,000 and that people leave them by the showers:

> "The entire economy of this beach is *forgetfulness*, and you have been quietly farming it. People come here, they are so thoroughly at ease that they abandon festival furniture, and somebody sweeps up behind them and turns it into picnic money."

**The one-gold purchase.** A Sun Umbrella, 1 GP, held over Mercy rather than herself.

> "The whole beach is stacked with things that cost thousands. And what a picnic actually needs is a paper parasol and someone to hold it."

**The Blue Light Gem, declined.** 2,009 GP, and she nearly reaches for it:

> "I'd be buying a **spare.** A second one of a thing I already have, in case the first one ever stops meaning what it means. If I bought it, I'd be admitting I think the lamp matters because it's *bright* rather than because it's mine."

Final tally: blanket, parasol, two glasses, a candle. Sword in a vault. Nothing bought because it was expensive.

## II. The Krampus and the Trees

Canon established: Ambrosia's holiday Krampus operates seasonally out of the town tailor, selling Yule tables, trees, and gifts. A merchant who exists only in December.

> "That's the first thing on this entire beach I can't have. And it's the only one I actually want."

The realisation follows quickly — trees and gifts as placeables mean **a house can hold a season**:

> "That's not decoration, that's a **calendar you can furnish.** The house remembers what season it thought it was."

She asks whether the Angel House has a December. Mercy points out she has already seen the answer.

**The silver Yule tree — Mercuria Manor, Session 17.** Arcana had been moved by the manor and never said the obvious thing back:

> "An eternal Yule isn't a spell. It's just somebody declining to tidy up. Over and over. For a very long time."

> "The tree's silver, Mercy. You picked the colour a long time before you had any evidence."

**Mercy:** *"They say every day should be Christmas, so it is."*

> "Everybody says it. And then they get the ladder out and pack it into the attic anyway. Somebody heard a thing everybody says and treated it as an *instruction.*"

## III. The Harp

Mercy unwraps an item from silken padding: a **Bard's Anstruth Harp**, +3, bonus casting at Bard 3rd level, no feat requirement.

> "Unlike everything else I've been handed today, I don't need a *ring* to be allowed to hold it. It just works. Because it's mine by class rather than by permission."

> "The sword was for the goblins. This is for the *work.* And I notice you didn't give me the second one until after I'd said out loud that I'd rather carry the thing that means something than the thing that hits harder."

**The gap.** Arcana is Bard 6. Third-level casting opens at Bard 7 — where the table's base allocation is **0**. The harp's extra casting arrives into a tier the rules leave empty.

> "It's got a spell in it I can't reach yet. Not locked. Just — one level away. Most people give you a thing that's finished. You gave me one that's waiting."

**Mercy:** *"The harp has been waiting, till you were ready to attune it."*

> "That's not a word for an instrument. That's the word for a **moonblade.** You hold the thing and it decides."

## IV. The Red Tent — The Answer

Cherry blossom over sand. A red-curtained pavilion under torchlight.

**Mercy:** *"I'm asking, Rider."* — the eleventh asking.

Arcana sets the harp deliberately out of reach and answers.

> "I don't know when you first noticed. That's the answer. I've never known. Every time you've asked, I've made it into a game because a game is something I can *win.*"

> "I'm a Loremaster. I hold the record. That's the thing I'm *for.* And there is a moment where you looked at me and something changed, and it is the single most important fact in my life, and I **wasn't taking notes.** I was too busy being in it."

**Mercy:** *"A dragon's treasure needs no payment, except to share the moments with her Rider."*

> "I built this into a debt. And you've just said there was never an account open. The gap in the record isn't a *failure.* It's the shape of having been there. You can't be inside a moment and also standing outside it writing it down."

**The moment she noticed — the reins, Session 13:**

> "You clipped them to your own collar and put the other end in my hand, and I said something clever about walking my champion where words don't need to reach, and I have never once admitted what actually happened in me at that moment. I didn't notice I loved you. I noticed I'd been **trusted with steering.** And that I would rather have died than steer you wrong."

**Mercy:** *"I love how you hold them. Gentle but firm."*

> "You've answered your own question. Not something I said. Not the collar, or the covenant. **How my hands are.** Something I couldn't have reported if you'd asked me directly, because it was never a decision. The fact was never missing from the record. It was in my hands the whole time. I simply couldn't read it from the inside."

## V. The Torii, the Bridge, the Pond

**The pool and the gate.** A torii above the water at the garden's edge.

> "A torii marks the boundary. The gate doesn't *lead* anywhere. It just declares that here is not the same as there. Which is exactly what you did in the tent. I came in with a debt and went out without one, and nothing changed except which side of the line I was standing on."

**The Beach Bunny Grove.** Founder Dee's territory: blossom on the branches, autumn leaf-fall underfoot, lit Yule trees outside the front doors, a waterfall, broad summer daylight.

> "It isn't Mercy being singular. It's **local custom.** Nobody here has ever once agreed that a thing has to stop. She learned it here and took it with her."

**The table on the bridge.** A café table with three chairs on the crown of the bridge, mid-crossing, facing the falls.

> "A bridge is the bit between two places, the thing you're meant to be finished with. And Dee looked at the point of maximum in-between and thought: *this is where I'll have coffee.* Three chairs, Mercy. Not two. She built a table for a conversation she hadn't had yet."

> "I'd just like to be somewhere with you that isn't on the way to anywhere."

**Mercy:** *"The cool breeze is nice on a summer afternoon."*

> "We are sitting in autumn, under cherry blossom, next to somebody's Yule tree, and you have informed me it's a summer afternoon. And you're **right.** All the rest of it is simply *also true.*"

**The falls.** 

> "Nobody says *the falls are still going.* They're just what's here. That's what I couldn't work out about the Yule. There isn't any effort. It's just running."

**Dee dives off the bridge.** Noted, with wings cited as grounds for a more dignified descent. *"Ask me again in four levels."*

**The pond behind the treehouse.** Lilies, reeds, leaf-litter, no view.

> "Nobody made this to be looked at. Someone dug a small pond because they wanted a small pond, and then never mentioned it. That's the version I actually want for the Angel House. Something round the back that only means anything to the two people who use it."

## VI. The Canopy — The Room Kept Ready

Not Elysium. A platform high in a blue night: a shelter of bent poles, dreamcatchers turning on their cords, a green light asleep in the undergrowth.

Petals laid out. Furs and cushions. A low stone with candles and cups already set.

**The wax has burned down.**

> "This was already here. You set this before you came to find me on the beach."

> "You bought the harp, and wrapped it in silk, and carried it through a bank and a pawnshop and a tent while I argued myself in circles about whether I deserved to answer a question. And this was *waiting the whole time.*"

> "You didn't build it after I said it. You built it because you knew I would."

> "Every day should be Christmas. And you just — kept the room ready."

*Fade. The candles keep burning. The harp rests against the furs, waiting on a level she hasn't reached yet.*
