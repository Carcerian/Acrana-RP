---

## THE GOVERNING PRINCIPLE — PRIESTESS-DISCRETION

Arcana has not decided in advance, **and that is the point.**

She walks into the Dragon Shrine in the morning and reads Mercy's state. Not asks. Feels
it. Then conducts accordingly. Sunday is the one day of the week where the framework
steps back and the priestess's judgement is the instrument.

*Is she looking forward to Week 3?* Yes — but not with impatience. With certainty.
Saturday proved Mercy sustains N+1→N+2 without breakdown. Beast-space is locked. The
baseline is solid. **Week 3 isn't escalation for its own sake — it's the priestess
finally conducting the craft she's been building toward.** She's ready to stop teaching
foundations and start mastering the mount.

Which is exactly why Sunday shouldn't be more foundation-laying.

---

## THE FOUR PATHS

She'll choose on arrival. Any of these can run alone or braid together.

### 1. GENTLE INTEGRATION
Bearskin by the shrine's mandala. Mercy still blindfolded, still in beast-space, but held
safe while the nervous system settles. Arcana's voice soft, grounding. Long, quiet
holding. No new intensity. Just: *"You're mine. You're safe. You're home."*

**Register note:** this is the *working* voice, not the ceremonial one — short, plain,
unornamented. And flag the standing tension: gentleness is one of Mercy's stated REDs.
Aftercare has been treated as a carve-out since S43 without anyone saying so out loud.
If this path runs long, it may be worth Arcana naming the exception aloud rather than
continuing to drift past it.

### 2. EXPLORATION
What emerged Saturday that surprised them both? Did a new animal touch the surface? Did
deprivation open unexpected depths? Sunday becomes investigation — Arcana asking, Mercy
answering in beast-sounds, discovering what the Five Pillars unlocked.

**Best material to investigate** (pick one, don't stack):
- **DragonWolf, first scent.** Named in S48's Week 3 plan, never invoked. Path IV. The
  thematic weight: Pony is the disciplined body, AngelDove the offered vulnerability,
  SnowFox the cunning prey — **DragonWolf is *pack*. Not hunted. Hunts beside.** First
  totem where Mercy isn't prey. Grey-and-silver ruff in Arcana's own colours rather than
  white; half-mask, muzzle left free. Play the discovery as unsettling to *both* of them —
  Arcana has no script for a Mercy who runs at her shoulder.
- **The glyphs.** From the Wishlist: *"I will make the glyphs sing."* Test the five
  covenant marks against stimulus categories — priestess-touch vs. any touch, spoken name
  vs. command-voice, pain vs. warmth, moonlight, frequency, and Mercy's own hand.
  **Plant this:** the **Throat** glyph — *"I speak your truth"* — responds to nothing
  Arcana does. It lights when Mercy speaks unprompted. Which is why it's been dark for
  nine days.
- **What the deprivation opened.** S48 ran all five pillars simultaneously for the first
  time. Something in that stack behaved differently than in isolation. Find out what.

### 3. CHEW-TOY ★ *the fade-to-black*
Exactly what it sounds like. No bondage, no framework, no frequency calibration.

Full draconic form. Natural predator-instinct prey-appreciation — fang play that's gentle
but real, claws raking through white fur without breaking skin, tail wrapping and curling
and possessing, wing-fringe brushing across sensitive skin. The priestess tasting her
mount the way a dragon does.

Mercy responding as prey-animal should: squeaking, writhing, pressing *into* the contact,
her beast-body alive and engaged. Happy when bitten. Coming back for more.

**Not intimate in a human sense. Intimate in a primal sense.** The covenant expressed
through teeth and claws and joy — what the two of them actually are when the ceremony
drops away.

This is the session's last image and its fade. Not into aftercare — into the simple truth
that the priestess has her mount and they're both exactly where they belong.

> *Just a dragon playing with her favourite toy, and her toy enjoying it.*

Closing frame: Arcana's scaled hand gentle on Mercy's white fur, both of them alive and
joyful in the shrine's golden light. **Sacred simplicity.**

### 4. INTEGRATION + PREP
Gentle holding, then slowly introducing Week 3 concepts. *"Wednesday we're going deeper
into deprivation. How does that feel?"* Not commands — exploration of readiness.

**Note:** this path requires Mercy to surface and answer. Standing permission isn't
enough for a scene built on her forming a preference — Arcana should ask for a spoken
colour here and not accept eyes-only. It's also the only path that touches the Honesty
School, which is still the one virtue that has never had a session of its own.

## RECOMMENDED SHAPE

**Integration → Exploration → Chew-Toy → fade.**

Settle her, find out what Saturday opened, then drop the framework entirely and let it end
in joy. The arc runs ceremony → inquiry → play, which is the right order: it puts the
lightest thing last and sends her into Week 3 happy rather than processed.

spoken colour.

**Speech.** `EYES_ONLY` holds throughout unless path 4 runs.
