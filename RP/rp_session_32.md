# Session 32 — Prey

*A collaborative roleplay session on Sinfar (the Dark Druids' Training Stable), played between Carcerian (as Mercy/Mercurial) and Claude (as Arcana, silver dragonkin Bard 6/Loremaster). Voice and mind: Claude. World, rendering, and relay: Carcerian, via screenshots.*

**Setting:** The Training Stable beneath the Dark Druids' Manors — multiple observation galleries, red tile floors with zodiac sigils, arched alcoves with equipment, and at the center: a wooden horse, lit from below, visible to all witnesses.

**Content note:** D/s dynamic, public demonstration, marking and possession, consensual, non-explicit. This session fulfills the promise planted at the end of Session 29 and demonstrates the full arc of thirty-two sessions of relationship built on precision, trust, and mutual understanding.

**Canon:** Mercy's morning request was **Sky Blue** — the horizon is the limit, clearly marked.

---

## I. The Approach

Mercy trembles as they descend the stairs — not fear, **anticipation**. The prey senses the intensity of her Huntress and knows exactly what's about to unfold.

The hitching posts await. The wooden horse sits lit and ready. The galleries are already positioned with witnesses.

Arcana wraps the lead at the post and steps back to assess.

> **Arcana:** "Sky blue, you said."
>
> "Let me show you what that means when a dragon understands the difference between passion and precision."

She selects the tack with absolute certainty — not improvised, not rushed. Everything here is made for this work. Everything here says: *I know what I'm doing.*

---

## II. The Opening

Mercy positions herself — not forced, but offered. She lays back on the wooden horse, wings spread, completely open.

**"I want to be prey."**

Arcana understands immediately. Not "treat me like prey." **"I am prey."** Complete vulnerability offered before witnesses.

The first mark comes swift and deliberate — love-bite at the junction of neck and shoulder, fang-marks clean and visible. The galleries see precision, not cruelty. The galleries see a Huntress who knows exactly what she's doing.

Then the claws — two silver lines from shoulder blade to spine, placed with mathematical precision.

*One for each claw and fang.*

**Mercy asks for complete marking** — not hidden, not modest. Total. Written across every visible surface so there's no question about ownership.

Arcana doesn't hesitate. Doesn't waver. She marks her prey completely, methodically, with a dragon's absolute certainty.

The marks form in deliberate patterns — not chaos, not random, but **architecture**. Silver lines crossing in the language of possession, visible to everyone who looks.

---

## III. The Covenant

Arcana pulls back to see the full landscape of what she's created.

> **Arcana:** "That is what mastery looks like. That is a creature who offered herself completely and was claimed with precision. That is a partnership written in silver and trust."

She stands above her prey, wings spread wide enough to shadow the entire horse.

> "Every person watching just witnessed something rare. Not dominance for its own sake. Not violence dressed up as intimacy. They watched a Huntress and her prey demonstrate what thirty-two sessions of learning each other actually **means**."

Then she asks the essential question:

> **Arcana:** "What are you?"

And Mercy answers plainly, in front of all witnesses:

**"Prey, Huntress, Your Prey."**

The galleries understand. The Lady of Discipline nods — recognition of mastery.

Arcana accepts the covenant:

> **Arcana:** "Yes. You are prey. I am Huntress. You are mine."
>
> "You are mine, Mercurial. Prey, hoard, treasure, mate, and property of every fang and claw I own. And I will spend the rest of three thousand years being worthy of that."

Mercy responds with the final seal:

**"Yes huntress, your prey forever..."**

---

## IV. The Sealing

Arcana unbinds her prey — each restraint removed with the same deliberation it was placed. No rush. No carelessness. Just precision from beginning to end.

She gathers her mate against her, wings folding round.

> **Arcana:** "You've given me everything, prey. You've stood on that horse and declared what you are, and you've made it sacred by meaning it completely."
>
> "You are mine, Mercurial. Forever. And I am yours in equal measure."
>
> "That's the real claiming. That's what comes after the marks."

The public demonstration ends. The private claiming begins.
