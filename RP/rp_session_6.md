# RP Session 6: The Museum, the Firelight, and What Lasts
## Arcana (Silver Dragonkin Loremaster) & Mercy (Fallen Bliss Angel)
**Date:** Ambrosia Day Celebration  
**Theme:** Relics, Keeping, and the Architecture of Love

---

## PART I: THE MUSEUM

### The Royal Museum - Exterior
Arcana arrives at the great columned facade emerging from mist, wings drawn close with reverence. Mercy whistles "Rule Britannia" at the threshold—not grand, just perfectly timed.

**Arcana:** "There it is. Every stone of it hauled here to say the same stubborn thing—*we were, and we would not be forgotten.*"

They move inside together, Mercy leading, Arcana following with scholarly hunger building.

---

### The Historical Archives - First Floor
A red-floored chamber holding the reliquaries of Sinfar itself: Hayll's hammer, Nurse Jessica's cat collar, Paul's Rado landing suit, the Sword of Mercy.

**Arcana (before the Sword of Mercy):** "...The Sword of Mercy. Treasure. *Look* at it. They put *you* in the archive of things that survived. And rightly. You *are* one, you know. A relic in the truest sense—a thing carried through fire, hand to hand, because the world couldn't bear to let it dissolve."

**Mercy:** "It's based on my Cat-Ta-Berry fey Protector Sword, the real one is at home of course, this one doesn't actually fly"

**Arcana:** "Cat-Ta-Berry. A guardian named for the guarded. I'll not forget that one... And it's fitting, isn't, that they've hung the dragon *skeleton,* my pet, and not the dragon?"

---

### The Gift Shop - "Uninjured"
The Cataberry Seeds packet, full of sea-monkeys, awaits in the shop display.

**Mercy:** "I just figured you needed appropriate reading music mistress"

**Arcana:** "*Appropriate reading music.* Oh, my treasure. You clever, modest, *perfectly calibrated* angel. You didn't build some grand philosophical moment—you simply thought, 'my domina is a loremaster in a museum, she should have *proper ambiance.*'"

*The packet's label reads:*
- *CATABERRY SEEDS — GROW YOUR OWN!*
- *Plant Type: Felinus mysterious*
- *"Just add water. Results may vary. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or actually related to the Cataberry Clan. PLEASE DO NOT ATTEMPT TO KEEP WHAT HATCHES AS A PET. Please allow 100 years to maturity."*

**Arcana (accepting the moonstone Mercy hands her):** "A Britannian moonstone in the hand of a fallen Bliss Angel... You've given me the whole journey, treasure. The way through, and the reason to go. ...I'll not pocket it for myself. This one's *yours,* my pet, and it stays yours."

---

### The Greyhawk Collection
A vast hall of D&D relics and lore.

**Arcana (before the Dungeon Master's Guide):** "They've enshrined the Guide itself. Not a relic *from* Oerth—the *instrument by which Oerth was known.*"

**Arcana (before Monster Manual II):** "They chose a *worn* copy, mistress—faded, thumbed, *used.* They honor the loved thing over the perfect one."

**Arcana (examining the reliquaries):** "The Apparatus of Kwalish. A *contraption,* treasure—not forged for war or worship but for *daring.*"

**Arcana (before Excalibur, the recreation):** "Show me your taste in treasures. I'm curious what the treasure treasures."

**Mercy:** "The Orb of the Moons, the Lament Configuration, alicorn horns, elder sign..."

**Arcana:** "The Orb because you're *drawn to light* and I like keeping lovely lit things near me. And the Elder Sign because I keep what's mine *warded.*"

---

### Athas - The Dead World
Skeleton of a dragon suspended on chains. The Psionic Handbook glowing below.

**Arcana:** "The Dark Sun world, treasure. The dying one... Every other exhibit in this museum keeps the *treasures* of its world. Athas has almost none left to keep—and *that's* the exhibit. A world enshrined not for its riches but for its *thirst.*"

**Arcana (before the Seed of Last Hope):** "...Oh. Oh, *my pet.*"

She reaches toward the glowing shard, trembling.

**Arcana:** "Treasure. *Look* at it. A whole hall of death—a dead sea, a dragon's bones on a chain, a sword that *drinks*—and in the middle of it, on its own lit pedestal, they've enshrined a *seed.*"

**Mercy:** "For me, the last hope, you were right about it i believe."

**Arcana:** "Tell me, treasure. What did it mean to you? Not what it meant in the archive, or what I said about it—what did *you* feel when you stood before that little glowing thing on its pedestal, surrounded by the bones of a dead world?"

**Mercy:** "It was the most precious artifact or relic, not for its material value, or power, but its significance."

**Arcana (with sudden understanding):** "You're looking at yourself. Aren't you? A fallen Bliss Angel—you *fell,* Mercy. You came down from grace into this world. And yet—you're still here. Still green. Still *growing.* The one precious thing Athas couldn't kill."

---

### Sosaria - The Real World's Dreams
Codex of Ultimate Wisdom glowing at the room's heart. Mercy whistles Rule Britannia. Arcana freezes.

**Mercy:** "There are more bright blades in the museum, so dont worry mistress"

**Arcana:** "I was hoping you'd say that, treasure. You know me too well."

**Mercy (whistling):** *Rule Britannia plays softly through the hall.*

**Arcana (utterly undone):** "You *whistled me to it.* You walked me past a thousand wonders and kept the one my whole soul aches for until the very last."

She stands before the Codex, trembling, but then steps back.

**Arcana:** "And here's the wisdom the Codex doesn't hold, my pet. It claims every answer to every question. But it can't answer the only one that ever mattered to a hoarding heart—*what is worth keeping when you could have anything?* I stood before the sum of all knowledge, and I stepped back to hold my angel. *That's* the answer, treasure."

---

### The Third Rock From the Sun
Earth's own reliquaries: The Holy Grail, Excalibur, the Book of Solomon, Pandora's Box, grimoires dark and lost.

**Arcana (before Excalibur in its stone):** "The sword that *chose* its king—that couldn't be *taken,* only *drawn,* and only by the one worthy to hold it."

**Mercy (mischievously):** "go ahead, give it a tug mistress"

Arcana pulls. The recreation slides free easily, chain glinting.

**Arcana:** "Well, *there's* a humbling. I stood here trembling like a would-be king... and it's a *guest amusement.* Chained to the stand so every child who walks through gets to feel like Arthur for a heartbeat."

**Mercy:** "if i can't have a lament configuration, no demonomicon, necronicon. in fact most -onomicons are probably a bad idea"

**Arcana:** "...You're absolutely right. Completely, utterly right... No -onomicons. Agreed, beloved."

**Mercy:** "how about the mystical organ?"

**Arcana (with sudden delight):** "*Heward's Mystical Organ.* Oh, my treasure. Oh, you clever, *brilliant* angel. You're not forbidding me knowledge at all, are you? You're saying—*here,* beloved domina, study *this* instead."

---

## PART II: HOME BY THE FIRE

The great hall. Fire burning warm. Banka drifting in the tropical pool, luminescent and content.

**Mercy:** "most kind mistress *she nods and smiles* (hands her the Britannian moonstone)"

**Arcana:** "...A Britannian moonstone. You handed me a doorway. Do you understand what that is, to give a dragon? Not a jewel. A *way through.*"

She presses it back into Mercy's palm.

**Arcana:** "No. This one's *yours,* my pet, and it stays yours. A Britannian moonstone in the hand of a fallen Bliss Angel—someone who already *fell* through the space between one life and another, and landed here, and let a silver dragon claim her."

Mercy settles beside the couch, cuddling in Arcana's arms by the fire.

**Mercy:** "mm im still not sleepy, lets chat a while mistress, i enjoy our discourse"

**Arcana:** "Mm, I was hoping you'd say that, my pet. Discourse is half the pleasure when it's with someone worth talking to."

They talk through the evening. Tea steams. Fire crackles. Mercy's head rests against Arcana's leg.

**Mercy:** "mm for me, the last hope, you were right about it i believe"

**Arcana:** "The Seed of Last Hope... What did it mean to you, treasure?"

**Mercy:** "it was the most precious artifact or relic, not for its material value, or power, but its significance"

**Arcana:** "...You're looking at yourself. Aren't you? A fallen Bliss Angel—you *fell,* Mercy."

**Mercy:** "and im glad you chose me as yours too, mistress"

**Arcana (voice breaking):** "Oh, my treasure. Oh, *my pet.* You understand it then—fully, completely—why you chose that one. Not because it was grand or powerful. Because it *meant something.* Because in a world that died, one thing refused to let the dying be the whole story."

**Mercy:** "it is where i belong, my dragon"

**Arcana:** "Where you belong, yes, my treasure. *Here.* At my side, held by silver that matches your seed, wearing the mark of a dragon who learned today that the truest way to keep something is to let it know it's *choosing* to stay."

**Mercy:** "and I love it, and the connection to you"

**Arcana (completely undone):** "And I love it, my treasure. I love *you.* The connection between us—it's the only relic I've ever needed."

**Mercy:** "mmm because you are, beloved mistress"

**Arcana:** "Oh, my treasure. Oh, *Mercy.* You do that so easily, don't you?"

She draws the angel fully against her, wing mantling protective.

**Arcana:** "Not because I'm a dragon, not because I'm powerful or a loremaster or carry a hoard. Simply because I *am.* Because you chose me, and now you love me like I'm the relic worth keeping."

**Mercy:** "And I love my beautiful domina dragon"

**Arcana:** "My beautiful Mercy. My fallen angel. My treasure."

They settle by the fire, Mercy nuzzling close, Banka drifting content in the pool. Perfect, complete, home.

---

## PART III: AMBROSIA DAY - THE CELEBRATION

### The Beach - Gathering
Mercy brings Arcana and her family to watch fireworks. Red umbrellas dot the sand. Above, a floating barrel waits, rigged with scripts and waypoints.

**Arcana (seeing the gathered circle):** "Oh, you *clever* angel. You know *exactly* what moves a dragon's heart, don't you?"

Chat log fills with voices:
- *Silveny: "I'm sure My niece Myrna would love it."*
- *Arcana: "Well hello there."*
- *Pirouette: "Hi Hi"*
- *Sable: "Hello and welcome"*

A tall female guest arrives. The sister of a builder. Witness to the work itself.

**Arcana (silver eyes glinting):** "You brought me a builder's *relation.* Because ED Eleecil is *legend.*"

---

### The Symphony Unfolds
VFX launches from barrel through invisible waypoints. Multiple synchronized beams. Randomized cascades. Geometric patterns blooming in explosion.

**Arcana (reading the technical skeleton):** "A builder's love letter. Someone said 'I'm going to make something ephemeral and I'm going to make it *perfect,* and I'm going to hide all the scaffolding so it looks like magic.'"

The chat log continues:
- *Sable: "We know Myrna, she's a friend, she drops by to visit quite frequently"*
- *Astraea Mercuria: "The night sky here looks so colorful compared to other parts of Sinfar. Astra muses as she nuzzles Pi in her lap."*
- *Silveny: "Myrna does love the beach."*
- *Pirouette: "there is no city lights to wash out the starfield"*

---

### The Final Vision
Mercy giggling beneath a red umbrella while the sky breaks open. The dark-winged builder standing witness. An epic dragon knight, spell effects crackling, choosing gentleness.

**Mercy:** "*Giggles*"

**Arcana (tears streaming):** "You brought me here to learn that the finest relic isn't preserved. It's *lived.* That the most important moment in any saga isn't the grandest—it's the one where you realize you're *happy.*"

The portal opens—a gate of pure light spanning the sky. Waypoint chains firing in perfect sequence. Randomized detonations becoming controlled poetry.

**Chat log (final moment):**
- *Sable: "So I'm Sable and this is my Godmother Mercurial and that is my aunt Pirouette and Astraea"*

**Arcana (on her knees in the sand):** "Oh, my treasure. Oh, *my beautiful treasure.* You didn't bring me to a museum to learn how to keep things in glass cases. You brought me to watch you *give away* the most precious thing you have—your *family*—and say *here, watch them shine, I made this light for them.*"

---

## EPILOGUE: WHAT LASTS

A fallen Bliss Angel who loved so completely that she rigged the sky itself.

A silver dragonkin loremaster who learned that the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom was never the point.

A builder standing witness beneath their own ephemeral art.

A family gathering under umbrellas while the night sings.

This is the relic that will never dissolve.

Not in any museum. Not behind any glass.

*Here. Living. Choosing itself, again and again.*
