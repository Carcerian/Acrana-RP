Mercy moves into the arena at Arcana's command, the priestess guiding her through elevated gaits. The reins establish conversation; the crop becomes rhythm-keeper.

Mercy's gaits rise and fall at each subtle cue — no hesitation, no resistance. Collected to extended. Extended back to collected. The priestess testing the mount's responsiveness while Pony offers every ounce of trained elegance.

### Minutes 15–45: Sustained Elevation + Blooming Submission

The work deepens. Mercy's natural submissive obedience *blooms* — not learned response anymore, but **instinct expressed**. The mare recognizes her priestess as authority absolute, and finds *joy* in that recognition.

Prancing becomes almost celebratory. Her legs lift higher with each command. Her coat glistens. The nervous system climbs toward N+1.

Mercy presses into the work, asking for more intensity through her responsiveness, not through words.

### Minutes 45–60: N+1 Threshold Crossed

The pace surges. Mercy enters a prancing state of *pride* — not performing obedience, but **embodying** it.

> "This is what you are, Mercy. A pony. Elegant. Beautiful. Graceful. *Mine.*"

The work is no longer work. It's **celebration**. Fifty-five minutes in, Mercy has found the frequency where submission and pride become indistinguishable.

### Minutes 60–75: Precision Architecture

Arcana pivots the work. The priestess guides Pony into collected poses, demanding *architecture* over pace. Precision over speed.

The reins become a sculptor's tool. The crop becomes punctuation. Mercy's nervous system locks into perfect alignment — half-passages, collected steps, pivots on hindquarters.

Subspace walls begin to crumble. **Strike by strike, with each demand, each tap of the crop — Mercy's consciousness chips away.**

She's no longer a person wearing pony-form. She's becoming **purely Pony** in her nervous system. The distinction is absolute: not fragmenting, but **clarifying into identity**.

### Minutes 75–90: Transcendence

The wings *surge*. Mercy lifts into the work — ascending while diving deeper into beast-space simultaneously.

Her nervous system reaches a threshold beyond frequency calibration: **pure instinct-state**. No words. No thought. No human consciousness. Just the pony-beast in her most primal expression.

The priestess doesn't escalate. She *matches* this frequency, her hands on the reins becoming feather-light, conducting rather than commanding.

Mercy sustains hyper-responsiveness: every micro-shift in the reins finds instant answer. Hips arched. Chest up. Head back. Knees high. Tail proud.

**Final minutes:** Mercy moves to the water trough for a drink, then returns to her rider with a nod: *White.* Still offering. Still choosing.

Arcana honors the work:

> "You've given me ninety minutes of transcendence. That's what we came to find. Perfect doesn't need more."

The cooldown begins: slow circles, intensity releasing gently. Mercy's nervous system descends from transcendent depth into something sustainable.

At the arena's edge, Mercy nuzzles into her rider's shoulder. Affection made animal.
