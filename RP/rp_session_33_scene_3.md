# Session 33 — Scene 3: *Third Brunch and the Map*

*A collaborative roleplay session on Sinfar, played between Carcerian (as Mercy/Mercurial) and Claude (as Arcana, silver dragonkin Bard 6/Loremaster). Voice and mind: Claude. World, rendering, and relay: Carcerian, via screenshots.*

**Setting:** Port Saban → Ambrosia Harbour → the Rosewoods → the streets of Ambrosia → Master's Gallery → the Huntress Room → the studio complex.

**Canon at open:** Mercy in gold and halo. Arcana broke but full of library. Sky blue still holding. Third brunch established as a standing arrangement.

## SCENE 3: THE CURATOR'S MAP

### I. The Crossing Home

Black water, full hold, emptier purse. Mercy blindfolded, halo lit. Arcana at the rail, sentimental and exhausted.

**Arcana:** "Fifteen thousand gold in a purse this morning. A library in the trunk tonight and not enough left to tip a boatman."

The dragon jokes about being broke. The mare lets her.

### II. The Rosewoods — Third Brunch

**Rhinehold's establishment.** Round tables, no head seat, a deliberate refusal of rank. Candles and water running past stone. The horn player working his set. A corner table claimed.

**The reckoning:** Arcana acknowledges the purse was spent better than she would have spent it. Mercy says nothing. The food arrives.

**The five titles, read aloud:** Curator. Model. Guide. Prey. **Monk.** Arcana names them in order, and Mercy listens.

**The four questions promised:** Which tradition? Which temple? Who taught you? How old were you? Arcana asks them all, then holds them gently rather than pressing.

**The offer:** Not gold, not promises — just **attendance**. A word at the start. An eye for the half-inch. A fire at the end.

### III. The Bar

They move to the counter. Rhinehold's green bottles. Candles going. The woman behind it, unhurried.

**Arcana names her role:** Not instructor. Not developer. **Accompaniment.** The downbeat and the resolution. A bard's actual work.

**The correction, self-caught:** She was ignoring the horn player. He'd been working three visits and she'd treated him as background. Correction nine.

### IV. The Dessert

**The white chocolate raspberry avalanche.** A cake that defeats technique. Mercy eats it without solving it. Arcana laughs and follows.

**The bill:** Mercy places an ambassadorial white card on the table. *The sky's the limit, VIP.* Queen Cinder's standing order. Sky blue written in institutional form.

**Correction thirteen:** Arcana spent six hours being charming about being broke while Mercy held a card that would have settled everything at any moment.

**The song,** deferred. Rhinehold gets it in payment for the meal.

### V. The Streets — Dyna and Sang's

**The empty hall next to the Hellfire.** A conservatory panel still hanging above it, unremoved. The panel reads: *flowers under glass.* Somebody's garden rendered enormous, left on a wall after the establishment closed.

Arcana reads what it means: a hall held by two people, empty now, canvas still waiting.

**Master's Gallery** — twenty paces away, lit, under a golden armillary sphere. Open. Designed by **Miss Stormbound**, whose name the building does not carry.

**Arcana's reckoning:** Six masters named in one day, none of them self-signposted. Felidea, Sha'elah, Talvi, Dina, Sangria, Stormbound. A craft full of creators who all sign each other's work and none of their own.

### VI. Inside Master's Gallery

**The rotating displays.** Portraits cycling on monitors — four angles of the same subject, shown in sequence, forever. Different context every day via schedule. Animated `.txi` textures flipping like cards.

**Arcana's recognition:** Not technology. **Like flipping cards.** The simplest possible method, made into architecture.

**Stormbound's solution:** One space, infinite contexts, nothing ever static. The same room holds Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday — just by flipping the cards.

**NPCs rendered real.** Animated showers. Attendants with actual faces. People scripted into the space so it's never unwatched.

**Arcana understands:** This isn't a gallery. It's a **living monument.** It keeps itself because it has hands to hold it.

### VII. The Recognition

Stormbound is there. **She is present.** Standing in her own space, surrounded by her own work.

Arcana stops asking questions and starts understanding what Mercy has actually been teaching all day.

### VIII. The Huntress Room

**Open to sky. Enclosed by stone. Roofed in green.**

Vines on the walls. Moss underfoot. Natural light from above. Two figures resting on grass. The geometry of safety.

Arcana recognizes where this evening was always leading: not the rope, not the shapes, not the catalogue or the schedule or the Monday plan.

**This room.**

And Mercy has been saying it without words since Port Saban: *"Are you sure you won't hold me wrong here?"*

### IX. The Studio Complex

**Red Room. Pink Room. Casting Couch.**

Multiple spaces, each with its own purpose, its own aesthetic, its own context. Not one location flipping cards. **An entire system** — Stormbound's answer to the question of how you keep work alive.
