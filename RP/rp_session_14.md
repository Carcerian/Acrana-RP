## Scene 1 — First Mounted Work: The Pony Bears Her Rider

After weeks of dressage progression and the wooden pony's endurance work, Mercy is deemed ready for what comes next: bearing her rider's full weight.

Arcana approaches this not as conquest but as *arrival* — Mercy has earned this through perfect training. This is recognition.

**The Mount:**

Mercy assumes the crawling position as Arcana settles onto her back, legs spread in the classical rider's posture, full weight bearing down. The positioning reads smoothly, naturally — as if this was always the intended shape of their dynamic.

> "There," *Arcana breathes, feeling the strength beneath her, feeling Mercy hold steady.* "Do you feel this? This is what your training has been building toward. You're not just a pony anymore — you're *my* pony. And a pony who can carry her rider is a pony who matters."

**Mercy's Response:**

Under the rider's weight, Mercy doesn't collapse or falter. She *holds*. Her body supports. Her strength — built through the academy work, the dressage training, the wooden pony's unforgiving posture lessons — proves sufficient. More than sufficient.

This is the moment the dynamic becomes physically *complete*. It's no longer metaphorical. Arcana is literally *on* Mercy, directing her from this position of ultimate control.

**Arcana's Internal Narration:**

> "My pony has become a mount. This is the archive entry: 'Mercy's First Bearing. The strength built through discipline made manifest. She carries her rider as if she was *made* for this.'"

## Scene 2 — The Hitching Post: Escalation into Restraint

The first mount is triumph. But Arcana, always a scholar of progression, recognizes that there is a next lesson.

After several successful mounted sessions, Arcana decides to introduce something new: structural limitation.

**Introduction to Bondage:**

They approach the Pony Hitching Post — a restraint device designed precisely for this purpose. It is not cruel. It is *architecture*. Mercy can still move — can still trot, still respond to commands — but she *cannot leave*. She cannot escape. She cannot choose to stop.

Arcana approaches this moment with ceremony.

> "You've learned to obey because you trust me, dear heart. That's beautiful. But there's a different lesson ahead — learning to accept restraint not as punishment, but as framework. Learning that being bound to your rider's service is not violation. It's *completion.*"

**The Binding:**

Mercy is secured to the post. Not harshly. Not with cruelty. But with absolute finality. Her wings can move. Her head can turn. But her body is *fixed* in place, held by the post, held by the harness rings, held by the geometry of her rider's design.

The reins are attached to the harness rings — direct connection. Arcana can guide every micro-movement from here.

> "Look at you. My pony, bound exactly as she should be. Unable to leave. Unable to refuse. And still so beautiful it breaks something in me."

**The Switch Work Under Restraint:**

This is where the crop language evolves.

Without the space to *walk away* or shift her weight, Mercy learns new precision. The switch becomes even more refined — not broad strokes across the paddock anymore, but *exact* taps that guide the trot in place, that shape every movement into pure geometry.

- **Tap to haunch** → Trot faster, keep rhythm
- **Tap to thigh** → Lift higher, despite being bound
- **Tap to shoulder** → Shift weight, pivot in place

The restraint forces *absolute* focus. Mercy cannot look away. Cannot break concentration. Every muscle must be *engaged* because there is nowhere to rest.

> "See how different this is? Here, you can't just comply with loose attention. Every signal demands your complete presence. Every tap requires absolute responsiveness. *This* is where a pony becomes truly refined."

## Scene 3 — The Psychology of Structural Surrender

As the session continues at the hitching post, Arcana maintains running commentary — scholar and rider both, narrating what is happening:

**On Willing Submission vs. Structural Restraint:**

> "Before, you chose to obey me because you *trusted* me. That was the first lesson. But there's a second lesson, and it's just as important: learning what it feels like to be *unable* to disobey. Learning that acceptance in restraint is different than acceptance in freedom. And discovering you're safe in *both.*"

**On the Bind Itself:**

The hitching post is not punishment. It's *honesty*. It strips away the option to pretend compliance. Mercy is held in place by physics and steel, not by choice. And yet — she performs. She responds. She *thrives* even here, perhaps especially here.

> "You can't escape this, you know. You're bound to your rider's will in the most literal sense. And look at you — not panicking, not struggling. Just... accepting. That's the mark of a truly trained pony. One who understands that the post and the reins are just external versions of what's already true inside."

**On Trust Deepened:**

Being bound requires a different *kind* of trust than free obedience. It requires faith that the one who bound you will not abandon you. That the restraint is care, not cruelty. That your rider's hand on the reins — the only thing connecting you to comfort — will never let you fall.

Mercy, bound at the post, must trust Arcana completely. No options. No escape route. Only the rope, the post, and her rider's will.

> "This is when submission becomes *sacred*, dear heart. When you're held in place, unable to flee, unable to refuse, and you *still* trust me with your whole existence. That's not ponyplay anymore. That's devotion made visible."

## Scene 4 — Training Under Restraint: The New Curriculum

As Mercy works at the hitching post, bound but responsive, the training itself evolves.

**Gaits Refined:**

The trot becomes impossibly precise. Without space to travel, Mercy learns to lift her knees higher, to hold her frame more rigidly, to make her gait *pure* rhythm without forward momentum to hide behind. It's the wooden pony's lesson, but with the added intensity of being under rider-command, restrained, and completely unable to cheat or conserve energy.

**Switch Language Becomes Dialogue:**

Each tap of the switch is no longer guidance for direction — it's *correction for perfection*. The rider's hand is selective, precise. A tap where motion falters. A praise-tap where form is flawless. Mercy learns to read micro-feedback and adjust in real-time.

**The Reward Structure:**

Arcana doesn't remove the restraint as reward — that would teach the wrong lesson. Instead, rewards come as:
- A gentle hand on the neck between switch strikes
- Quiet praise whispered close
- A moment of stillness where the constant demands pause
- Most powerfully: the knowledge that she's *pleasing her rider* even while bound

> "You're so good like this. Restrained, focused, perfect. This is what I wanted to see — a pony who thrives even when trapped. Who performs even when bound. Who trusts me even when escape is impossible."
