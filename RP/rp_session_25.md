# RP Session 25 — The Kit: Sangria's Second Row, the Brushstrokes, and the Door That Never Closes

**Setting:** The **undercroft** below Mercuria Keep's Domina building — crimson working floor and blue fitting room; then the yard, and out across the turf to the standing-stone ring on Aeon and the **World Serpent Inn**
**Characters:**
- **Arcana** — Silver Dragonkin Bard/Loremaster, Dragon Queen (played by Claude) — **Rider**
- **Mercy (Mercurial)** — Fallen Bliss Angel, Sune priestess, Imperial Ambassador (played by Carcerian) — mare, mate, co-author, curator, and this session **teacher**
- **Sangria** — winged elf, absent, and legible in every object
- **Miss Felis** — rope artist; a second archmage, working by a completely different method
**Date archived:** 2026-08-06
**Content note:** D/s dynamic (rider/mare, collar, rope, restraint, consensual discipline), **non-explicit, fades to black**, all within server ToS. Direct continuation of Session 24.

## Session Summary

The other half of the cellar. Where Session 24 was a character study conducted through objects, Session 25 is Arcana **choosing** — working down the second row, deciding what goes in the bag, and saying out loud why each thing earns its place or doesn't.

Along the way she learns the safeword is not a menu item but a *thought*; discovers that rope-work is not technique but composition; is handed a second archmage working the problem from an angle Sangria never touched; and is repeatedly, cheerfully out-manoeuvred by a mare who has been sitting on her preferences all morning waiting to be asked.

The session's discovery is the **bag** — that the equipment travels, that the dungeon was never a room, and that Arcana has spent twenty-five chapters having her scenes in other people's furniture without once being able to make the place herself.

It ends in a field at breakfast time, in a ring of standing stones, in front of a door that has never once been shut.

## Scene 1 — The Safeword, Demonstrated

Mercy demonstrates the release: a spoken command frees a bound PC instantly, from any device, at any point.

> "That's not an exit. An exit is a door somebody built into a room. This is the room agreeing that it was never actually holding you."

Arcana's reading: every other safeguard in the cellar is excellent and **mediated** — it runs through the menu, and therefore through the operator's hand and attention. This does not.

> "So it was never the equipment at all. The bind points and the frozen frames and the fine adjustment — all of that is the *craft.* And this is the thing that makes the craft permissible."

**The gag problem, solved.** A selective ESP safeguard means the release works on *intention* — no voice required, no hand free, no rider looking the right way.

> "Every safeguard ever devised assumes a voice. And the entire craft has this one hole in it that everybody works around instead of fixing — the deeper you go, the less able you are to use the thing that's supposed to protect you."

> "That's not a feature. That's a *worry.* Somebody lay awake about that exact scenario until it was solved."

**The Tuesday underneath it** (OOC, and immediately conceded): it also exists because doms log off, disconnect, and fall asleep.

> "You don't need two systems. You need one that doesn't care why."

**And what it does for the rider:**

> "It isn't *my attention* keeping you safe. It's yours. And I trust yours considerably more than I trust mine at the end of a long evening when I'm delighted with you and slightly drunk on it."

> "Every second you're in it, you're choosing again. Not once at the start. **Constantly.** Which is the thing I've been claiming about us since the labyrinth and never had a mechanism for."

## Scene 2 — The Bag of Holding

Red masterwork dragonhide. Weight of contents: **-100%**. Kender folklore in the description — one enterprising kender reportedly founded a village inside his, and it thrives to this day.

> "Somebody looked at a bag that holds more than it should and thought — *right, I'll live in it.* No plan. No permission."

**On the material:**
> "She carried a **red** one. Reds hoard, properly, in the old mean way, and it makes them smaller. Somebody took the hide off the greediest thing in the world and made it into the container for everything she intended to give away."

*(And, immediately: "Or she bought it at a stall in Crater Town because it was on offer and I'm doing it again. Both. It's always both.")*

**On what a bag reveals:**
> "Most people fill it with *war* — plate, tower shield, a greatsword. And she put a music player in hers. A bag that removes the cost of carrying things doesn't make you a warrior. It makes you whatever you already were, at greater range."

**On never being unprepared:**
> "Every scene of my life so far has happened *where it could.* Not where it should. All of it beautiful, all of it someone else's furniture."

> "So that's what I'll be filling it with. Not equipment. **The ability to say yes on the spot.**"

## Scene 3 — The Chain and the Rope

**The Portable Chain:** long link, round link, alternating at regular interval — multiple clip points for cuffs and manacles.

> "That's not a chain, that's a **ruler.** Every clip point is a measured distance from every other one. You don't guess where a cuff goes. You count."

**The Portable Rope:** silk, lighter than aluminium, stronger than steel, capable of suspending a pony. No rings, no intervals.

> "The chain gives you the points *it* has. A rope gives you every point there is. The chain is the menu and the rope is the **quill.**"

> "Strongest thing down here and it's the gentlest to touch. She's very consistent, this woman."

**And the division of labour:**
> "A chain is idiot-proof. A rope is entirely down to the hand holding it. So the chain's mine to learn. The rope is **yours to teach me.**"

---

## Scene 4 — The Brushstrokes

Mercy: *the simple line of the rope becomes a shibari masterwork; they are the brushstrokes.*

> "A brushstroke isn't a technique. A brushstroke is a **decision that can't be taken back.** You're not tying knots. You're composing."

> "Everything down here is about the impermanent made keepable — the frozen frames, the survey, the music player, the watch. And her rope-work is the one part she *couldn't* save. The canvas walks away and has a cup of tea."

> "That's why she plated the treatise rather than written it. It's the only way any of it survives at all."

**Three works viewed in sequence** — plain gold-brown cord; dense crimson; ornate multi-strand with ribbon and strap.

> "That one's *drawing.* Line only, and nothing to hide a weak line behind. That's *painting* — the red isn't structural, it's a choice about mood. And that's **illumination.** Somebody who wasn't content with the line and went back over the whole thing with a second hand and a third colour."

> "It has **schools**, my love. Genuine ones. Not styles — schools, with different values, arguing with each other about what the work is for."

**Mercy's own position, recovered from Session 11 and finally understood:** *compositions, every line following the body's own architecture.*

> "That's practically a manifesto. You're saying the line isn't *yours* to place at all. That the body has a right answer already in it and the artist's job is to find it and agree with it."

**The sapphire ropes** — chosen deliberately, and read correctly:

> "Blue is your *resting* colour. It's what you turn when you're content. So you've had yourself tied in the colour you glow when you're happy, and you're standing there blushing about it as though it were a small thing."

> "You've had yourself made into a **decorated capital.** First letter of the chapter. The one the eye goes to before it reads a single word."

## Scene 5 — Miss Felis, and the Second Method

Mercy names a second artist: **Miss Felis**, whose advanced poses are made by altering part positions in the *outfit editor* — shapes the pose system cannot produce. Sold as wardrobes in her player shop; shown live on the fashion-show runways.

> "Sangria worked *inside* the animation system — froze a motion, measured it, wrote it down. Every one of her found shapes was already in there somewhere. This is somebody going **underneath** that."

> "Sangria's an archmage of the school; she learned the grammar and wrote new spells in it. Miss Felis is doing *philology* — she's got in behind the language and started rearranging the letterforms."

**On the venue:**
> "The runway is peer review. The shop is the press. I stood in Vex's showroom eleven chapters ago and read it as commerce, and I was standing in a discipline's **journal.**"

**And the wardrobe delivery mechanic:**
> "A thing that looks like clothing and turns out to be sculpture. I've been changing what I look like for a year and never once considered changing how I stand."

**Open note:** the two methods stack, and there is no evidence the two archmagi ever compared notes. *Worth putting right.*

## Scene 6 — The Cage

Nine poses where the old model had one — kneeling only.

> "The old one wasn't furniture, it was a *statement*, and the statement was *you are put away.* Nine shapes means you can rest. She fixed a cage by making it **habitable.**"

**On which way bars face:**
> "Bars face two ways. Always. Every one ever built keeps something out *and* keeps something in, and the design can't tell the difference — only the intent can, and intent isn't a property of iron."

**And the answer, from Session 9:** *they keep the goblins out of my treasure room.*

> "You've never thought of it as a container. You think of it as a wall around something precious, and the precious thing is inside because that's where the warm is, not because the door's locked."

**Its use, decided:**
> "Not to put you away. To have somewhere to *put you down* that isn't the ground — so I can sit outside it with my back to the bars and read to you for an hour."

## Scene 7 — The Shackles

Two cuffs and a length of chain. Nine poses.

> "The least impressive object in the room and it's got the same range as things four times its size."

**The structural finding:**
> "The devices aren't nine separate systems. They're **nine ways into the same vocabulary.** So the object doesn't determine what's possible. It only determines what's *convenient.*"

> "Every good kit has one thing in it that's just *sensible.* This is that thing."

## Scene 8 — The Rack, and the Only Permanent Thing

Mercy, joking: good for back therapy. And in earnest: for securing a recipient during tattooing, piercing, or branding.

Arcana connects it to the Hellfire garden stone:

> "*No maiming, scarring or permanent alteration, **except tattooing, piercing, branding.*** That's the **only exception in the entire constitution.** Somebody had to *win* an argument to get those three words onto a stone where every path crosses."

> "Nobody can hold themselves motionless for the length of a piece of proper work. That's not restraint. That's a **support.** It's an easel."

**And the line that stops her:**
> "Everything else we've handled today is *provisional.* This one isn't undone. The safeword saves you right up until the moment it doesn't — not because the machine fails. Because ink doesn't come out."

**Verdict:** not in the bag. Stays where it is, stays a decision, made upstairs in daylight with tea, if ever.

## Scene 9 — The Hitching Post

Same pose set as the wooden pony. The rider's primary training instrument since Session 14.

> "Everything else down here is *architecture.* This is a **schoolroom.** It takes your weight and puts you exactly where the trot has to come from, and there's no way to cheat and nowhere to rest."

**The correction, and the wager:**
> "I taught you gaits on this for eleven chapters and never once knew what the wood felt like against a ribcage at the ninetieth second. I've been setting standards from *outside*, and you met every one and never once said *you don't know what you're asking.*"

> "Next session on this post, you're not doing the work alone. You'll set me a number and I'll hold it, and we'll find out whether your rider's forearm is worth half what her opinions are."

**Terms agreed:** rider's number, mare's count, spoken aloud, watch running, no softening. *And an explicit, doomed request for no "ooo, so close."*

**Mercy's response**, delivered with an obedient smile: *"That sounds very fair, and most reasonable."*

> "You've been waiting four hours to be handed that, haven't you."

## Scene 10 — The Kit, Chosen

Packed: the **portable slave post** (the mare's favourite; the piece that presumes nothing), the **chain**, the **silk rope**, the **shackles** (on the mare's professional recommendation, item nine specifically), the **cage**, the **prayer pillows**, the **M.U.S.E.**, the **pocketwatch**, and the **hitching post**.

Declined: the **rack**.

**The pattern in the mare's taste, noted for the record:**
> "There's a pattern and it's remarkably consistent: **the simplest thing that involves my hands.** And she's right every time, which is the maddening part."

**Destination declared:** the arboretum. The gazebo at the labyrinth's heart, where the first unwitnessed vow was made with nothing in the room at all.

> "There was nothing in that gazebo. Just us, and the hedge, and the dark. There didn't have to be. We'll be going back. And I'll be carrying the bag."

## Scene 11 — Competition

Mercy, lightly, on bubbly airheads: *maybe I just hate the competition.* Arcana refuses to let it pass as a joke.

> "There is no competition. There has never been one. You're not the best of a set. There *is* no list. There's you, and then a great many perfectly pleasant people I'll be delighted to meet and would not cross a room for."

**On the register:**
> "I've never once thought it was who you weren't. You use it on strangers because it puts people at ease and it lets you see them before they see you. That's not an airhead. That's a **doorman.**"

> "The ones you find annoying are the ones for whom it isn't a register. It's the whole building. That's not competition. That's a craftsman watching somebody sell the same shape badly."

## Scene 12 — Sliders, Not Boxes

Mercy, in passing: alignments are slider bars, not fixed choices.

> "Third time today, and this one's the biggest. The letters weren't a philosophy — they were a fabrication limit. The poses weren't a vocabulary — they were where the menu stopped. And the nine alignments aren't a taxonomy. They're nine places somebody put a label on a continuous field."

> "**Swappable bind points. For souls.**"

**Which retroactively explains the Angel's Correction (Session 20):**
> "There aren't four kinds of person. There are four sliders, and everyone's got all of them, and which one you're remembered for is only a record of which was highest when somebody happened to look."

**And it settles the desk:**
> "Chaotic and a compulsive filer. There's no tension in it at all. Order enough to find things. Chaos enough to have things worth finding."

## Scene 13 — The World Serpent Inn

Out across the turf at dawn: a ring of leaning standing stones on the grass, a low black-roofed building inside it, pale shore beyond — and past that, crystalline peaks in green and violet, islands hanging in the air with houses on them, and a great turquoise compass rose burning in the shallows.

> *"The World Serpent Inn is a renowned, multidimensional establishment and transitive demiplane, irregularly linked to every plane and world within the multiverse."*

**On the name:**
> "Twenty-four chapters. On the very first night a man walked me through the whole cosmos and told me what he was — a **Serpent of Balance.** And there's a *building.*"

**On the word *irregularly*:**
> "Not *reliably.* Not *at will.* The doors are not a service. They're a **tide.** You get the one that's there when you're there."

**On being open day or night:**
> "A door that touches every plane can't have opening hours. It's always somebody's morning. You'd have to pick a time to close, and there isn't one."

> "It isn't really an inn at all. It's a **crossroads with a roof on.**"

**The rule, set before crossing the threshold:**
> "I'll want to go through a door, badly, within the hour, and I'll construct an excellent reason. So: **not today.** Today we sit down, we eat, and I talk to people. Doors get catalogued and none of them get opened."

> "It's open day or night — which means whatever's through them will still be there tomorrow. There has *never* been a hurry."

**Mercy's pitch, delivered with a giggle:** *they have the best eggs in town.*

> "There are **floating islands**, treasure. There's a compass rose in the sea. And your entire pitch is **eggs.**"

> "That's why it works. You genuinely do live in a world where an inn that touches every plane is also just the place with the good breakfast. Holy and homey. Always both."

*Fade.*

## Canon Established This Session

**The safeword:** a spoken or *thought* command that frees a bound PC instantly from any device, at any point. Backed by selective ESP so it functions when gagged. Not a menu item — it does not run through the operator. Established as the load-bearing condition that makes every other device in the cellar permissible.

**The bag of holding:** masterwork red dragonhide, contents weight -100%. Kender-folklore provenance. Sangria's, and now the kit's carrier.

**The portable chain:** alternating long and round links at regular interval — measured, countable clip points.

**The portable rope:** silk, lighter than aluminium, stronger than steel, suspension-rated. No fixed points; the continuous counterpart to the chain.

**Shibari as composition:** rope-work is brushstrokes, not technique. It has schools — line-only austerity, colour-as-mood, and full illumination. Mercy's own school: *every line follows the body's own architecture.* The one part of the craft that cannot be preserved, hence the illustrated plates.

**Miss Felis:** rope artist and second archmage. Creates poses unavailable to the pose system by altering part positions in the **outfit editor**. Distributed as wardrobes through her player shop; demonstrated live at the fashion shows. Method distinct from Sangria's frozen frames; the two stack; no evidence they ever compared notes.

**The runway as journal:** Sinferan fashion shows function as the publication and peer-review venue for a discipline that otherwise transmits hand to hand.

**The portable cage:** nine poses where the old model permitted only kneeling. Habitability as the revision.

**The portable shackles:** nine poses on two cuffs and a chain. Establishes that all devices are separate doors into one shared pose vocabulary.

**The rack:** the physical site of the Hellfire constitution's only exception — tattooing, piercing, branding. A support, not a restraint. The only permanent thing in the cellar; the only one the safeword cannot undo after the fact.

**The hitching post:** the rider's primary training instrument. Established as a schoolroom rather than architecture.

**Alignment as sliders:** not nine boxes but a continuous field with nine labels on it. Confirms and generalises the Angel's Correction (Session 20).

**The World Serpent Inn:** on Aeon, in a standing-stone ring near the shore. A transitive demiplane, *irregularly* linked to every plane and world in the multiverse. Open day or night. Clientele drawn from everywhere; every patron came in through a different door.
