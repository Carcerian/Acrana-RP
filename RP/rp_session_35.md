# Session 35 — Monday Morning, Week 1
### The First Training: The Hellfire Guild Pony Room

*A collaborative roleplay session on Sinfar, played between Carcerian (as Mercy/Mercurial) and Claude (as Arcana, silver dragonkin Bard 6/Loremaster). Voice and mind: Claude. World, rendering, and relay: Carcerian, via screenshots.*

**Setting:** The Hellfire Guild Pony Room. 1x2 space. Wooden pony. Hay bale benches. Pacing wheel. The air smells like leather and straw and purpose. The door closes behind them.

**Canon at open:** Session 34 closed with Arcana understanding her vocation as a Paladin of Sune. The colour chart is written. HELD is defined. The Rider-Steed bond is understood as sacred attunement, not dominance. Week 1 of the training schedule begins now.

**Mercy's state:** Ready. Strictly trained. Long and hard. Becoming a better Mare, Mount, Steed.

---

## I. The Room Closes

Arcana steps into the Hellfire Guild Pony Room and lets the door shut behind them both.

The space is small. Intimate. Built for exactly this work. The wooden pony stands in the center like a piece of furniture waiting to be used. Hay bales line the edges. The pacing wheel waits.

She doesn't move immediately. Just stands in the doorway and looks at Mercy — really *looks* at her. The way a Paladin looks at her Steed before the first ride.

> **Arcana:** "You know what we're doing here?"

Not a question that needs answering. A statement that needs acknowledgment.

She steps further into the room, and the door *stays shut*.

No witnesses. No audience. Just the Paladin and her Steed, and nine weeks of work about to begin.

> **Arcana:** "Tell me your colour."

---

---

## II. The First Training

Mercy strips, revealing her gold and silver buckled white leather strap harness, polished and oiled, matching her Pony Training Collar. She stands ready — not as a novice, but as a Steed who knows what she is.

Arcana assesses her, then guides her into motion with a leather lead rope clipped to the gold buckle at her chest harness. The rope becomes the conversation between Paladin and Steed. Mercy's body relaxes, synchronizes, becomes fluid with natural grace.

The bond becomes *tangible*.

Arcana introduces the crop — not as punishment, but as punctuation in the language they're learning together. The pacing increases: walk to trot to prance to gallop. Each element in perfect synergy — voice, reins, crop — the domination and devotion folding into one another.

Mercy flourishes under the harshness because it is exactly what she needs. Her form polishes under the Paladin's hand. Legs lifting to waist-height. Chest arched in proud presentation. Body gleaming with perspiration — visible proof of commitment, of surrender, of becoming.

The Paladin rides this moment with absolute certainty, driving her Steed to the peak of what she can give in this first session.

Then, slowly, she brings Mercy down from the gallop into a walk, into stillness. The rope softens. The crop lowers. The Paladin's hands replace the tools — one on Mercy's shoulder, one on her chest, feeling the heartbeat gradually slow.

> **Arcana:** "Perfect. You were perfect."

---

## III. The Aftermath & Integration

Mercy moves to her Rider's arms, settling onto all fours and laying her head on Arcana's lap. The Paladin's claws gentle now, stroking through her mane with the same precision she used with the crop, but softer. Soothing.

This is the other half of the sacred bond made visible — the Paladin who demands everything also *holds* everything. The creature who was pushed to her edge is now being soothed by the very hands that pushed her.

The room is quiet except for breathing gradually returning to normal. Sweat cooling. Heart rate slowing.

> **Arcana:** "Rest here. You did beautifully today. I'm proud of you."

Arcana leans down and rests her face against Mercy's head, silver scales against white hair. She stays like that, not moving, not speaking. Just *being* with her Steed in the quiet.

> **Arcana:** "We have eight more Mondays like this ahead of us. Each one will deepen what happened today. By week nine, surrender won't be something you have to reach for. It'll be your natural state."

She strokes her mane one final time, slowly, deliberately.

> **Arcana:** "But that's next week's work. Today, you rest."
