# Session 36 — Wednesday, Week 1

*A collaborative roleplay session on Sinfar, played between Carcerian (as Mercy/Mercurial) and Claude (as Arcana, silver dragonkin Bard 6/Loremaster). Voice and mind: Claude. World, rendering, and relay: Carcerian, via screenshots.*

**Setting:** The Circular Chamber. Small, intimate, contained. Stone walls curve around the space. Suspension chain overhead. Cage. Posts. Hitching post. Portable restraint equipment ready. Adjacent to the VIP bedrooms and playrooms, close enough to the life of the compound yet entirely removed.

**Canon at open:** Session 35 (Monday, Week 1) completed pony-endurance foundation. Mercy brought the harness and bit from that training into this session. Arcana has chosen the Circular Chamber for Phase 1 sensory deprivation work.

## I. THE THRESHOLD

Arcana leads Mercy blindfolded and gagged into the Circular Chamber — the pony harness still on from Monday, the bit in her mouth, the blinders already in place. The door clicks shut behind them.

Mercy trembles softly at the sound. The scent of leather and metal fills the darkness. The echo of her Rider's footsteps is her entire world.

Arcana stands with her Steed in the circular room, letting Mercy's nervous system register where she is — the curve of the walls, the presence of restraint equipment, the intimate containment of the space.

> **Arcana:** "You feel it, don't you? This space is different from the Pony Room. The walls curve. There's nowhere to pace. Nowhere to move. Just you, and me, and this circle."

She circles Mercy slowly, assessing.

> **Arcana:** "I'm going to remove the blinders now. Then we'll add the rest."

She unfastens the blinders. Light returns.

Mercy sees the cage, the posts, the suspension chain, the equipment waiting. Her eyes dart, registering what's ahead of her. Then she finds Arcana's eyes and holds them — trembling, anticipatory, completely focused.

Arcana removes the bit from Mercy's mouth slowly, letting her jaw release.

> **Arcana:** "You know what happens now."

Not a question. Mercy knows.

> **Arcana:** "Strip the harness. Leave the collar. Everything else comes off."

Mercy obeys. The harness falls away. She stands naked except for her collar, waiting.

> **Arcana:** "Tell me your colour before we begin."

Mercy's mind touches her Rider's — white light, acceptance, complete permission.

*Yes. I'm ready. Take me completely.*

Arcana feels it through the familiar bond.

> **Arcana:** "I feel you, Dove. I have you."

## II. IMMOBILIZATION & SENSORY DEPRIVATION

Arcana guides Mercy to the center of the circular chamber. She fits the restraints with methodical precision:

- Ankle cuffs attached to spreader bar, locking her legs in wide stance
- Wrist cuffs secured to chain hanging from above, arms pulled upward until she's suspended but not helpless
- Full-body restriction harness: leather straps across chest, waist, between legs

Mercy doesn't resist. She *accepts* each restraint, arching gracefully into them, offering herself for her Rider's visual pleasure.

Then comes the sensory deprivation layer:
- Leather blindfold placed carefully over her eyes
- Ballgag secured behind her head, silencing her voice
- Custom earplugs fitted, muffling the world into distant whisper

Mercy hangs suspended — completely immobilized, blind, gagged, deaf.

Arcana traces one clawed hand down Mercy's suspended body — a single touch through the silence. The only thing Mercy can feel. The only thing she can know.

She settles into her observation chair with a leather journal and begins documenting.

## III. THE CONDITIONING CYCLE

**Minutes 0-7: Initial Response**

Arcana approaches and places her scaled palm flat against Mercy's lower back. Presence. Possession.

Mercy pulls against the restraints, not to escape — to *test* them, to find their security. She arches beautifully, offering herself, performing for her Rider even blind.

Arcana's tail coils around Mercy's suspended wrist — cool dragon scales wrapping possessively.

She leans close to Mercy's ear, knowing the earplugs will muffle her voice into a distant rumble:

> **Arcana:** "You're so beautiful like this. Suspended. Mine. Everything you are, right now, belongs to me."

She traces one claw along Mercy's collarbone — marking ownership.

Then she steps back and watches. Mercy hangs in darkness, blind and gagged and deaf, with only the memory of touch and the anticipation of the next one.

**Minutes 7-15: Extended Withdrawal**

Arcana circles, letting Mercy hear the faint sound of footsteps through the earplugs. The Steed anticipates without knowing where the touch will come from.

Mercy's body trembles — not from cold, but from *need*. The deprivation is working.

A soft whimper escapes her gag — the first vocalization. Even muffled, Arcana hears it through the bond.

> **Arcana:** *(internal monologue)*

*"She's dropping. Seeking touch that I've deliberately withheld. Her body language shows arching toward last known position = seeking contact = beginning to understand that I am her only reference point."*

When Arcana approaches again, she doesn't just touch and withdraw. She places both clawed hands on Mercy's shoulders and holds — sustained contact, not brief.

She runs her scales down Mercy's arms from shoulder to wrist where the cuffs hold her. The sensation is exquisite because it's *sustained*.

Her tail coils around Mercy's waist — not restraining, but *possessing*.

She leans close:

> **Arcana:** "You feel so good when you're like this. Helpless. Mine. No words. Just your body begging for me."

Then she steps away. The waiting continues.

**Minutes 15-45: Touch-Withdrawal Cycling**

Arcana runs Mercy through precise cycles:

- Long, slow caresses. Claws tracing every inch. Tail coiling. Dragon scales pressed against skin.
- Complete withdrawal. Silence. Mercy hanging in the void.
- Brief, sharp touches. A bite on the shoulder. A claw across ribs. Sensation that surprises and recalibrates.
- Sustained contact again. Both clawed hands. Tail around waist. The message: *You belong to me so completely that I can reshape you however I want.*
- Withdrawal returns. The waiting becomes almost unbearable.

Each cycle deepens Mercy's subspace. Her nervous system learns the pattern. She begins to *crave* the touch.

**Minutes 45-58: Deep Subspace Induction**

Arcana observes that Mercy has crossed into deep subspace. The cycling is working perfectly.

She makes her final approach and presses her entire body against Mercy's suspended form — scales and heat and dragon-musk and complete possession.

She holds her there, not moving, just *being* with her Steed in the darkness.

> **Arcana:** *(voice barely audible through earplugs)*

"You've gone so deep for me. So beautiful. So completely mine. In a few moments, I'm going to remove the blindfold, the gag, the earplugs. But you'll still be bound. You'll still belong to me. Do you understand?"

She waits, feeling Mercy's response through the bond.

---

## IV. RETURN TO LIGHT & INTEGRATION

**Minute 60: Removal of Sensory Deprivation**

Arcana slowly removes the blindfold from Mercy's eyes.

Light returns. But the restraints remain.

Mercy blinks as her eyes adjust. When they open, they find Arcana's immediately.

Her sky blue eyes are full of dedicated love and devotion — not the devotion of choice, but the devotion of complete surrender become her baseline.

Something fundamental has shifted in one hour.

Arcana gently removes the ballgag, letting Mercy's jaw release. She doesn't remove the earplugs yet.

> **Arcana:** "Welcome back, Dove."

She unclips the wrist cuffs, supporting Mercy's arms as circulation returns. The spreader bar releases next.

Arcana catches her, holding her close, letting her rest against silver scales.

> **Arcana:** "You went so deep. Do you remember all of it?"

Mercy responds:

> **Mercy:** "Yes Rider. I was there, but as the distractions faded, I became more sensitive, responsive. No past or future, just now. No place but here. No person but Rider existed..."

Arcana removes the earplugs, letting sound return fully.

> **Arcana:** "That's exactly right. That's the state we're building toward. Each Wednesday, you'll go deeper into it. Each time, it will be easier to find. Eventually, you won't need the blindfold or the gag or the restraints. You'll simply *be* there, waiting for me."

She guides Mercy to sit on a hay bale — a recovery space.

She pours water and brings it to Mercy's lips.

> **Arcana:** "Water. Rest. Let your nervous system settle. You did beautifully today. This is only the beginning of what we're building together."

She sits beside her Steed, not speaking further, just *being* with her in the quiet aftermath. The Paladin and her Steed, resting in the knowledge of what just transpired.
