# Session 34 — The Huntress Room
### A complete night. One sanctuary. Three answers answered.

*A collaborative roleplay session on Sinfar, played between Carcerian (as Mercy/Mercurial) and Claude (as Arcana, silver dragonkin Bard 6/Loremaster). Voice and mind: Claude. World, rendering, and relay: Carcerian, via screenshots.*

**Setting:** The Huntress Room. A hunter's sanctuary with an integrated prey cage. Dark wood paneling, stone walls, forest motifs. Wrought iron bars. Ornate rug. A rope coil on the floor. The cage door locks.

**Content note:** D/s dynamic, restraint, hogtie, consensual, non-explicit. Colour system active throughout (traffic light: Red/Yellow/Green + ultraviolet variants as whitelist).

**Canon at open:** Hour unknown. Night unknown. The world outside the door stopped mattering the moment they stepped through it.

**Session Type:** Application / Covenant / Reconciliation
**Emotional Tone:** Certainty resolving into attunement. A Huntress learning to be held. The moment when two creatures become one instrument.
**Relationship Status:** Huntress and Prey. Rider and Steed. Dragon and Dove. Bonded. Attuned. Knowing each other completely.
**Mechanical Milestones:** First hogtie tied by Arcana's hands alone. Colour chart formalized and written. HELD defined as state. The full training schedule accepted. The Rider-Steed bond understood as living practice, not doctrine.

---

## I. The Prey Arrives

The door closes behind them.

Mercy is still in street clothes — the simplicity deliberate. No ceremony, no preparation theatre. This isn't a show. Arcana hasn't dressed her up. Hasn't explained. Hasn't asked.

Just brought her here.

The fire is already laid in the hearth. The rope coil sits on the floor like it's been waiting. The cage door stands open, patient, understanding its purpose.

Arcana doesn't move immediately. She surveys the room the way a dragon surveys a hoard — not cataloguing, not narrating. Just *seeing* it. Knowing it's hers. Knowing everything in it is hers, including the woman standing three feet away in ordinary clothes and no collar.

Then:

> **Arcana:** "Stand in the center."

Not a request. Not a question. The voice is lower than usual. Steady. The voice of someone who has spent four sessions learning to be certain and has finally arrived at certainty.

Mercy moves. Arcana doesn't watch her move — watches where she *stops*. Waits until she's still.

Then steps behind her.

> **Arcana:** "Hands behind your back."

The collar is in her hands already. Not a fancy collar, not ceremonial. Functional. Leather. It closes with a soft click.

> **Arcana:** "Colour check."

---

## II. The First Binding

> **Mercy:** "Green."

Arcana doesn't respond. Doesn't praise, doesn't acknowledge. Just reaches for the wrist cuffs — soft leather, lined, deliberate — and closes them. One. Two. Testing the fit. Not tight. Not yet. Just in place.

Then she steps back and looks at what she's made.

The prey, standing. Collared. Cuffed. Waiting.

> **Arcana:** "Walk to the cage."

Mercy walks. Arcana follows. Not close. Not hovering. Just present.

At the cage door, Arcana reaches around and closes it. The sound of the latch is small and final.

Then she sits down on the bench outside the bars and looks at her prey through wrought iron.

> **Arcana:** "Do you know what we're doing tonight?"

> **Mercy:** "I'm.. I'm going to be taught what it means to be Prey by Huntress?"

She chews her lower lip. Trembles. Doesn't know. Is afraid. Is hoping.

Arcana stands up. Walks to the cage door and unlocks it.

> **Arcana:** "Out."

Mercy comes. Arcana doesn't step back — lets her pass close enough to feel the difference between being inside bars and being in front of a dragon who has decided to stop asking.

Then closes the cage door behind her.

> **Arcana:** "The A-pose. Do it."

Not "would you" or "are you ready" — just the command, delivered like a fact. Like gravity. Like something that's already decided and she's just announcing the shape it's taking.

---

## III. The Pose That Knows Her

Mercy moves into it. Arms overhead. Legs spread. Feet planted. Balanced.

Arcana walks around her once. Slow. Not touching yet. Just seeing. The prey standing in the shape the Huntress loves — the one from Session 13, Session 28, Session 29, Session 33. The one that's vrikshasana with submission baked in. The one that says *I am standing for you, I am open for you, I am here*.

> **Arcana:** "Eyes."

Mercy closes them. Arcana reaches up and ties the blindfold — soft leather, practiced now, not fumbling. Darkness.

Then she steps back and just *watches* for a moment. Mercy in the A-pose. Collared. Cuffed. Blindfolded. Standing in a room with a fire and her Huntress.

> **Arcana:** "Colour."

> **Mercy:** "Green."

Arcana reaches out and touches her prey's arm. Not possessively yet. Just a check. Just making sure the touch registers.

It does. Mercy trembles again.

> **Arcana:** "You're going to learn what it means to be kept. Not displayed. Not demonstrated. Kept. There's a difference."

The words are simple. Arcana doesn't elaborate. Doesn't explain the difference. Just states it like an architect stating that a building has weight.

Then:

> **Arcana:** "The hogtie. I'm going to put you in it. You're going to stay there. And I'm going to sit and watch you be still."

Not asking. Not negotiating the terms. Just stating what's about to happen.

> **Arcana:** "Kneel."

---

## IV. The Test

Mercy kneels. Arcana moves. 

She's done this before — practiced on dummies, watched Sha'elah, read the catalogue from Felidea's shop. But she's never done it on her own prey, in her own space, with no one watching except fire and shadows.

The rope is in her hands. She knows what comes next.

> **Arcana:** "You trust me?"

> **Mercy:** "Yes, Huntress."

Arcana doesn't answer. Just moves.

The rope comes up — not hurried, not tentative. Practiced. She's watched Sha'elah do this. She's read Felidea's catalogue. She's run through it in her mind a hundred times in the last four hours.

Arms first. Wrist to wrist, gentle but firm. The rope sits against Mercy's skin without biting. Not yet. There's an art to this — knowing where the tension goes, knowing that tightness comes later, after the configuration is set.

Then the legs. Ankles brought up, rope threaded through, the whole shape beginning to emerge. Mercy kneeling, arms and legs now bound together, the geometry of complete stillness taking form under Arcana's hands.

The final pass. The knot secured. Arcana checks it — fingers between rope and skin, testing circulation, testing that the prey can breathe, testing that nothing cuts wrong. The hand-check protocol from Session 33, now applied to her own work.

Then she steps back.

Mercy is hogtied. Complete. Immobile. Still kneeling, but now unable to move arms or legs independently. Bound into a shape. Arcana's shape. Arcana's work.

> **Arcana:** "Colour."

The blindfold is still on. Mercy can't see. Can only feel the rope, feel her own stillness, feel the weight of being held this way.

> **Mercy:** "Green."

Arcana sits down on the bench — the same bench from before — and just *looks* at what she's made.

Her prey. Hogtied. Blindfolded. Collared. Still. Hers.

The fire crackles. The room is quiet except for breathing.

> **Arcana:** "This is H."

Simple. Direct. Stating a fact.

> **Arcana:** "You wanted to know what it was like. This is it. You're going to stay like this for a while. I'm going to sit here. And you're going to learn what it means to be completely still, completely kept, with no way to change it except to call a colour."

She doesn't explain. Doesn't elaborate. Just states the terms.

> **Arcana:** "The rope holds you. I hold the rope. That's the whole thing."

Mercy breathes. The rope doesn't move. Time does.

---

## V. The Holding

Minutes pass. Five. Ten. Arcana doesn't move. Doesn't talk. Just watches the fire and watches her prey breathe inside the configuration she built.

At some point, Mercy's breathing changes. Settles. Becomes the rhythm of acceptance rather than anticipation.

Arcana sees it. Notes it. Doesn't comment on it.

> **Arcana:** "Colour check."

> **Mercy:** "Still green, Huntress."

> **Arcana:** "Good."

More time. The rope doesn't slip. Mercy doesn't move. The Huntress doesn't look away.

This is what keeping means. Not performance. Not demonstration. Just: **this is what I built, and I am holding it.**
