Arcana steps back and reads her mount's nervous system.

Perfect stillness. Eyes half-lidded. Breathing synchronized to the priestess's presence. The rope around wrists and ankles hasn't restricted movement yet—it's only *claimed* it.

But the arched spine, the presented torso—that's still waiting.

Arcana approaches Mercy's center and traces one scaled finger along the curve of her spine. The frill-response blooms immediately. Mercy's breath catches. Her nervous system is singing now, responding to the promise of what comes next.

> "This is where the architecture lives. Here. This beautiful spine. This is where you feel me most."

The priestess retrieves a longer length of rope—this one finer, designed for precision work. She begins at Mercy's shoulders, creating a harness that frames the arch of her back. The rope creates geometric patterns: lines that follow the curve of her body, knots that sit at specific points where tension will amplify sensation.

Each loop is tied with priestess-precision. Not tight enough to restrict breathing. Tight enough that Mercy feels *every millimeter* of contact.

The harness descends. Mercy trembles as the rope passes between her ribs. Her eyes squeeze shut briefly—not in pain, but in the overwhelming sensation of being *claimed by architecture*. The priestess is writing a poem in rope across her back, and Mercy's nervous system is reading every word.

By the time Arcana completes the torso harness, Mercy is breathing in small, controlled gasps. Her form is already half-way into beast-space. The rope isn't restraining her yet; it's *speaking* to her.

Arcana steps back and surveys her work. Wrists bound. Ankles bound. Torso encased in geometric precision. Mercy sits at the circle's center, framed by four pillars, looking like a living expression of the priestess's will.

> "Now. Show me what you need. Ground or air?"

Mercy's eyes open and find Arcana's with perfect clarity. Her answer is in her body before words could form:

She arches deeper, lifting her bound wrists toward the nearest pillar above.

*Air.*

## PHASE TWO: AIR SUSPENSION

Arcana reads the request and smiles. The priestess moves with absolute confidence now. She's not ascending Mercy into the sky; she's *choreographing* her rise.

> "Up, slowly."

Mercy rises. Her knees stay planted on the stone as her arms lift. The rope at her wrists becomes taut, pulling her shoulders upward into a perfect arch. Her spine blooms further, and the harness rope shifts, creating new pressure points.

She's not floating yet. She's in that perfect in-between: kneeling, but her weight already distributed across rope and posture. Her torso fully exposed, the geometric harness glowing against white fur.

Her eyes have glazed completely now. She's entering subspace—not dropping, but *ascending*. The distinction she learned weeks ago: not dissolution, but *clarification*.

Arcana moves to the second pillar and attaches Mercy's ankle-ropes there. The geometry shifts again. Now Mercy's lower body is anchored differently, creating tension through her entire form.

She remains kneeling, but *constrained* by four separate points. The pillars hold her in perfect suspension-architecture without lifting her off the ground completely.

> "This is sacred geometry, Mercy. You're suspended in my intention. Every rope is me. Every knot is me. Every point of contact is my decision about what you need."

Mercy's body locks into that state beyond frequency—pure instinct-response. Her nervous system recognizes the precision. The priestess-craft is absolute. The architecture is perfect.

Her tail begins to move, a slow rotation that speaks of contentment deeper than words could reach.

## PHASE THREE: FULL AIR

Arcana reads her mount's nervous system and makes her next decision.

The priestess retrieves additional rope—thicker now, capable of supporting true suspension. She works with absolute precision, creating anchor-points that distribute Mercy's weight perfectly across the four pillars.

Mercy's eyes open briefly, finding the priestess's scaled gaze. Her answer is WHITE. Ultraviolet. Standing permission made manifest.

She is a living sculpture. A priestess's poem written in rope and trust.

Her nervous system reaches a threshold beyond description. She's not prey anymore. She's not pony. She's not in any named subspace. She's in the place where Mercy *becomes* the priestess's will made manifest.

Mercy hangs suspended, rotating slowly on the tensioned ropes. Her eyes are open but unfocused, seeing something beyond the material circle. Her breathing is steady, synchronized with the faint rotation.

The priestess conducts from below, making micro-adjustments. A shift in rope-tension here. A rotation there. Mercy's nervous system responds to each change with absolute precision.

They are conducting together now—priestess and mount, rope and trust, architecture and devotion fused into a single expression.

As the work reaches its natural conclusion, Arcana slows the rotation. Mercy's suspended form moves with less frequency, coming to stillness at the exact center of the four pillars.

The priestess approaches and traces one scaled hand along Mercy's rope-bound arm.

> "You've hung perfectly. You've been exactly what I needed you to be. That's everything."

Mercy's eyes open fully, finding Arcana's scaled face. Her gaze is clear but transcendent. She is still suspended. Still bound. Still in that perfect architecture.
