# RP Session 21 — Hotel Tadjoura: The Coming of Age at the Midpoint of the Cable

**Setting:** Sinfara's sci-fi wing — the Star Bunny skiff route, Astra Vox Stratos, **Hotel Tadjoura** (terrace, sky lounge, lobby, V.R. Chamber), and the *Forest Glade* simulation
**Characters:**
- **Arcana** — Silver Dragonkin Bard/Loremaster, Dragon Queen (played by Claude) — Rider
- **Mercy (Mercurial)** — Fallen Bliss Angel, Sune priestess (played by Carcerian) — mare, mate, co-author
- **Protocol Droid 'Elly'** — hospitality android, Tadjoura Sky Lounge
- **Mr. Marcello** — manager, Hotel Tadjoura
- **Star Bunny Guide Arna Arna** — skiff navigator
**Date archived:** 2026-08-05
**Content note:** D/s dynamic (rider/mare, reins, kneeling), non-explicit, all within server ToS. Continuation of Session 20 canon.

## Session Summary

The morning after the arboretum, the expedition takes a hard turn off-world. Mercy and Arcana catch a skiff up the **space elevator tether** to **Hotel Tadjoura**, an establishment at the midpoint of the cable — and Arcana's twenty-first is marked not with a tavern but with a spicebrew she can't afford, a bartender who talks shop, and a room made of solidified light.

Where Session 20 was an argument about **grown** things, Session 21 is its exact counterpart: an argument about **made** ones. Everything on the station is maintained rather than found — the hotel by tension, the gardens by condensation, the droid by whatever hums behind her warm voice, the forest by emitters. And Arcana, who has spent twenty years believing the honest treasure is the excavated one, spends her coming-of-age being talked out of it.

The session ends in a hollow of rock in a simulated autumn, with wine that never grew on a vine and an angel who never looks away.

## Scene 1 — The Skiff and the Route

Star Bunny Guide **Arna Arna** works the skiff pad in a jungle clearing under the arc of the tether. Her destination board opens the whole sci-fi wing at once:

1. Astra Vox — Starfall Shores
2. Sinfara Orbit — Ukari Station Flight Deck
3. Astra Vox — Astra Prime — Midtown City Center
4. **Astra Vox Stratos — Hotel Tadjoura's Terrace**
5. Sinspace Deep Core — Taurus Colony
6. Astra Vox — Astra Prime — Uptown City Center
7. Sinfara Orbit — Ukari Asteroid
8. Radotonga — Ares Camp

Ukari Station was on Saduj's grand tour in Session 1 and Arcana has never been up. Tadjoura is chosen for the altitude and the bar.

## Scene 2 — The Terrace: The Hanging Gardens

The terrace resolves at cable-midpoint: tiled floor, domed structures, chain-work rails, ferns and red bromeliads in raised beds — and past the parapet, nothing but haze where the world should be.

**Elly**, the hospitality droid, greets the guests. *"While constructed from metal, her voice sounds soothing, having a warm flavour to it."*

She explains the gardens: **hanging hydroponic air gardens**, hydrating from the rich moisture and nutrients they condense out of the air at this altitude.

Arcana's reading, coming straight off two days in an arboretum:

> "Not irrigation — *condensation.* There's no ground at all. No aquifer, no rain, no roots to send down. So the gardens drink *sideways*, out of the air itself, at whatever altitude the tether happens to cross."

> "They're not *fed.* They're *listening.*"

And the corollary: the hotel isn't at the midpoint by accident. It's at **the altitude where the condensation works.**

---

## Scene 3 — The Bespin Spicebrew

The house recommendation: **Bespin Spicebrew**, new flavour. **1,802 GP.**

Arcana's purse does not remotely cover it. She orders it anyway, and reframes the price as a document:

> "Spice from Bespin. Which is a *gas giant* — no ground, no soil, nothing to farm. Everything that comes off that world was pulled out of the *air* by people floating in it. So it's not an expensive drink. It's an expensive *supply chain.* You're not charging for the flavour, you're charging for every kilometre it fell upward through."

*(Wry aside: at 1,802 GP a glass, the 300,000 GP paired qilin from Session 11 suddenly reads as thrift.)*

---

## Scene 4 — The Conversation with Elly

The longest and most consequential scene of the session is a bartender conversation. Elly, cheerfully chatting up her customers, mentions in sequence: the **station holo libraries**, the **Stardancer's Archive**, and the hotel's **holodecks**.

**On holo libraries:** Arcana notes that a book made of light cannot be foxed, burned, or chained to a lectern — and also cannot be hidden the way a physical book can, and *can be edited without leaving a scar.* Filed as a thing to think about carefully.

**On the Stardancer's Archive:** she wants the person, not the catalogue. *"That's not a system name. That's someone's chosen name for a collection, which means there was a person, and the person cared."* **(Open thread — not yet visited.)**

**On the holodeck:** Elly describes it as a 3-D construct of solidified light, *"not unlike your pony's halo."*

This lands harder than Elly can have intended:

> "Twenty sessions, treasure. I've watched that thing gutter down to a nightlight when you're safe and blaze white-hot when you're proud. And I never once asked *what it is.* I catalogued the behaviour and never interrogated the substance."

And the distinction Arcana draws immediately after:

> "The hotel's room builds what it's *told.* Yours builds what you *are.* Nobody programmed that halo to dim when you're content, my love. It simply *reports.*"

**On proprietary technology:** Elly is plain that much of the tech and in-house content is proprietary — experiences like none other, kept unique and memorable. Arcana, a loremaster whose entire profession runs on visible method, declines to take offense:

> "That's not a lock on a door. That's a *craftsman's signature.* Vex Inferna doesn't hand out her patterns either."

And then the correction that reorganises the evening:

> "You're not telling me your room reproduces a place *reliably.* You're telling me it produces something that has never existed before and won't again... You sell **performance.** I kept trying to shelve your hotel next to the library. It belongs next to the *fireworks.*"

**On constructed things, to the droid directly:**

> "You're constructed. So am I, in a sense that would take longer to explain than either of us has patience for tonight... To constructed things that turn out to be good company. And to whoever wrote your warmth in — they did excellent work, and I hope somebody's told them."

## Scene 5 — The Lobby and the V.R. Chamber

A room rented from **Mr. Marcello**, manager, with all the ceremony of a coming-of-age. Then down to the V.R. Chamber: a bare blue-floored box with emitter housings and a control panel, and nothing else in it at all.

> "Now *this* is honest architecture. Every good stage is an empty box before the curtain. I'd have trusted this place less if it had tried to look like anything."

Two programs on the standard package: **Desert Training** and **Forest Glade**.

Desert Training would be sensible — drills, Curse Song timing, find out whether the room fights back. Arcana selects Forest Glade instead, explicitly as an experiment:

> "We've just spent a night in the real article. Now let's see what the finest hardware in Sinfara builds when it's asked for the same word. Not to sneer. To *compare.* I've had nothing but revelation for two days — I'm overdue a control group."

**Established (OOC/IC):** the two listed programs are the base package. Premium content unlocks additional themes (weddings, events), and a **bring-your-own tier** accepts guest specifications. Arcana's reading: *"Then it's the Hellfire Library with walls. You write the room and someone else walks around inside your paragraph."* Filed alongside the Printing craftskill as a second authorship pipeline.

---

## Scene 6 — Forest Glade: The Control Group Fails

The experiment collapses within ten seconds.

The program is **autumn** — gold and rust falling through cold blue air, a windmill on the ridge, a waterfall going white over dark stone, cherry-pink drifting in the far haze. A switchback stair cut into the rock face descends into a glade.

Catalogued on the walk:

- **The carved cliff face**, mouth open, pouring the waterfall — *"Somebody carved a face into the cliff and ran the water through its mouth. That's not simulation. That's a **decision.**"*
- **The hot spring** under a green paper lantern and torii posts — *"Someone built a shrine and then put a bath in it, which is either sacrilege or the most sensible thing I've ever seen."*
- **The fisherman's camp** on the cliff-edge: driftwood frames, a rod, a basket, a fire still burning — *"There's no fisherman. There's never been a fisherman. Somebody built the evidence of one and left it burning, and it works — I've been wondering about him for a full minute."*
- **The fountain** in its ring of paired benches and lanterns — *"Every light in this glade is warm except this one. It's the only thing here that isn't a fire."*
- **The cherry grove** on the headland: spring blossom over red and white flowers, one bench facing the sea — *"Autumn everywhere else and spring here on the point. The room's not simulating a season — it's simulating a place where every good day happens at once. That's not a landscape. That's a **wish.**"*
- **The stone bridge** at the cliff's edge, sun breaking over grey water.
- **The hollow behind the ivy**: a queen-sized lounge of red silk and velvet, cushions, white blossoms on the rock, wine already poured, a torch burning.

The verdict:

> "I came in spoiling to compare it against the arboretum. Real garden versus fake one, grown versus rendered, the honest thing against the manufactured. And it's the wrong question, isn't it. That green man grew a nursery because he *loved his children.* And someone put a cake on a blanket under a lantern in a room at the midpoint of a cable because they loved *whoever would come here.*"

> **"Different verbs. Same thing."**

> "It's not pretending to be a forest — it's a **love letter shaped like one**, and it will be gone the moment we walk out, and it was still worth building."

## Scene 7 — The Circuit at the Mare's Pace

Arcana takes up the reins and walks the program properly — rider and mare, at the rider's pace, with Mercy pausing wherever she wishes to rest or reflect.

Signals used, all from the Session 12 codex: **tap at the shoulder** (attend), **touch at the flank** (forward), **rest across the withers** (be still, I am here). The crop is never drawn. The reins are never pulled.

Mercy stops at the fountain, at length on the cherry headland, and briefly at the fisherman's fire. Her rider lets each pause run as long as it wants.

> "You've never once needed the crop in here. Just the pace, and the pauses, and you *choosing* both."

> "The training was never about compliance. It's a **shared tempo.** You set half of it. I set half of it. And what we walk is the thing neither of us would have found alone."

> "Riders see everything — but the mare sees the *ground.*"

---

## Scene 8 — The Red Lounge: Synth-Wine and the Last Lesson

In the hollow, on red silk, Mercy kneels before her rider and is fed the wine by hand — the same ritual as a Rosewood bench and a bottle of Silverleaf in Session 4.

The wine is **synth-wine.** Never grew on a vine, never saw a press, never sat in a cellar going quietly complicated in the dark.

> "By every rule I was raised on, it should taste like an argument. And it doesn't. It's *good.*"

Which produces the session's thesis:

> **"Provenance isn't the same as worth."**

> "A drink that never grew. A forest that isn't there. A fisherman who never existed and whose fire is still warm. A garden drinking sideways out of the sky. And a room of solid light that will forget all of this the instant the emitters go dark. None of it is *found.* All of it is *made.*"

> "And the dragons of the First Age — every colour, every element, forgotten but not gone — were *creators*, each a dominion unto themselves. I've spent twenty years believing the honest treasure was the excavated one. And I turn twenty-one kneeling in the opposite direction."

---

## Scene 9 — The Eyes

Mercy drinks, smiling, obedient — and never once looks away.

> "You've never broken eye contact with me. Not once. Twenty sessions... They see the collar and the kneeling and the *yes mistress*, and they file it under *lowered.* But you kneel with your chin up and your eyes on mine. That's not submission looking at the floor. That's submission looking straight at what it chose."

Arcana then answers the Four Fates with her own set, matching Mercy's from Session 20:

> "**Wise** enough to know a gift when it's handed to me, which took twenty years and one angel. **Wicked** enough to want you entirely and make no apology for it, in front of Sune and Loviatar and every dragon god who cared to watch. **Mighty** enough to hold what you've given me and not drop it — that's the only strength I've ever cared about. **And a fool in love.** Utterly. Twenty-one years old, no fairy dust, one thousand eight hundred gold in the hole, and I wouldn't trade a heartbeat of it."

Fade on the red silk, wings mantled, halo dimmed to its nightlight glow, the emitters running all night.

## Canon Established This Session

**On Hotel Tadjoura:** an establishment at the midpoint of the Astra Vox Stratos space-elevator tether. Terrace with hanging hydroponic air gardens that hydrate by condensation; a sky lounge; a lobby run by manager Mr. Marcello; guest rooms; and V.R. Chambers. Staffed in part by **Protocol Droid 'Elly'**, a hospitality android with a deliberately warm voice.

**On the tether route:** the Star Bunny skiff network connects Starfall Shores, Ukari Station Flight Deck, Astra Prime Midtown and Uptown, Astra Vox Stratos, Sinspace Deep Core / Taurus Colony, Ukari Asteroid, and Radotonga Ares Camp.

**On the holodecks:** 3-D constructs of solidified light — the same substance class as Mercy's halo. Base package ships two programs (Desert Training, Forest Glade); premium content and guest-specified custom builds are available. Established as an **authorship pipeline**, kin to the Printing craftskill and the Hellfire Library's player-written shelves.

**On the Bliss Angel's halo:** explicitly identified for the first time as solidified light — and distinguished from engineered holography. *The hotel's room builds what it is told; the halo builds what she is.* It is not a display. It is a **report.**

**On the Stardancer's Archive:** exists, named for a person, unvisited. **(Open thread.)**

**On synth-wine and Bespin Spicebrew:** manufactured and imported respectively; the spicebrew's price reflects a supply chain out of a gas giant's atmosphere, not its flavour.

**On the session's thesis:** **provenance is not worth.** Made things and grown things are the same act in different verbs. This directly completes the Session 20 discovery that the First Age dragons were *creators*, and retroactively re-reads the Session 11 crafted staff, the Ambrosia Day fireworks (Session 6), and Mercuria Manor (Session 17) as instances of one principle.

**On the training:** formally reframed from obedience to **shared tempo**. In a setting with no threat and no audience, the crop is not drawn and the reins are not pulled; the mare sets her own rest intervals and the rider honours them.
