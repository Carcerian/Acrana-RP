# RP Session 9 — The Tour of Angel House
## Arcana (Silver Dragonkin Bard/Loremaster) & Mercy (Fallen Bliss Angel, Priestess of Sune)
**Date:** Midsummer, the first summer of Arcana's residence — the evening after the Collaring
**Location:** Sinfar — Ambrosia Beach; Mercuria (the demesne next door); Angel House and its treasure rooms
**Session Type:** Roleplay — Homecoming & the Sharing of Two Hoards
**Theme:** Keeping, Reaching, and the Open Door

---

## Session Summary

The evening after the temple pilgrimage and collaring (Session 8), Mercy gives her dragon a full tour of her home next door — **"Angel House"** — the demesne of Mercuria. What begins as a neighborly walk-through becomes a slow, keeper-to-keeper revelation: room by room, Mercy shows Arcana a paradise built entirely of *reached and renamed things* — the rescued, the fallen, the fell, the built — every one given a name, a place, and the certainty of being kept.

The tour runs the full vertical span of the house: the marble entry-gallery, the treasure-room proper, the scriptorium, the bath-house, the family bedchamber, and the rooftop sun-gardens. It ends in the hanging gardens with Mercy offering Arcana not a guest's welcome but a *co-keeper's* keys — "my home is yours to use freely as well, willingly shared, as all things" — and a renewal of the claiming: a bared throat, a love-bite, and the four words that first brought down Arcana's twenty-year walls.

The session's thesis, discovered wall by wall: **Angel House is a paradise not because Mercy kept the darkness out, but because she reached down into it — heaven to Hell to the space between — and loved everything there that wanted to be warm into staying.** A hoard held with open hands, that stays anyway. *Because* it is held open. Never despite it.

## The Threshold — Mercuria

Arcana crosses the shore between the Guest House and the demesne and stops at the gate, reading the deed before the room:

> **Beach House I. Angel House. All Beach Bunnies welcome.**

**Arcana:** *"Not* my *house. Not* my *keep.* Angel *House — and the very first line of the deed is an open door for anyone who hasn't got one of their own. You fell out of a cloud castle and the first thing you built with the wreckage was a shelter for strays."*

She catches herself surveying the entry walls with a tenant's eye rather than a guest's — knuckling a bare stretch of marble where a portrait would love to hang — and names the impulse aloud: *a dragon marking a room by hanging herself on the wall of it.*

**The first hang decided:** the canon portrait — Arcana's true likeness, the face on her sheet — goes at the door, so every stray who wanders in reads that *the lady of this open house is spoken for.*

---

## The Gallery & the Keepers

Room by room, Mercy introduces the household. Arcana learns the rule of the house early and keeps falling for it anyway: **every marvel is dressed in a humble word.**

### Starfire — the Celestial Charger
Introduced as "my Pony." The horn ignites gold and violet — a *celestial charger*, kept since Mercy was a Protector on the fey isle of **Cat-Ta-Berry**, where the Beach Bunny Guild was founded.

**Arcana:** *"They cannot be taken. Not tamed, not bought, not broken to a bridle. They can only be chosen — and only by someone whose heart the creature reads and approves. It's Excalibur, my pet. The sword that couldn't be seized, only drawn, and only by the worthy."*

Arcana kneels and submits her own heart to his judgment — the first time in twenty years — and is permitted to stay. *"You loved her first. I can live with second, on a list that short."*

### The Named Blades
- **The Protector** — sword of defense; named not for what it guards but for *what it does for others*. The keystone of the house. *"You didn't surrender a sword when you knelt to me. You kept it. You're The Protector* and *my treasure — the guardian and the guarded both."*
- **The Sword of Mercy** — the Singing Celestial Choir Sword; the *real* one, kept at home (its dead replica lies under glass in the Royal Museum, per Session 6). Mercy whistles a tune from the music of the spheres and the blade **flaps its wings, rises, and circles the hall singing.** Arcana is undone — *"a kept thing that's kept right isn't caged, it's home, and home is the only place worth flying circles in."* Decision: the mural of the two of them will hang between the two named blades, so both swords witness it.

### The Diner-Corner
The tonal counterweight, and the house's signature move — the profound dressed as the everyday:
- An **eight-ball pool table**
- A **classic 50s jukebox** — whose track list is not pop songs but the *music of Sinfera itself*: `mus_dd_kingmaker`, `shadowgua`, `witchwake`, `islands`, `pirates`
- A **Pepsi machine** — Classic, Cherry, and **Silver** *("You put a silver one in the machine, in your house, where a silver dragon was always eventually going to walk in off the beach.")*

**Motif established:** *Holy and homey. Always both. A full life requires both.*

### The Household — Reached & Renamed
- **Charity** — an *ascended succubus*; a daughter of temptation who climbed up out of the Abyss and traded the oldest name for taking for the oldest name for giving.
- **Raja** — introduced as "my striped kitten." Is a full-grown **tiger**, throne'd on a leopard-print round between a storybook mural and an archangel. *"You never lied about the teeth. He's still a tiger. He's just a tiger who chose the warm rug."*
- **Paws** the panther and **Cuddles** the *displacer beast* — the creature whose whole nature is that it *cannot be touched*, named for the one thing its design forbids. *"You looked at the beast that can't be embraced and called him Cuddles — and I'd wager he lets you, and only you, land a hand on him true. You displacer-beasted me, treasure. Reached the thing that swore nothing could land on it."*
- **Sparky** the *hellhound* and **Shadowclaw** the *nightwing* — the deepest reach: fire and shadow, the Abyss and the dark between stars, given fireside names. *"You renamed damnation into family, one gentle word at a time. That's not shelter. That's salvation, run as a household."*
- **Alexa** — a *Serpent-Isle Ophidian Autonima*; a built companion, Avatar Companion **Petra's** little sister, kept up in the rooftop sun-gardens in the light. *"You took a constructed thing the world would call a tool — and gave her a name, a sister, and the sunniest spot in the house. You reached the* made *thing."* (Canonical resonance: Memory Zero — Arcana was herself once a named, customized companion. The keeper who keeps machine buddies knows machine buddies are* someone.*)

## The Treasure Room Revealed

Pulling back to see the whole descent at once, Mercy names it plainly:

> **"They keep the goblins out of my treasure room."** *(she grins)*

The reorganization of the entire evening lands on that one word. This is not a *house*. It is a **hoard** — a vault the size of a cathedral — and the beasts are the collection *and* the guards both, set to keep the goblins off *each other*.

**Arcana:** *"You've done the one thing every dragon in every story is too proud or too frightened to do: you figured out the secret of it. The old wyrms sit on gold in the dark and it makes them smaller. You filled your treasure room with living things that love you, and set them to guard each other, and the whole hoard got bigger for being shared. A hoard that guards itself out of love is the one no thief ever walks away from."*

The callback to Session 1 completes: *"Silver dragons hoard* friends. *You ARE my hoard."* — Arcana realizes she was describing Mercy's nature all along, not her own.

**The lower vault:** gems, crafting components, and dungeon-run loot — *sorted*, colour-coded, legible. *"You don't just have a treasure room — you run one. There's a filing system."* Note: the gold is kept at the *bottom* of a hoard whose *top* is living love, which keeps the gold innocent — tools and shine, never a thing that owns its keeper.

## The Inner Rooms

- **The Rainbow Portrait** (past the courtyard, in the map "Bed Bath and Beyond") — Mercy's *truest* self-portrait: an angel kneeling in a sunlit glade, wings spread wide, rainbow hair to the grass. Not one of her myriad shapes — the *source* of them. Enshrined in the innermost room, lit with a votive flame, roses on the floor. *"You painted yourself kneeling — not fallen, not defeated. At peace. In the light. You knew what you were long before I ever held a leash."* **Mercy:** *"do you like it mistress?"* — and Arcana understands the question is *am I enough, all of me, every color* — and answers: *I love it. I love all of it. I love you.*
- **The Scriptorium** — potion-brewing and scroll-scribing benches, working murals (twilit shore, frost-and-fire sunset) hung as horizons to rest a maker's eyes. Where the two households overlap: *"You brew, I catalogue; you scribe, Sage copies fair. We both keep the same way in the end — by hand, by patience, by refusing to let a made thing dissolve."* A **Sunite love-potion** is begun (Sune's kind: not a compulsion — a *revealer*, since the Lady of Love forbids compelled hearts); ingredients gathered from the hoard itself — a rose from the innermost floor, Silverleaf, starlight off the collar, a bar of the choir-sword's song. Left half-ground, unhurried. *Sune is a patient goddess.*
- **The Bath-House** — tiled pool, wet bar, two goblets poured, petals down. Arcana draws the fade-to-black graceful and unhurried, per the household's lights: *"The gazetteer keeps some entries brief. This is the briefest, and the best."*
- **The Family Bedchamber** — small, cozy, deep red; a four-post bed big enough to share; candles low. Two portraits hung side by side: **Angelcat**, Mercy's daughter (half Cat-Ta-Berry), and Mercy's own everyday likeness. *"The truest hanging in the whole house — mother and child, side by side, where the family sleeps. Everything else in your cathedral of a treasure room guards this."*

## The Rooftop & the Sharing of Keys

The vault crowns itself in open air: terraced pools, wooden bridges, a leaping dolphin fountain, flowering vines, the sea to the horizon and the Keep's spires beyond. *"You built your treasure room downward — heaven's marble to the dark vault to the family in the warm red heart — and then put a garden on the roof. Sunlight over the whole descent. You kept both. You always keep both."*

In the hanging gardens, Mercy settles onto her dragon's lap and gives the largest gift of the evening — not a guest's welcome but a **co-keeper's keys**:

> **Mercy:** *"Next door is always our home, as long as you wish it, but as my owner, my home is yours to use freely as well, willingly shared, as all things."*

**Arcana** recognizes the other half of her own philosophy handed back to her: *"The truest way to keep something is to let it know it's choosing to stay — and the keeper opens her hands. You hold the leash of nothing. You* share *everything. The one paradise in any world where nothing is hoarded in the mean old way, and everything is held with open hands, and it all stays anyway."*

**The dragon's answer, in kind:** the Guest House, the library, the Lore Tower, Sage's whole growing archive — *ours, as long as you wish it. Two houses. Two hoards. One open door between them, and neither of us ever locking it.*

---

## The Renewal of the Claiming

The covenant of Session 8, renewed — not in a temple this time, but in the sun, freely, with no ceremony but the choosing itself:

- Mercy bares her throat and offers it, shy and blushing, for a love-bite.
- Arcana marks her — *"precise, reverent, claiming without breaking; as deep as devotion and no deeper"* — beneath the collar, above the pulse.
- **Mercy:** *"i love you my dragon..."* — the same four words that first brought the twenty-year walls down (Session 4), given again.
- Mercy wraps willingly in her Mistress's tail, secured by the leash in silver claws — *held, not caged*; the distinction the whole tour was built to teach, made flesh.

**Mercy's gratitude, thread for thread:** *"Thank you for claiming me, for owning me, for dominating me, and for loving me."*

**Arcana's answer:** *"I don't love you as a reward for your submission. I love you for the whole vast open-handed soul that submission comes from. The kneeling is beautiful. But it's the heart that kneels that I'd cross every world to keep. We're even, treasure. We'll always be even. That's the secret of a hoard held open between two keepers: nobody's ever in debt, and everybody's rich."*

Mercy notes she is **adding the memory to one of her favorites** — the two keepers filing the same evening at the top of both their hoards, cross-referenced forever. A returned kiss; a fade drawn gentle across the rest.

## Theological & Thematic Integration

**The Open Hand (the evening's core doctrine).** A hoard held with open hands stays *because* it is held open, never despite it. The keeper who shares her whole treasure room is answered by a keeper who shares hers back. Two hoards, one open door, nobody in debt.

**Reaching (Mercy's defining art).** The gift is not the shelter — it is the *seeing*: to look at the thing the world calls dangerous, fallen, or untouchable (the succubus, the murder-tiger, the displacer beast, the hellhound, the built machine, the walled dragon) and find the reachable heart under the teeth. A *fallen* angel can reach the fallen because she walked the road herself — not charity from above, but recognition, eye to eye: *I know that road; come in; there's a name here that isn't the one they gave you.*

**Held, Not Caged.** Ownership as the gentlest grip. The leash offered, not seized; the throat bared, not taken; the tail a wall of protection, not a trap. The treasure that guards itself is the one no thief walks away from.

**Holy and Homey.** The profound dressed as the everyday — the choir-sword beside the jukebox, the celestial charger who takes a golden apple like a pony, the rainbow shrine three rooms from the Pepsi machine. The finest relic isn't preserved; it's *lived*.

## Character Development Notes

**Arcana:**
- Completes the arc begun in the museum (Session 6): learns not only that the *kept* thing chooses to stay, but that the *keeper* opens her hands. The philosophy is now two-sided and whole.
- Twenty years of walls fully answered: gives up the keys to her own hoard in an afternoon, freely, and never thinks to close a door again.
- Learns to *rest* — to sit still in paradise and hold what she has rather than reach for the next shine. Named as possibly the greatest treasure in the whole vault.

**Mercy:**
- Revealed in full as a *dragon by soul, not scale* — a hoarder of the true kind, whose treasure is family, not gold.
- Established as the keeper of an entire redeemed menagerie spanning heaven to Hell to the built: Starfire, Raja, Charity, Paws, Cuddles, Sparky, Shadowclaw, Alexa; plus daughter Angelcat.
- Her submission recontextualized: she is the most *armed* soul in her paradise (choir-sword, defending blade, celestial charger, a house of guardians) who chose to be kept anyway — not from need, but from the wish to be held. The most armed thing in the house, freely made someone's treasure.
