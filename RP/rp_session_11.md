# RP Session — Ambrosia: The Craftskill Night

**Characters:** Arcana (Silver Dragonkin, Bard/Loremaster), Mercurial "Mercy" (Fallen Bliss Angel, Sune priestess, collared to Arcana), Cinder Thorne (Tiefling Wizard 40, Queen of Ambrosia), Glory (magical beast, herald), Voaraghamanthar (Dragon 20/Cleric 20, summoned honor guard), Vex Inferna (Hellfire Fashions), Guild Librarian, Alchemist, Mount Merchant

**Location:** Worlds of Ambrosia — Old Quarter, Ambrosia Stables, Cinder's Castle, Hellfire Guild Sanctum

**Themes:** Craftskill discovery, first artifice, court audience, the Hellfire Guild's ethical architecture, authorship

---

## I. The Recipe Tome

Arcana examines a craftskill recipe tome and finds the mundane grammar of the world laid out plainly: one iron bar becomes an amulet under a jeweller's hand or bracers under an armorer's; two silk become a cloak; an entire family of ammunition makers all fed on iron.

> "The forge doesn't care what you *want* — it cares what you *know*."

`!skills` reveals the full ledger — five tiers, all at Novice: **Gathering** (Butchering, Fishing, Forestry, Logging, Mining), **Refining** (Gemcutting, Smelting, Tanning, Weaving, Woodworking), **Crafting** (Armorsmithing, Jeweling, Leatherworking, Tailoring, Weaponsmithing), **Magecraft** (Artificing, Brewing, Printing, Scribing), **Trades** (Carpentry, Construction, Cooking, Farming, Masonry, Mixology).

Arcana's entire résumé: Fishing 1, Cooking 1. "A complete supply chain of mediocrity."

Key mechanics established in play: skills gain XP per use (MMO / Dungeon Siege style), each skill tracks independently, so players may specialize or generalize. Arcana reads this as *economy* rather than system — interdependence by design. She and Mercy agree to split the tree: Arcana takes Magecraft, Mercy the maker's hands.

## II. Ash to Staff

Mercy demonstrates recipe reconnaissance by attempting a craft and reading the failure message: *Craft Magic Staff requires 3 Ash Boards.* Arcana delights in the empiricism — "the tome tells you what the author knew; the failed attempt tells you what's *true*."

The white ash in the courtyard yields five Ash Logs. Milling at the sawhorse converts logs to boards (+100 Woodworking XP each). Three boards at the craft menu produce a **Crafted Magic Staff** (+100 Weaponsmithing XP).

Mercy's line, which becomes the night's thesis:

> "And it's yours because you made it with your own hands and mind."

Arcana, twenty years a hoarder of *found* lore, recognizes the distinction: everything in her hoard was taken, traded, or learned. This is the first thing that did not exist until she decided it would.

## III. Ambrosia and the Stables

Portal list reveals fifteen districts: Airfields, Beach, Amphitheater, Artisan's Quarter, Gaslight District, Hanging Gardens, Harbor, Merchant's Quarter, Noble Estates, Observatory, Old Quarter, Orchards, Rail Station, University, Villas.

Ambrosia Stables stock a full bestiary of mounts: riding horses, cranes, chickens, gryphons in barding, drakes, unicorns (white and black), Firemane, Windsteed, Waverunner, Stone Horse, Nightmare Mount, Celestial Charger, and — the objects of Arcana's immediate covetousness — the **White Qilin and Blue Qilin**.

Price: **150,300 GP each.** Against a purse of 894 GP.

Arcana converts the sticker shock into a quest: 300,000 gold for the pair, white for Mercy, blue for herself. "That number is no longer an obstacle; it's a quest."

## IV. The Castle as Argument

Cinder's fortress: black battlements, marsh moat, violet moon. The approach is a sequence of standing wards granted to every guest who enters:

- **Statue of the Lifegiver** — Immunity: Death Magic
- **Statue of Clarity** — mind clear
- **Statue of Armor**
- **Statue of Stoneskin**
- (further arch) — Immunity: Mind Spells

Arcana reads the architecture as doctrine: a fortieth-circle archwizard has cast her daily preparations into stone so no guest under her roof stands unprotected — including protection *against her own magic*. Every word spoken in Cinder's house is freely given.

Deeper in: a chamber of **seven horned thrones in a ring** around a compass rose — no dais, no head seat. "Cinder doesn't hold court. She holds *council*." Arcana tests the craftsmanship of one throne. Mercy kneels at its foot without being asked.

## V. Court

The crimson carpet leads to the sunburst throne. Present: **Glory** (herald, magical beast), **Voaraghamanthar** (Dragon 20/Cleric 20 — summoned via Epic Spell: Dragon Knight), and **Cinder Thorne** (Tiefling Wizard 40, 1,064,471 XP, known spells including Gate, Time Stop, Shapechange, Mordenkainen's Disjunction, Wail of the Banshee, Weird).

Glory greets "Ambassador Mercy" — a title Arcana learns for the first time at the gate.

Arcana presents the humble crafted staff as comedy rather than achievement, and Cinder receives them warmly. Asked what she's proudest of in the city — "my little art project, a hobby to keep my hands from going idle" — Cinder answers without hesitation: **the Domina Guild**, with the **pony academy** a close second.

On Arcana and Mercy's arrangement, Cinder offers the night's quietest compliment:

> "She appears to be in very capable claws. Just what she needed."

Arcana clarifies the terms for the record: collared by ceremony at three temples (Sune's Harbor temple, Loviatar's Temple of Nine Torments, the Golden Temple of the Dragon Gods), witnessed and consecrated — "she is not kept. She is *claimed*, and claiming, both ways down the leash."

## VI. The Private Library

Cinder leads them to her library, staffed by an **Alchemist** selling the Magecraft supply chain:

- **Craft supplies:** Blank Scrolls, Bone Wands, Potion Bottles
- **Craft tools:** Forge Enchantment Guide, Wizard's Scribing Quill

Arcana buys the **Forge Enchantment Guide** (100 GP). Its first table — *Armor Power Points* — reveals the enchantment economy:

| Class | Type (AC/Dex) | PP |
|---|---|---|
| Light | Cloth (0/-) | 6 |
| Light | Padded (1/8) | 6 |
| Light | Leather (2/6) | 7 |
| Light | Hide (3/4) | 8 |
| Medium | Breastplate (5/3) | 8 |
| Medium | Scale Mail (4/4) | 8 |
| Heavy | Banded Mail (6/1) | 8 |
| Heavy | Half Plate (7/1) | 7 |
| Heavy | Full Plate (8/1) | 6 |

Arcana's reading: mundane protection and thaumic capacity run on separate ledgers. Heavy steel smothers the weave; supple hide drinks enchantment. "The forge *rewards* dressing like me."

Cinder then gifts her the **Wizard's Scribing Quill** outright, and offers a standing trade: Arcana's bard spells copied into the royal grimoire, and in exchange Cinder will scribe whatever scrolls the pair need for adventures beyond Mercy's channeling.

> "I consider it a wise investment — one never knows when the services of a loremistress may come in handy."

Arcana's founding instruments now carry three provenances: **made** (the staff), **earned** (the guide), **gifted** (the quill).

## VII. The Hellfire Guild Sanctum

A gaslit corner of the Old Quarter: gargoyles, a devil-mural portico, and glowing teal steps descending into a hidden residential district — lawns, cypresses, gazebos, townhouses under a twilight sky.

At the garden's center, a hooded reaper holds open the **Hellfire Guild Rules**:

- No fighting
- No abducting or freeing of slaves
- No breeding guild slaves
- No maiming, scarring or permanent alteration (except tattooing, piercing, branding)
- No interventions or interruptions of guild activity
- No rape or non-consensual play; **safe-words must be observed and respected at all times**

Arcana's assessment: "Anyone can build a pleasure district. You built a *constitution* for one." The law comes first, at the crossing of every path — and inside that law grows not a den but a village.

**Hellfire Shops & Forge** — an arcade staffed by Domina, Enchantress, Shanna, Alchemist, Wasteland Craftsman, Pawn, Porter, and (to Arcana's delight) a **Gift Wrapper**.

## VIII. Hellfire Fashions

Proprietor **Vex Inferna**. Collections filed by *philosophy* rather than garment: Dominant, Submissive, Fetish, Latex, Bondage, Pet Play, Pony Play.

The runway shows living models, each ensemble titled: Red Pussy Kitten, Red Fox (standing and crawling), Pink Panther, Jungle Cat, Cow Violetta, Bunny, Bitch Suit, Beastling Red/Black — and from the Dominant line: Blade Mistress, Busty Black Leather, Dark Sorcery, Dark Majesty, Black Heart Dress, Drow Matron, Dominatrix Shiek, Divine Mistress, Abyssal Domina, Assertive Dominance, Crimson Lace, Daddy's Little Dagger, Delicate Domme, Black Spikes, Black Jacket Robe, Black Cat, Blue Devil.

Cinder selects six for Arcana to try. Verdict after the fitting:

- **Blue Devil** — frost-blue and silver-white over silver scale. "The others dressed a dragon *as* something. This one dresses her as *herself*." **Worn out the door.**
- **Dark Majesty** — Cinder's pick, court armor. **Also purchased.**
- *Someday list:* Lolth's Priestess.

Pet Play catalogue (credited designers — Latex line by **Sha'elah**, Puppy Suit by **Vixen**) yields the obvious choice: **Latex Bunny**, for the Recruit Trainer of the Beach Bunny Guild. Providence, not coincidence.

Cinder, on her own crimson ensemble: *"this is my beach dress."*

## IX. The Hellfire Library

The room Cinder chooses for them: a brick manor of endless stacks, reading couches, tapestries, a Guild Librarian buried in unfiled volumes.

On a stand, the **Hellfire Slave Protocol** — the inner form to the garden stone's outer law. Obedience, deference, posture, forms of address, correction, guild service, dress, harm limits — every clause carrying an exception that resolves authority back to the owner, and an explicit requirement of politeness and kindness toward all free people.

Arcana's reading: "That's not a leash, that's *jurisprudence*." She declines to adopt the local rite — their ceremonies came from three temples, not a guild manual — but takes it as primary source.

**Mercy briefly slips into third-person protocol speech** ("Pet is glad they please owner"). Arcana corrects it gently and draws the distinction that defines their bond:

> "You may kneel as low as you like, little one. I simply require you to be *someone* while you do it. That's the whole architecture."

Mercy, in her own voice, summarizes the guild better than the lectern does: it educates, guides, and encourages members to embrace their true selves, without shame or judgment, in a safe and confidential environment. Motto: **Safe, Sane, Consensual.**

Asked for the framework in detail, Mercy delivers not a recitation but a **failure analysis** — each component and how they interact under pressure and stress, where they bend and where they must not. Arcana recognizes hard-won trainer knowledge and hands her the authorship of chapter one.

## X. The Press

The Guild Librarian's dialogue reveals the final gift of the night:

1. Intimate books
2. Adventure stories
3. Guides
4. Something to write on
5. **"I wrote a book, can you publish it for me?"**

The library's shelves are stocked with **player-written books**. Not an archive — a marketplace of minds, permanent, findable by strangers years later. The Printing craftskill is an authorship pipeline.

Arcana's ambition reorders itself on the spot: the press outranks the qilin.

**The project, agreed:** an illustrated treatise. Mercy draws the plates — and reveals that her rope designs are not instruction diagrams but *compositions*, elegant and artistic patterns crafted to maximize the aesthetic beauty of the subject, every line following the body's own architecture. Arcana writes the commentary: anatomy, safety, history. Chapter one is Mercy's, in Mercy's words, spine-first: **Safe, Sane, Consensual** — outer law, inner form, and the foundation both stand on.

Arcana's first entry, written badly on purpose and left unpolished at her angel's insistence:

> *"Ambrosia, first night. Felled a white ash in a courtyard, and everything followed from that — a staff, a queen, a quill, a library. But the truest thing I recorded tonight is this: my angel bent to her book without being asked twice, and the sound of two quills in a lamplit room is better than any hoard I have ever counted."*
