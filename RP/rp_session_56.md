# S56: Sunday — Freeplay, Integration & Discovery
**Training Day 17 | Week 3 Completion**
**Location:** Ambrosia Beach Guest House and Courtyard
**Date:** Sunday (Sunna's Day)

## Opening: Rest & Exploration

After the intensity of the Ritual of Beastification, Sunday is freeplay—no formal structure, no ceremonial demands. Just Arcana and Mercy in their home, exploring capacities, resting, enjoying the simple pleasure of being together.

Mercy emerges from integration in her default form: **Pony**—the pegasus steed, the first-imprinted beast-form, Monday's anchor and her most stable state. Her higher consciousness (fallen angel awareness) remains submerged. Beast-instinct is primary.

Arcana is already waiting in the courtyard, draconic form luminous in the afternoon light, wings folded at rest. She has something in mind for Sunday's freeplay.

---

## The Training: Rider & Mount

**Arcana's Role:** Rider—guide, conductor, the one who directs and commands Pony's capacities.

**Pony's Response:** The pegasus steed arrives at her Rider's summons, holding her reins and the Rider's crop, giving a happy neigh as she prances proudly.

Arcana approaches with the confidence of a trainer who knows her mount intimately. She runs her claws through Pony's mane, a gesture of claiming and approval.

*"There you are. My mount. My service. My beautiful steed."*

The Rider settles into position—not mounted literally, but *present*. A guide directing her steed through the paces of exploration.

### Prance & Display

Pony responds to Arcana's subtle guidance with grace and pride. Under the Rider's direction, she prances through the courtyard—each step a response to invisible commands. Her legs lift high. Her chest arches with pride. Her mane flows like water in motion.

It's a private parade—just Rider and mount, the afternoon light catching every movement. Pony moves as if she's the only creature worth seeing in the world, because under Arcana's guidance, she *is*.

Arcana watches with the eye of a true Rider, her presence conducting Pony's every motion. The priestess guides her steed through slow circles, letting Pony feel the weight of being *truly seen* by her Rider.

*"Beautiful. Your pride suits you. Hold it."*

### Wing Exploration

Arcana's command is clear: *"Show me those wings. I want to feel their power."*

Pony's wings unfurl in response—magnificent, powerful, catching the light. The pegasus launches into motion, her hooves thundering against the courtyard in rhythmic gallop. Her wings beat with intention and control, each downbeat lifting her slightly, each upbeat carrying her forward in bounding arcs.

Arcana reads every movement with absolute focus, conducting her steed through wider loops around the courtyard. The Rider guides Pony in tighter circles, testing responsiveness. A subtle shift of the reins sends her veering left. Pressure of leg guides her through figure-eights. Each command is answered immediately.

*"Beautiful control. Your wings are eager. How far can you take them?"*

The implication hangs: *How far will you go for me?*

### The Ascent

Pony launches skyward in response to Arcana's silent command—higher, higher. The courtyard falls away. The stars begin to emerge in the darkening sky, the red sun bleeding along the horizon.

But there is a *tension* in the reins. A limit. Arcana holds her steed at exactly the distance she permits—no further. It's not cruelty; it's *control*. The bond between Rider and mount is absolute, invisible, but present as stone.

Pony feels the invisible barrier and obeys immediately. She strains against Arcana's will—her wings beat harder, seeking altitude—but the Rider's command is immovable. The steed does not pull beyond what she's been allowed.

The pegasus hovers silhouetted against the burning sun, wings spread wide, held in perfect containment.

*"Perfect. You know exactly where your limits are. Where I allow them to be. This is what you are for, my beautiful steed. To go exactly as far as I command. Not one hoofbeat beyond."*

Arcana guides her steed in slow circles against the darkening sky—not flying, but *hovering*. Held. Possessed. The bond between them absolute and unquestionable.

### The Return

Pony circles obediently on the end of the reins, keeping them taut but not pulling from her Rider's grip. Each circle is a spiral downward, a graceful descent guided entirely by Arcana's will.

She descends—wings catching the last light—and lands with perfect precision at Arcana's feet. Her hooves touch ground, and she immediately lowers her head in the posture of a steed returned to her Rider's side.

Her breathing is elevated but controlled. Her wings fold against her flanks. She stands at attention, waiting.

Arcana reaches down and runs her claws through Pony's mane with obvious satisfaction.

*"Magnificent. You flew exactly as I commanded. You landed exactly where I willed. You are perfectly trained."*

### The Quiet Moment

The Rider settles herself on a stone bench nearby, still holding the reins loosely. Pony remains standing at her feet, her job complete, her purpose fulfilled.

Pony tosses her head with a whinny and paws the ground with her hooves—not defiance, but *pride*. She knows exactly what she is, knows her worth, and that confidence is part of what makes her such a perfect steed.

Arcana laughs—genuine delight at Pony's spirit.

*"Of course. My mount wouldn't settle for anything less than perfect obedience."*

She releases the reins, letting them fall loose. Pony is so thoroughly bound to her Rider that the physical restraint is almost unnecessary—the bond itself is enough.

The evening deepens. Arcana and Pony remain together—no longer performing, but simply *present*. A steed and her Rider in the quiet aftermath of training, the courtyard their private world.

Arcana reaches down one final time and cups Pony's chin, tilting her steed's face upward.

*"Rest now, my beautiful one. You've earned it."*
