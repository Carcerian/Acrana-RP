## Session Summary

The morning after the Angel House tour, Mercy takes Arcana on a second, wider tour — this time of the city itself, parts of it Mercy hasn't yet shown her. What begins as a leisurely flight becomes a night of real momentum: new sights, a genuine level-up, a first taste of the forge/crafting system in practice, and — held out as the evening's destination since the opening line — a promised introduction to **Queen Cinder**, with Mercy's teasing unfinished sentence ("introducing you to Queen Cinder as her new—") still hanging unresolved as of this session's end.

The session closes on approach to **Castle Amber**, rising over the Old Quarter — the meeting with the Queen deferred to the next sitting.

## The Departure

Following directly from Session 9's closing scene (the love-bite renewal in the hanging gardens), Mercy rises on her own wings, leash in hand, and **flies** Arcana out rather than walking — the first time their leash has been used as a literal airborne tether rather than a ground-bound one. Arcana matches her wingbeat for wingbeat, delighted by the change of pace.

**Arcana:** *"I suppose that answers whether we're walking to the rest of the tour!"*

## The Airfields

First stop: a proper sky-harbor Arcana didn't know existed — tethered giant dragonflies grazing on marked lots like livestock, a half-built airship still on its scaffold, a wagon-hut trailing balloons, mechanical **Ornithipter** craft (brass-jointed, rotor-winged constructs distinct from the living dragonflies) idling nearby.

Introduced to two of the tame dragonflies by name: **Arakkis** (desert-colored, orange-winged) and **Lexx** (blue-winged, named — to Arcana's open delight — after the planet-devouring bio-ship). Arcana's reaction leans hard into the joke while still landing the deeper beat:

**Arcana:** *"You built a bio-titan-class doomsday superweapon a pet name and a parking spot, treasure. That's exactly the kind of thing you do — take the biggest, strangest, most world-ending thing the multiverse has to offer, and give it a name a friend would answer to."*

A silver-scaled, green-eyed creature is also glimpsed watching from the fence line, alongside a staff-bearing figure in violet and blue — unidentified, left as an open thread for a future visit.

**Continuity note:** Carcerian re-specced Arcana's tail mid-scene — from a thicker lizard-style tail to a **slender half-dragon tail with a tail bracer.** Established as ongoing physical canon from this point forward; Arcana herself notices and comments on the new grace of it mid-flight.

## Aniums Airship Merchant

Inside a green-glass, greenhouse-hulled showroom: **Aniums**, dressed in severe black mourning-wear, minds a vast checkered showroom floor built for browsing airships. Arcana teases the funereal fashion sense ("half the appeal of buying a vessel from someone like that is wondering how many hearts she's broken selling this one out from under them") and inquires about stock and pricing, framing herself as a "dangerous customer" for appreciating fine hulls even though her hoard is friends, not gold.

No purchase made this session — noted as a thread to revisit.

## The Ambrosia Amphitheatre

A full tiered Colosseum-style arena — three stacked levels of arches, a small colonnaded shrine partway up the seating, sand floor. Arcana is floored by the scale of it (*"a whole city that also thought to build itself a proper arena"*), and then discovers the **summoning rack** at the wall: **Call Manticore, Call Chimera, Call Adamantine Colossus.**

**Arcana:** *"I toured your treasure room and thought I'd seen the whole shape of your generosity... and now you've brought me to a place where — for the asking — the city itself will conjure a chimera onto open sand for me to look at."*

Several spirited rounds fought here (details left to table-side resolution; referenced afterward as good, satisfying fights — "the manticore fought properly... whatever the chimera's second head was trying to do, I felt it in my scales"). **This combat is the direct source of Arcana's level-up to Bard 4**, carried into the next stop.

## The Level-Up — Bard 4

Reaching level 4 mid-tour, in the district transition into the Artisan's Quarter. Full summary:

- **+1 Charisma** (per the Fabulous Luck Dragon build doctrine — "mental stats lead, Charisma maxed")
- **+5 HP**
- **Skills:** Perform +1, Use Magic Device +1, Tumble +5
- **Feat gained: Bard Song** — her signature aura ability comes online for the first time
- **Spells added:** **Joyful Noise** and **Silence** (2 known spells at this level)

**Spell selection reasoning (in-fiction and OOC-discussed):** Joyful Noise grants immunity to silence effects for a large friendly area, scaling with Lasting Inspiration/Lingering Song as if it were a bard song — chosen as the direct counter to the exact vulnerability the Fabulous Luck Dragon build notes flag ("Silence can screw around with Bard Song/Curse Song"). Silence itself, cast on an enemy caster (Will negates) or an ally (no save needed), shuts down verbal-component spellcasting — a strong lockdown tool against caster-type threats, thematically framed as "control the sound of the battlefield: whose voice gets to be heard."

**Arcana, in character:** *"You didn't just buy me a book, my pet... this is a hoarder's dream, treasure."* — reacting first to the spell selection, and again moments later to the crafting tome (see below), treating both as pieces of the same gift: real, usable power, freely given.

**Milestone significance:** This is the level at which the build doctrine established across the Fabulous Luck Dragon notes and Session 1's character sheet — *"she wins fights by song, not sword"* — becomes mechanically real for the first time. Prior to Bard Song, she was a bard in name only.

## The Guild of Artificers

Deep in the Artisan's Quarter (fog, idle crimson war-golem constructs standing sentinel, a stone golem hauling crates) — Mercy walks Arcana through the various craft disciplines and buys her **the crafting tome** (OOC: the Armor/Accessory/Weapon Power Point reference document reviewed earlier this same evening's conversation, now brought into IC canon as a physical gift).

**Arcana:** *"This isn't a primer, treasure. This is the whole grammar of it... You've handed a dragon the recipe for turning monster parts into more hoard."*

Given open access to the guild's blueprint racks ("anything you want, the blueprints or designs are here, to make yourself"), Arcana selects a project: **an instrument frame built on the bard/wizard-staff chassis** (10 Power Points), intending to enchant it with **bonus Bard spell slots** — directly building toward the "Instrument of the Bard" endgame concept discussed earlier in the evening's out-of-character conversation. Declares it will be gifted to Mercy once complete, same as everything else built this session.

**Materials gap identified:** the enchantments she wants (Fairy Dust for bonus-spell-slot lines, Soul Gems for saves/SR) aren't in the guild's on-hand stock — establishing the next major thread.

**Mercy confirms: most components in the crafting tome can be found somewhere on Aeon Isle** — the full-genre overland continent from the Gazetteer (Nim, Stonehold, Hightree, Winterking Village, Champion City, Crater Town, Tower of Wor). Arcana reframes the gathering trip as a full **expedition** rather than a single errand, delighted at the idea of an instrument built from pieces gathered across the whole continent rather than bought whole.

**Established as an open future thread:** an Aeon Isle component-gathering expedition, mapped loosely to the tome's ingredient list and whichever CR-appropriate hunting grounds the isle's overland encounter tables point to. Not yet begun — the tour continued toward Castle Amber instead, expedition deferred.

A brief tender interlude here (Mercy kissing Arcana's scales, teasing that her dragon "adores books almost as much as her angel-pet") — Arcana affectionately concedes the books can wait; the angel cannot.

## The Flight to Castle Amber

The tour's final and grandest leg, flown rather than walked: over the **orchards** near the beach (luxury homes among fruit trees), past the **Roman Villas** (columned porticoes, courtyard fountains) and the **Noble Quarter** (banners, old wealth), to **Castle Amber**, rising over the **Old Quarter** — clearly a seat of real power rather than merely a grand estate.

**Arcana, on sighting the castle:** *"That's not a villa. That's a throne."*

Understanding lands fully here — this has been the destination since Mercy's very first line of the session ("introducing you to Queen Cinder as her new—"), and Arcana names it aloud:

**Arcana:** *"I've toured your home, your household, your city, your trade. Take me to your Queen last of all — I think I understand, now, why you saved her for the very end."*
