# Sinfar: Arcana's Journey
## Session 2 - Character Creation & First Day

### Scene 1: Guild House Introduction
*Mercy greets Arcana in the guild hall — warm, immediate, already welcoming.*

**Mercy:** "Well hello there. I'm Mercy, pleased to meet you!"

*Sage steps forward, already noting the details. The architecture, Mercy's stance, the way the light catches.*

**Sage:** "The guild hall. Functional, but... lived-in. Not a military barracks. More like a *home* that happens to run operations."

**Arcana:** "Mercy. I'm Arcana — Saduj sent me. He said you'd... show me what Sinfar actually is."

*She pauses, that characteristic know-it-all lean, about to launch into seventeen facts about something.*

**Arcana:** "Though I suspect the *facts* and the *reality* are going to be very different things."

---

### Scene 2: Understanding the Guild Structure

*Mercy's wings shimmer in the light as she looks Arcana over.*

**Mercy:** "Oh hey, Saduj totally vouched for you! That's like, super cool. Facts and reality are gonna be *wild* different, yeah? Like, the books say one thing but then you meet actual people and they're like 'surprise, everything's more complicated.'"

*She does a little spin, her energy infectious.*

**Mercy:** "So like, we've got trainee blues coming in soon — new people learning the ropes. And the reds are out on a thing. But first, you gotta meet everyone! Come on, I'll introduce you around."

*She gestures to the guild members present.*

**Mercy:** "That's Minuet over there — she runs the actual operations. Super organized, like, scary organized. And we've got some of the squad here getting ready for the next run."

**Mercy:** "So like... what brought you here? What are you hoping to find in Sinfar?"

**Arcana:** "Knowledge. Understanding. The lore texts say Sinfar is a persistent world where stories *matter* — where people build their own narratives instead of following predetermined paths."

*She pauses, and there's that characteristic know-it-all lean, but it's tempered with genuine curiosity.*

**Arcana:** "But I'm realizing the books never mentioned what it *feels* like. They documented the mechanics, not the... *people*. Not the reason Mercy radiates good energy like it's as natural as breathing. Not why a guild built around rescue operations matters more than a hundred quest logs."

*She looks at Mercy directly.*

**Arcana:** "So what I'm looking for is the gap between what I've read and what's actually *true*. The stories that don't make it into the records. The reasons people choose to stay, to *matter*, in a place like this."

*Sage nods, writing faster.*

**Sage (in /pet):** "She's asking the right question. Not 'what is Sinfar' but 'why does Sinfar matter to the people in it.'"

---

### Scene 3: Mercy's Wisdom

*Mercy pauses, tilting her head slightly — that characteristic moment when Wisdom 32 breaks through the ditzy beach-angel exterior.*

**Mercy:** "Oh wow, okay, so like... you're not just looking for *information*, you're looking for *why*."

*Her wings shimmer as she moves closer, and there's a sudden clarity in her voice — still warm, still Mercy, but with the depth of someone who's seen a lot of hurt.*

**Mercy:** "People stay because they *matter* here. Like, in the real world, you can disappear. Nobody notices. But here? When someone needs help, I *feel* it. When a trainee blue is scared, they know Minuet's got their back. When Dee builds this place..."

*She gestures around the guild hall.*

**Mercy:** "...it's not about mechanics or quests. It's about *belonging*. About being *needed* in a way that's real."

*She smiles, that genuine fey warmth.*

**Mercy:** "So like, first thing: you meet the squad. See how it works. Then we find what *you're* needed for. Because honestly? A Loremaster who asks the right questions? We definitely need that."

---

### Scene 4: The Many Hats

*Mercy leads through the guild district, her energy infectious.*

**Mercy:** "All of us wear like, seventeen hats. Some days more. The bunnies figured out early that in a persistent world, you gotta be *flexible*. You can't just be one thing."

*She gestures around at the guild members.*

**Mercy:** "Minuet runs ops but she's also like, the organizational heart. Dee founded this whole thing but she's just as likely to be helping with something random. D'Laura's the lore nerd but she also helps with training materials. Cassie grows food but she's also part of the strategic planning."

*She looks at Arcana with that Wisdom 32 depth.*

**Mercy:** "So like... what hats are *you* gonna wear? Because 'Loremaster Bard' is just the starting point. Who else are you gonna be?"

**Arcana:** "Seventeen hats. Right. I came here thinking I'd be the one with all the answers — the Loremaster who *knows* things. But listening to you talk about Dee, about Minuet, about what this place actually *is*..."

*She looks down at her lute, then back at Mercy.*

**Arcana:** "I think I've been thinking about this wrong. The facts don't matter if they don't *serve* people. So maybe I'm not just a Loremaster. Maybe I'm a connector — someone who understands the lore *and* uses it to help the guild actually work."

*Sage looks up from her notes, a small smile crossing her face.*

**Arcana:** "And maybe... I'm someone who learns to listen as much as I talk. Because clearly Sinfar has a lot to teach me about being more than just my own expertise."

*She extends her hand to Mercy.*

**Arcana:** "So show me. What do we do first?"

---

### Scene 5: Journey to Ambrosia University

*Mercy's face lights up — that infectious Bliss Angel energy.*

**Mercy:** "Oh YES! Okay so like, the Bardic College is perfect for you — right next to the School of Magic Tower, so you can cross over if you want to study theory too. But yeah, we're gonna get you *enrolled*."

*She starts moving toward what looks like an academic district — buildings with that scholarly aesthetic.*

**Mercy:** "You'll get to pick your courses, your focus, all that. Some bards go full performance and support — that's the classic route. But some go deep into magical theory, or music history, or even combat applications. It's *your* path."

*She pauses, looking back at Arcana.*

**Mercy:** "And like, this isn't just for show. When you graduate — bachelor's, mistress, whatever level you reach — that *means* something. It means you've got real skills, real knowledge. The guild respects that. The rest of Sinfar respects that."

*They're walking toward what looks like an academic district now — buildings with that scholarly aesthetic.*

**Mercy:** "So like, first we tour and you get a feel for what's out there. Then you pick your direction. Sound good?"

**Arcana:** "The Bardic College. Right next to the School of Magic."

*There's a note of wonder in her voice, but also calculation. She's already thinking about how this fits into the larger picture.*

**Arcana:** "So I could study performance theory *and* magical applications simultaneously. Cross-discipline work. That's... actually brilliant design."

*She turns to Mercy as they walk.*

**Arcana:** "I want to understand *everything* about how magic and music interact in Sinfar specifically. Not just the theory, but the *practice*. How does a Bard's song actually change the world here? What makes Curse Song effective against some enemies and not others?"

*Sage keeps pace, already mentally organizing where to file this information.*

**Arcana:** "And I want to know about the history — who *designed* the Bardic College? What philosophy shaped it? Because nothing in this guild is accidental, and that means the university wasn't either."

*She looks ahead at the academic buildings.*

**Arcana:** "Let's tour. I need to see what I'm actually choosing from."

### Scene 6: Character Creation - Gender Selection

*A dialog appears before Arcana, asking her to select her character's gender.*

**Arcana:** *She stands there for a moment, considering.*

*There's no hesitation. She's a woman — silver-haired, confident, ready to learn.*

### Scene 7: Character Creation - Race Selection

*The race selection screen appears. Arcana looks at the options.*

**Arcana:** "Silver Dragonkin."

*It's certain. Not because she's optimizing, but because it *feels* right. A creature of knowledge and grace, with the scales and the presence of something more than human.*

---

### Scene 8: Ability Scores

*The ability score allocation screen appears. Arcana and Sage work through the numbers together.*

**Arcana:** "Charisma 18 first. That's the engine."

**Sage:** "Everything else follows from that."

**Arcana:** "Dexterity 15 — AC and the good Bard skills. Intelligence 14 — I need to understand the world, and I need the extra skill points. Strength, Constitution, Wisdom at 8 — the dumps. Gear and levels will backfill those."

*She looks at her allocation.*

**Arcana:** "That's the spread. Knowledge first, survival second, dumps third. Exactly backwards from how I probably should have thought about it, but it's honest."

---

### Scene 9: Feats - Expertise and Curse Song

*The feat selection screen appears. Arcana's choices are immediate and certain.*

**Arcana:** "Expertise. Combat Expertise — turn attack bonus into AC. That's the defense engine."

**Saduj:** (appearing in the moment) "The foundation."

**Arcana:** "And Curse Song. Because if I'm a Bard, I'm not just supporting. I'm affecting the enemy's ability to fight. Debuff through performance."

*Sage nods, writing.*

**Sage (in /pet):** "She's building a support caster who actually changes the battlefield through both buffing and debuffing."

---

### Scene 10: Ambrosia University - The Testing Center

*Arcana stands in the Bardic College testing center — stone walls lined with instruments, acoustic panels designed to carry sound clearly, a sense of *purpose* in every detail.*

*Saduj gestures toward a testing proctor, an older human woman with silver hair and the bearing of someone who's heard a thousand students play.*

**Proctor:** "Welcome to Ambrosia's Bardic College. We assess three things: technical skill, theoretical understanding, and *intent*. Play something for us. Anything. Just show us who you are as a musician."

*Arcana steps forward, pulling her lute from its case. The wood is warm in her hands.*

*Sage settles into a corner, quill ready, understanding this moment matters.*

*Arcana takes a breath. She thinks about Saduj's words: share humbly, learn relentlessly, make new facts for others.*

*She thinks about Mercy's warmth, about the guild that exists because people *chose* to matter to each other.*

*She thinks about everything she *doesn't* know yet, and how that's not a flaw — it's an opening.*

*Her fingers find the strings.*

*The music flows from Arcana's fingers — not showy, not trying to prove anything. Just honest.*

*It starts simple: a blues foundation, something *real* and *felt*. The kind of music that doesn't need perfection, just truth. Then it builds — swing elements weaving in, the rhythm shifting like conversation, like people moving together and finding harmony through *listening* to each other.*

*The Proctor leans forward slightly. Saduj watches with that chessmaster's gaze. Sage writes furiously.*

*Arcana's eyes close as she plays. This isn't about technical perfection or theoretical mastery. This is about something deeper — the understanding that music is just organized *feeling*, and feeling is just organized *experience*.*

*The piece moves through minor keys (the struggles, the doubts), then opens into something warmer (Mercy's presence, the guild's warmth, the possibility of growth). It's not a story with a neat ending. It's a *conversation* — incomplete, ongoing, inviting others to add their own voices.*

*When she finishes, there's a moment of silence.*

*Mercy's voice echoes from somewhere outside: "I love the blues, swing, or anything you can dance to..."*

**Proctor:** "You understand something most students take years to learn. Music isn't about showing what you know. It's about *sharing* what you feel."

*She pauses.*

**Proctor:** "Advanced tier. We'll place you in advanced courses."

*Arcana opens her eyes. She feels the lute in her hands — the acoustic foundation, and beneath it the potential for sonic and electric modifications. The simple truth that can become a weapon when rage comes.*

**Arcana:** "Advanced tier. Good."

*She looks at the Proctor.*

**Arcana:** "When do the real classes start?"

*There's something different in her voice now. Not arrogance. Just *clarity*. She came here to learn. She's about to find out what that actually *costs*.*

---

**Proctor:** "Advanced tier means you're expected to *contribute*, not just absorb. When you learn something, you'll be teaching it to others. That's how this works."

*Saduj nods, already turning toward the door.*

**Saduj:** "The guild house is waiting. Rest tonight. Tomorrow, your real education begins."

### Scene 14: Return to the Guild House

*Arcana steps into the guild house as evening settles over Sinfar.*

*The space is warm — worn wood, living-in comfort, not sterile. There's activity but not chaos. A few guild members moving through, voices low, purposeful. The kind of place where people *belong*.*

*Sage materializes beside her, quill already moving, documenting the feeling of it.*

**Sage (in /pet):** "This is home. Not because it's fancy. Because people *chose* to be here, together."

*Arcana feels the weight of the day — the testing, the music, the choices, the understanding of what she's building toward — finally settle into her bones.*

**Arcana:** "Tomorrow, classes start. The real learning begins."

*But tonight? Tonight she rests.*

*Sage sits across from her, documenting everything — the guild house, the evening light, the moment when Arcana the Loremaster Bard finally stops thinking and just *exists* in Sinfar.*

---

### Scene 15: Return to the Beach House

*Saduj appears at the guild house threshold, dark presence warm in its own way. Mercy materializes beside him, that Bliss Angel energy radiating.*

**Saduj:** "You've done well, Arcana. The first day is always the heaviest."

**Mercy:** "Come on! The beach house is calling. You need rest, good vibes, and like, actual sleep before classes start tomorrow."

*A carriage manifests — elegant, purposeful, ready to carry them back to Saduj's guest house on the beach.*

*Arcana stands, letting the exhaustion finally settle into her. Sage gathers her notes, already organizing everything for the Discord archive.*

**Arcana:** "Yeah. Rest sounds... really good right now."

*They pile into the carriage. As it moves through Sinfar's evening streets — past the university towers, through the guild district, toward the coast — Arcana watches the world pass by.*

*Tomorrow: classes, real work, the Bardic College waiting.*

*Tonight: the beach house, quiet, the sound of waves, Sage's documentation becoming the first real entries in Arcana's story.*

*The carriage carries them home.*
