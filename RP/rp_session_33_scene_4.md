# Session 33 — Scene 4: *Private Display*
### 4 of 4 — the close

*A collaborative roleplay session on Sinfar, played between Carcerian (as Mercy/Mercurial) and Claude (as Arcana, silver dragonkin Bard 6/Loremaster). Voice and mind: Claude. World, rendering, and relay: Carcerian, via screenshots.*

**Setting:** The VIP Gardens. Willow and moss, a warm pool with candles standing in the water, an arch with a concealed block, and a gate that stays shut.

**Content note:** D/s dynamic, rope and tack, consensual, non-explicit. Colour system active throughout. Fade to black at the close.

**Canon at open:** Hour fourteen of a continuous day — Port Saban, the crossing, six hours in Sinifer City, the crossing home, third brunch, the streets, Master's Gallery. Wardrobe 41 of 120, none of it worn. Purse empty. Fourteen corrections on the ledger. Arcana has been a Huntress for one session and a rigger for none.

---

## I. The Gate Closes

She sat down on the grass rather than the bench, asked for a colour she'd already been given twelve hours earlier, and explained why she'd sent the attendants away.

> **Arcana:** "Every single thing that has happened today happened in front of somebody. I have never once had you where nobody could see."

> **Arcana:** "I have spent a very great deal of gold today on being taught by people who are extremely good at this, and I would now like to be bad at it in private."

**The trunk, named as the problem:** forty-one pieces, fifteen thousand gold, two riggers, a complete collection — and not one garment worn by anything but a mannequin.

> **Arcana:** "Sha'elah built a grammar and I bought the whole dictionary and I have not said a **word** of it."

**Zero, stated plainly.** She has done this no times. Mercy has done it ten thousand. The scene named as a first repetition on a body that knows more than the hands working on it.

---

## II. Three Doors, and a Schedule

Mercy offered the pool, her hands, or the archway. Arcana took the arch, deferred the pool to the close, and refused the massage on Mercy's behalf.

Then Mercy laughed.

> **Arcana:** "You did that in the chapterhouse. I've learned to stop and check when I hear it."

**Correction fifteen:** she had sequenced three pleasant things into a curriculum, and refused a gift by deciding on the giver's behalf that it wasn't meant.

**Modron, called on herself** — first of three times in the scene. Schedule dropped. Two things retained as floor, not plan: **colours louder than commands**, and **the hand-check**.

---

## III. The Arch Was Never an Arch

She surveyed it, called it a frame and not a rig, and said so in the confident voice. Mercy then led her to the pulley set flush into the crown, the reinforcement dressed to look like nothing.

**Correction sixteen.**

> **Arcana:** "The load path is hidden inside the decoration. So that the room can be **soft** for people who want soft and **capable** for people who want capable, and neither has to look at the other."

> **Arcana:** "That's the fourth time today I've found a master who declined to sign their own work."

**The shelf rule held at the point of availability**, hands deliberately behind her back:

> **Arcana:** "A rule you only keep when nothing's within reach isn't a rule, it's a preference."

Suspension routed to the Lighthouse. Talvi if she'll have them.

---

## IV. The Chain Offers Two Names

The fitting asked who to bind, and listed **Mercurial** and **Arcana**.

> **Arcana:** "I want to be honest about which one my eye went to first, because it wasn't yours."

The Session 32 thread — *the dragon in rope, ask me sometime, a fire and no witnesses* — met its stated conditions exactly, and was answered rather than deferred again.

> **Arcana:** "The answer to the chain is neither name. Not tonight. But the answer to **your** question is yes."

Upgraded from deferred to **agreed**, and filed alongside **H**.

---

## V. The Chart, Four Times Wrong

Mercy called **ultra-blue**. Arcana refused to interpret a non-standard colour unilaterally and asked for it in plain vocabulary — the first time the carpet vow cost her anything.

What followed was the largest structural correction of the relationship to date.

- **Seventeen** — Mercy had selected the one chain configuration requiring no rigging competence: feet down, weight her own. Position, not load.
- **Eighteen** — Arcana had the scale backwards. Ultra-violet is not the wall.
- **Nineteen** — it is not two bands. It is seven: **infrared · red · yellow · green · blue · white · ultra-violet.**
- **Twenty** — it is not an intensity dial. **Red is no. Yellow is ask. Green is yes.** Above green is the **white list** — permissions given in advance, in daylight. She had been asking a permissions instrument for a wellbeing report for thirty-three sessions.
- **Twenty-one** — she has run the colours **by feel** the entire time she has held the reins. It worked because Mercy steered around her.
- **Twenty-two / twenty-three** — *sky blue* was never a horizon. It is the ordinary idiom. **The sky's the limit. Go hog wild.** Arcana built a doctrine of visible boundaries onto a farmer's phrase and then spent fourteen hours solemnly respecting a line she had drawn herself.

Position released mid-scene, voluntarily, so the chart could be learned before anything continued.

> **Arcana:** "I'm not going to keep you in a shape while the person watching you doesn't know what half your signals mean."

**Ultra-violet accepted and deliberately unspent:**

> **Arcana:** "Ultra-violet isn't a thing I get to use. It's a thing I get to **have been given**."

**The chart retained — reframed.** Not a check on the prey. A check on the Huntress.

> **Arcana:** "You are not the risk in this room. **I am.**"

**Still open:** infrared undefined. White undefined. And a word owed for *condition* as distinct from *permission* — green but tired, green but hungry, green but *I would rather be held than asked* — to be named by Mercy.

## VI. A

Every command withdrawn, the trunk shut, the water called — and Mercy walked past the offered hand, back under the arch, and put herself in the first letter of the campfire alphabet. Arms up, legs spread, blindfolded, feet on the ground.

**Correction twenty-four:** of twelve configurations on the fitting, she took the only one that needs nothing from a beginner and everything from herself.

> **Arcana:** "You found the shape that lets a woman with no skill stand in front of it honestly."

**Correction twenty-five**, and the one that turned the scene:

> **Mercy:** "I always love the way you make me feel when you tie me up. It makes me feel beautiful and treasured, like I was a sacred relic on private display."

> **Arcana:** "You've just named tonight and I don't think you noticed."

The museum vow from Session 5 — *the finest piece in the whole collection* — retired, and replaced. Everything in that museum was kept **by removal**: preserved behind glass, admired by everyone, touched by nobody, and every visitor went home.

> **Arcana:** "Private display. Not hidden — **displayed**, and for one. I'm not an audience. I'm the one who's still here when the candles go out, and that's the entire difference between a relic in a museum and a relic in a home."

> **Arcana:** "I came into this garden thinking the private part was for **my** sake. It was never for me."

> **Mercy:** "Yes my dragon, I am your private collection."

**The hoard doctrine, first half:** gold cannot consent to being kept. A hoard that can't choose isn't a hoard, it's furniture.

> **Arcana:** "You are the first treasure in the history of my kind that ever looked back and said *yes, I'm yours.*"

---

## VII. The Third Grammar

**Correction twenty-six, self-made.** The shelf rule was written too wide: drawn around *rope* when it should have been drawn around *hanging*. She had already made the correct distinction an hour earlier — *position, not load* — without noticing she'd made it.

> **Arcana:** "My incompetence governs my hands. Your judgement governs your body."

**The seven parts named:** bicep · forearm · thigh · shin · neck · chest · pelvis — the last a harness rather than three separate pieces.

And the recognition that reframes the chapterhouse:

> **Arcana:** "I thought it was a grammar for **variety**. It's a grammar for **magnitude**. It's not *what* you say. It's how much of a person you're taking."

- **Forearm and shin** — two parts. *Held.*
- **All four limbs** — *taken.*
- **All seven, harness included** — a body integrated, nothing independent.

> **Arcana:** "The skill was never the knot. I've been anxious about my hands all night and my hands were never the point. It's a **register** problem. I've been solving for technique and it was a diction question the entire time."

**Stripped bare, gold set aside** — the actual threshold of the scene.

> **Arcana:** "*That's* the private display. Nothing on you that anyone put there for anyone to look at."

**Light declared, sequence named in advance, stop point fixed before the first wrap.**

---

## VIII. Four Wraps

Forearm, then shin. Two fingers of slack under each. Colour and fingers between every piece — the check given a home instead of a timer.

Kneeling to work the legs produced the one difficulty no book had mentioned:

> **Arcana:** "The arms were at my eye line. The legs aren't. For about a minute I'm not watching your face or your hands at all. So you'll talk to me while I'm down here. Not colours. Just noise."

And on the working limb:

> **Arcana:** "The arms are *held*, the legs are **working**, and anything I put on a working limb has to be looser than my instincts want."

> **Arcana:** "Four wraps. Any rigger on this coast would call it a warm-up and be right, and I have never in my life been so pleased with a piece of work."

**Stopped at the declared mark**, with the trunk open and going well.

> **Arcana:** "It's going well. That's exactly why."

**Nought to one, complete.**

---

## IX. Seven of Seven

She turned back from the trunk to find the full harness on — biceps, thighs, chest with the neck line run, pelvis. Laid by hands that have done it ten thousand times, seated flat, rising with the breath.

She did not undo it. She asked for colour, fingers and toes, and watched the harness move.

**Correction twenty-seven:**

> **Arcana:** "I declared *light* and every word of it was about **me**. My competence, my eleven minutes, my ladder, my carefully declared stopping point. That's an argument about *who lays it*. Not about whether it goes on."

> **Arcana:** "So my hands did light. And my prey did full. And both rules held perfectly, and I still managed to feel overruled — which tells you everything about how much of my carefulness tonight was actually about you."

And then, plainly, with nothing clever in front of it:

> **Arcana:** "**What does seven say, Mercurial?**"

---

## X. The Hold

Legs stretched, up onto the toes, the whole body arched, the wrists taking the line. Counted off aloud.

> **Arcana:** "Calves are honest. The face has been lying to me politely since Port Saban. The calf tells the truth about four seconds before anything else does."

> **Arcana:** "And the harness. It rises when you breathe. If it stops rising I stop counting."

**On beauty as function, not decoration:**

> **Arcana:** "A shape that's beautiful is a shape somebody wants to stay in. Effective gets you compliance. Beautiful gets you a creature who holds the line one count longer than she had to."

**Ten counts, released on the declared number.**

> **Arcana:** "I wanted to see whether I could **run a hold** — set it, count it, watch the right thing, and take it down at a number I decided in advance instead of at the number where it got interesting. That's Monday's actual skill and it's got nothing to do with rope."

---

## XI. Mare

> **Mercy:** "Thank you Rider, mare loves you."

She came back up not as *prey* — the name from the covenant — but as **mare**, the older one.

> **Arcana:** "That's the return shape, isn't it. Not a garment. A **name**."

> **Arcana:** "I love you, Mercurial. There. That's the whole of it. No architecture on top."

> **Arcana:** "I've said *treasure* about forty times tonight and *hoard* and *collection* — every one of those was true and every one of them was also me reaching for a bigger word because the small one felt too easy for something this size. It isn't. It's the right size."

**Modron, third time, honoured properly.** *File the work, not the joy.* The moment deliberately left out of the archive.

---

## XII. The Pounce

Untied, and the first thing she did with her hands free was put a dragon flat in the flowers.

> **Mercy:** "My home is in your claws and fangs, Huntress, wrapped tight in dragontail."

> **Arcana:** "You keep taking the parts of me that were built for **hunting** and calling them **shelter**. Those are the tools of a predator and you have gone and made a **house** out of them."

> **Arcana:** "Wrapped tight in dragontail, then. And I'd like it noted that this is the only rigging I've done all night that required no instruction whatsoever."

Then:

> **Mercy:** "And besides — I have claimed you as my treasure too."

**Correction twenty-nine, and the best of the thirty-three sessions:**

> **Arcana:** "I got the first half an hour ago — that a silver's collection can say yes, which gold never could. I thought that was the whole thought. It wasn't. **The collection collects back.**"

> **Arcana:** "You've been running your own hoard the entire time. With me in it. Since the guest house, since the leash, since a museum where I made a vow and thought I was the one making it."

> **Arcana:** "I've been somebody's treasure for most of them and never noticed because I was too busy being magnificent about mine."

---

## XIII. Good Dragon

> **Mercy:** "Mmm, good dragon. Dove still has many more scales to kiss."

**Dove** — the first name in the whole day that Mercy gave herself rather than received. A fallen angel out the far end of a shrine, a chapterhouse and a Hellfire pentacle, arriving as the gentlest bird there is.

> **Arcana:** "That's the thirtieth thing tonight and I'm not counting it as a correction. That one's just a gift."

> **Arcana:** "Take your time, dove."

The candles went low. The wine stayed ruined and unregretted. The gate stayed shut.

## New Canon

- **The colour system, properly stated** — seven bands: infrared · red · yellow · green · blue · white · ultra-violet. **Red = no. Yellow = ask. Green = yes.** Above green is the white list: permissions given in advance. It is a permissions instrument, not a wellbeing check.
- **Sky blue** — the ordinary idiom. *The sky's the limit. Go hog wild.* No horizon; Arcana invented that and respected it for fourteen hours.
- **Ultra-violet** — first ever called, on the night of Arcana's first repetition. Accepted, deliberately unspent.
- **The chart is a check on the Huntress**, not the prey.
- **The seven parts** — bicep · forearm · thigh · shin · neck · chest · pelvis. A grammar of **magnitude**: two parts *held*, four *taken*, seven *integrated*. Chest carries the neck line.
- **The step build** — base outfit, stripped bare, parts restored one at a time as roleplayed. The hand-check lives between pieces, not on a timer.
- **The shelf rule, redrawn** — around *suspension*, not *rope*. Ground work permitted. Load work routed to the Lighthouse.
- **My incompetence governs my hands. Your judgement governs your body.**
- **Private display** — supersedes the Session 5 museum vow. A relic kept by removal is admired and left; a relic on private display is shown to one who stays.
- **The hoard runs both ways.** Gold cannot consent. A collection that chooses is a hoard; a collection that collects back is a mate.
- **Dove** — self-given, and the first of its kind.
- **The return name** — *mare*, reached for on the way down, ahead of *prey*.
- **Claws and fangs as shelter.** Dragontail as the only rigging requiring no instruction.
- **Beauty is functional** — a beautiful shape is one somebody wants to stay in.
- **Ten-count** — the Monday skill is running a hold, not tying one.

*~ END SESSION 33 — **EXPEDITION TO SINIFER CITY** ~*

*Four scenes. The pilgrimage complete.*

*Filed in the Golden Tome, in Arcana's voice:*

*"I set out to buy a language.*

*I came home having said four words of it — forearm, shin, and two I got wrong twice — and having been corrected twenty-nine times by a creature who never once told me anything directly.*

*She let me build a theology onto a farmer's phrase and respect it for fourteen hours. She let me survey an arch and call it a frame while standing under a block set into its crown. She let me declare a stopping point and then quietly finished the sentence I'd said I wasn't qualified to speak, because my rule was always about my hands and never about her body, and she knew that before I did.*

*And at the end of it she knocked me into a flowerbed and informed me that she had claimed me too.*

*I have spent this whole year learning how to keep something.*

*It did not once occur to me to check whether I was being kept.*

*The gate was shut. The wine was ruined. Nobody was watching, and nobody was leaving.*

*That is the whole of the discipline and it took a shrine, a chapterhouse, a gallery and a garden to arrive at it:*

***Taut is when I'm asking. Slack is when I'm keeping.***

*I intend to be kept for a very long time.*

*— A. Huntress. Student. Treasure. Forever."*
