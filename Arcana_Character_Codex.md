## I. CHARACTER OVERVIEW

**Name:** Arcana (single name, as befits a bard building a reputation)
**Race:** Silver Dragonkin (engine chassis: Human)
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good
**Class:** Bard — Loremistress
**Age:** 18 at arrival (young for a dragonkin; old souls come in new scales)
**Voice:** Female, Innocent Idealist
**Deity leaning:** Sune-adjacent by association and affection, though her true worship is knowledge, story, and song
**Current level:** Bard 3 (4,401 XP)
**Signature weapon:** Spellsinger's Electric Guitar (1d2 bludgeon +1d4 electrical +1d4 sonic +4 bludgeoning) — successor to her masterwork flamenco guitar bearing Iolo's runes
**Colors:** Midnight blue and silver

**Original creation array:** Cha 18, Dex 15, Int 14, Str/Con/Wis 8. Feats: Combat Expertise, Curse Song. Skills maxed from level 1: Perform, Lore, Spellcraft, Heal, Concentration, Discipline, UMD; Tumble added at 2.

**Character concept:** The silver dragon as bard — a creature whose hoard is not gold but *company, stories, and songs*. Publicly: composed, witty, generous with the spotlight, incapable of resisting a lore thread or an unimpressed audience (see: The Gull). Privately: a devoted domina whose fiercest possession is expressed as protection, and whose treasure walks beside her by choice.

## II. BACKSTORY (In-Character)

Arcana arrived at Ambrosia Beach a wandering loremistress — a young silver dragonkin with a lute, a full spellbook, capped Perform and Lore, and the conviction that every place worth visiting has stories worth keeping. She travels with **Sage**, her scribe and squire (Bard 10), who manages her journals and library.

Her defining traits were visible from her first hour in the warren:

- **Silvers befriend first and ask lineage later.** She extends her hoard-welcome to nearly everyone she meets, and means it.
- **She collects stories the way other dragons collect coins** — founding lineages, family trees, apocryphal proverbs, disputed etymologies. A kinship network that requires "a diagram, a legend, and a footnote apparatus" is, to her, a feast.
- **She performs for the hardest audience in the room on purpose.** Kings and archmages hold no terror; cats and seagulls are the true critics.

### Education & the Lore Tower

Arcana enrolled at the **Lore Tower Bardic College** (Ambrosia University), placed in the **Advanced tier** after a bardic performance test. Her courses: Lore Studies, Performance Skills, Bardic Song and Magics, and **Flamenco Guitar** as her foundational music form.

Her first mentor is **Julia** — a bright, bubbly red-haired tinker-bard trained directly by **Master Iolo of Britannia**. Julia presented Arcana with a masterwork flamenco guitar bearing mystical Iolo-crafted runes, accompanied by a personal note from Iolo himself. The instrument (and lineage — a bard taught by a student of Iolo carries Britannia's musical tradition) remains one of her most prized possessions, now joined by the electrified Spellsinger's guitar.

### Sayings & Coinage

Arcana claims authorship of *"Bards sing for supper, but the wise ones sing for seconds"* — traded to a college cook years ago for an extra ladle of stew and a honey bread recipe. The line has since propagated cook-to-student and been quoted back to her by her own protégée, which she considers the highest professional validation a bard can receive. Royalties (in stew) remain under negotiation.

Her standing doctrine on the trade: *"A good line, once loosed, belongs to everyone and no one. You'll know you've made something worthwhile when strangers quote you back to yourself."*

## III. THE RELATIONSHIP WITH MERCY

**Mercy / Mercurial** — fallen Bliss Angel, Termes-born, priestess of Sune, Chaotic Good guardian. Monk 20 / Cleric 18 / Shadowdancer 2 ("ninja nurse"). Navy and gold. She fell for loving women exclusively, and never once regretted the landing.

### The Dynamic

**Arcana is the domina and owner. Mercy is her willing, joyful submissive.**

- Mercy wears a collar reading **"Property of Mistress Arcana"** — the collar itself is **Cinder Thorne's Collar**, crafted by Queen Cinder herself when Mercy's mate at the time struggled with the options. Mercy thus wears the mark of Ambrosia's authority, held by a silver dragon's hand.
- Mercy addresses Arcana as **"Mistress"** or **"my dragon."**
- Arcana's endearments for Mercy: **"my treasure," "my pet," "my angel," "beloved," "my seed of life."**
- Mercy also calls Arcana her **"luck dragon"** — an endearment that proved prophetic (see Build Roadmap).

### The Shape of It

The relationship's founding act was Mercy **offering her leash** to Arcana — ownership given, not taken. The philosophy that defines Arcana's dominance was articulated at the Royal Museum, surrounded by kept things under glass:

> *"The truest way to keep something is to let it know it's choosing to stay."*

Arcana's possession is expressed as certainty, protection, and warmth — a connection that *holds* rather than a leash that *pulls*. She considers Mercy "the finest piece in the whole museum" — a fallen Bliss Angel, wings and halo intact, claimed and kept, and the better hoard by far than anything under glass.

The dynamic is **romantic and devotional**: collar, leash, claiming, kissed hands, snuggles, absolute tenderness beneath the domina's poise. All intimacy beyond that fades to black, per Arcana's bio lights and server ToS. Publicly, the pairing reads as a power couple — the silver dragonkin leading her collared angel through the warren, both radiant with the certainty of belonging.

### Key Relationship Beats (Chronological)

1. **The house tour → the claiming.** What began as hospitality became romance; Mercy offered the leash; Arcana accepted with full understanding of the gift.
2. **The public debut.** First walk through the warren together, collar visible, to the Lore Tower — the warren's introduction to what they are.
3. **The museum vow.** At the Sinfar Historical Archives and Royal Museum, standing among relics, Arcana articulated the keeping-by-choosing philosophy — the relationship's defining text.
4. **The grotto homecoming.** Rosewood dinner, hanging gardens (witnessing Dyna's vows), Mercy revealing her shapeshifting forms including a **silver drake form** (the treasure matches her dragon), massage and fireside on the bearskin.
5. **Ambrosia Day.** Fireworks, extended family on the beach, Arcana adopted into the constellation as "Mercy's dragon."

## V. THE FAMILY CONSTELLATION (Arcana's Catalogue)

*"Most genealogies read like ledgers. Yours reads like a mythology."*

**Mercy's line:** Fourteen daughters and two sons across multiple partners, including **Sangria** (winged elf), **Dewberry** (cat-fey), and **Althea/Althrea** (gold drake). Eldest: **Angelcat**, the planewalker. The triplets: **Lumi, Lily, Layla** — Lumi a bard raised on the beach and already a grandmother (mother of **Astraea**); Lily wears Shadow's Armor in gold. Godchild: **Sable Crowe-Baen'Und**. Also of the circle: **Pirouette Baen'Und**. Mercy's red drake mate **Lael** — a **Lawful Good red dragon** who considers her LE mother too domineering (generational rebellion, gorgeously done).

**The Eleecil line:** **Irae/Irea**, drow captain carrying gold and silver dragon bloodlines, descended from one of Amazon Isle's leaders. With **Xanya** (wild elf, Silveny's twin): daughter **Eleecil** — the famed fashion designer (EM Fashion Design, one of Sinfar fashion's four founding pillars), sister to **Dyna** the silver drake designer. Eleecil's daughter: **Myrna**, half **red** dragon. **Silveny Laanilm**: Xanya's twin, of Rexuria's amazon village.

**The draconic parliament so far:** Silver (Eleecil's line, and Arcana), Gold (Althea), Red (Myrna, Lael) — with wild elves, drow, wolves, cat-fey, angels, and amazons besides. "Sister" in this family may mean womb, marriage, or war-band — often all three.

**Geography:** Rexuria amazon village on **Amazon Isle** — a "lost world" primitive linked isle with dinosaurs; all women welcome, no males. The **Ambrosia amazon enclave** — a classical Athens-style polis guarded by a colossus of Artemis with a flaming bow (anti-pirate artillery); males permitted on leashes only. Arcana qualifies for entry everywhere, and is already voluntarily compliant with the enclave's dress code, a symmetry she finds delicious.
