## NOTES FOR RP

**Tier Psychology:**
- Platinum = Partnership potential (equals)
- Gold/Silver = Serious practitioners (peers)
- Bronze = Community broadcasters (multipliers)

**Meeting Strategy:**
- Platinum VIPs get private 1-on-1 sessions
- Gold/Silver can be mixed 2-3 person conversations
- Bronze tier better suited to group/community events
- Each tier has different conversation depth

**What They Want:**
- Platinum: Intellectual partnership, collaboration
- Gold/Silver: Validation of methodology understanding, feedback loop
- Bronze: Personal recognition, permission to continue their work

**Red Flags (for RP boundary-setting):**
- Anyone seeking "personal access" vs. knowledge exchange
- Obsessive focus on priestess-prey physicality (vs. philosophy)
- Boundary-testing language or requests
- Seeking private photos/content not offered publicly

**Green Flags:**
- Questions about methodology and safety
- Offering skills/knowledge, not just consumption
- Building community, not isolation
- Clear consent and negotiation language
