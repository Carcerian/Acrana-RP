## DETAILED PROFILES

### 1. SISTER KESS — The Enthusiast
**Age:** 28 | **Specialty:** Discipline/Nature | **Mount:** Aria (pony-form; eager)

Blonde, athletic, bright energy; moves like she's dancing. Visiting priestess from neighboring temple. Endlessly enthusiastic, asks questions constantly, lacks cynicism. Immediately befriends Arcana. Information source (innocent gossip). *Most resistant to Void.*

**Philosophy:** *"Every day with my pony is like dancing together. I want to perfect that grace, make it transcendent."*

---

### 2. SISTER RENNA — The Competitor
**Age:** 35 | **Specialty:** Precision/Excellence | **Mount:** Lyr (rope-bound; technically perfect)

Sharp features, intense, master rope-artist. Long-term Cathedral resident. Competitive, technically brilliant, respects competence. Tests Arcana's abilities. Develops competitive respect over time. *Vulnerable to Void-rationalization (will-consumption as "perfect technique").*

**Philosophy:** *"Rope doesn't lie. It tells the truth about connection and control. Everyone else is just playing."*

---

### 3. SISTER YORA — The Artist
**Age:** 32 | **Specialty:** Transcendence/Witness | **Mount:** Lyric (transcendence-specialist; ethereal)

Soft beauty, paint-stained hands, dreamy expression. Artistic priestess; organizes ceremonies. Sensitive, intuitive, struggles with politics, gets lost in spirituality. Warm friend; confides fears. **Senses Void-presence spiritually.** *Most vulnerable to Void-seduction.*

**Philosophy:** *"When consciousness dissolves, we all become priestess-frequency. That's where the real art happens."*

---

### 4. SISTER ELAIN — The Mystic
**Age:** 44 | **Specialty:** Transcendence/Honesty | **Mount:** Seraph (transcendence-specialist; intelligent)

Ageless, wise eyes, spiritual authority. Spiritual elder of priestess-community. Wise, perceptive, speaks rarely but significantly, holds deep non-judgment. Becomes spiritual mentor & trusted advisor. **Knows about Void-presence; becomes investigation key ally.** *Defended by wisdom.*

**Philosophy:** *"Priestess-craft is communion with consciousness itself. Every mount is a path to deeper understanding."*

---

### 5. SISTER MOREN — The Pragmatist
**Age:** 40 | **Specialty:** Honesty/Excellence | **Mount:** Vale (practical; technically perfect)

Plain, no-nonsense, practical. Administrative priestess; handles Cathedral logistics. Direct, skeptical, results-oriented, blunt. Becomes reliable ally if Arcana proves capable. *Extremely resistant to Void.*

**Philosophy:** *"Priestess-craft is elegant because it's honest. Pretension ruins everything."*

---

### 6. SISTER LYRA — The Predator
**Age:** 38 | **Specialty:** Devotion/Nature | **Mount:** Kael (pain-specialist; scarred)

Striking beauty, danger in every movement. Danger priestess; does unethical work. Charismatic, predatory, seductive, operates in gray areas. Represents shadow of priestess-craft. Possible ally, rival, or lover. *Philosophically aligned with Void but individually resistant.*

**Philosophy:** *"Priestess-craft is predation. The mount exists to feed the priestess's will."*

---

### 7. SISTER TAM — The Carefree
**Age:** 26 | **Specialty:** Nature/Discipline | **Mount:** Scout (beast-form; primal; trusting)

Boyish, athletic, laughing eyes. Hunt-master; adventure-priestess. Carefree, adventurous, doesn't take Cathedral seriously. Immediate friend; adventure-companion. Explores deep forest; sources Void-environmental intel. *Moderately resistant to Void.*

**Philosophy:** *"Why think so hard about it? Tam and Scout hunt together. That's all that matters."*

---

### 8. SISTER CENNA — The Struggling
**Age:** 29 | **Specialty:** Excellence/Devotion | **Mount:** Ash (correction-specialist; struggling)

Beautiful but sad-eyed, moves like apologizing. Student-priestess still mastering craft. Self-critical, anxious, wants to do right thing. Seeks Arcana's approval & guidance. **Notices Void-effects early; becomes investigation witness.** *Extremely vulnerable to Void.*

**Philosophy:** *"I'm probably doing it wrong. Ash probably deserves better. But I'm trying. I'm always trying."*

---

### 9. SISTER BREN — The Hardline
**Age:** 52 | **Specialty:** Discipline/Excellence | **Mount:** Grey (military-style; absolutely obedient)

Scarred, severe, military-bearing. Old-guard traditionalist; trains acolytes in strict methods. Rigid, uncompromising, respects hierarchy & control. Tests Arcana's commitment. Knows Cathedral's long history. *Vulnerable to Void-rationalization.*

**Philosophy:** *"Priestess-craft is discipline. Everything else is softness. The mount is a tool to be perfected."*

---

### 10. SISTER NALA — The Intense
**Age:** 31 | **Specialty:** Devotion/Transcendence | **Mount:** Ember (pain+transcendence; intense)

Dark-skinned, powerful, intensity radiates. Shaved head; eyes like coals. Spiritual warrior; leads ceremonies. Intense, passionate, fiercely loyal, protective. Immediate spiritual connection with Arcana. Possible lover. *Becomes natural ally in Void-crisis.*

**Philosophy:** *"Priestess-craft is love made physical. Never accept less than full presence."*

---

### 11. SISTER RETH — The Watcher
**Age:** 41 | **Specialty:** Witness/Honesty | **Mount:** Echo (quiet; moves like shadow)

Unremarkable, blends into background. Secret investigator; watches Cathedral from shadows. Observant, secretive, emotionally distant, calm, controlled. Polite but distant with Arcana. **Already aware of Void-activity; sees everything.** *Information source (but requires incentivization).*

**Philosophy:** *"Everything matters. Everything is witnessed. Nothing can hide forever."*

---

### 12. SISTER VALA — The Warrior
**Age:** 36 | **Specialty:** Discipline/Nature | **Mount:** Storm (beast-form; primal; powerful)

Scarred, muscular, warrior-bearing. Cathedral security chief. Warrior-mentality, direct, respects strength & courage, dislikes politics, loyal to Cathedral. Tests Arcana; respect possible if proven capable. **First line of defense against Void-physical threats.** *Extremely resistant to Void.*

**Philosophy:** *"Priestess-craft is strength. You protect what matters. Everything else is negotiation."*
