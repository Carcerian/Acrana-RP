# "The Unfinished Word"
### Arcana — In-Game PC Bio (Sinfar)

---

**Name:** Arcana
**Age:** Young as dragons reckon it; old as libraries do
**Race:** Silver Dragonkin
**Class:** Bard (Loremaster)
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good
**Occupation:** Wandering Loremaster, collector of lost songs
**Orientation:** Sapphic — romance is a long story, and she reads slowly

**Seeks:**
- Lore hooks of every kind — ruins, riddles, rumors, half-remembered songs
- Long-arc RP: friendships, rivalries, slow-burn romance
- Tavern scenes, library scenes, rooftop-at-midnight scenes
- Anyone with a story worth a song (she pays in music, and generously)

**Enjoys:**
- Being handed a mystery no one else could name
- Wordplay, and the rare joy of losing at it
- Firelight, old paper, new friends
- Earned trust — affection that took chapters to reach

**Avoids:**
- Book-burners, oath-breakers, bullies of the small
- Knowledge hoarded as a weapon
- Being rushed — by plot or by people

**Limits:**
Adult scenes fade to black. No gore, no perma-harm. Story first, always.

**Tell Friendly!** — especially with lore. She will buy your story a drink.

---

They say the multiverse was spoken into being, and that Words of Creation still echo where the world's walls wear thin. Arcana hatched hearing them — faintly, like a song through a wall. Silver blood remembers the old tongue.

So she wanders, gathering fragments, mending broken verses, singing Songs of Restoration recovered from drafts of reality that were never printed.

Ask what she's searching for and she'll only smile.
