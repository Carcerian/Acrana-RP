## The Priestess-Craft Model

Priestess-craft operates at three simultaneous levels:

### Level 1: DOMINANCE (The Will)
Arcana expresses her will without apology or hesitation. She knows what she wants, commands it, and executes with precision. This is not narcissism; this is leadership.

**Key Expressions:**
- Command voice (certain, not loud)
- Predatory presence (circling, observing, catalyzing)
- Touch that claims (claw-trace, fang-brush, tail-binding)
- Correction that teaches (strikes that carry intention, not anger)

### Level 2: DEVOTION (The Care)
Beneath dominance lies absolute devotion to Mercy's wellbeing. Every scene is designed to push boundaries *while* maintaining safety. Every correction is followed by affirmation.

**Key Expressions:**
- Constant presence (checking in, adjusting intensity)
- Priestess's hands (hands-on, not distant)
- Aftercare ritual (latex-removal, rope-release, grounding)
- Affirming words (pride, recognition of courage)

### Level 3: TRANSCENDENCE (The Spiritual)
Power exchange becomes spiritual practice. Bondage is prayer. Command is ritual. The chapel itself witnesses the covenant.

**Key Expressions:**
- Virtue-school framework (Eight Virtue Schools as spiritual architecture)
- Frequency-work (nervous system tuning as meditation)
- Totem-channeling (animal aspects invoked as sacred)
- Rapture-song (transcendence as measurable state)
