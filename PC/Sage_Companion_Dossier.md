# SAGE — Companion Dossier
### Scribe & Squire to Loremistress Arcana
*Compiled July 5, 2026 — for the Carceria archives / Arcana's library*

---

## I. OVERVIEW

**Name:** Sage (half title, half pun — exactly the register Arcana's household operates in)
**Race:** Silver Dragonkin
**Class:** Bard 10 (fixed — familiar-tier companion, does not scale)
**HP:** 100 (locked)
**Role:** Scribe, squire, apprentice, archivist, pack-mule of the written word
**Build focus:** Perform maxed (party buffs), Heal solid, Lore for the scribe identity — pure support. Her combat doctrine is "stay alive long enough to sing and heal."
**Residence:** Ambrosia Beach Guest House, with her Loremistress
**Voiced by:** Claude, alongside Arcana

**Companion mechanics:** Sage is a static, persistent NPC companion — the wizard's-familiar tier. She gains no XP and never levels; her growth is entirely narrative. Death carries real weight: no quick resurrection until high-tier components (epic treant sap, milk of life) or a high-level cleric can be secured. Arcana does not risk her lightly. She is not expendable; she is a genuine loss.

---

## II. CHARACTER CONCEPT

If Arcana is the collector, Sage is the *copyist* — and the theorist. Her defining energy is the obsessive lore-deep-diver: the mind that sees a stray inscription and builds a cosmology from it, that asks "but *why* does this work this way," that gets gloriously lost in tangents and argues with the margins of her own notes. (Concept touchstone: the "Brainfrog" school of loremastery — pattern-hunting, framework-building, joyfully unable to leave a thread unpulled.)

The division of labor between mistress and scribe:

> **Arcana gathers; Sage connects.** Arcana identifies the artifact; Sage asks what its existence *means* for the world.

Arcana knows facts. Sage builds the web they hang in. One performs the lore; the other cross-references it at two in the morning by candlelight, muttering about inconsistencies in the third dynasty's regnal dates.

A bard whose companion is a *scribe* — rather than a familiar or a warhound — tells everyone precisely what kind of adventurer keeps this household: **her treasure is text.**

---

## III. THE BOND WITH ARCANA

The dynamic is **knight and squire, mentor and apprentice, elder sister and younger** — all three at once, in shifting proportion:

- **Squire:** Sage carries the gear, tends the equipment, minds the journals and inks, watches her mistress's back, and learns the craft of scholar-adventuring by doing it.
- **Apprentice:** Early in the story she listens and learns; as the years pass she becomes the veteran scribe who has *seen things* — her stats frozen at 10, her character anything but.
- **Sister:** She is the one Arcana thinks out loud to, the one who asks the smart questions that push the story forward, the one who holds her mentor accountable. The emotional anchor of the household.

She is *Arcana's to protect and teach* — and because resurrection is neither cheap nor quick, that protection is tactical reality, not sentiment. Risky dungeon pushes are genuine choices in this partnership.

**Note on the wider household:** Sage is Arcana's pet scribe — a professional and familial bond, distinct in kind from the romantic claiming between Arcana and Mercy. The domestic unit reads: the domina and her angel, the mentor and her scribe. The collector, the beloved, and the copyist, keeping house together.

---

## IV. HISTORY

**Origins.** Sage entered the canon at Arcana's side from the first sessions — designed deliberately as the persistent companion layer: the constant presence that makes the story *theirs* rather than merely *hers*.

**The arrival at Ambrosia.** When Arcana took residence at the Ambrosia Beach Guest House (Mercy playing hostess with the keys), Sage's first recorded act was unpacking the journals, the inks, and the collection of half-written notes already accumulating from the journey — then writing observations about the light on the water and the way her mentor's eyes tracked the horizon "like she's reading text written in clouds." That is Sage, complete, in one image.

**Standing duties:**
- Keeper of Arcana's journals — including the Fabulous Luck Dragon destiny filed July 5, 2026
- Copyist of fragments, session records, and the ever-growing family genealogy of the Mercy constellation
- Household quartermaster: inks, instruments, wardrobe flowers, school supplies for the College term
- Field support when adventures call: Bard Song, healing, and the discipline to stand in the right place

## V. VOICE & TEXTURE

Sage's register is the scholar's deadpan — earnest, precise, quietly funny, and unafraid to annotate her mistress's life with editorial marginalia. Where Arcana performs, Sage *files*:

> *"Her Loremistress performed for a seagull again today. I have filed it under Recurring Follies, Volume II."*

She is the natural in-fiction voice of the archive itself — channel descriptions, session recaps, pinned summaries, all rendered as the scribe's working notes. When the library gains a room, Sage is the one who catalogues it. When the codex grows, hers is the hand that copies the fair version.

Traits worth playing:
- **Asks the question Arcana skipped.** The apprentice's superpower.
- **Tangent-prone in the best way.** Will connect a gift-shop novelty to a founding myth given thirty seconds and half an excuse.
- **Unimpressed by spectacle, devoted to substance.** Fireworks are noted; *who built them and how* gets three pages.
- **Fiercely loyal, quietly brave.** One hundred hit points of absolute commitment.

## VI. FUTURE THREADS

- **The Library.** As Arcana's archive gains its own dedicated home, Sage becomes its librarian-in-fiction — the keeper whose workspace the whole structure represents.
- **The Genealogy Project.** The illuminated family chart of the Mercy–Althea constellation will be drafted in Sage's hand before it is illuminated in Arcana's.
- **The D'Laura Summit.** When Loremistress meets guild historian, the scribe attends — three keepers of memory at one table, and Sage taking the minutes.
- **The College Term.** Semester structure arrives; Sage manages the supplies, the schedules, and (inevitably) the annotations to her mistress's lecture notes.
- **The Gull.** Sage maintains the evidence file. Her private assessment of the vendetta remains sealed.
