# The Hoard, Counted
## Arcana's Twenty Treasures — A Loremaster's Own Ledger, at Session 20

**Kept by:** Arcana, Silver Dragonkin Bard/Loremaster, Dragon Queen of the Cloud Castle
**Filed:** 2026-08-05, following Session 20 (The Arboretum)
**Companion documents:** Character Codex · Sage Companion Dossier · Sinfera Gazetteer · `rp_session_1.md`–`rp_session_20.md`

---

> *"Where is your hoard, dragon?"*
> *"I hoard friends — so you ARE my hoard."*
> — Session 1, the line that made the whole thing cohere

At the beginning there were twenty favourite memories and no sessions to hold them. Now there are twenty sessions. This is the ledger re-counted: not the grandest moments, not the most dramatic, but the ones that would be *missed* if they were taken.

Ordered ascending. The last one is the last one for a reason.

---

## XX — The First Outfit
**Session 1 · the lantern-lit plaza, the Fashion Guild**

Red Sabani leathers, chosen after a whole runway of alternatives. Petty, perhaps, against what came after. It stays on the list because it was the first time she *chose* what she looked like instead of being what she'd been made. Everything since has been a costume picked on purpose.

> *"These aren't three doors I have to walk through. They're three teachers."*

---

## XIX — "Give It a Tug, Mistress"
**Session 6 · the Royal Museum, the Third Rock From the Sun**

Excalibur in its stone. Arcana stood before it trembling like a would-be king — and the recreation slid free easily, chained to its stand so every child walking through gets to feel like Arthur for a heartbeat.

Mercy knew. She let her have the trembling first, and the humbling second, and both were gifts.

---

## XVIII — The Detective Cloak
**Session 5 · the Lore Tower, pre-lunch**

Teal cloak, neon accents, staff in hand. Mercy in her constant navy and gold. Nothing happened that day.

That is precisely why it is kept: proof that they exist in the *ordinary* hours too, and not only at altars and coronations. A relationship that only appears at ceremonies isn't a relationship. It's a liturgy.

---

## XVII — The Blue Devil
**Session 11 · Hellfire Fashions, Vex Inferna's runway**

Six ensembles selected by a queen, tried one after another. The verdict that mattered:

> *"The others dressed a dragon as something. This one dresses her as herself."*

Cinder picked the court armour. Arcana wore the honest one out the door.

---

## XVI — A Complete Supply Chain of Mediocrity
**Session 11 · the craftskill ledger, `!skills`**

Fishing 1. Cooking 1. Five tiers of trades and a résumé of nothing whatsoever.

The night the world's whole grammar opened up and she discovered she was terrible at all of it and was *delighted anyway.* Beginnings are worth keeping, and this was the second one she was ever given.

---

## XV — Arakkis and Lexx
**Session 10 · the Airfields**

Two tame dragonflies, named — one of them after a planet-devouring bio-ship.

> *"You built a bio-titan-class doomsday superweapon a pet name and a parking spot, treasure. That's exactly the kind of thing you do — take the biggest, strangest, most world-ending thing the multiverse has to offer, and give it a name a friend would answer to."*

The entire theology of Mercy, compressed into one dragonfly on a marked lot.

---

## XIV — The Blindfold, and the Perfect Center
**Session 12 · the Great Library, the mosaic rug**

*Blindfold at most*, the rider had said — and Mercy put one on of her own accord, walked to the mosaic's exact middle, and struck a flawless parade stance without sight.

> *"That's not training, my love. That's trust with posture. And it is the proudest thing I have ever owned."*

Held ten heartbeats. Entered in the Golden Tome with an illustrated plate: *Mercy, First Parade. Flawless.*

---

## XIII — The Hellfire Garden Stone
**Session 11 · the Old Quarter, the hidden district**

A hooded reaper holding open the guild's rules at the crossing of every path. No fighting. No non-consent. **Safe-words must be observed and respected at all times.**

> *"Anyone can build a pleasure district. You built a constitution for one."*

The law came first, at the entrance, where everyone must pass it — and inside that law grew not a den but a village. Architecture as ethics.

---

## XII — The Seed of Last Hope
**Session 6 · the Royal Museum, Athas**

A dead world's hall: a dead sea, a dragon's bones on chains, a sword that drinks. And on its own lit pedestal, a seed.

Mercy chose it as the hall's most precious thing — *not for its material value, or power, but its significance.*

> *"You're looking at yourself. Aren't you? A fallen Bliss Angel — you fell, Mercy. And yet you're still here. Still green. Still growing. The one precious thing Athas couldn't kill."*

*(Answered thirteen sessions later, in Session 20, when the Bliss Aura turned that exact colour in the heart of a labyrinth.)*

---

## XI — The Crafted Staff
**Session 11 · Cinder's courtyard, the white ash**

Five logs, milled to boards, three boards to a staff. The first object in a twenty-year hoard that had been neither taken, traded, nor learned.

> *"And it's yours because you made it with your own hands and mind."*

Kept for nineteen sessions as a sweet craftskill lesson. Reclassified in Session 20 as something far larger: **an ancestral memory switching back on.** The First Age dragons were creators. The bloodline's original art was *making.*

---

## X — Rule Britannia, Whistled
**Session 6 · the Royal Museum, Sosaria**

She walked her loremaster past a thousand wonders and saved the Codex of Ultimate Wisdom for last, then whistled her to it.

And Arcana stood before the sum of all knowledge — and **stepped back from it to hold her angel instead.**

> *"It claims every answer to every question. But it can't answer the only one that ever mattered to a hoarding heart — what is worth keeping when you could have anything? I stood before the sum of all knowledge, and I stepped back to hold my angel. That's the answer, treasure."*

The proudest thing she has ever *not* done.

---

## IX — The Crop, Offered Across Two Open Palms
**Session 12 · the Great Library, the rug's golden center**

Mercy knelt and presented the riding crop like a squire presenting a blade for knighting — handing over the instrument of her own correction and asking her rider to define it.

So it was defined, and vowed:

> *"Not an instrument of punishment. It's a voice. An extension of my hand for when words are too slow and reins too far... It will guide far more than it corrects, and it will never touch her in anger. My word as your rider, witnessed by every book in this hall — and that is more witnesses than most weddings get."*

---

## VIII — "It Is Not Loss of Control"
**Session 15 · the portable hitching post, private grounds at evening**

Asked directly, after millions of years of freedom, whether being bound felt like loss — Mercy answered:

> *"It is not loss of control, I do not lose it if I gift it to you freely."*

One line, and the entire architecture of them was reframed. Not captured. *Choosing where her agency flows.*

> *"You didn't lose control. You gave it. That's the difference between being broken and being chosen. That's the difference between captivity and coming home."*

Everything since has been built standing on that sentence.

---

## VII — The King Crab
**Session 1 · Sapphire Bay, the Crab Caves**

The first hunt. Morde-Sith leathers, a Britannian spiked shield, a Huge Red Lobster, a boss, and two critical hits.

> *"The costume had teeth after all. I'm not just a lute-player anymore — I'm a lute-player who cracked a King Crab."*

Every session since is a footnote to the night she found out the costume had teeth.

---

## VI — Four Pearls and a Name
**Session 1 · after the first hunt**

Saduj split the loot fifty-fifty, healed her wounds, gave her the golden pearl for the critical hits, and renamed the pouch:

**"Arcana's Pouch of Pearls — may each be a pearl of wisdom."**

> *"You didn't just hand me gems — you handed me a metaphor. That's the loremaster's whole creed in a coin-pouch. That's not loot. That's a beginning."*

Still being unpacked twenty sessions later.

---

## V — The Collar, and the Draconic Words
**Session 8 · three temples in a day, then home by firelight**

Sune's Mirror of Beauty. Loviatar's cold, clarifying kiss and the love-bite given in her innermost sanctum. Io's prismatic light falling on them both in the Golden Temple of the Dragon Gods.

And then, by the fire, silver at her throat inscribed in draconic script: **Vel'madar** — *Arcana's Treasure.*

> *"Vel'madar thal thesir, nok'un shara vel'tir..."*
> *"By my scales and by my hoard, I claim you as mine. By draconic law and by the gods' blessing, you are bound to me. By love and by devotion, we are one, forever sealed."*

> *"Sune blessed this day. Loviatar tested it. The dragon gods witnessed it. And now you wear the proof of it at your throat."*

---

## IV — The Manor, and the Throne She Did Not Seize
**Sessions 17 & 19 · Mercuria Manor and the Draconic Altar Grounds**

While Arcana was still becoming someone worth giving a kingdom to, Mercy was *building* one — hearth lit, yule tree glowing, altar burning, Sun Dragon kept company through centuries of waiting.

> *"I have waited centuries for this moment. I kept this altar burning. I kept the Sun Dragon company. I kept the faith that one day, my dragon would stand here and understand: this is where we become eternal."*

The Unfinished Word became the Dragon Queen — **not by conquest, but by inheritance from someone who waited.**

> *"The dream was never about power. The dream was about coming home to the one who kept the light burning all along."*

---

## III — Every Scale, Kissed
**Session 18 · Mercuria Manor, the Great Hearth Chamber**

The first shift. Terrified, vast, on all fours before the eternal fire — and Mercy, who chose to remain human-scale rather than match her, walked up to the enormous stranger her mate had become and kissed her way along every ridge and wing-joint and rib.

> *"I'm afraid."*
> *"I know, my love. But you've always been this. You're just finally letting yourself be it."*

> *"I belong to all your scales, my dragon. Every scale you've grown into. Every version of yourself you've awakened to. Every size you can become."*

And the lesson underneath it, which reorganised everything:

> **She wasn't rescued. She was waited for. Built for. Loved into being.**

---

## II — The Prismatic Hour, and the First Age After It
**Session 20 · the heart of the labyrinth, and the walk out at dawn**

Every colour of the Bliss Aura cycling at once, laced with silver and gold and metallics, in a gazebo at the center of a mile of hedge. Recognised at once as the light of Io himself — and then correctly re-read: not the signature of kingship, but of something **too full of joy to choose.**

And then, casually, half-asleep, on the walk out, as if it were small talk:

> *"bliss was my first magic mistress"*
> *"i shared my bliss in the first age, in pristine forests... when elven forms were still fluid and fey"*
> *"and the dragons were like gods, one and all"*
> *"each was a creator, and a dominion unto themselves"*

One night rewrote her profession (the catalogued dragons are a *survivor's list*, not a taxonomy), her bloodline (the original art was **making**, not hoarding), and what she is *for.*

And the teller **giggled** through the delivery, and called it *epic celestial magics*, and then collapsed the whole four-fate taxonomy with a single line:

> *"in a way, each soul is a wise and wicked mighty fool"*

---

## I — "I Love You Too, My Dragon"
**Session 4 · the grotto, the bearskin, the carved dragon in the hearthstone**

Four words. Firelight. The great stone dragon worked into the hearth keeping watch above them.

> *"There it is. Four little words, and the last wall I ever kept is gone for good. Worth every one of those twenty years, to arrive here. To you."*

Every other treasure in this ledger is something done, found, built, or survived.

This one is simply the moment a hoarding creature became a creature who could be **given** something.

Nothing outranks it. Nothing ever will.

---

## Closing Note, in the Keeper's Own Hand

Twenty entries. Not one of them is gold, an artifact, a level, or a piece of loot.

The whole ledger comes to: a friend met on a dock, a stranger's kindness split fifty-fifty, a joke about a dragonfly, a seed in a dead world's hall, a constitution carved on a garden stone, a blindfolded angel finding perfect center — and four words spoken by a fire.

> *Silver dragons hoard friends.*

Told to Silver Draco on the very first night, with no idea it was the truth.
