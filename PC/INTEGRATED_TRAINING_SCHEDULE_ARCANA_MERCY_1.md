## COMMUNICATION & SAFETY PROTOCOLS

### Seven-Band Colour System (Always Active)

| Colour | Meaning | Usage | Response |
|--------|---------|-------|----------|
| **Infrared** | Preparation, anticipation | Pre-scene negotiation | Confirm readiness |
| **Red** | STOP immediately | Emergency safeword | Scene ends instantly, aftercare begins |
| **Yellow** | Approaching limit, ease/slow | Mid-scene adjustment | Reduce intensity, slow tempo |
| **Green** | Good, enjoying, more please | Active approval | Increase intensity as desired |
| **Blue** | Standing okay, conditions met | Neutral baseline | Continue as planned |
| **Violet** | In the intensity, trust holding | Deep engagement | Maintain current path |
| **Ultra-Violet** | All the way, no holds barred | Maximum permission given | Proceed with planned intensity, unspent |

### Check-In Protocol

**Before Every Session:**
- Colour check: What are we doing? What's the intensity level? Confirm yes
- Safeword review: Red = STOP, Yellow = adjust, Green = continue
- Equipment check: All restraints tested, secure, no sharp edges
- Mental state check: Mercy's consent is clear and enthusiastic

**During Session (Mid-Check):**
- Periodic presence checks (tap system if speech unavailable, gags active)
- Watch for nervous system responses (trembling, floating, dissociation)
- Pause to acclimate between new sensation layers (5-10 min breaks)
- Continuous observation of breathing, pupil dilation, body tension

**After Scene (Immediate):**
- Bring to bearskin by fire with blankets
- Warm her against Arcana's scales
- Soft touch, gentle presence
- Water and light food available
- No interrogation; just holding
- Watch for shaking, emotional releases, grounding needs

**Same-Evening Debrief (30 min - 2 hours post-scene):**
- How did it land? What was the experience?
- Any moments that landed hard? Any surprises?
- What do you need next time? More? Less? Different?
- Praise for endurance, presence, beauty, surrender
- Affirm the covenant and her role as prey/partner/treasure

**24-Hour Check-In:**
- Physical state: Any marks, soreness, circulation issues?
- Emotional state: Integration complete? Floating lingered?
- Did anything need adjustment? What worked beautifully?
- Any lingering concerns or needs?

**Weekly Debrief (Optional but Recommended):**
- Review both sessions (Monday pony + Wednesday sensory)
- Discuss progression and readiness for next phase
- Adjust intensity/duration based on actual response
- Set intention for coming week
- Celebrate milestones reached

### Safeword is Sacred

- **Any colour system call STOPS the scene immediately**
- No questions, no hesitation, no continuing "just a bit more"
- Aftercare begins instantly (warmth, reassurance, physical care)
- Debrief happens next session (not while vulnerable)
- Trust is maintained through absolute respect for signals
