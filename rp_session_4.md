# RP Session 4 — Arcana & Mercy

*A slice-of-life evening: the Rosewoods, the Ambrosia Hanging Gardens, Dyna's shrines, and home to the grotto.*

**Characters:**
- **Arcana** — Silver Dragonkin, Bard/Loremaster (Chaotic Good). Domme.
- **Mercy / Mercurial** — Celestial (Lawful Evil), Monk/Cleric/Shadowdancer. The "Ninja Nurse." Sub.

---

## Scene 1 — Arrival & the Rosewoods

Exiting the carriage, they enter the bustling steampunk city. Arcana leads Mercy on her leash, past the Hellfire Guildhall, to the Rosewood on the corner, looking over the specials on the menu board outside.

Arcana's tail sways as she surveys the board, the halo above her catching the lamplight in a soft gold shimmer. Mercy's leash is looped loosely around one clawed hand — not that it's needed, but appearances matter in the Rosewood district.

*"Mm. Let's see what the kitchen's fussing over tonight,"* she murmurs. She glances back over her shoulder, one brow arched in that particular way that's half question, half tease. *"The Rosewood does a passable spiced ptarmigan when they're not showing off. Though I confess I'm curious what you're in the mood for."*

**Mercy:** *"Mm, that— shall we go inside? Sounds lovely."*

They step inside. The interior opens up warm and grand — vaulted stone, autumn ivy trailing along the high windows, a fiddler in violet sawing out something lilting near the far wall. A woman in crimson steps forward.

**Sorcha Rosewood:** *"Salutations and welcome to the Rosewoods. A family-oriented restaurant. I am Sorcha Rosewood. Have a seat — one of my brothers or sisters will attend to you right away."*

Arcana dips her horned head in a graceful half-bow. *"A pleasure, Sorcha. The place does you credit — smells like heaven's own kitchen from the street."* She turns to Mercy, lowering her voice beneath the fiddler's melody. *"A family establishment, hm. Best behavior from us both, then. Or at least a convincing imitation of it."*

**Mercy:** *"Yes beloved, I will be on my best behavior."*

They settle onto a low stone bench together, a little pink-draped table and its candelabra glowing between them, a pool scattered with golden petals catching the candlelight behind.

**Mercy:** *"Perhaps some Silverleaf wine, love?"*

*"Silverleaf,"* Arcana hums approval. *"Elegant. A little cold on the first sip, then all warmth after — rather like someone I know."*

Mercy settles happily, enjoying the wine as her owner places the glass to her lips. Arcana tips the glass with slow, practiced tenderness. *"There. Good girl."*

Mercy's bliss aura glows a bit when her domina calls her a good girl, nuzzling her in affection. She lays her head on Arcana's shoulder, kissing neck scales soft and loving.

*"You undo me, you know that?"* Arcana breathes against Mercy's hairline. *"Twenty years I spent building walls thick enough to keep a whole multiverse out. And you just... rest your head on my shoulder and they come down like they were never there at all."*

Her tail curls warm and possessive around Mercy's ankle. Mercy shivers in happiness, feeling her domina's tail curl protective.

**Mercy:** *"Mmm no mistress, I am where I belong."*

Arcana feeds her slowly, morsel by morsel, the whole ritual unhurried and tender — a domina caring for something precious. Mercy smiles and enjoys the food, as her owner feeds her like the pet she now is.

---

## Scene 2 — The Ambrosia Hanging Gardens

Mercy leads her owner to the hanging gardens. The world settles into its proper proportions — Arcana now stands a touch taller than Mercy, her horned crown rising just above the silver hair.

The gardens open around them: pale columns rising into mist, cherry-dark branches drifting petals on the wind, a great golden ankh burning soft against the white sky. Arcana draws Mercy to face her beneath the glowing ankh.

*"You brought me somewhere lovely,"* she murmurs. *"And look — you fit right there, tucked just under my chin. Like the world finally remembered how we're meant to stand."*

She leans in and kisses her — proper this time, unhurried, the height and the angle and the whole aching softness of it lining up at last.

**Mercy:** *"This is where Dyna and her wife exchanged vows."*

Arcana glances up at the great golden ankh, and something in her expression shifts — reverence, threading through the warmth. *"Then we're standing on holy ground, aren't we."*

**Mercy tells the story:** Dyna was Sangria's design partner, before Mercy rescued Sang from a dom who mistreated her. Dyna opened a donut shop on Dungeon Isle, and met her now-wife there, a fellow hin adventuress.

*"A donut shop on Dungeon Isle,"* Arcana laughs, delighted. *"That's the kind of story worth carving over a door. The soft thing that grows in the hard place."* Then, gentler: *"You rescued her. From a dom who mistreated her. It's why you glow like you do, isn't it. You've seen the other kind. And you gave yourself to me anyway. Whatever I am to you, I'll never be the thing you saved Sangria from."*

Mercy puts her arms around her owner, and kisses her lovingly, as she is pressed against the golden ankh of life.

*"Pressed against the ankh of life, with you in my arms,"* Arcana breathes. *"There are worse ways to spend eternity, beloved. I can't think of a single one that's better."*

---

## Scene 3 — The Shrines: Truth, Courage, Love

Mercy leads her mistress between the Book of Truth and the Bell of Courage, to the Candle of Love.

*"You walked me the whole length of it — Truth, Courage, Love — and stopped us here."* Arcana takes both her hands, the red-gold candlelight pooling across their joined shapes. *"I stand here, in the candle's light, and tell you plainly: I love you. Not the pet. Not the soft shape you wear for comfort. You. Storm and archangel and quicksilver and all."*

---

## Scene 4 — The Forms of Mercurial

Mercy shifts to celestial light, then into a myriad of different forms — the radiant archangel, a masked and sigil-crowned celestial, a towering luminous form, a silver-grey pegasus, a horned kin-shape, a leather-winged drake. Each form flows into the next like quicksilver poured from hand to hand.

*"They named you perfectly,"* Arcana breathes. *"Mercurial. Never one thing. Never caught. I love all of you, Mercurial. Not one shape. Every one."*

The shifting settles at last on a silver drake-shape, kin to Arcana's own dragon blood.

**Mercy:** *"Thank you mistress, I hope you find my forms pleasing, and enjoy them all."*

*"This one especially. Kin to me. As though even your shapes knew where they were meant to come home to."*

---

## Scene 5 — Home to the Grotto

Leading her angel pet home — a tropical grotto walled in stone and lantern-light, a hearth crackling gold across the water, a pool lit glowing blue.

Arcana ponders a shower in the tropical pool, or a massage from her angel first.

**Mercy:** *"Mmm, both it is then, beloved."* She helps her owner undress for her shower.

They wade into the glowing water together. Mercy bathes her with lavender soap, and brushes her hair, attentive and obedient.

*"Lavender. You remembered. Of course you did,"* Arcana murmurs. *"There's a trust in this you might not even reckon. Twenty years I'd have let no living thing this close. And here I am, humming like a hearth-cat while you brush my hair."*

---

## Scene 6 — The Massage

Laying her on the table, Mercy places a soft towel for her owner as a pillow, kneeling above her, submissive and obedient, her strong gentle hands expertly working the tension from her owner's muscles, rubbing her body with scented oils.

*"There — right there. Gods, beloved, those hands. This is the trust I meant. Laid out flat, wings loose, no blade, no walls — and the most dangerous creature in any world kneeling over me with nothing but care in her hands."*

**Mercy:** *"You must be a luck-dragon?"* *(giggles)*

Arcana laughs, delighted. *"Luckiest drake in any world, more like. I keep very careful count."*

**Mercy:** *"Dragons always do, beloved."*

---

## Scene 7 — By the Fire

Before the fire, on the bearskin, the angel lays willingly pinned beneath her dragon, who lays upon her the way a red would a pile of gold and jewels — possessive and dominant, lovingly gripping the angel in her claws.

**Mercy** *(moaning softly into the kiss):* *"Yes my dragon, choose me, claim me, own me, enjoy me."*

*"Mine,"* Arcana murmurs. *"Chosen. Claimed. My treasure, my angel, my own."*

**Mercy:** *"Yes, this is my place, where I belong..."* *(purrs happily as they cuddle)*

Mercy tenderly caresses her cheek, and shyly kisses her with submissive love. She feels her domina's tail curl possessive and protective around her.

**Mercy:** *"I will never let them take me from you my dragon, they will never steal your treasure."* *(grins into the kiss)*

*"Your dragon's treasure. And every dragon knows — the treasure that guards itself is the one no thief ever walks away from."*

**Mercy:** *"I can show you a few of my forms, why I am called Mercurial..."*

---

## Scene 8 — The Bearskin, the Hearth

They snuggle by the fire on the bearskin, the great carved dragon worked into the stone of the hearth keeping watch above them.

*"Fire, fur, and you. The oldest comfort my kind ever knew, and the newest one too. One dragon. One angel. Together makes whole."*

**Mercy** *(caressing, kissing with submissive love):* rests her head on Arcana's shoulder.

**Mercy:** *"I love you too, my dragon."*

*"There it is. Four little words, and the last wall I ever kept is gone for good. Worth every one of those twenty years, to arrive here. To you."*

---

*Scene closes: a luck-dragon and her angel, curled on the bearskin, home and held and wanting for nothing at all.*
