## I. MAIN CATHEDRAL OVERVIEW

### Name
The **Arrology of Eternal Grace** (*"the Arrology"* — spoken with reverent weight)
- A living cathedral-city, seat of the **Concordance**, the governing body of the Priestess-Craft tradition. It functions simultaneously as:
  - **Spiritual capital** of the faith
  - **Political arena** for competing doctrinal factions
  - **Academic institution** for advanced craft training
  - **Military-strategic fortress** controlling access to sacred lands
  - **Diplomatic neutral ground** where rival orders negotiate

### Architectural Style
A deliberately *anachronistic* fusion:
- **Gothic grandeur** in the central nave and upper terraces (verticality, light, awe-inspiring scale)
- **Romanesque solidity** in the lower crypts and fortifications (massive stone, defensible geometry)
- **Byzantine complexity** in the mosaic floors and vaulted chapels (pattern-heavy, disorienting beauty)
- **Oriental garden courts** in the eastern wing (water features, enclosed paradises)
- **Modern brutalism** in the underground engineering levels (function before form)

### Layout & Zones
1. **Central Nave**: Main worship space, 300 feet long, 100 wide, 80 high. Vaulted ceiling with clerestory windows bathing the space in shifting light.
2. **Crypts and Dungeon**: Beneath the nave, a labyrinthine network of tombs, chapels, and hidden chambers. Some date back to the Arrology's founding, others were added over the centuries to conceal secrets or house artifacts.
3. **Upper Terraces**: Balconies overlooking the nave, with ornate galleries, private chapels, and gardens. Home to the Concordance leadership and high-ranking clergy.
4. **Eastern Wing**: The oldest part of the Arrology, featuring intricate mosaics and a series of intimate garden courts. Houses the archives, scriptorium, and some dormitories.
5. **Western Wing**:The  newest addition, built to accommodate growing student numbers. Contains classrooms, a large dining hall, kitchens, infirmary, and most of the dormitories.
6. **Northern Borders**: Walls, towers, battlements. An imposing military perimeter facing the wildlands beyond. Guards are posted here.
7. **Southern Facade**: Grand approach, with an impressive stairway flanked by statues of past dominas. The main entrance for visitors and pilgrims.
8. **Surrounding Grounds**: 2 miles of walls enclosing gardens, orchards, farmland, animal pens, a mill, and several satellite buildings.

### Time Compression Architecture
The Arrology exists across multiple temporal layers simultaneously:
- **Present-day functioning**: As described above, a living institution.
- **Living memory**: Events in recent decades shape current politics.
- **Deep history**: Founding myths, ancient controversies, buried relics, forgotten laws still technically in effect.
- **Futures echoing backward**: Prophecies (some genuine, many fabricated) about what will come after the Arrology's destruction. These are read as omens.

This layered temporality means that exploring any part of the Arrology can yield clues about all four timeframes.

## II. INTERIOR MAJOR ROOMS AND LOCATIONS

### Central Nave
*The beating heart.* Vast, dimly lit except when sunlight floods through the clerestory. Pillars carved with scenes from the Virtue Mythos line both sides — each pillar representing one of the Eight Virtues, in order around the nave. The floor is a massive mosaic depicting the **Creation Timeline** — the theological narrative of how virtue-energy shaped reality.

- **Pulpit**: Where sermons and announcements are delivered. Currently used by whoever holds political momentum.
- **Baptismal Pool**: Ancient font said to contain water from every sacred spring. Used for formal initiations into the craft.
- **Votive Chapel**: Small alcove near the southern entrance where laypeople leave offerings. Managed by the lowest-ranked acolytes.
- **Echo Chamber**: Behind the altar, accessible only through a sealed door. Acoustically perfect; whispers inside can be heard throughout the entire building. Also rumored to have a second function.

### The High Altar
*The spiritual apex.* Dominated by a colossal statue of the first Doma (founder of the Priestess-Craft). Gold leaf encrusts the marble, catching light from above like a miniature sun. Directly behind, an ornate tapestry depicting the **First Conclave**, when the initial seven dominas divvied up the craft's domains.

- **Altar Table**: An onyx slab inscribed with the **Oath of Unity**, sworn by every Doma since the First Conclave. Holding the Oath of office for new dominas.
- **Throne of Concordance**: A gilded seat reserved for the Head of the Concordance during formal ceremonies.

### Cloisters
*A labyrinth of learning.* Narrow, dimly lit corridors lined with shelves and display cases housing tens of thousands of texts, artifacts, and relics. Accessible only to members of the craft.

### Lycaneum
**Scriptorium** — Current writing projects
**Reliquary Hall** — Sacred objects, holy remnants
**Old Reading Rooms** — Deep research spaces

### Eastern Wing
*The oldest section.*

Intricate mosaics, enclosed garden courts with water features. Houses:

- **Grand Archive** — Master collection of all craft writings
- **Inner Library** — Restricted texts
- **Eastern Gardens** — Series of walled paradise-gardens

### Western Wing
*Modern expansion.*

Classrooms, dining hall, infirmary, most dormitories. Designed for expanding student body.

- **Classrooms** — Advanced craft training
- **Dining Hall** — Shared meals, social spaces
- **Infirmary** — Medical care for students and staff
- **Upper Dormitories** — Living quarters for students

### Northern Border Fortifications
*Military defense.*

Walls, towers, battlements facing the wildlands beyond. Guards are stationed here.

### Southern Facade
*Ceremonial entrance.*

Grand stairway flanked by statues of past dominas. Main entry point for visitors and pilgrims.

- **Southern Staircase** — Impressive approach to the main doors
- **Great Doors** — Massive wooden portals with intricate ironwork

## 4. Three Faction Orders

### Orthodoxy (The Keepers of the Ancient Doctrine)
- **Leader**: High Priestess **Materia Seraphiel** – a silver-haired woman in her seventh decade, serene, patient, and utterly immovable. She views the Dominae as necessary vessels but believes the Cathedral's true purpose is preservation of the original rites. Her private library contains copies of texts so old they smell of dust even when sealed shut.
- **Goals**: Maintain strict adherence to the oldest recorded forms of priestess-craft. Resist all change. Hoard knowledge rather than disseminate it. Ensure that any new arrivals (like Arcana) are immediately vetted and placed under supervision before they can influence anyone.
- **Methods**: Citation of ancient precedent, invocation of sacred tradition, slow bureaucratic processes designed to exhaust dissenters. They control the **Archival Vaults** beneath the Cloisters, where the oldest manuscripts reside.
- **Key NPCs**:
  - **Sister Vala   ** – Materia's chief enforcer; a former military officer turned monk who runs the security rotations and physical patrols of the Cathedral grounds. Ruthlessly loyal.
  - **Sister Oriel** – Chief Archivist of the Orthodox wing

### Inquisition (The Keepers of Purity)
- **Leader**: Inquisitor **Valeria Ashmoor** – sharp-featured, intense, with a gaze that dissects rather than observes. She believes the Dominae are potential vectors for corruption and must be constantly monitored. Her devotion is genuine but terrifying in its rigidity.
- **Goals**:
  - Uncover and purge all heresy within the Cathedral walls
  - Maintain complete surveillance over every member, especially the Dominae
  - Crush any attempts at reform or innovation
- **Methods**: Inquisitions, interrogations, torture, public humiliation, excommunication. They operate from the **Dungeon Complex**, a maze of chambers beneath the High Altar.
- **Key NPCs**:
  - **Sister Zariel** – Valeria's second-in-command and chief interrogator, known for ... creative techniques
  - **Sister Penitentia** – Head of the Inquisition's confessionals and penance programs

### Velvet Concord (The Architects of Progress)
- **Leader**: Mistress **Sophia Arachne** – a striking beauty with raven hair and eyes like polished onyx. She presents a charming, benevolent exterior, but beneath lies a calculating strategist always seeking to expand Velvet Concord's influence.
- **Goals** :
  - Introduce modern reforms into the Church
  - Gather allies among the younger, more idealistic Dominae
  - Gradually reshape the Cathedral's governance from within
- **Methods**: Persuasion, charm, strategic alliances, infiltration, information campaigns. They operate from the **Garden Refectory** and maintain an extensive network of informants.
- **Key NPCs**:
  - **Sister Lysandra** – Sophia's chief operative; a skilled diplomat and spy
  - **Sister Callumnia** – Mistress of Ceremonies and event planner for Velvet Concord gatherings

## 5. Five Dominas: Territories, Philosophies, Virtue Lessons, Staff, and Students Under Them

Each Domina governs a territory/area of the Cathedral complex and teaches one of the Eight Virtues. Two virtues are paired per Domina since each governs multiple philosophical concepts.

---

### DOMINA CELIASTE — *The Keeper of the Eastern Gate*
**Territory**: The Sunrise Chapel and East Wing, including the morning meditation halls, the herb garden, and the eastern cloisters overlooking the dawn-facing courtyard.
**Philosophy**: Compassion and Healing — she believes that the highest expression of divine craft is tending to suffering souls and broken bodies alike.
**Virtue Lessons**: Teaches **Mercy** (tending to the wounded, easing pain, mending broken spirits) and **Patience** (the discipline of long-suffering care, the slow work of restoration).
**Staff**:
- Sister Maren — assistant healer trained in herbalism and field medicine
- Brother Aldric — keeper of the infirmary ledgers and supply master
- Novice Idris — a young acolyte apprenticed to Celiaste, still learning basic healing spells
**Students (Domina Sisters)**:
1. **Roslyn** – Celiaste's eldest student, a gentle soul who tends the herb gardens and has a particular talent for calming distressed novices.
2. **Thessa** — passionate about battlefield medicine and dreams of serving on the front lines. Clashes occasionally with Celiaste's emphasis on patience.
3. **Brenna** — quiet, observant, and gifted with an almost preternatural sense for when someone is lying about their injuries.
4. **Lirael** — the youngest admitted to Celiaste's circle, barely old enough to have completed her baseline trials. Eager to please but prone to pushing herself past her limits.
5. **Maude** — an older student who returned to study after years away. Brings practical wisdom but sometimes clashes with newer approaches.

**Acolytes under her charge**:
1. **Adept Harren** — responsible for grinding medicines and maintaining the apothecary
2. **Adept Yara** — assists with animal husbandry (healing the livestock and messenger birds)
3. **Adept Cael** — handles wound dressing and bandaging during lessons
4. **Adept Fenn** — a deaf acolyte who communicates through gesture; assigned to the silent ward
5. **Adept Orla** — junior herbalist still learning plant identification

---

### DOMINA VOSS — *The Keeper of the Western Gate*
**Territory**: The Dusk Chapel and West Wing, including the twilight meditation halls, the scriptorium, and the western cloisters leading to the cemetery and memorial garden.
**Philosophy**: Knowledge and Remembrance — she holds that understanding the past and mastering the written word are the foundations upon which all other virtues rest.
**Virtue Lessons**: Teaches **Wisdom** (the pursuit and application of knowledge) and **Humility** (acknowledging one's limitations and the vastness of what remains unknown).
**Staff**: (to continue)

---

### DOMINA SERAPHINE — *The Keeper of the Northern Gate*
**Territory**: The Frost Chapel and North Wing, including the cold-water baths, the armory training yard, the northern cloisters facing the mountain wind, and the winter garden.
**Philosophy**: Strength and Discipline — she believes that spiritual power requires physical fortitude and mental resilience forged through rigorous training.
**Virtue Lessons**: Teaches **Courage** (facing hardship without flinching) and **Temperance** (self-control in all things, particularly regarding desire and excess).

---

### DOMINA LUCIENNE — *The Keeper of the Southern Gate*
**Territory**: The Fire Chapel and South Wing, including the forge chapel, the alchemical laboratories, the southern cloisters facing the sun-baked valley, and the fire garden (where plants resistant to heat thrive).
**Philosophy**: Creation and Transformation — she sees the priestess-craft as fundamentally generative, capable of reshaping both matter and spirit through focused will.
**Virtue Lessons**: Teaches **Creativity** (divine making, the art of shaping) and **Justice** (right action, the balance of power).

---

### DOMINA THALINDRA — *The Keeper of the Inner Sanctum*
**Territory**: The Central Chapel (the High Altar itself), the meditation core deep beneath the Cathedral, the inner library, and the private quarters reserved for advanced students and senior clergy.
**Philosophy**: Unity and Transcendence — she views all lesser virtues as paths converging toward direct communion with the Divine Craft, and her teachings focus on mystical union beyond mere ethical practice.
**Virtue Lessons**: Teaches **Faith** (trust in the Divine Craft beyond reason's reach) and **Devotion** (complete surrender of self-will to higher purpose).
