# PART 1: CATHEDRAL EVENTS
## Scheduled Ceremonies, Seasonal Occurrences, Political Moments

Events are **scheduled happenings** that structure the campaign calendar. Some are public; some restricted to priestesses. Some are weekly; others seasonal or rare. Each event creates narrative opportunities, faction interactions, and relationship progression.

---

### EVENT 1: THE WEEKLY DEVOTION CEREMONY
**Schedule:** Every Sunday evening, Upper Transept, all priestesses + their mounts attend

**What Happens:**
All priestesses gather in the Cathedral's main ceremonial space. The Dominae lead coordinated priestess-work; mounts enter transcendent states; the ceremony culminates in synchronized release of Psyche energy (visible as subtle shimmer in the air to trained observers).

**Narrative Function:**
- Weekly ritual that structures Arcana's schedule
- Opportunity for priestess interaction (group bonding, competitive observation, alliance-building)
- Mercy experiences group energy; learns she's not alone in her bond with Arcana
- Void-hint: The ceremony's energy occasionally flickers (Void-seduction weakening the collective connection)

**Complications/Hooks:**
- A sister's mount misbehaves (suggesting Void-corruption beginning)
- Arcana's performance is critiqued by competitive sisters (Renna takes notes)
- Elain notices something wrong during the ceremony (spiritual sensing)
- Sister Cenna almost collapses (showing Void-seduction effects)

---

### EVENT 2: THE MONTHLY GRAND FEAST
**Schedule:** Full moon (approximately monthly), Great Refectory, entire Cathedral community

**What Happens:**
Massive communal meal in the refectory. Hundreds of people: priestesses, acolytes, monks, support staff, occasionally invited guests. Formal seating hierarchy; entertainment (musicians, performers, formal speeches); political declarations sometimes made here.

**Narrative Function:**
- Information-gathering opportunity (rumors flow freely)
- Faction politicking happens visibly
- Arcana sees the Cathedral's social hierarchy in action
- Opportunity for both bonding and conflict

**Complications/Hooks:**
- A sister publicly challenges Arcana's techniques (rivalry display)
- Mercenary visitors arrive with news from outside (temporal anchor for outside events)
- A guest becomes ill (poisoning? Chronological sickness? Void-contact?)
- Someone whispers a secret to Arcana (information dump; trust-building)
- Idina and her mount perform a perfect synchronized dance (intimidation display)

---

### EVENT 3: THE VIRTUE TRIALS (Quarterly)
**Schedule:** Once per season (4 times yearly), rotating venues in Cathedral grounds

**What Happens:**
Formal testing and advancement ritual. Priestesses attempt to progress in their specialty Virtues. Public observation permitted for lower tiers; higher tiers are restricted. Tests involve both technical demonstration and spiritual/philosophical evaluation. Advancement is prestigious; failure is private shame.

**Narrative Function:**
- Arcana participates in her first Virtue Trial (major milestone)
- Witness other priestesses advancing (see their strengths and weaknesses)
- Competitive moments (if Renna is also testing)
- Void-hint: A priestess attempting advanced trial becomes unresponsive during test; withdraws; later admits to Void-dreams

**Complications/Hooks:**
- Arcana's test is unexpectedly difficult (testing her resolve)
- A sister fails publicly (social consequence; vulnerability shown)
- The evaluating Domina asks an unexpected philosophical question (deepening relationship)
- Temporal echo: Arcana briefly sees a previous Arcana undergoing the same test

---

### EVENT 4: THE INITIATION GALA (Once per year, Spring)
**Schedule:** Spring equinox, grand ceremonial event

**What Happens:**
Grand celebration welcoming new priestess-pairs to the Cathedral (both arrivals mid-year and formal initiations of those who trained the full year). Formal vestments, dancing, elaborate meals, public recognition. Major social event; all factions attend; politics swirl.

**Narrative Function:**
- If Arcana arrives at spring equinox, this is her formal welcome
- Political factions position themselves
- New priestesses are introduced; each one a potential ally or rival
- Public display of priestess-craft mastery

**Complications/Hooks:**
- Arcana is required to demonstrate her baselined-lock state (public scrutiny)
- Someone publicly questions her credentials or background
- A new priestess-pair arrives with unclear faction allegiance (mystery)
- Void-hint: During the dancing, a priestess's partner moves with unsettling synchronization (possession-like)

---

### EVENT 5: THE SANCTUARY SILENCE (Quarterly, Winter)
**Schedule:** Deepest winter, 48-hour vow of silence throughout Cathedral

**What Happens:**
Every priestess, acolyte, monk, support staff enters 48-hour silence. No speaking, only written communication. The Cathedral becomes eerily quiet. Meditation, contemplation, deep spiritual work. Mounts are kept in comfortable isolation; priestesses do not conduct scenes. It's a period of genuine rest and reset.

**Narrative Function:**
- Forces introspection and character reflection
- Mercy gets genuine rest (Psyche regeneration opportunity)
- Void-seduction pressure is briefly reduced (silence disrupts whispers)
- Arcana experiences genuine Cathedral spirituality without performance

**Complications/Hooks:**
- Someone breaks silence (heresy; investigation)
- Arcana receives anonymous written message (mystery communication)
- Void-presence is weakest during this period (priestesses recover slightly)
- Temporal echo: During deep meditation, Arcana hears *previous silence ceremonies* from other eras

---

### EVENT 6: THE BLOOD MOON CEREMONY
**Schedule:** Rare; triggered when full moon coincides with aurora (uncertain; perhaps twice per campaign)

**What Happens:**
Spontaneous, rare ceremony that occurs when astronomical conditions align. All priestesses are summoned. The ceremony's purpose is mysterious (strengthening seals? Confronting Void? Ancient ritual of unknown origin?). Participants emerge exhausted but spiritually renewed.

**Narrative Function:**
- Major plot moment (something cosmic is happening)
- Priestesses experience something beyond normal (minor revelation)
- Void-presence either manifests visibly or retreats entirely
- Potential confrontation with Void or its representatives

**Complications/Hooks:**
- Mercy becomes intensely ill during the ceremony (Void-resistance conflict)
- A sister becomes will-consumed during the ceremony (dramatic loss)
- Temporal echoes become *very* visible (Arcana sees multiple eras overlapping)
- The ceremony is interrupted (something breaks the astronomical alignment)

---

### EVENT 7: THE PILGRIMAGE PROCESSING (Twice yearly: Spring & Autumn)
**Schedule:** Spring equinox + Autumn equinox, entire priestess community processes to Sacred Springs

**What Happens:**
All priestesses, all mounts, all Dominae, formal procession to the Sacred Springs. Multiple-day ritual. Priestesses perform specific ceremonies at each spring. Mounts are blessed. The ceremony renews the Cathedral's connection to the valley's spiritual energy.

**Narrative Function:**
- Introduces Arcana to the Sacred Springs as group experience
- Bonding opportunity with other priestesses during travel
- Spiritual moment (genuine connection to larger purpose)
- Void-hint: The Void's presence is felt during processing (it's *angry* about the ceremony)

**Complications/Hooks:**
- Travel to Springs is interrupted (dangerous weather, enemy interference, or temporal anomaly)
- A sister goes missing during the processing (recovered later, claims to have been lost in time)
- At the Sacred Springs, a temporal echo is extremely vivid (Arcana sees the *first* pilgrimage ceremony)
- The Void attempts direct intervention (dramatic confrontation opportunity)

---

### EVENT 8: THE DOMINA CONCLAVE (Twice yearly: Midsummer & Midwinter)
**Schedule:** Midsummer + Midwinter, restricted to Eight Dominae (but rumors leak)

**What Happens:**
The Eight Dominae meet in closed session to discuss Cathedral governance, policy changes, Void-related concerns, and spiritual matters. Exact content is secret; lower priestesses speculate wildly. This is where major decisions are made.

**Narrative Function:**
- Establishes that leadership exists and makes choices
- Rumors of Conclave decisions filter down (information hooks)
- Arcana can gather intelligence about what was discussed
- Possible that Conclave discusses how to handle Arcana/Mercy situation

**Complications/Hooks:**
- Arcana overhears Conclave members arguing (factional tension)
- A Domina privately debriefs Arcana about Conclave decisions (trust-building)
- Rumors suggest a Domina dissented strongly (alliance/betrayal hint)
- Void-hint: Conclave becomes heated when discussing Void-containment (seals are weakening)

---

### EVENT 9: THE ACOLYTE ADVANCEMENT CEREMONY
**Schedule:** Quarterly, public event, restricted to acolytes + their trainers

**What Happens:**
Lower-rank acolytes attempt advancement to higher ranks (equivalent to priestess tiers). Tests are less intense than priestess trials but follow similar structure. Successful advancement means better status within Cathedral hierarchy.

**Narrative Function:**
- Demonstrates Cathedral's hierarchy and advancement paths
- Opportunity to notice missing acolytes (Void-consumed ones)
- Chance to interact with younger, less politically-complex priestesses
- Shows that not everyone succeeds (some acolytes fail; face shame)

**Complications/Hooks:**
- An acolyte Arcana befriended fails the advancement test (emotional moment)
- A suspiciously perfect acolyte advances (possibly Void-influenced?)
- One of the missing acolytes briefly appears at ceremony (haggard, confused; quickly escorted away)
- Temporal echo: Arcana sees this same ceremony from a previous era

---

### EVENT 10: THE NIGHT HUNT (Seasonal, Autumn peak)
**Schedule:** Autumn evenings when game is abundant, led by Sister Tam + Nature-specialty priestesses

**What Happens:**
Formal hunting expeditions into the Primal Grounds. Priestesses and mounts work as pack. Kill, field-dress, return with spoils. The hunt is both practical (provides meat for Cathedral kitchens) and spiritual (primal bonding experience). Success is celebrated; unsuccessful hunts are analyzed for lessons.

**Narrative Function:**
- First major bonding with Sister Tam
- Mercy experiences primal joy in her beast-form
- Arcana learns Nature Virtue through practice
- Opportunity for group bonding and friendly competition

**Complications/Hooks:**
- The hunt goes wrong (dangerous predator, unusual animal behavior, or temporal anomaly in the forest)
- A priestess is injured; Mercy must help carry her back (testing Arcana's priorities)
- Scout (Tam's mount) behaves strangely (warning of danger? Or Void-influence?)
- Discovery of something unusual in the forest (ruins, bones, or a temporal echo)

---

### EVENT 11: THE ROPE COMPETITION
**Schedule:** Annual, summer, Rope Gardens, public demonstration

**What Happens:**
Formal rope-work competition. Priestesses demonstrate technique, creativity, speed, and artistry. Judged by master rope-workers. Winners receive recognition and minor status boost. Spectators (other priestesses, invited guests) observe and vote on "People's Choice" awards. Highly competitive, somewhat showy.

**Narrative Function:**
- Arcana's chance to show Precision skills
- Direct competition with Sister Renna (relationship-defining moment)
- Opportunity to display creativity or technical mastery
- Mercy experiences artistic rope-work (beautiful, not painful)

**Complications/Hooks:**
- Arcana's rope-work is criticized harshly (humbling moment or conflict)
- Renna's winning performance is unsettlingly perfect (hints at Void-influence or just mastery?)
- A priestess's demonstration becomes unintentionally intimate (boundary blur)
- Temporal echo: Arcana briefly sees rope-workers from a previous era competing in the same space

---

### EVENT 12: THE COMPASSION VIGIL
**Schedule:** Irregular, triggered when a priestess or mount dies/disappears

**What Happens:**
If a Cathedral member dies or is presumed lost, a formal vigil is held. All priestesses gather; Elain leads spiritual ceremony; the community grieves collectively. This is serious, somber, and reveals genuine emotion beneath political facades.

**Narrative Function:**
- Stakes become real (people *die* in this world)
- Community shows vulnerability and genuine care
- Factions set aside conflict temporarily
- Void-hint: The person being mourned might have been will-consumed (grief for someone still technically alive)

**Complications/Hooks:**
- A sister Arcana has bonded with is being mourned (emotional investment)
- The circumstances of death/disappearance are unclear (mystery)
- Someone suspects heresy or Void-involvement (investigation trigger)
- A ghost or temporal echo appears during the vigil (supernatural element confirmed)

---

### EVENT 13: THE SPRING WATER BLESSING
**Schedule:** Spring (annual), Sacred Springs Pilgrim's Fountain, public event

**What Happens:**
Formal ceremony blessing the waters of the Pilgrim's Fountain for the coming year. High priestess (often Elain or another Domina) performs the blessing. Pilgrims gather; many are healed or receive "spiritual cleansing." A renewal ritual; hopeful and public.

**Narrative Function:**
- Arcana experiences priestess-craft as *public good* (not just private power)
- Opportunity to interact with pilgrims (learn about outside world)
- Display of Domina's spiritual power (awe-inducing)
- Void-hint: Some pilgrims report dreams intensify after blessing (Void-presence in the water?)

**Complications/Hooks:**
- The blessing goes wrong (water behaves strangely, or someone becomes ill)
- A pilgrim recognizes Arcana (connection to her past?)
- The Void's presence becomes visible during blessing (manifestation moment)
- Temporal echo: Arcana briefly sees previous blessings from other eras

---

### EVENT 14: THE ARCHIVE RENEWAL
**Schedule:** Every five years, restricted, intense work period over several weeks

**What Happens:**
Major archival project. The Cathedral's forbidden library and records must be periodically renewed (bindings repaired, vellum protected, texts recopied if degrading). This requires all hands-on-deck with senior scribes and priestesses working around the clock. Outsiders are strictly excluded.

**Narrative Function:**
- Arcana might gain restricted access (opportunity to learn secrets)
- Priestesses reveal their scholarly sides
- Opportunity to discover hidden information in archives
- Shows how knowledge is actively maintained

**Complications/Hooks:**
- Arcana is allowed to help; discovers ancient text about the Elders
- A text is found missing (heresy? Theft?)
- During renewal work, a temporal echo overwhelms a priestess (she becomes confused about which era she's in)
- The condition of certain texts is deteriorating faster than expected (Void-corruption of physical objects?)

---

### EVENT 15: THE DOMINA'S OPEN INSTRUCTION
**Schedule:** Weekly, each Domina teaches her Virtue during scheduled hours, open to all priestesses

**What Happens:**
Not a formal ceremony, but a structured teaching session. The Domina demonstrates, explains, answers questions, observes priestesses practicing. These are less intense than Virtue Trials but more formal than casual practice. Regular attendance is expected; irregular attendance is noted.

**Narrative Function:**
- Arcana's primary learning mechanism (attends at least one per week)
- Opportunity to ask questions and show dedication
- Bonding with individual Dominae
- Competitive observation (seeing how others learn)

**Complications/Hooks:**
- Arcana asks an unexpected question that impresses the Domina (relationship building)
- Or: Arcana's question reveals ignorance (humbling moment)
- Another priestess demonstrates advanced technique (inspire or intimidate)
- A Domina privately pulls Arcana aside after class (secret conversation)

---

### EVENT 16: THE HERESY INQUISITION (Irregular)
**Schedule:** Triggered by accusations, investigations of theological/behavioral violations

**What Happens:**
Formal investigation into potential heresy. An accusation is made against a priestess or acolyte. A tribunal is convened (Dominae + neutral observers). Evidence is presented; the accused can defend themselves. Outcome ranges from acquittal to minor discipline to severe punishment (expulsion, memory-wiping, execution in extreme cases).

**Narrative Function:**
- Shows consequences for breaking Cathedral rules
- Political factions use inquisitions to eliminate rivals
- Arcana might be accused (threatening) or defend someone (risk)
- Stakes are *real* (this is how power is enforced)

**Complications/Hooks:**
- A friend of Arcana's is accused (must decide whether to defend her)
- The accusation is false (political maneuvering)
- Or: The accusation is true, but the sister has sympathetic reasons
- Inquisition is manipulated by a factional leader (injustice demonstrated)

---

### EVENT 17: THE SEASONAL TRANSFER
**Schedule:** Roughly quarterly, some priestesses move between Cathedral and external domains (Rope Gardens, Sacred Springs, hunting grounds, etc.)

**What Happens:**
Administrative rotation. Priestesses and mounts move to temporary assignments (sabbaticals, training intensives, investigations). Some leave for weeks or months. This is normal but creates network disruption and opportunity for secret assignments.

**Narrative Function:**
- Explains why certain priestesses are sometimes absent
- Opportunity for secret assignments (Arcana investigating Void)
- Sisters can have separate adventures during their transfers
- Potential for missed connections and misunderstandings

**Complications/Hooks:**
- A sister Arcana was bonding with is transferred (separation scene)
- Someone is transferred as punishment (disguised inquisition result)
- A priestess is transferred but rumors suggest she was actually captured (Void-seduction escalation)
- Arcana is offered a transfer assignment (opportunity or threat?)

---

### EVENT 18: THE NOCTURNAL CEREMONY (Monthly, new moon)
**Schedule:** New moon, Crypt of the First Domina, restricted to senior priestesses

**What Happens:**
Mysterious ceremony in the Cathedral's deepest chamber. Purpose is unclear (maintaining seals? Confronting Void? Ancient ritual of unknown origin?). Only Dominae and their most trusted senior priestesses attend. Returns from this ceremony never fully explain what occurred.

**Narrative Function:**
- Builds mystery around Cathedral's true function
- Suggests hierarchy beyond what's publicly known
- Eventually Arcana will attend (major plot advancement)
- Hints at Keeper duties and seal-maintenance

**Complications/Hooks:**
- A priestess who attends becomes visibly changed (spiritual advancement or trauma?)
- Arcana is invited (major character progression; something important)
- The ceremony's nature is revealed (dramatic revelation of Cathedral's true purpose)
- Temporal echoes are strongest during this ceremony (Arcana might glimpse the Imprisonment)

---

### EVENT 19: THE FACTION CONFERENCE (Irregular, during major crises)
**Schedule:** Called when political tensions require resolution

**What Happens:**
Representatives from each faction (Traditionalist, Reformer, Keeper, possibly others) gather to debate Cathedral policy. These conferences are contentious, heated, sometimes result in new alliances or divisions. Major decisions are made; smaller priestesses learn about them afterward.

**Narrative Function:**
- Shows factional politics at work
- Arcana learns that priestess-craft has philosophical diversity
- Potential for Arcana to be political pawn or ally
- Sets up larger ideological conflicts for Acts III+

**Complications/Hooks:**
- Arcana is asked to publicly support a faction (political commitment)
- Or: Arcana is accused of being a factional spy
- A sister is revealed as secretly aligned with another faction (betrayal)
- The conference ends without resolution (tension remains)

---

### EVENT 20: THE ANNIVERSARY COMMEMORATION (Annual, on Cathedral founding day)
**Schedule:** Annual, exact date varies by tradition (perhaps spring or autumn)

**What Happens:**
Cathedral-wide celebration of the founding. Historical reenactments, formal ceremonies, public displays of priestess-craft mastery, feasting. The Dominae give speeches about priestess-craft's history and purpose. This is a moment of genuine pride and unity within the Cathedral community.

**Narrative Function:**
- Shows Cathedral's sense of continuity and tradition
- Opportunity for Dominae to hint at hidden history (Imprisonment, Elders, etc.)
- Priestesses display full range of skills (impressive spectacle)
- Void-hint: The celebration's energy becomes chaotic (Void-seduction disrupting collective joy)

**Complications/Hooks:**
- During historical reenactment, something goes wrong (reveals suppressed history?)
- A Domina's speech contains coded warning about Void
- The celebration is interrupted (Void-presence manifests visibly)
- Temporal echo: Arcana sees the *original* founding ceremony overlapping with current one

# PART 2: CATHEDRAL ENCOUNTERS
## 20 Narrative Moments, NPCs, & Discoveries

Encounters are **unscheduled situations** Arcana might stumble into or that find her. Some are NPCs she meets once; others develop into ongoing relationships. Some are discoveries; some are confrontations. Each encounter should change something about Arcana's understanding or relationships.

---

### ENCOUNTER 1: THE CRYING ACOLYTE IN THE CRYPT
**Where:** Deep crypt level, Arcana exploring alone or with a trusted companion

**What Happens:**
Arcana finds a young acolyte (maybe 16-17) crying in a side chamber. The acolyte is distressed, alone, trying to hide. Upon discovery, the acolyte is startled but gradually opens up.

**The Situation:**
- The acolyte's name is Kira; she failed her advancement test (humiliation)
- She's terrified of telling her trainer; fears expulsion
- She's questioning whether priestess-craft is right for her
- She's been having strange dreams (Void-hint? Or just stress?)

**What Arcana Can Do:**
- Comfort and reassure her (building future ally)
- Help her prepare to confess to her trainer (courage-building)
- Or: Help her hide the failure (teaching her to be deceptive)
- Offer to talk to her trainer (risking Arcana's reputation for Kira's sake)

**Outcomes:**
- If Arcana helps: Kira becomes devoted to her; potential disciple for Acts III+
- If Arcana ignores her: Kira disappears within weeks (Void-consumed? Expelled? Never clarified)
- If Arcana is harsh: Kira flees Cathedral entirely (refugee consequences)

**Void-Connection:**
The Void might be actively targeting Kira (vulnerable, self-doubting). Arcana's intervention could protect her or confirm the Void's grip.

---

### ENCOUNTER 2: THE GHOST IN THE ARCHIVE
**Where:** Cathedral archive, restricted section, Arcana exploring

**What Happens:**
While exploring the archives, Arcana witnesses a transparent figure moving between shelves—a ghost or temporal echo of a previous priestess. The figure doesn't acknowledge Arcana directly but seems to be searching for something.

**The Situation:**
- The ghost is a previous Keeper (from an earlier era)
- She's searching for a specific text that no longer exists (or has been moved)
- Her presence indicates that temporal echoes can become semi-conscious
- She's agitated, distressed, desperate

**What Arcana Can Do:**
- Attempt to communicate (risky; might break the echo)
- Note what the ghost is searching for (investigation clue)
- Follow the ghost (might lead to hidden archive chamber)
- Report it to another priestess (political choice; risks being disbelieved)

**Outcomes:**
- If Arcana communicates: She learns something about the Imprisonment or previous Arcanas
- If Arcana follows: She discovers a hidden archive section
- If Arcana reports it: Different priestesses react differently (Elain believes her; Bren dismisses her)

**Void-Connection:**
The Void doesn't create temporal echoes; something *else* does. This encounter begins Arcana's understanding that the Cathedral's mysteries are layered.

---

### ENCOUNTER 3: THE BLACKMAIL LETTER
**Where:** Arcana's private chambers, morning discovery

**What Happens:**
Arcana finds a sealed letter in her room. It's unsigned. The letter contains:
- A specific detail about Arcana's baseline-lock that shouldn't be publicly known
- An accusation (or implication) that Arcana's baseline-lock is fraudulent
- A demand (or threat): "Stop investigating the Void, or I expose your secret"
- Or: "Help me investigate the Void, or I expose you"

**The Situation:**
- Someone has intelligence about Arcana
- Someone knows about Void-investigation (if Arcana has started investigating)
- The sender is powerful enough to have access to Arcana's chambers
- This is either a threat or a recruitment attempt (unclear which)

**What Arcana Can Do:**
- Ignore the letter (risky; sender might escalate)
- Confess to a trusted priestess (seeking allies; risking reputation)
- Investigate who sent it (dangerous; might reveal enemies)
- Send her own message in reply (establishing dialogue with unknown contact)

**Outcomes:**
- If Arcana ignores it: Similar letters arrive, escalating threats
- If Arcana confesses: Ally learns her secret; power dynamic shifts
- If Arcana investigates: She might discover the letter-sender's identity (or walk into trap)
- If she replies: She's initiated dialogue with someone playing a long game

**Void-Connection:**
The letter-sender might be:
- A network operative (recruiting or threatening)
- A sister priestess (warning or blackmailing)
- An external agent (spy from outside the valley)
- The Void itself (communicating through letters?)

---

### ENCOUNTER 4: THE COLLAPSED PRIESTESS
**Where:** Cathedral corridors, during transition between areas

**What Happens:**
Arcana witnesses (or stumbles upon) a priestess collapsing mid-stride. The priestess falls, twitches, and becomes unresponsive. Other acolytes rush to help; there's chaos and confusion.

**The Situation:**
- The priestess is Sister Cenna (or another Void-vulnerable priestess)
- She's having a crisis from accelerated Void-seduction
- The Cathedral's healers are called; she's rushed to the infirmary
- Rumor spreads: Was it poison? Illness? Spiritual attack?

**What Arcana Can Do:**
- Help carry Cenna to infirmary (showing care; building bond)
- Ask questions about her symptoms (gathering intelligence)
- Visit Cenna during recovery (emotional support; trust-building)
- Investigate what caused the collapse (physical evidence hunt)

**Outcomes:**
- If Arcana helps: Cenna becomes indebted; potential ally
- If Arcana investigates: She gathers evidence that Void-corruption is spreading
- If she visits: Cenna might confess to Void-dreams (confirmation)
- If ignored: Cenna disappears from Cathedral within days (abduction? Voluntary departure?)

**Void-Connection:**
This is a dramatic escalation showing that Void-seduction has side effects and isn't perfectly subtle. Physical health deteriorates as will is consumed.

---

### ENCOUNTER 5: THE SECRET MEETING IN THE GARDENS
**Where:** Rope Gardens, deep evening, Arcana passing through

**What Happens:**
Arcana stumbles upon two figures meeting privately in the Rope Gardens upper terraces. They're talking urgently, in hushed tones. One is definitely a Cathedral priestess; the other is harder to identify (shadowed, face concealed, or deliberately unrecognized).

**The Situation:**
- The priestess is Sister Sura or another politically active priestess
- The other figure is a network operative, external agent, or factional representative
- They're discussing something sensitive (Void? Factions? External concerns?)
- They break off immediately upon noticing Arcana

**What Arcana Can Do:**
- Confront them (risky; they'll deny or threaten her)
- Leave silently and pretend she saw nothing (safe but uninformed)
- Follow them (gather intelligence; high risk of discovery)
- Secretly ask the priestess about the meeting later (diplomatic approach)

**Outcomes:**
- If Arcana confronts: She makes enemies or discovers allies (50/50)
- If she follows: She learns information but risks being caught (leading to confrontation or threat)
- If she asks later: The priestess might confess or deny (depending on bond strength)
- If she ignores it: The meeting's significance remains mysterious (tension remains)

**Void-Connection:**
This meeting might be:
- Sura investigating Void with an external contact
- Network operatives recruiting a priestess
- Faction representatives plotting Cathedral politics
- None of the above (innocent reason misunderstood)

### ENCOUNTER 6: THE ARCHIVIST'S CONFESSION
**Where:** Archive, private conversation with Father Malachi

**What Happens:**
Father Malachi (the Cathedral's ancient, blind archivist) requests private audience with Arcana. He's heard about her (through Cathedral gossip or direct observation). He has something to tell her—and only her.

**The Situation:**
- Malachi is ancient (80+), blind, but mentally sharp
- He's been archivist for decades; knows secrets
- He doesn't trust most priestesses (political reasons)
- He senses something different about Arcana (he recognizes her as similar to previous Arcanas?)

**What Malachi Tells Her (varying by campaign moment):**
- Early: "The Cathedral wasn't built to teach priestess-craft. It was built to contain something. You should know that."
- Mid: "There were others like you. Priestesses who arrived asking the right questions. Three of them stayed. One left. One tried to free the imprisoned ones."
- Late: "The seals are breaking. Not just the Void's seal. The others too. If you don't understand why that matters, ask me again."

**What Arcana Can Do:**
- Ask him for more information (he'll trade for favors/objects)
- Report him to the Dominae (betrayal; consequences)
- Seek him out regularly (building relationship; ongoing information)
- Ask about specific historical moments (checking his knowledge)

**Outcomes:**
- If Arcana builds relationship: Malachi becomes ongoing information source and ally
- If she reports him: Malachi is removed from archive (consequences cascade)
- If she uses him: She gains historical knowledge but might become indebted to him
- If she ignores him: He fades into background; opportunity missed (or not; he might appear again later)

**Void-Connection:**
Malachi knows the Void existed before current sealing; he's studied historical records about the Imprisonment. He might know *why* it was sealed and *by whom*.

---

### ENCOUNTER 7: THE MOUNT WHO SPEAKS
**Where:** Rope Gardens or private chamber, Arcana alone with another priestess's mount

**What Happens:**
Arcana is alone with another priestess's mount (perhaps Seraph, Elain's philosophically-minded mount, or Echo, Reth's quiet mount). The mount initiates communication—direct, verbal conversation.

**The Situation:**
- Mounts typically don't speak (or do so rarely and carefully)
- This mount has chosen to break silence with Arcana specifically
- The mount shares information, warnings, or philosophical observations
- The mount might be partially will-consumed (Void-contact making mounts more vocal?)

**What the Mount Communicates:**
- Warning about a specific priestess (Void-influence affecting her)
- Historical information (mounts have memories spanning their priestess's career)
- Philosophical observation about Arcana's bond with Mercy
- Warning about the Void itself ("It speaks to us too, not just priestesses")

**What Arcana Can Do:**
- Listen and trust the mount (building inter-mount alliance)
- Share this with the mount's priestess (trust-building between priestesses)
- Keep it secret (maintaining bond with the mount independently)
- Report it to authorities (political choice; consequences vary)

**Outcomes:**
- If Arcana trusts: The mount becomes information source and secret ally
- If she tells the priestess: The priestess-mount bond deepens (or fractures if priestess is compromised)
- If she reports it: Authorities investigate; consequences unclear
- If she keeps it secret: She has a hidden ally in the mount community

**Void-Connection:**
Mounts experience Void-seduction differently than priestesses. They might see threats priestesses miss. Or: The Void might be speaking to mounts more clearly than to priestesses (parallel seduction happening).

---

### ENCOUNTER 8: THE FORBIDDEN TEXT
**Where:** Archive, hidden chamber, or trading with black market contact

**What Happens:**
