# ARCANA'S VIRTUE PROGRESSION SYSTEM
## Learning All Eight Virtues (Expert Tier Ascent)

**Core Mechanic:** Arcana begins at Tier 0 (novice) in all Eight Virtues. As she trains with each Domina and practices priestess-craft, she gains **Virtue Points** across different schools. Reaching Tier 3 in a Virtue unlocks that school's master-level techniques. Full Domina-avatar status requires Tier 5 in all Eight Virtues (endgame Master-tier achievement, not Expert-tier goal).

---

## VIRTUE POINT SYSTEM (Expert Tier: S62-S90)

### How Virtue Points Work

**Each Virtue has a progression track: Tier 0 → Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Tier 3**

- **Tier 0:** Novice; learning basics
- **Tier 1:** Competent; can apply techniques; understands philosophy
- **Tier 2:** Advanced; nuanced application; teaching others basics
- **Tier 3:** Master-level (Expert Tier cap); expert technique; deep philosophical integration

**Points earned through:**
1. **Training sessions** with Domina (1-2 points per focused session)
2. **Successful application** of technique with Mercy (1-2 points)
3. **Insights/breakthroughs** during practice (2-3 points; GM discretion)
4. **Teaching or mentoring** other priestesses (1 point)
5. **Exploration discoveries** that deepen understanding (1-2 points; context-dependent)
6. **Relationship milestones** with Domina (1 point when achieving new tier in that Virtue)

**Tier Progression:**
- **Tier 0 → Tier 1:** 5 points
- **Tier 1 → Tier 2:** 10 points
- **Tier 2 → Tier 3:** 15 points

**Total Expert Tier commitment:** ~120 points across all Eight Virtues (achievable over 20-30 sessions of focused training)

## MECHANICAL BENEFITS OF VIRTUE TIERS

### Tier 1 Benefits
- Can cast Tier 1 spells in that Virtue school
- Technique lands reliably (MAB vs. MAC system works normally)
- Can teach basics to others
- Domina in that school becomes mentor/ally

### Tier 2 Benefits
- Can cast Tier 1-2 spells in that Virtue school
- Technique lands with increased precision (bonus to MAB)
- Can mentor intermediate students
- Domina offers advanced private training
- Unlocks related Virtue schools (e.g., Precision + Transcendence = suspension sensory-work)

### Tier 3 Benefits (Expert Tier Cap)
- Can cast Tier 1-3 spells in that Virtue school
- Technique lands with mastery-level precision (significant MAB bonus)
- Can independently teach full school curriculum
- Domina treats you as equal authority in that Virtue
- Unlock Tier 3 capstone techniques (unique, powerful spells)

### Companion Tier (Future)
- Tier 4-5 unlock as Arcana builds her own Church
- By Tier 5 in all Eight, she achieves Domina-avatar status
- Becomes founder-priestess of new spiritual authority

## MERCY'S RELATIONSHIP TO ARCANA'S PROGRESSION

### How Virtue Points Affect Priestess-Mount Work

**Arcana's growing mastery = Mercy experiences deeper priestess-effects:**

- **Tier 0:** Priestess-work is functional but emotionally surface-level
- **Tier 1:** Priestess-work carries intentionality; Mercy feels priestess-will more clearly
- **Tier 2:** Priestess-work reaches nervous-system level; Mercy's consciousness responds more dramatically
- **Tier 3:** Priestess-work is transcendent; Mercy's being aligns with priestess-frequency

### Mercy's Psyche Pool Interaction

**Arcana's Virtue mastery affects Psyche drain:**
- Tier 0 casting drains 2-3 Psyche/hour (inefficient)
- Tier 1 casting drains 1-2 Psyche/hour (effective)
- Tier 2 casting drains 1 Psyche/hour (efficient)
- Tier 3 casting drains 0-1 Psyche/hour (masterful; minimal drain)

**This means:** As Arcana levels up, her priestess-work becomes *less draining* on Mercy, allowing for longer, deeper sessions.

### Mercy's Seduction Resistance

**Arcana's Tier progression affects Void-resistance:**
- Tier 0: Void can easily seduce Mercy (Arcana's priestess-bond is weak)
- Tier 1: Void's pull is noticeable but Arcana's bond is stronger (Mercy torn between two calls)
- Tier 2: Arcana's priestess-frequency is strong enough to compete with Void (contested)
- Tier 3: Arcana's priestess-frequency is *powerful enough to override* Void's pull (if she commits fully)

**This creates stakes:** Arcana must level up fast enough to keep Mercy anchored as Void seduces her. If Arcana falls behind in Tier progression, she loses Mercy to shadow.
