# CATHEDRAL OF ETERNAL GRACE: REFRAMED
## Arcana as Visiting Priestess, Not Arriving Authority

**Core Shift:** Arcana doesn't arrive to *lead* or *reform* the Cathedral. She arrives as a **visiting priestess-scholar**, invited to study and teach alongside resident sisters. She's learning their techniques, comparing methodologies with other priestesses, building friendships and rivalries, while exploring a new spiritual landscape. The Void emerges as background mystery, hints dropping as she ventures into unexplored zones.

**Tier 1 Campaign Feel:** Like Harry Potter entering Hogwarts — new world, specialized professors, other students, discovery through exploration, mystery woven into fabric rather than front-stage.

## PART ONE: THE EIGHT DOMINAE AS VIRTUE SPECIALISTS

Each Domina specializes in ONE Virtue (not two). Each is like a Hogwarts professor: expert teacher, distinct personality, unique relationship to Arcana and other sisters.

### **DOMINA SERAPHINE — DISCIPLINE (Obedience as Grace)**

**Role:** Master of Ponyplay; Dressage Instructor  
**Age:** 58 | **Appearance:** Silver-haired, military bearing, scar on jawline  
**Teaching Style:** Precision, repetition, perfectionism; creates beautiful form through disciplined practice  
**Personality:** Stern but fair; respects competence; cold to those who don't commit

**Her Specialty:** Ponyplay transformation through *discipline as liberation*. Her students learn that perfect posture, perfect movement, perfect obedience create transcendent grace. She teaches that the body's mastery is the path to priestess-consciousness.

**Relationship to Arcana:**
- Initially evaluates Arcana's technique critically
- Respects Arcana's priestess-authority but tests her method
- Over time: Becomes mentor-ally if Arcana demonstrates commitment to precision
- Potential rivalry: If Arcana's style is too free/unstructured for Seraphine's taste

**Signature Lesson:** The Dressage Yard session where she teaches Arcana how to read Mercy's posture for micro-adjustments; teaches precision as priestess-language.

## PART TWO: ARCANA'S ARRIVAL & INTEGRATION (S62-S70)

### S62: THE ARRIVAL & BASELINE LOCK

**Setting:** Arcana and Mercy arrive at Cathedral gates; greeted by Elara (Supreme Administrator) and the Eight Dominae

**Scene Structure:**
- Formal welcome by all eight Dominae (each assessing Arcana; each offering specialty)
- Baseline Lock ceremony (conducted by all eight together, not single conductor)
- Arcana presented with Cathedral robes, quarters, access-levels

**Tone:** This is *arrival as peer*, not *arrival as authority*. Dominae are welcoming but watching. Arcana must *earn* their respect through demonstrated technique and commitment.

**First Void Hint:** During ceremony, strange echo in Echo Chamber (Thalindra notices it; looks unsettled). Nothing dramatic — just atmospheric disturbance that might mean nothing.

- Morning: Virtue-school sessions with Dominae

- Afternoon: Exploration, study, or specialized training

- Mercy starts at 16-18 points
- Heavy priestess-work drains 1-2 per hour

- Each priestess has relationship meter (Neutral → Friend → Ally → Confidante)
- Different priestesses respond to different approaches
- Relationships unlock different information and support
- Rivalry with some priestesses creates dynamic tension

### Investigation Mechanics
- Clues discovered through exploration and relationship-building
- Void-hints drop throughout weeks; Arcana pieces them together
- Multiple investigation threads: missing acolytes, strange behavior, magical interference, corrupted Domina
