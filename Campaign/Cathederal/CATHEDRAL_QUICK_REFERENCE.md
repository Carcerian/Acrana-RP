## THE THREE FACTIONS

### **TRADITIONALISTS (Keepers of Ancient Doctrine)**
- **Leaders:** Seraphine (military), Scholar Yvenne (intellectual)
- **Goal:** Preserve oldest priestess-forms unchanged
- **Method:** Bureaucratic obstruction, knowledge-hoarding, surveillance
- **Suspicious of:** Arcana (outside authority), Mercy (disturbance to order)
- **Strength:** Control of oldest archives
- **Weakness:** Regressive, resented by younger acolytes

### **REFORMERS (Architects of Progress)**
- **Leaders:** Lucienne (charismatic), Sophia Arachne (operative)
- **Goal:** Modernize priestess-craft, share knowledge
- **Method:** Persuasion, charm, strategic alliances, intel-network
- **Friendly to:** Arcana (sees her as change-catalyst)
- **Strength:** Appeal to youth, external contacts
- **Weakness:** Morally compromised (seduction as coercion), Sophia's methods contradict Honesty-virtue

### **KEEPERS (Seekers of Honest Path)**
- **Leaders:** Domain Prior Aldric (administrator), Watcher-Sister Maren (investigator)
- **Goal:** Expose corruption in both factions, create accountability
- **Method:** Documentation, investigation, consensus-building
- **Neutral to:** Arcana (invite her to observe; not aggressive recruit)
- **Strength:** Moral high-ground, access to administrative data
- **Weakness:** Smallest faction, perceived as fence-sitters

## THE VOID (Acts III-IV Endgame)

**What It Is:** Ancient priestess-entity (consciousness, not person); awoken by Mercy's arrival  
**Exists In:** Deep beneath Cathedral; can manifest through dreams, visions, possession  
**Communicates Via:** Transcendence-work, Echo Chamber, synchronized nightmares  
**Goals:** Reclaim Cathedral, rewrite priestess-craft's purpose, claim Mercy as Void-kin  
**Current Activity (Acts I-II):** Subtle awakening; hints only; communing with Elestren and possibly Thalindra

**Acts I-II Hints:**
- Echo Chamber echoes sound *wrong* (extra layer)
- Statue moves overnight (impossible)
- Acolytes have synchronized nightmares
- Certain priests notice "old presence stirring"
- Mira/Desta study things they weren't taught

**Acts III-IV:** Void becomes central antagonist; Mercy becomes contested prize (Arcana vs. Void)

## MECHANICS REMINDER

**Spell Resolution (d20):**
- Arcana's MAB: d20 + Cha mod + Spell level
- Mercy's MAC: 10 + Int mod (typically 10-12)
- Success: MAB ≥ MAC
- Psyche Cost: Varies by intensity (Tier 1-2: 1-2/hr; Tier 5: 9-15/hr)

**Baseline-Locked State:**
- Mercy's consciousness now *native* to beast-form
- She doesn't "transform into" form; she *exists* in form as fundamental state
- Switching back to humanoid possible but *not* her preferred reality
- Priestess-command now reinforced by nervous-system integration (physical reality, not just will)
