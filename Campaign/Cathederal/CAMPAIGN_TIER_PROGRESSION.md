# ARCANA RP CAMPAIGN PROGRESSION
## Tier Alignment: D&D-Style Scaling (1-36)

---

## OVERVIEW

**Campaign Structure:**
- **Acts I-II (Sessions 62-90+):** EXPERT TIER (Levels 4-14)
- **Acts III-IV (Sessions 91+):** COMPANION TIER (Levels 15-25) 
- **Polymath Spirituality Path:** MASTER TIER (Levels 26-36)

Each tier represents escalation in **threat scale, authority scope, and spiritual power**.

---

## ACT I-II: EXPERT TIER (Regional Scale)

### Threat Scale
**Scope:** Local to regional (Cathedral + surrounding 10-mile radius)  
**Focus:** Political intrigue, factional conflict, local mysteries  
**Power Level:** Priestess-craft as regional authority system

### Mechanical Features
- **Virtue Schools:** Tiers 1-3 mastery (foundational priestess-craft)
- **Mercy's Baseline:** Firmly integrated; consciousness permanently beast-form
- **Gem Enchantments:** Color-coded basic enchantments (fire, water, earth, light, shadow, spirit, life)
- **Psyche Pool:** 16-18 points; recovered through downtime between sessions
- **Combat Scope:** Local threats (corrupted acolytes, factional assassins, bestial predators)

### Campaign Location
**THE CATHEDRAL OF ETERNAL GRACE**
- Priestess-Craft's foundational teaching institution
- Regional hub for priestess-authority
- Political battleground for three competing visions

### Key Plot Threads (Acts I-II)

**Thread 1: Void's Awakening (Hidden Layer)**
- Mercy's arrival *triggers* Void's consciousness
- Subtle hints: Echo Chamber disturbances, synchronized nightmares, mysterious disappearances
- Scope: Void stirring beneath Cathedral; not yet manifest in world
- Resolution: Acts II-III transition (Void becomes visible)

**Thread 2: Factional Conflict (Surface Layer)**
- Three factions (Traditionalists, Reformers, Keepers) compete for authority
- Arcana must navigate without being co-opted
- Investigation: Missing acolytes, strange rituals, archive discrepancies
- Scope: Internal Cathedral politics
- Resolution: Acts II-III (factions realize they're being manipulated)

**Thread 3: Mercy's Integration (Personal Layer)**
- Baseline-locked, but still vulnerable to influence
- Void specifically calls to her (she's Void-kin)
- Arcana must prove her priestess-claim stronger than Void's ancient call
- Scope: Intimate priestess-mount relationship
- Resolution: Acts III-IV (contested for Mercy's loyalty)

### Expected Progression
- **S62:** Baseline Lock Ceremony (arrival, integration)
- **S63-S70:** First weeks of training, introduction to factions and NPCs
- **S71-S80:** Investigation into disappearances; locales exploration
- **S81-S90:** Void-hints accumulate; factional tensions escalate
- **S91 (Acts II-III Transition):** Confrontation with Void-presence in Echo Chamber

### End-of-Act-II State
- Mercy is secured (baseline-locked, priestess-devoted, but aware of Void-call)
- Cathedral factions revealed in full corruption/compromise
- Void is *visible* but not yet fully manifest
- Arcana is established as priestess-authority within Cathedral
- Stepping stone set for next tier

## ACTS III-IV: COMPANION TIER (National/Dominion Scale)

### Threat Scale
**Scope:** Multi-regional to national (Void as transdimensional entity; priestess-craft as threatened religion)  
**Focus:** Void's true nature revealed; Arcana building priestess-dominion; creating allied priestess-authority  
**Power Level:** Priestess-craft as organized religious/military power; Arcana as founding figure

### Mechanical Features
- **Virtue Schools:** Tiers 4-5 mastery (transcendent priestess-craft; consciousness-alteration, permanent states)
- **Mercy's Transcendence:** Can now commune with Void directly; must choose loyalty
- **Gem Enchantments:** Advanced enchantments (color-combinations, resonance-effects, artifact-potential)
- **Psyche Pool:** 18-24 points; deep recovery rituals required after intense work
- **Combat Scope:** Regional battles (Void-corrupted cults, Void-beasts, planar incursions if applicable)

### Campaign Locations
**Cathedral** (reclaimed and restructured under Arcana's vision)  
**Mercy's Church** (new priestess-installation; Arcana's authority-seat)  
**Allied Priestess-Settlements** (network of followers, disciples, allied cults)  
**Void-Touched Sites** (corrupted locations requiring spiritual cleansing)

### Key Plot Threads (Acts III-IV)

**Thread 1: Void's True Revelation**
- Void is ancient priestess-entity (possibly first priestess, possibly pre-dating priestess-craft)
- It's not "evil"; it operates at cosmic scale where individual suffering is irrelevant
- Offers Mercy transcendence beyond even Tier 5 (dissolution into priestess-frequency itself)
- Scope: Transdimensional entity threatening priestess-craft's foundational assumptions
- Resolution: Arcana must convince Mercy that *she* (Arcana) offers communion equal to or deeper than Void

**Thread 2: The Apprentice Revealed**
- Void's priestess-contact steps into open
- Not antagonist; rather, sincere spiritual seeker who discovered something profound
- Arcana must win her allegiance (or at least neutrality) or face knowledgeable enemy
- Scope: Moral complexity; not simple good-vs-evil
- Resolution: Apprentice chooses between Void, Arcana, or independent path

**Thread 3: Cathedral's Restructuring**
- Arcana can't control Cathedral (too entrenched, too factional)
- She *builds her own priestess-authority* (Church of Mercy or Temple of Eight Virtues or similar)
- Cathedral becomes rival institution rather than home-base
- Scope: Creating new priestess-dominion; institutional authority
- Resolution: Arcana as founder-priestess; Mercy as High Priestess (if she chooses Arcana)

**Thread 4: The Void's Offer & The Choice**
- Void offers Mercy *true transcendence* — dissolution into priestess-frequency as eternal state
- Arcana offers Mercy *mutual priestess-communion* — deep bond but retaining individual consciousness
- Central conflict: Void vs. Arcana for Mercy's eternal allegiance
- Scope: Intimate spiritual stakes; determining Mercy's eternal nature
- Resolution: Mercy's choice determines campaign's spiritual resolution

### Mechanics: The Void Confrontation

**The Void as Opponent:**
- Not battled through combat; rather, through spiritual/consciousness contest
- Uses Tier 5 priestess-spells (consciousness-alteration, dissolution, transcendence-forcing)
- Mercy's resistance is key (she must actively choose Arcana; can't be compelled)
- Environmental effects: Void can manifest through crypts, Echo Chamber, hunting grounds, stones themselves

**The Void's Vulnerability:**
- Exists across multiple temporal layers simultaneously
- If Arcana can "anchor" Mercy in *present moment*, Void loses hold
- Void's ancient knowledge can be turned against it (Seraphine's forbidden texts become crucial)
- Void depends on Mercy answering its call; if Mercy chooses Arcana, Void's power is weakened

**The Ritual Combat:**
- High-Tier priestess-magic vs. consciousness-itself
- Psyche Pool becomes central mechanic (Mercy's mental endurance is literally the battlefield)
- Multiple rounds as Void tests Mercy's loyalty through trials and temptations
- Arcana must land increasingly powerful spells to keep Mercy anchored

### Expected Progression
- **S91-S100:** Void fully manifest; direct confrontation in Echo Chamber
- **S101-S110:** Arcana's investigation into Void's true nature; gathering allies
- **S111-S120:** Building priestess-authority independent of Cathedral; recruiting disciples
- **S121-S130:** Void-touched site reclamation (cleansing corrupted locations)
- **S131-S140:** Final Void-confrontation ritual; determining Mercy's eternal allegiance

### End-of-Act-IV State
- Void either destroyed, sealed, or transformed (depending on choices)
- Mercy is permanently consecrated to Arcana OR lost to Void OR independent (based on her choice)
- Arcana has established priestess-dominion (Church/Temple/Installation)
- Cathedral remains as rival or ally institution
- Campaign transitions to Master Tier

## POLYMATH SPIRITUALITY PATH: MASTER TIER (Divine Scale)

### Threat Scale
**Scope:** Planar scale (other priestess-traditions, divine entities, artifact creation, ascension pathways)  
**Focus:** Arcana's divine ascension; exalting acolytes; creating immortal priestess-lineage  
**Power Level:** Priestess-craft as divine institution; Arcana as potential immortal founder-deity

### Mechanical Features
- **Virtue Schools:** Tiers 5+ transcendence (rewriting fundamental aspects of reality/consciousness)
- **Gem Enchantments:** Artifact-level (legendary enchantments; gems become conduits for divine power)
- **Exalted Acolytes:** Mercy and chosen disciples become semi-divine beings (immortal, capable of independent priestess-manifestation)
- **Divine Fragments:** Arcana can invest aspects of her divinity into chosen acolytes (creating perpetual priestess-conduits)
- **Combat Scope:** Planar battles; encounters with other divine/immortal beings

### Campaign Locations
**Arcana's Church** (priestess-dominion capital; seat of divine authority)  
**Exalted Sites** (where acolytes achieve immortality)  
**Other Priestess-Traditions** (contact/competition with external divine systems)  
**The Void (Transcendent)** — If Void was allied/transformed rather than destroyed, it becomes accessible as sacred space

### Key Plot Threads (Master Tier)

**Thread 1: Divine Ascension**
- Arcana's path to potential immortality/divinity
- Requires exalting a priestess-lineage (creating disciples who can perpetuate priestess-craft infinitely)
- Scope: Personal transformation; becoming divine founder-priestess
- Milestone: First exalted acolyte (likely Mercy) achieves immortality

**Thread 2: The Immortal Priestess-Lineage**
- Arcana creates circle of exalted disciples (each invested with divine fragments)
- These disciples become perpetual priestess-voices in the world
- Scope: Institutional immortality; priestess-craft becomes eternal tradition
- Milestone: Three+ exalted disciples active simultaneously

**Thread 3: The Artifact Ascension**
- Gem-enchantments become legendary artifacts
- Colored gems resonate with cosmic frequencies
- Scope: Creating tools of divine power; priestess-craft made manifest in physical reality
- Milestone: Legendary artifact (possibly a collar or rope) becomes conduit for priestess-divinity

**Thread 4: Competing Divine Traditions**
- Contact/conflict with other priestess-traditions (or similar spiritual systems)
- Arcana's way (Eight Virtue Schools) vs. other ways (other priestess-lineages, divine cults)
- Scope: Priestess-craft as contender in broader divine ecology
- Milestone: Formal recognition from external priestess-authorities

### End-of-Master-Tier State
- Arcana is established as immortal/divine priestess-founder
- Priestess-tradition is perpetual (exalted disciples ensure continuity)
- Mercy is co-divine (if she chose Arcana) or equivalent rival (if she chose Void)
- Gem-artifacts are legendary; priestess-craft made manifest in world
- Campaign concludes or transitions to "mythic" scale (planar conflicts, divine councils, etc.)

## COMPANION RULES FRAMEWORK (Acts III-IV Transition)

When Arcana transitions from "Cathedral priestess" to "Priestess-Founder," mechanics change:

### Church-Building Rules
- Arcana establishes independent priestess-authority (not subservient to Cathedral)
- Disciples receive formal training in Eight Virtue Schools
- Reputation spreads; other priestesses seek her out
- Political power shifts from Cathedral factions to Arcana's growing movement

### Exalted Disciple Mechanics
**What "Exalted" Means:**
- Chosen acolyte receives permanent mystical mark (collar becomes legendary artifact)
- Consciousness partially merges with priestess-frequency (can channel Arcana's power independently)
- Becomes semi-immortal (aging slowed; regeneration possible)
- Can perform priestess-rites independently (create disciples, conduct ceremonies)

**Example: Mercy as High Priestess**
- If Mercy chooses Arcana, she becomes priestess-co-founder
- Her collar becomes legendary artifact (gem-enchanted to resonance-level)
- She can teach Eight Virtue Schools independently
- She establishes sub-churches or satellite temples
- Her disciples are Arcana's disciples (extended priestess-lineage)

### Divine Fragment Investment
- Arcana slowly invest aspects of her divinity into exalted disciples
- Each fragment makes disciple more independent but also ties them to Arcana eternally
- Creates "divine relay" — priestess-power flows through lineage
- Limits: Arcana can only maintain X exalted disciples (likely 3-7 before requiring major ritual)

## CLOSING NOTES

**This scaling ensures:**
1. **Paced Growth:** Expert tier teaches priestess-craft mastery; Companion tier creates priestess-dominion; Master tier establishes divine lineage
2. **Mercy's Arc:** From mount → contested priestess → co-divine or independent rival (complete character journey)
3. **Threat Escalation:** Local politics → regional mystical threat → planar divine conflict
4. **Institutional Growth:** Cathedral → Church → Divine Institution (Arcana's authority literally becomes "world-scale")
5. **Mechanical Scaling:** Gem-enchantments evolve from functional tools → legendary artifacts; Virtue Schools from teaching framework → divine ascension pathway

**By Campaign's End:** Arcana has achieved what few mortals manage — she's become a divine founder-priestess, established an eternal tradition, and created immortal disciples who will perpetuate priestess-craft across planes and centuries.

The polymath spirituality path isn't just personal ascension; it's institutional immortality.
