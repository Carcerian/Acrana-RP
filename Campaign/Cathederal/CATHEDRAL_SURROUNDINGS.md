# THE CATHEDRAL SURROUNDINGS
## Natural Features, Locales, Shrines & Ruins

**Scale:** The Cathedral sits in a contained valley system approximately 15 miles across at widest point, 40 miles north-to-south. Accessible by several mountain passes. The valley floor is fertile; the surrounding peaks are dramatic and often snow-capped. Multiple natural water features (springs, streams, underground rivers) feed the valley ecosystem.

---

## PART 1: NATURAL FEATURES & GEOGRAPHY

### THE VALLEY STRUCTURE

**The Valley Itself:**
- Central floor: Approximately 2,000 feet elevation; fertile, well-watered, green year-round
- North Wall: Dramatic cliff faces (2,000-3,000 ft rise); harsh, exposed; winds constantly
- South Wall: More gradual, rolling highlands; forested upper slopes; alpine meadows
- East: Natural pass (The Sunrise Gate); primary travel route; well-maintained; merchant caravans use regularly
- West: Narrow canyon pass (The Dusk Ravine); treacherous in winter; used by locals who know the way; feels haunted

**The Central Water System:**
- **The Mercy River:** Primary river flowing south-to-north through valley center; fed by multiple springs; name is coincidental (predates current campaign); clean, cold, fast-moving; used for rituals by Cathedral priestesses
- **The Sacred Springs:** Three natural hot springs along the southern valley wall; mineral-rich; one is public (pilgrims bathe there), one is reserved for priestess-training, one is forbidden (ancient, untouched)
- **The Echo Wells:** Series of deep underground wells scattered throughout valley; some visible as stone rings, some hidden; water echoes strangely when dropped into them; natural resonance creates harmonic frequencies
- **The Broken Sluice:** Ancient aqueduct system (built centuries ago, purpose forgotten) that was designed to channel water from the Sacred Springs northward. Now runs backward; water defies gravity; humming sound; Void-hint

### GEOLOGICAL FEATURES

**The Twin Peaks (North):**
- Two sharply pointed mountains forming the northern boundary
- Visible from everywhere in the valley
- Snow-capped year-round
- Accessible by skilled climbers; routes are known to local herders
- Rumored to be where the *first* imprisonment ceremony took place (temporal echo location?)

**The Spiral Caverns (Southwest):**
- Vast system of limestone caverns running beneath the southern valley wall
- Multiple entrances; some obvious (natural cave mouths), some hidden
- Underground rivers, lakes, mineral formations
- Contains the Sunken Wellspring (see locales section)
- Also rumored to contain other cavern systems (pre-Cathedral origin; purpose unknown)

**The High Stone Redoubt (East/Northeast):**
- Sheer cliff face with natural fortress-like formations
- Ancient fortification built into the cliff face (not the Google Domina's "retirement home," but rather a genuine defensive structure dating to Cathedral's founding)
- Accessible by hidden stair carved into cliff; barely wide enough for two people
- Houses the Archive of the First Keepers (temporal records? living ghosts of previous Arcanas?)

**The Primal Grounds (Northwest):**
- Deep forest; old-growth trees; primordial feeling
- Natural meadows for hunts
- Home to wild game (deer, boar, occasionally larger predators)
- Sacred hunting grounds for Nature-Virtue priestesses
- Feels genuinely untamed; humans are *guests* here, not rulers

**The Barren Scree (West):**
- Landscape of loose stone, sparse vegetation, exposed earth
- Result of ancient landslide or geological upheaval
- Feels unsettling; echoes carry strangely
- Void-presence strongest here (dreams more vivid for those who sleep on the Scree)
- Used for solo spiritual trials and vision quests

## PART 2: THE FIVE MAJOR LOCALES

### LOCALE 1: THE SACRED SPRINGS (South Valley Wall, 4 miles from Cathedral)

**Geography:** Three natural hot springs emerging from the southern cliff face, each fed by underground thermal sources. Mineral content varies; waters range from clear to rust-colored to milky white.

**The Three Springs:**

**Spring #1 — The Pilgrim's Fountain (Public)**
- Accessible by maintained stone stairs; temperature ~110°F (hot but survivable)
- Large stone pool carved by centuries of use; rough, worn edges
- Water is sulfurous but healing; pilgrims believe it cures ailments
- Staffed by two low-level acolytes who maintain order and collect tithes
- Deeply public; pilgrims, locals, merchants all bathe here
- Generates rumors, gossip, information flow (excellent intelligence-gathering location)
- Void-hint: Some bathers report dreams of dissolving into the water, becoming part of something vast and ancient

**Spring #2 — The Priestess-Training Pool (Restricted)**
- Accessible only to Cathedral priestesses and their mounts
- Secluded; hidden from public view by rock formations
- Temperature ~105°F; water is clearer, mineral-composition more refined
- Used for:
  - Transcendence training (deep meditation in warm water)
  - Bonding rituals (priestess + mount synchronizing breath underwater)
  - Physical recovery (Psyche regeneration; wounds heal faster)
- Staffed by a single elder priestess (Keeper of the Spring) who maintains protocols
- Void-hint: Water occasionally swirls counterclockwise when no one is stirring it; temperature fluctuates without reason

**Spring #3 — The First Source (Forbidden)**
- Accessible only to the highest Dominae and Keepers of the Cathedral
- Hidden behind waterfall curtain; requires knowledge of specific path
- Temperature ~115°F; water is perfectly clear; mineral content creates faint luminescence at night
- *This is where temporal echoes are strongest*
- Rumored to be where the First Imprisonment ritual took place
- Entering this spring without authorization is highest heresy
- Void-hint: The water *remembers*; those who immerse hear voices from other times

**NPCs at Sacred Springs:**
- **Sister Merida (Keeper of the Pilgrim's Fountain):** Low-rank acolyte; gossipy; knows everything happening in the valley; information broker
- **Elder Priestess Thorne (Keeper of the Priestess Pool):** Stern, professional, protective of the space; knows the protocols by heart; resistant to Void-seduction (protected by long experience)

**Encounters & Hooks:**
- Pilgrims reporting strange dreams at Pilgrim's Fountain (Void-whispers reaching public)
- A prominent merchant's daughter becomes ill after bathing; symptoms match "chronological sickness" (temporal anomaly)
- Arcana's first joint bathing with a Sister Priestess (bonding moment; intimate without being sexual)
- Sura (rebel priestess) hiding near the springs to investigate the water's anomalies

### LOCALE 2: THE ROPE GARDENS (Southeast Valley Floor, 3 miles from Cathedral)

**Geography:** Sprawling terraced gardens built into the gentle southeastern slope of the valley. Originally designed for growing specific plants used in rope-making (hemp, flax, silk-like plant fibers). Over centuries, evolved into a unique hybrid space: part agriculture, part training ground, part meditation garden.

**The Layout:**
- Multiple terraced levels connected by stone stairs and winding paths
- Each terrace specializes in different fiber plants and different rope-training focuses
- Lowest terrace: Working gardens (plants actively harvested)
- Middle terraces: Training areas (exposed; where basic rope-work is taught)
- Upper terraces: Meditation gardens (private; intimate; where advanced rope-work becomes art)
- Summit platform: The Rope Shrine (tiny stone structure; purpose ambiguous)

**The Gardens by Function:**

**The Lower Gardens (Working & Basic Training):**
- Hemp fields (rough, strong fibers)
- Flax patches (finer, softer)
- Drying racks for processed fibers
- Rope-braiding workshops (open-air; communal)
- Young priestesses practice basic rope-work here; loud, social, energetic
- Mercy-type mounts familiarize with rope sensations in low-stakes environment

**The Middle Gardens (Intermediate Training):**
- Suspension frames (permanent wooden structures for practicing ties)
- Smaller, more private terraces for one-on-one training
- Sister Renna spends significant time here perfecting techniques
- Rope-work becomes more technically complex; aesthetics start to matter
- Intimate but not isolated; other priestesses nearby

**The Upper Gardens (Advanced Artistry):**
- Scattered stone benches and platforms
- No equipment; just priestess and rope and landscape
- Used for spiritual rope-work (rope as meditation, not control)
- Sister Yora paints here sometimes; the ropes become art
- Intensely private; priestess must request permission to enter
- Temporal echoes faint here (beautiful, peaceful, temporally stable)

**The Rope Shrine (Summit):**
- Tiny circular stone structure; no roof; open to sky
- Contains:
  - Stone altar (unmarked)
  - Collection of ancient ropes (frayed, preserved; clearly centuries old)
  - No religious iconography; clearly predates current Cathedral structure
- Purpose unknown; possibly related to First Imprisonment? Possibly temporal anchor?
- Void-hint: Ropes here sometimes move without wind; pattern suggests they're trying to spell something

**NPCs at Rope Gardens:**
- **Master Cordwainer Thom:** Elderly man who maintains the gardens and teaches basic rope-work; neutral; knows everything about rope history; resistant to faction politics
- **Sister Renna (Resident Competitor):** Spends hours here perfecting techniques; might befriend or challenge Arcana
- **The Garden Keeper (Unknown):** Mysterious figure who tends the Shrine; rarely seen; appears silently; no one knows their name or faction

**Encounters & Hooks:**
- Arcana's first rope-training session (learning Precision Virtue)
- Competitive rope-work demonstration with Sister Renna (rivalry or respect-building)
- Discovery that ancient ropes in Shrine are moving/communicating
- Temporal echo: Arcana touches ancient rope, briefly sees a previous Keeper teaching rope-work centuries ago
- Hidden message in rope-patterns that only becomes visible from specific angle/time of day

### LOCALE 3: THE SHRINE OF SILENCE (Deep Forest, North/Northwest, 7 miles from Cathedral)

**Geography:** Deep within the Primal Grounds; old-growth forest; natural clearing approximately 100 yards across. Contains small stone shrine; predates Cathedral by unknown time period. Accessible only by following barely-marked forest paths; feels deliberately hidden.

**The Shrine Structure:**
- Stone platform/foundation (weathered; possibly 500+ years old)
- Small circular building (20 ft diameter); stone walls; no door; open on all sides
- Interior: Single stone bench facing north; nothing else
- Exterior: Stone ring around the building (boundary marker?)
- Forest completely encircles the shrine; trees press close; creates sense of enclosure despite openness

**Atmosphere:**
- Profoundly quiet; bird sounds muted; wind barely audible
- Sound dampening effect (unclear if magical or acoustic)
- Entering the shrine feels like entering a different space entirely
- Time feels *different* here; hard to judge how long you've been inside
- Spiritual priestesses (Yora, Elain) feel immediate spiritual presence; non-spiritual priestesses feel unease

**Purpose (Unknown):**
- Not clearly dedicated to any deity or virtue
- No text, inscriptions, or identifying markers
- Rumored to be site of:
  - Original priestess-craft teaching (pre-Cathedral)
  - First Keeper's meditation place
  - A temporal anchor (one of the four Cathedral moments?)
  - Something much older than priestess-craft entirely

**NPCs at Shrine:**
- **None regularly stationed here** — the Shrine is *unguarded*, which is unusual for something this old
- Occasionally priestesses visit alone for meditation
- Rarely, non-Cathedral seekers are found here (hermits, refugees, people lost in the forest)

**Encounters & Hooks:**
- Arcana first visits with Sister Yora (bonding experience; Yora's spiritual sensitivity shines)
- Discovering that time passes *differently* in the Shrine (an hour feels like 10 minutes, or vice versa)
- Temporal echo: Arcana sits on bench, briefly experiences memory of *another Arcana* sitting in same spot (different era)
- Finding evidence that someone *else* has been meditating here recently (scuff marks, disturbed moss, fresh offerings)
- The Void's presence is *weakest* here; priestesses being seduced report dreams *stop* temporarily when they visit
- A previous Keeper's ghost appears (only visible to Tier 2+ Transcendence priestesses)

### LOCALE 4: THE DEEP FOREST HEART (Northwest Primal Grounds, 10+ miles from Cathedral)

**Geography:** The deepest, most untamed part of the Primal Grounds. Old-growth forest so dense that sunlight barely reaches ground level. Natural clearings are rare but magical when found. Home to herds of wild deer, boar, occasionally dangerous predators (wolves, bears). No paths; navigation requires skill or local knowledge.

**Features:**

**The Hunter's Clearings (Multiple):**
- Several natural meadows where Sister Tam leads hunts
- Clearing #1: "The Stag's Run" — open meadow; good sightlines; deep grass; herds gather at dawn/dusk
- Clearing #2: "The Wounded Thicket" — dense but navigable; ambush-friendly; where wounded game retreats
- Clearing #3: "The Elder Stone" — ancient massive stone at center; unclear origin; used as gathering point for larger hunts

**The Primal Shrine (Deep Forest, Unmarked):**
- Different from Shrine of Silence; this one is *alive* with energy
- Constructed from branches, bone, stone in organic arrangement (not architectural; more like nest)
- Offerings scattered: fur, feathers, bones, tokens left by hunters over decades
- Dedicated to something *primal* (not priestess-craft; older)
- Strongly Nature-Virtue location; priestesses feel their animal-instincts heightened here

**The Bone Cairn (Forest Deep):**
- Massive pile of weathered bones (unclear what animal or how old)
- Arranged in deliberate spiral pattern
- Surrounded by natural circle of bare earth (no vegetation grows)
- Void-hint: The bones sometimes *arrange themselves* differently than they were left; pattern changes based on who's watching

**The Memory Trees (Scattered Throughout):**
- Ancient, massive trees (300+ years old) with hollow interiors
- Carved into hollows: Names, dates, symbols (centuries of etchings)
- Some carvings are legible (dating back 200+ years); oldest ones are worn nearly smooth
- Priestesses and mounts leave their own marks here as ritual
- Temporal echo: Touching the oldest carvings allows brief flash of memory from that era

**NPCs in Deep Forest:**
- **Sister Tam (Resident Hunt-Master):** Often here; knows the forest intimately; guides hunts
- **Scout (Tam's Mount):** Beast-form; native to this forest; guides priestesses without words
- **The Hermit (Possible):** Rumor of a lone hunter who lives deep in the forest; has been there longer than anyone remembers; sightings rare

**Encounters & Hooks:**
- First hunt with Sister Tam (bonding; Nature Virtue training; Mercy learns primal joy)
- Discovery of unmarked shrine (nature-worship predating Cathedral?)
- Witnessing the bones rearrange themselves (Void-hint or temporal anomaly?)
- Finding carved names that match previous Arcanas (historical confirmation of earlier iterations)
- Encounter with dangerous animal (test of courage; bonding moment with Mercy)
- Temporal echo: Touching memory trees allows brief communion with a previous Keeper who carved their name

### LOCALE 5: THE MARKET QUARTER (Valley Floor, Near Eastern Pass, 2 miles from Cathedral)

**Geography:** Semi-permanent settlement at the base of the Eastern Pass (primary travel route). Dozens of merchant stalls, temporary structures, pilgrim hostels, taverns. Active most days; becomes a fair several times yearly when pilgrim traffic peaks. Secular, chaotic, vital information hub.

**The Layout:**
- Central Market Square (large plaza; permanent stone stalls for regular merchants)
- Pilgrim Hostels (6-8 large buildings; basic accommodations; constant turnover)
- Merchant's Row (semi-permanent structures; cloth, food, livestock, trade goods)
- The Tavern Quarter (multiple drinking establishments; social hub; rumor central)
- The Black Market (hidden; behind legitimate merchants; restricted goods)
- The Pass Station (inspection point; pilgrims checked before entering Cathedral grounds)

**Key Establishments:**

**The Wanderer's Rest (Tavern):**
- Largest, most established tavern; frequented by merchants, guards, acolytes, local workers
- Proprietor: Old Mag (sharp-eyed woman who hears everything)
- Information source: Rumors, news from outside the valley, gossip about Cathedral factions
- Neutral ground; all factions tolerate its existence because information flows here
- Void-hint: Travelers report strange dreams at the Wanderer's Rest; beds are haunted; water runs cold

**The Pilgrim's Hostel (Primary):**
- Managed by a low-level Cathedral priest (Brother Aldwyn)
- Cheap, clean, basic: rows of beds, communal dining, scheduled prayers
- Pilgrims stay here; cheap labor pool; source of fresh information from outside
- Void-hint: Pilgrims report dreams while staying here; some wake confused about how long they've been there

**The Stable Yards:**
- Multiple separate stables for merchant caravans, pilgrims' animals, pack beasts
- Stable Master: Kael (elderly man; knows every merchant's business)
- Source of practical information: Which merchants are reliable, which are moving goods illegally, who's hiring, who's fleeing
- Good place to acquire unusual supplies, hire guides, find people trying to disappear

**The Black Market (Hidden):**
- Operates behind legitimate merchant stalls; restricted access
- Managed by Velvet Concord sympathizers
- Sells: Restricted texts, forbidden materials, services that Cathedral officially prohibits
- Sister Lucilla (from Google's notes, but recontextualized) might have contacts here
- Source of black-market information; requires trust/reputation to access

**NPCs at Market Quarter:**
- **Old Mag (Tavern Keeper):** Neutral; hears all gossip; will trade information for coin or favors; knows Cathedral politics intimately but claims neutrality
- **Brother Aldwyn (Hostel Manager):** Low-rank priest; sympathetic but cautious; concerned about pilgrim welfare; potential ally
- **Kael (Stable Master):** Cynical; knows everyone's business; has connections to smugglers and merchants; possible information broker
- **The Black Market Merchant (Unknown Name):** Mysterious; operates from hidden stall; only appears irregularly; true allegiance unclear

**Encounters & Hooks:**
- Arcana first ventures to Market Quarter (culture shock; secular, chaotic, so different from Cathedral)
- Meeting travelers from outside the valley (different priestess-craft traditions? Or skeptics of Cathedral claims?)
- Discovering that a traveling merchant is actually a network operative (recruiter for Void-worshippers?)
- Finding wanted posters for heretics (previous priestesses who tried to flee? Previous Arcanas?)
- Overhearing conversations about "chronological sickness" spreading in the valley
- Sister Sura secretly meeting someone at Market Quarter (investigation opportunity)
- Temporal echo: An old merchant briefly recognizes Arcana, claims to have met "someone who looked just like you" decades ago

## PART 3: SCATTERED RUINS & MINOR SHRINES

### THE LESSER RUINS

**The Broken Tower (South Valley Wall, 6 miles from Cathedral):**
- Ruined stone tower; clearly ancient; purpose unknown
- Partially collapsed; interior is maze of rubble and stone
- Accessible but dangerous (unstable; climbing required)
- Local legends: Wizard's tower, ancient sentinel, site of first priestess-craft lesson
- Void-hint: Shadows inside the tower don't match light sources; as if they're shadows from a different time

**The Stone Circle (East Valley Floor, 8 miles from Cathedral):**
- Twelve large standing stones arranged in perfect circle; purpose unknown
- Predates Cathedral by unknown time; no marking, no inscription
- Local herders avoid it; pilgrims sometimes make offerings there
- Site of ancient rituals (unclear if priestess-craft or something older)
- Void-hint: The circle's shadow moves in wrong direction at sunset

**The Twin Tombs (West Valley, accessible only through Dusk Ravine):**
- Two large burial mounds; clearly centuries old; presumably contain bodies
- Never excavated; sealed entrances
- Local stories: First Priestesses buried here; or something older than priestess-craft
- Void-hint: Sounds of movement inside during certain nights; or mournful singing when no one is near

**The Forgotten Temple (North Valley Wall, 4 miles up the Twin Peaks trail):**
- Ruins of a building larger than Cathedral; clearly ancient; style predates Cathedral architecture
- Purpose unknown; completely abandoned; no one maintains it
- Accessible by skilled climbers or those with local knowledge
- Void-hint: Time moves differently inside the ruins; priestesses lose track of time; emerge having experienced hours that felt like minutes

### THE MINOR SHRINES

**The Wayfarer's Shrine (At Eastern Pass):**
- Small stone structure; shelter for travelers
- Purpose: Blessing travelers entering/leaving valley
- Maintained by pilgrim volunteers; no permanent staff
- Shrine contains: Stone altar, offering box, symbols of protection (unclear which tradition they represent)

**The Shepherd's Stone (High Meadows, South Slopes):**
- Single large stone in open meadow; unclear origin
- Herders use as landmark and shelter
- Rumored to have powers (safe from storms, animals, bad fortune)
- Void-hint: Herders report dreams at this stone; sometimes wake in different location than where they fell asleep

**The Water-Speaker's Pool (Near Broken Sluice):**
- Small pool where the backward-running water collects temporarily
- Locals claim the water "speaks" if you listen; warns of danger
- Hermits and seekers visit to receive prophecies
- Void-hint: The water's whispers are seductive; listeners sometimes disappear

**The Ancestor's Cairn (Deep Forest, Unmarked):**
- Simple stone pile; could be natural or constructed; unclear
- Local priestesses leave offerings; purpose ritualistic but specific tradition unknown
- Void-hint: Offerings left at dawn disappear by dusk, only to reappear days later elsewhere in the forest

## PART 4: THE TEMPORAL ANCHORS (HINTS)

As Arcana progresses and gains access to temporal echoes, she discovers that certain locations are **stronger anchors** to the four time-moments where the Cathedral exists:

**Primary Anchors (Strongest Echo Access):**
- The First Source (Sacred Springs #3) — Moment 1 (The Original Imprisonment)
- The Shrine of Silence — Moment 2 (Early Priestess-Craft Era)
- The Rope Shrine Summit (Rope Gardens) — Moment 3 (Recent Past)
- The High Stone Redoubt Archive — Moment 4 (Current Era / Future Branching)

**Secondary Anchors (Moderate Echo Access):**
- The Broken Tower
- The Stone Circle
- The Forgotten Temple
- The Bone Cairn

**Tertiary Anchors (Faint Echo Access):**
- Memory Trees (scattered)
- Any "old" location where priestesses have gathered for centuries

As Arcana's Virtue progression deepens, she gains ability to access these echoes more reliably and stay longer within them.

## PART 5: VOID-PRESENCE GEOGRAPHY

The Void's influence is *not* evenly distributed. Some locations are resistant to it; others amplify it:

**Void-Resistant Locations (where Seduction Stalls):**
- The Shrine of Silence (temporal stability resists shadow-corruption)
- The Rope Gardens Upper Terraces (spiritual aesthetics; Void can't find purchase)
- Memory Trees (past consciousness present; Void can't overwrite)
- The Primal Shrine (Nature-energy; predates Void)

**Void-Amplifying Locations (where Dreams Intensify):**
- The Barren Scree (exposed earth; Void-energy concentrates)
- The Broken Sluice (running backward; temporal disruption feeds Void)
- Deep cavern systems (darkness; isolation; Void whispers louder)
- The Water-Speaker's Pool (water conducts Void-presence)

**Neutral Locations (Void Present but Not Amplified):**
- Market Quarter (secular energy confuses Void-seduction)
- Rope Gardens Lower/Middle Terraces (active work distracts from Void)
- Sacred Springs Pilgrim's Fountain (public; Void can't isolate victims)
- Deep Forest Heart (Nature's presence conflicting; Void uncertain)
