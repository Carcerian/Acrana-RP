# THE BEACH HOME — Bastion Writeup
### Ambrosia Beach Guest House — Bastion of Loremistress Arcana
*Compiled July 5, 2026 — for the Carceria archives / Arcana's library*

---

## I. THE FRAMING

In proper D&D terms, Player Character **Arcana's** home base maps cleanly onto the classic structures:

- **Bastion:** The Ambrosia Beach Guest House — her residence, library, and base of operations
- **Cohort:** Sage, Bard 10 — the loyal follower gained through story rather than the Leadership feat, though she fits the 3E cohort math perfectly (a fixed-level companion roughly appropriate to a mid-level leader, gaining depth rather than levels)

The pairing is old-school correct for a game built on the 3E chassis: the adventurer, her stronghold, and her follower. Arcana simply acquired hers in the Sinfar way — the home was *given in welcome*, and the cohort *came with the luggage*.

---

## II. LOCATION

**Ambrosia Beach: Guest House** — Downstairs and Upstairs, sitting in the area list between the **Baths** and **Mermaid Cove**, in the Borough on Ambrosia Beach. Her address was on the map before she existed.

The surrounding geography is the Bastion's true wealth:
- **The beach itself** — the social hearth of the warren; picnic blankets, volleyball courts, umbrellas, fireworks on festival nights, and one proprietary seagull
- **The blessed falls** — singing in the near distance
- **The Baths** and **Mermaid Cove** — immediate neighbors
- **The Lore Tower** (Bardic College) — a short walk; Arcana's academic home
- **The tropical grotto** — fireside, bearskin, and the household's most treasured homecoming memories
- **The pool** — for the household's leisure hours

---

## III. PROVENANCE — A Bastion Given, Not Built

The Guest House belongs to **Saduj Thorne** — Chosen of Graz'zt, emperor with a palace he treats as merely *the office*. His true haunts, by his own accounting, are the social hearths: the open Guest House, Mercy's angel home, and the beach — the places with friends in them. He keeps the palace to rule from and the shore to *live* on.

The Guest House is therefore a bastion of a particular philosophy: **owned but thrown open** — because what is the joy of a lovely house if it's empty? Arcana holds residence not by rent or conquest but by *welcome*, which she recognized immediately as the only tenure worth having:

> *"The places worth reaching aren't throne-rooms a person schemes their way into. They're the warm, open, chosen spaces a person gets welcomed into. That's not a place you conquer your way to."*

**The handover:** Mercy played hostess with the keys — the beach bunny from Arcana's very first session (shared with Saduj and Cinder themselves) being the one to bring her home. The legends make an impression; the friendly one brings you home. Arcana arrived by summoning circle, violet glow underfoot, Sage trailing with luggage full of journals — and was invited to pick her chamber and shown the shared rooms for eating, studying, and relaxing.

## IV. THE FLOOR PLAN — Twin Paradises as Architecture

The Guest House expresses the warren's **Twin Paradises ethic as a floor plan**: common rooms where the household works and plays together; private chambers where each keeps their own.

**Downstairs — the shared hearth:**
- **The dining room** — where the household eats, and where guests are fed (bards sing for supper; the wise ones sing for seconds)
- **The study** — the room that derailed the original tour entirely; Arcana appraised it "the way a tenant does" while appreciating the rest as a guest. Sage's desk lives here; the archive grows here
- **The relaxing rooms** — the leisure spaces that make an open house worth opening

**Upstairs — the private chambers:**
- **Arcana's chamber** — chosen by the loremaster's single criterion: *where do the books go?* Morning light for reading, wall space for shelves, and a window facing the water — the room where the light lasts longest
- Additional chambers for the household and its guests

**Standing fixtures of note:**
- **The archive** — Arcana's journals, inks, and half-written notes, unpacked by Sage on arrival day and growing ever since; now including the Character Codex, the Fabulous Luck Dragon destiny, and the family genealogy in progress
- **The wardrobe flowers** — the 120-outfit wardrobe system, a permanent household feature
- **The lute wall** — where the first instrument went up on day one, since joined by the Iolo flamenco guitar and the Spellsinger's electric
- **Sage's post** — the armored scribe (white-and-gold, red accents, full helm) at parade rest; the archive's guardian as well as its copyist. *The quill and the shield, one job description.*

The domestic unit, fully assembled:

|---|---|---|
| **The Loremistress** | Arcana | Master of the house(hold); collector of stories, songs, and company |
| **The Cohort** | Sage (Bard 10) | Scribe, squire, archivist, guardian of the books |

Frequent orbit: the extended family constellation — Sable, Astraea, Pirouette, Silveny, the triplets' lines — plus College colleagues (Julia), guild connections (the Bunnies), and one seagull who has progressed from critic to houseguest to self-appointed chairman and shows every sign of annexing the porch next.

## VI. BASTION FUNCTIONS (In the 2024 DMG Sense)

For those keeping score against the modern Bastion rules, the Guest House already provides the classic special facilities — earned narratively rather than purchased:

- **Library** — the study and growing archive (Arcana's core facility, obviously)
- **Theater** — the beach itself serves; rehearsals at home, performances on the sand
- **Guest quarters** — definitionally; it is a *Guest House*
- **Sanctum** — the grotto: fireside, bearskin, the household's most private hours
- **Training area** — the beach again (volleyball courts, sparring sand)
- **Menagerie** — one (1) seagull, uninvited, undefeated

**Bastion orders** are issued in the traditional manner: Arcana decides, Sage records, Mercy makes it lovely, and the seagull files a dissenting opinion.
