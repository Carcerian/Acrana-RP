### TRUE FINAL BOSS: ASMODEA'S TOWER (Progressive Encounter)

**Setup:** Ascending an infernal tower, each floor features a progressively harder challenge. Asmodea waits at the top.

**Floor 1 (CR 5-6):** 2× Malebranche, 4× Chain Devils
**Floor 2 (CR 6-7):** 1× Pit Spawn Matron, 3× Damned Saint, 6× Imps
**Floor 3 (CR 7-8):** 1× Fell Angel, 2× Lady of Flame/Tides variants, 4× Demon Courtiers
**Floor 4 (CR 8-9):** 1× Pit Fiend (lesser), 2× Balor variants, 8× Elite Devils
**Top (CR 11):** **ASMODEA (Draconic Warrior Daughter)**
- Solo legendary encounter
- Full draconic/humanoid form-shifting
- Legendary actions every turn
- Lair control (infernal tower hazards)
- 200 HP, AC 20
- Can summon reinforcements (minions from lower floors return)

**Victory Condition:** Defeat Asmodea or destroy her pact-binding amulet (hidden in tower)

# PART 5: TACTICAL NOTES FOR GMs

## Environmental Encounter Modifiers

**High Ground:** Ranged creatures gain +1 to hit; melee attackers take -1 to hit going up
**Dense Forest:** Creatures gain +1 AC; ranged attacks disadvantage
**Magical Aura:** Spell casters gain +1 DC; non-magical spells disadvantage
**Infernal Territory:** Infernal creatures gain +1 AC/saves; celestials disadvantage
**Water:** Aquatic creatures gain +1 AC; land creatures Slowed

## Boss Modifiers (For Solo Bosses vs. Party Size)

**1-2 Party Members:** Boss has -20% HP
**3-4 Party Members:** Boss has normal HP
**5-6 Party Members:** Boss has +20% HP, one extra legendary action/turn
**7+ Party Members:** Boss has +40% HP, two extra legendary actions/turn, summon minions mid-combat
