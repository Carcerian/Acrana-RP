# DIVINE INFLUENCE CAMPAIGN FRAMEWORK

## How to Use the Pantheon System

The Arcana RPG pantheon is designed to grow with your campaign through **5 stages of divine influence**:

### **STAGE 1: SUBTLE OMENS (Levels 1-5)**
**Divine Influence:** Whispers in the wind, unusual animal behavior  
**What Happens:**
- Animals display odd behavior (crows gathering, peaceful animals acting aggressive)
- Minor elemental anomalies (unseasonable weather, strange plant growth)
- Dreams and visions (obscure at first)
- Celestial sightings rare but noticed

**Creatures to Use:**
- Mundane beasts (Crows, Doves, Hawks, Badgers)
- Lesser aberrations (Flying Eye, Thornling)
- Minor elementals (Mephits)

**How to Present:**
- Describe unusual animal encounters matter-of-factly
- Hints that nature is "responding" to something
- Priestess NPCs mention strange omens in temples
- Divine symbols appear coincidentally in treasure/loot

**Example Encounter:**
- Party finds rare white dove in dungeon (celestial messenger from Titania)
- Every crow they see seems to watch them (Morrigan's eyes)
- Embers fall from sky during fire cult encounter (Embera's presence)

---

### **STAGE 2: OBVIOUS ALLEGIANCES (Levels 6-10)**
**Divine Influence:** Clear factions emerge, creatures serve specific deities  
**What Happens:**
- NPCs openly discuss being priestesses
- Creatures are clearly tied to pantheon factions
- Divine conflicts affect the region (water vs. fire cults, seasonal priests)
- Players encounter priestesses & cultists with divine powers

**Creatures to Use:**
- Mythic Totems (Chimera, Dragon, Hydra, Griffon)
- Infernals (Succubus, Erinyes, Pact Devil)
- Elemental Lords (Ifrit, Sylph, Undine, Living Storm)
- Hive creatures (Swarm Queen, Elder Brain Matriarch)

**How to Present:**
- NPCs wear holy symbols and speak of their deity
- Creatures clearly serve a faction or pantheon
- Priestess enemies cast divine spells
- Divine artifacts appear as treasure

**Example Encounter:**
- Party battles fire cult warriors serving Embera
- Encounter priestess of Thalassa defending a sacred spring
- Rescue captives from hive collective thralls
- Find temple dedicated to specific deity

---

### **STAGE 3: ACTIVE INTERVENTION (Levels 11-15)**
**Divine Influence:** Deities actively meddle; priestesses command armies  
**What Happens:**
- High priestesses appear as major villains/allies
- Divine servants (CR 6-8) show up regularly
- Pantheon conflicts directly impact the campaign
- Players can become priestesses or divine agents

**Creatures to Use:**
- Leadership Tier: Infernal Nobility (Lilith, lesser devils)
- Leadership Tier: Magical Bioengineering (Chimera Alpha, Hydra Matriarch, Golem Sovereign)
- Elemental Princess avatars (Lesser forms of Embera, Thalassa, etc.)
- Powerful fey & celestial beings

**How to Present:**
- High priestesses command battles
- Entire faction armies serve a deity
- Divine magic blatantly reshapes the world
- Players make major choices between pantheon alliances

**Example Encounters:**
- Battle high priestess of Embera in volcanic temple
- Defend Thalassa's priestess against Embera's fire cultists
- Infiltrate hive mind colony to free enslaved minds
- Negotiate with fey archfey representative

---

### **STAGE 4: DIVINE WAR (Levels 16-20)**
**Divine Influence:** Open warfare between pantheons; reality warping  
**What Happens:**
- Gods openly fight through mortal agents
- Entire regions become battlegrounds
- Players choose sides in cosmic conflict
- Divine power reshapes the landscape

**Creatures to Use:**
- Leadership Tier: Archdevils & Infernal Lords (Asmodea, Malchanet, Pit Fiends)
- Leadership Tier: Archfey & Hybrid Rulers
- Void beings (Voidsphere, high-tier aberrations)
- Legendary creatures (Ancient dragons, Primordial Leviathan)

**How to Present:**
- Multiple pantheons openly battle for control
- Deities send major avatars to the material plane
- Priestesses command armies of thousands
- Landscape changes due to divine conflict (volcanos, floods, eternal storms)

**Example Encounters:**
- Battle between Embera's fire priests and Thalassa's water priestesses
- Defend against infernal invasion led by archdevil
- Navigate the Hive Collective's assimilation wave
- Witness Void Queen Nyx's influence spreading corruption

---

### **STAGE 5: COSMOLOGICAL SHIFT (Levels 21-40)
**Divine Influence:** Players become divine agents; reality fundamentally changes  
**What Happens:**
- Players are chosen as gods' champions or priests
- They ascend toward divine status themselves
- Pantheons can be defeated or merged
- World transforms based on campaign outcome

**Creatures to Use:**
- Tier 5 Leaders (Void Queen Nyx CR 11, Collective Overmind CR 17, Ancient Dragons CR 17)
- Divine avatars of major deities
- Primordial beings (Leviathan, etc.)
- Hybrid cosmic entities

**How to Present:**
- Players gain divine powers and divine minions
- They make decisions that affect entire planes
- Final confrontations with deity avatars (CR 10-11)
- Possible ascension to godhood

**Example Encounters:**
- Confront Void Queen Nyx directly
- Become priestess of chosen deity
- Battle for control of the Collective Overmind
- Decide the fate of the pantheon

# ADVANCED USAGE

## Creating Custom Divine Conflicts
1. Pick two pantheons with opposed values
2. Use their doctrines to frame conflict
3. Place their minions against each other
4. Have priestesses be quest-givers/enemies
5. Let players choose sides

## Integrating Divine Mechanics into Campaigns
1. Have priestess NPCs offer blessings (small bonuses, inspiration)
2. Introduce virtue/corruption tracking for player priestesses
3. Use holy symbols as clues & plot devices
4. Escalate divine minion power as campaign progresses
5. Allow players to become priestesses at key points

## Epic Divine Battles
1. Use Leadership Tier creatures (CR 6-11) as major opponents
2. Add divine minions (use Bestiary as followers)
3. Reference doctrines in NPC dialogue
4. Use sacred artifacts as treasures
5. End with pantheon political consequences
