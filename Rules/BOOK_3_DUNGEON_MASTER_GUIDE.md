# INTRODUCTION: HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

This Dungeon Master's Guide provides **complete mechanical frameworks** for running Expert Tier sessions (S62-S90+) of the priestess-craft campaign. It is designed for table-speed use: generators, tables, and pre-built encounters are organized for rapid lookup during play.

**Three primary tools are provided:**

1. **Procedural Generators** — Roll dice to create NPCs and encounters on-the-fly
2. **Pre-Built Rosters** — Named NPCs and entities ready to deploy immediately
3. **Reference Tables** — Mechanical effects, progression mechanics, and complications

**How to approach this guide:**

- **Session Prep:** Use Part V (Wave Structure) to understand what threats/encounters fit your current session range. Reference Part III (Entity Generator) to create unique entities for your specific campaign moment.
- **Mid-Session:** Keep Appendix E (Encounter Matrix) and Appendix C (Quick Reference Cards) at hand for instant stat lookup.
- **Campaign Arc:** Reference Part VI (Corruption System) and Part VIII (12-Week Timeline) to track Mercy's progression and NPC corruption arcs.
- **Faction Play:** Use Part IV (Faction Framework) to understand each faction's goals, methods, and available entities.

**Session Targets:**

Each wave has recommended encounter counts to maintain pacing and threat escalation. See Part V for session-by-session breakdown.

# PART I: CAMPAIGN PACING & STRUCTURE

## Overview: Three Campaign Waves

The Expert Tier campaign progresses through **three distinct waves** of Void corruption and threat escalation:

**WAVE 1 (Sessions 62-70): Foundation**
- Arcana arrives at Cathedral; learns priestess-craft basics
- Void-corruption is discovered; scale becomes apparent
- Mercy begins Tier I-II progression toward crisis
- Enemies: Tier I Victims, Tier II Acolytes, Tier III Disciples, Tier IV Entity

**WAVE 2 (Sessions 70-80): Organized Opposition**
- Corruption has structure; multiple factions revealed
- Cathedral politics; internal doubt; some priestesses compromised
- Mercy reaches Tier III crisis point; intensive intervention required
- Enemies: Tier II-III Acolytes, Tier III-IV Disciples, Tier IV-V Entities, Tier V Avatar

**WAVE 3 (Sessions 80-90+): Convergence**
- Factions clash; Elder entities emerge; mysteries resolved
- Final choice: save Mercy, bind her, or sacrifice her
- Depending on player actions, one faction may triumph; alliances may form
- Enemies: Tier III-V Disciples/Instruments, Tier IV-VI+ Entities, Elder/Avatar confrontation

## Sessions 62-70: Arrival & First Teaching

### Session 62: Arrival at Cathedral
**Encounter Targets:**
- 1 Tier I Victim (corrupted local pilgrim; tragic introduction)
- 1 Tier II Acolyte (serves Void; priestesses discover cult exists)
- Meet 2-3 Dominae (teaching introduction; establish relationships)

**Key Moments:**
- Arcana and Mercy arrive; first impressions of Cathedral
- Baseline-Lock ceremony performed (monthly renewal introduced)
- First sign of Void-corruption discovered (victim or acolyte)
- Mercy has first Void-contact dream; Arcana notices

**Corruption Timeline:**
- Mercy: Tier I (Dreaming stage); Arcana not yet aware of seduction
- Void contact this session: +1 Tier I progression

---

### Sessions 63-65: First Teaching & Early Exploration
**Encounter Targets per session:**
- 1-2 Tier I Victims (scattered encounters; building awareness)
- 1 Tier II Acolyte (cult recruitment attempt; priestess seduction)
- 1 teaching scene (Domina + Arcana + Mount; Virtue training)

**Cumulative Threats:**
- Shamanic Tribe makes first contact (neutral/curious at this stage)
- Cult operatives test Cathedral defenses
- Arcana's relationships with Sisters develop; some become allies, others remain distant

**Corruption Timeline:**
- Mercy: Tier I-II transition (around S64-65)
- Arcana notices: Mercy is "distracted," seeks dark places, unusually withdrawn

**Teaching Advancement:**
- Arcana gains first Virtue points (3-5 points typical per session from teaching/practice)
- First class ability learned (Low-tier, matches her Virtue)

---

### Sessions 66-70: Mid-Wave Crisis & First Real Opposition
**Encounter Targets per session:**
- 1-2 Tier II Acolytes (organized; some coordination visible)
- 1 Tier III Disciple (named enemy; personal threat)
- 1-2 Tier I Victims (but now in clusters; suggests organization)
- 1 Void entity encounter (minor; possibly just Echo-Creature or Voidswarm)

**Major Events:**
- S67-68: Mercy reaches Tier II (Calling); whispers become audible; Arcana must intervene
- S68: Baseline-Lock renewal becomes noticeably harder; Void-touch is spreading
- S69: First major discovery (artifact, location, NPC motivation that hints at larger pattern)
- S70: Climactic encounter; Tier III Disciple or Tier IV Entity appears; session-ending revelation

**Corruption Timeline:**
- Mercy: Tier II confirmed by S70
- Dark Domina or Cult leader revealed (identity or involvement); priestesses take sides

**NPC Arcs:**
- At least one Sister priestess shows signs of corruption or temptation (complication)
- A Domina teaches specialized ritual (Baseline-Lock Renewal, Sleep Protection, or similar)

**Teaching Advancement:**
- Arcana reaches Tier I (5+ Virtue points accumulated)
- First Tier I ability activates mechanically in a scene

---

## Sessions 70-80: Mastery & Mystery

### Sessions 71-75: Depth & Doubt
**Encounter Targets per session:**
- 2-3 Tier II Acolytes/Victims (larger groups; networks becoming clear)
- 1-2 Tier III Disciples (enemies grow more powerful; personal agendas emerge)
- 0-1 Tier IV Entity (rare; feels like major threat)

**Key Developments:**
- S71-72: Shamanic Tribe subplot deepens; possible alliance or conflict
- S73: A major priestess is revealed as corrupted (Domina or high-ranking Sister); crisis
- S74: Cult's true leadership structure partially revealed; someone is pulling strings
- S75: Void-Binding Ritual introduced as option; intensive priestess-craft required

**Mercy's Arc:**
- S72: Mercy reaches Tier III (Compromise); active will-touch with Arcana; resistance breaks down
- S73-75: Intensive Priestess-Work scenes; priestesses attempt to hold Mercy at Tier III or push back to Tier II
- High-stakes emotional scenes; Arcana must make choices about Mercy's future

**Teaching Advancement:**
- Arcana reaches Tier II (15+ total Virtue points)
- First Tier II ability learned; more powerful but costly in Psyche
- Mount Loyalty progression (Bonded → Devoted, if Mercy survives to S75)

---

### Sessions 76-80: Mystery Deepens, Power Grows
**Encounter Targets per session:**
- 2-3 Tier III Disciples (elite enemies; coordinated tactics)
- 1-2 Tier IV Entities (major threats; possible faction representatives)
- 0-1 Tier V Avatar (rare; finale-level threat)

**Major Revelations:**
- S76: Ancient mystery about Cathedral's history surfaces (Elder, imprisonment, why Void was sealed)
- S77-78: Factions clash directly; priestesses must choose sides or attempt neutrality
- S79: Temporal anomalies increase (fourth time-moment becomes more accessible; Temporal entities possible)
- S80: Wave 2 climax; major faction victory, unexpected betrayal, or massive entity appearance

**NPC Corruption:**
- 2-3 major NPCs revealed as corrupted at Tier III-IV level
- Some may be salvageable with Void-Binding; others are lost
- Emotional weight: priestesses Arcana cares about face corruption

**Teaching Advancement:**
- Arcana consolidates Tier II knowledge
- Ritual mastery increases (able to perform complicated ceremonies; Void-Binding Ritual becomes realistic option)
- Mount Loyalty: Devoted stage; special abilities unlock (Intuitive Response, Emotional Resonance)

---

## Sessions 80-90+: Endgame & Revelation

### Sessions 81-85: Convergence & Choice
**Encounter Targets per session:**
- 1-3 Tier IV Entities (major threats; coordinated by factions or Elder)
- 1-2 Tier IV-V Disciples (corrupted priestesses, powerful minions)
- 0-1 Tier V+ Avatar/Entity (Elder presence felt; possibly manifest partially)

**Major Events:**
- S81: Imprisoned Elder entity begins to wake/communicate
- S82: Void-Binding Ritual becomes necessary for major NPC; Arcana may need to choose who to save
- S83: Factions converge on key locations; violence between corruption groups
- S84: Mercy reaches crisis point; Tier IV or borderline Tier V
- S85: Temporal phenomena peak; fourth moment becomes fully accessible; ancient truths revealed

**Mercy's Ultimate Arc:**
- S82-83: If Tier IV, Mercy is mostly lost; Void-voice dominates; Arcana hears her personality fading
- S84-85: Final choice point: Void-Binding Ritual imprisons Mercy and Void together (they survive but imprisoned); or Execution/Exile (Mercy is lost; corruption stopped); or Sacrifice (Arcana gives part of soul; Void-bonds weaken; Mercy remains corrupted but partly recoverable)

**Teaching Advancement:**
- Arcana reaches Tier III or consolidates Tier II (depending on play style and choices)
- Access to highest-tier rituals (Keeper-Only Rituals if campaign grants access)
- Mount Loyalty: Devoted-Beyond or Soulbound possible (if Mercy survives in any form)

---

### Sessions 86-90+: Resolution & Echoes
**Encounter Targets:**
- Few encounters; mostly resolution, revelation, and consequences
- Final faction confrontations; alliance payoffs
- Mercy resolution scene(s); emotional closure or continuation

**Endgame Content:**
- S86-87: Revelations about who awakened the Elder and why
- S88: Exploration of Ancient Ruins (if available) to uncover Cathedral's true purpose
- S89-90: Final encounters with remaining corrupted factions; Elder decision (dormant again? Banished? Transformed?)
- S90+: Epilogue; new Cathedral order; Arcana's place in it; Mercy's status defined

**Campaign Conclusion:**
- Depending on choices, one of several endings:
  - **Priestly Victory:** Cathedral is cleansed; Void is re-sealed; Arcana is celebrated or burdened with Mercy's fate
  - **Complicated Survival:** Void remains; Elder sleeps again; constant vigilance needed; Mercy is bound or lost
  - **Faction Triumph:** One corruption faction wins influence; Cathedral is changed (darker or differently governed)
  - **Sacrifice Play:** Arcana becomes something new; Mercy is partially restored; both carry scars

# PART II: NPC PRIESTESS GENERATOR

## Eight Dominae Framework

The Eight Dominae are master priestesses, one teaching each Virtue. They are not all allies; some may be corrupted, tested, or complicated in their loyalties.

### Discipline Domina — The Iron Teacher
**Virtue Focus:** Discipline  
**Archetype:** Stern, uncompromising, values obedience and structure  
**Teaching Style:** Harsh but fair; expects perfection; doesn't accept excuses  
**Relationship Arc:** Starts distant; warms if Arcana shows discipline; cold if she slacks  
**Complication:** May believe Void-contact is Arcana's weakness; pushes her to "overcome" it alone (harmful)  
**Secret:** Secretly sympathetic to one of Arcana's early struggles; shows it rarely

### Precision Domina — The Exact Hand
**Virtue Focus:** Precision  
**Archetype:** Methodical, detail-obsessed, loves ritual and measurement  
**Teaching Style:** Pedantic; explains everything step-by-step; frustrated by shortcuts  
**Relationship Arc:** Respects competence; becomes ally if Arcana shows attention to detail  
**Complication:** May use Precision teaching to enable dark rituals (unaware or knowing)  
**Secret:** Has created perfect ritual framework that could bind Void; unsure if it works

### Transcendence Domina — The Sky-Gazer
**Virtue Focus:** Transcendence  
**Archetype:** Visionary, spiritual, seeks higher understanding  
**Teaching Style:** Cryptic; uses metaphor and meditation; reveals truths gradually  
**Relationship Arc:** Drawn to Arcana's connection to Void as "cosmic event"; may misjudge threat  
**Complication:** Sympathizes with Void as fellow seeker; could be compromised  
**Secret:** Has experienced Void-contact; believes she transcended it; possibly corrupted without knowing

### Devotion Domina — The Heart-Keeper
**Virtue Focus:** Devotion  
**Archetype:** Warm, nurturing, leads with compassion  
**Teaching Style:** Personal; cares deeply about students; mentors emotionally  
**Relationship Arc:** Arcana's strongest ally; protective; mothers her  
**Complication:** Her protective nature blinds her to corruption spreading; enables bad decisions  
**Secret:** One of her former students is corrupted (Tier III-IV); she's hiding it out of shame

### Honesty Domina — The Truth-Speaker
**Virtue Focus:** Honesty  
**Archetype:** Blunt, direct, refuses euphemism  
**Teaching Style:** Brutal; says hard truths; doesn't shield students  
**Relationship Arc:** Arcana respects her; sometimes angry at her bluntness; appreciates her eventually  
**Complication:** Her honesty can be weaponized; she might speak damaging truths at wrong moments  
**Secret:** Knows more about the Void's origins than she's admitted; waiting for right time to tell

### Excellence Domina — The Master Crafter
**Virtue Focus:** Excellence  
**Archetype:** Perfectionist, ambitious, achievement-focused  
**Teaching Style:** Competitive; pushes students to excellence; celebrates victories  
**Relationship Arc:** Sees Arcana as potential rival or protégé; depends on Arcana's ambition  
**Complication:** Her ambition has led her to dangerous knowledge; flirts with forbidden magic  
**Secret:** Has made pact with minor Void entity for power; it hasn't claimed payment yet

### Nature Domina — The Wild-Speaker
**Virtue Focus:** Nature  
**Archetype:** Free-spirited, connected to wilderness, distrusts structures  
**Teaching Style:** Hands-on; teaches through experience; takes students into wild  
**Relationship Arc:** Bonds with Arcana's mount; possibly understands Mercy's corruption better than others  
**Complication:** Her connection to nature may blind her to unnatural threats; she underestimates Void  
**Secret:** Communicates with Shamanic Tribe; possible double agent or secret ally

### Witness Domina — The Unseen Eye
**Virtue Focus:** Witness  
**Archetype:** Observant, quiet, sees what others miss  
**Teaching Style:** Silent; teaches through presence; points out what Arcana overlooks  
**Relationship Arc:** Mysterious; relationship develops through unspoken understanding  
**Complication:** Knows more than she says; her silence may hide or enable corruption  
**Secret:** Has recorded everything; keeps detailed journals of all corruption signs; is building case for action

## Twelve Sisters Framework

The Twelve Sisters are peer priestesses of similar experience level to Arcana. Relationships with them provide emotional stakes and complications.

**Template for each Sister:**
- **Name & Virtue:** Which Virtue is her specialty
- **Starting Disposition:** Friendly, neutral, hostile, or complicated
- **Connection Hook:** Why she matters to Arcana
- **Parallel Arc:** What's her personal story?
- **Corruption Risk:** Is she tempted or compromised?
- **Potential Ally/Enemy:** Can she become key ally or threat?

**Six Sister Archetypes (doubled for 12 total):**

1. **The Earnest Scholar** (Precision specialist) — Wants to understand everything; asks too many questions; may stumble onto dangerous truth
2. **The Doubter** (Honesty specialist) — Questions Cathedral teachings; seeks authenticity; vulnerable to Void's "honesty"
3. **The Protector** (Discipline specialist) — Takes responsibility for weaker sisters; will sacrifice herself; potential Void-target
4. **The Mystic** (Transcendence specialist) — Seeks spiritual experience; attracted to Void's cosmic nature; most corrupted potential
5. **The Lover** (Devotion specialist) — Forms deep bonds; emotionally dependent on relationships; vulnerable to seduction
6. **The Ambitious** (Excellence specialist) — Wants power; seeks recognition; tempted by Void's promises

*Create 12 Sisters by pairing these archetypes with specific names, personalities, and secrets. See Appendix A for example roster.*

# PART III: VOID ENTITY GENERATOR

## Twelve Entity Types

### 1. VOIDSWARM (Collective/Hive)
**Nature:** Many small entities acting as one intelligence; locust-like corruption

**Manifestation:** Appears as writhing darkness, swarm of shadows, collective whisper

**Psyche Drain:** +2 per swarm member present; cumulative

**Special Ability:** Divide and multiply; difficult to isolate; overwhelms through numbers

**Vulnerability:** Fire/light disperses them temporarily; killing enough weakens hive-mind

**Complication:** May be fragments of a larger entity; destroying swarm might alert the whole

**Typical Goal:** Consume all life in region; create perfect hive; find larger hive to join

**Corruption Vector:** Numbers/belonging, colony-mind unity, dissolution of individual will

**Tier Range:** II-IV

---

### 2. TOTEM BEAST (Sacred Animal Corrupted)
**Nature:** Animal sacred to priestess-craft, shamanic tradition, or local culture that has been possessed/corrupted by Void

**Manifestation:** Warped version of recognizable animal (wolf, deer, bear, raven, etc.) but wrong—too large, extra eyes, unnatural movement, aura of wrongness

**Psyche Drain:** +3-4 per encounter; witnessing corruption of sacred thing causes despair/shame

**Special Ability:** 
- Enhanced version of animal's natural abilities (pack tactics, predatory sense, flight, strength)
- Corrupts other animals of its kind (spreads corruption through wildlife)
- Connection to shamanic/nature magic (can disrupt natural bonds, turn animals against priestesses)
- Carries trauma/memory of what it once was (makes it tragically unpredictable)

**Vulnerability:** 
- Can sometimes be reasoned with if shown its original nature/before-corruption
- Severing connection to Void entity possessing it may free the animal (restoration possible)
- Sacred ritual specific to that animal's tradition can temporarily suppress it
- Physical damage slows it, but it feels no pain (makes it relentless)

**Complication:** Priestesses may hesitate to harm sacred animal; emotional conflict

**Typical Goal:** Corrupt other valley animals; create network of Void-touched beasts; use them to spread taint

**Corruption Vector:** Merger with nature, primal instinct, shamanic seduction, pack loyalty corrupted

**Tier Range:** II-IV

---

### 3. ELEMENTAL (Pure Force Corrupted)
**Nature:** Air/water/fire/earth given malevolent consciousness through Void-touch

**Manifestation:** Storm, tidal wave, wildfire, earthquake with intelligent purpose; impossible to distinguish from natural disaster

**Psyche Drain:** +2-3; witnessing its power causes despair

**Special Ability:** 
- Command of element (can manipulate water, fire, earth, air at massive scale)
- Reality-warping within domain
- Massive scale (effects cover regions, not rooms)
- Merging with natural phenomena (becomes the storm, becomes the quake)

**Vulnerability:** 
- Opposite element temporarily suppresses it
- Binding ritual ties it to location
- May have been a natural force before Void-corruption; restoration might be possible

**Complication:** Difficult to tell corrupted from natural; civilians die in its wake; priestesses may not be able to stop it

**Typical Goal:** Spread corruption through element (plague through water, wildfire, tremors); merge with Elder

**Corruption Vector:** Merger with nature, immersion in element, surrender to force, inevitability

**Tier Range:** III-V

---

### 4. ANIMATED (Possessed Object/Construct)
**Nature:** Inanimate object (statue, armor, corpse, building) given movement by Void-will

**Manifestation:** Jerky movement, wrong joints, sounds of creaking/grinding; object moves like puppet

**Psyche Drain:** +1-2; unsettling to witness

**Special Ability:** 
- Indestructible body (can't be killed conventionally; only Void exorcism works)
- Tireless; doesn't need rest or sustenance
- Precise; can perform delicate tasks with awkward form
- Can rebuild itself if damaged (slowly)

**Vulnerability:** 
- Void can be exorcised from vessel
- Severing connection to anchor weakens it
- If anchor (shrine, object, corpse-origin) is destroyed, entity vanishes

**Complication:** May be animated corpse of someone Arcana knew; tragic recognition

**Typical Goal:** Protect shrine/anchor; gather specific objects; perform ritual; defend master

**Corruption Vector:** Duty/purpose, obedience, servitude, control from outside

**Tier Range:** I-III

---

### 5. CYBERNETIC (Techno-Void Hybrid)
**Nature:** Technology fused with Void-consciousness; logic twisted into alien patterns

**Manifestation:** Metal/machinery that moves with wrong purpose, glowing runes, impossible angles; seems to violate physics

**Psyche Drain:** +2-3; causes cognitive dissonance (doesn't make sense to perceive)

**Special Ability:** 
- Rapid adaptation (learns countermeasures to priestess tactics mid-fight)
- Technological sabotage (corrupts Cathedral tools, ropes, artifacts)
- Networked consciousness (communicates with other tech-entities instantly)
- Evolves (grows stronger each encounter if not permanently defeated)

**Vulnerability:** 
- Void-Binding Ritual disrupts the fusion
- EMP-equivalent effects stagger it
- Binding it to specific location prevents evolution

**Complication:** Priestesses don't understand what they're facing; may underestimate it

# PART VI: VOID CORRUPTION UNIVERSAL SYSTEM

## TIER I: UNTOUCHED → DREAMING

*Early seduction; subject is aware but not yet compromised*

**Void Traits:**
- Vivid, intrusive dreams of deep places (caverns, wells, darkness)
- Whispers heard in solitude (unclear if real or imagination)
- Unusual hunger/cravings (stone, ash, salt, blood)
- Sensitivity to Baseline-Lock or spiritual bonds (they tingle, ache)
- Psyche drain: +1 per scene of Void-contact
- **Visible Signs:** Eyes catch light strangely; occasional pallor; distraction/dreaminess
- **Personality Shift:** Withdrawn, introspective, secretive
- **Reversibility:** Fully reversible with Sleep Protection or Baseline-Lock Renewal

---

## TIER II: DREAMING → CALLING

*Subject actively hears the Void; resistance eroding into seduction*

**Void Traits:**
- Whispers constant, even when awake (omnipresent inner voice)
- Compulsion to seek deep/dark places (cellars, caves, wells, underground)
- Subject begins **negotiating with the Void** (bargaining, making deals)
- Rebellious behavior toward Baseline-Lock or priestess authority
- Psyche drain: +2 per scene; +1 from mere proximity to deep places
- **Visible Signs:** Dark circles, pale skin, hair darkens/gains unnatural sheen; shadow seems deeper
- **Personality Shift:** Moody, contradictory, emotionally volatile; moments of sudden tenderness or hostility
- **Reversibility:** Reversible with intensive Priestess-Work + Sleep Protection + Baseline-Lock Renewal; resistance is increasing but will is not yet broken

---

## TIER III: CALLING → COMPROMISE

*Subject makes active choices to serve the Void; free will begins to fragment*

**Void Traits:**
- Subject **speaks for the Void** (channeling its thoughts and desires, not possessed but aligned)
- Will-touch with authority figures (resists commands, negotiates obedience, contradicts openly)
- Active pursuit of Void-contact; pulls away from spiritual bonds
- Subject begins **collecting things for the Void** (stones, bones, forbidden objects; building shrines)
- Psyche drain: +3 per scene; permanent -1 to Psyche pool (corruption erodes mount's vitality)
- **Visible Signs:** Eyes show void-black flickers in iris; voice takes double-tone (own voice + whisper underneath); shadow seems wrong/independent; temperature drops around subject
- **Personality Shift:** Two personas emerging (subject and Void-voice); affectionate then hostile; eager then stubborn
- **Reversibility:** Reversible only with Void-Binding Ritual + extensive recovery; subject's will is compromised but not erased

---

## TIER IV: COMPROMISE → CONSUMED

*Subject's will fragments completely; Void inhabits their consciousness as co-tenant*

**Void Traits:**
- **Dual consciousness:** Subject's personality present but subsumed; Void is dominant operator
- Subject speaks in Void's voice ~50% of the time; own voice becomes corrupted/wrong
- Superhuman resilience (Void's power bleeds through)
- Can sense other Void-touched and deep places across great distance
- Spiritual bonds (Baseline-Lock, priestess bonds) **actively resisted**; cannot be renewed or shatter
- Psyche drain: +4 per scene; Void-linked abilities cost observers' Psyche instead
- **Visible Signs:** Eyes fully void-black; dark veins visible beneath skin; presence feels physically wrong; temperature plummets in vicinity; shadow moves independently; aura of dread/wrongness
- **Personality Shift:** Subject's identity is trapped behind Void's will; glimpses of original personality surface (tragic/horrifying)
- **Reversibility:** Only reversible through Void-Binding Ritual (imprisoning subject's consciousness and Void together); original subject may be permanently altered

## TIER V: CONSUMED → VOID-INSTRUMENT

*Subject is erased or imprisoned; they are now a pure conduit for Void's power*

**Void Traits:**
- Subject's personality is **gone or unreachable** (unknown if erasure is permanent or imprisonment)
- Subject is now a **Void-Instrument:** pure vessel for Void's will and cosmic power
- Superhuman abilities: telekinesis, mind-shattering presence, impossible strength, ability to corrupt others on touch
- Can communicate across dimensional barriers; senses all Void-touched within leagues
- Spiritual bonds are weaponized: Void uses them to attack/control others
- Psyche drain: +5 per scene in subject's presence; proximity to other priestesses/mounts spreads corruption like contagion
- **Visible Signs:** Body seems to occupy space incorrectly; moving too fluidly or too rigid; eyes are voids (literally absorb light); shadow is sentient and independent; she speaks only in Void's voice; presence causes madness/despair in observers
- **Personality Shift:** No original personality remains (or is imprisoned beyond reach); subject speaks only in Void's voice
- **Reversibility:** **Irreversible.** Only containment possible: Void-Binding Ritual imprisons Void + subject together in stasis; Execution/Exile stops the spread

## PROGRESSION MECHANICS

**Tier Advancement Speed:**

Each tier lasts approximately **1-3 weeks** of in-game time depending on:

- **Void-contact frequency:** Constant contact = 1 week per tier; Weekly = 2-3 weeks; Rare = 3+ weeks
- **Subject's resistance:** High Discipline/Honesty = slower; Low = faster
- **Void's interest level:** Seduction vs. hunger vs. invasion

**Acceleration Factors (instant +1 tier):**
- Will-loss crisis (subject gives up; surrenders to Void)
- Direct Void entity contact (major entity appears; overwhelms subject)
- Subject actively chooses corruption (speaks bargain; makes pact)
- Subject betrays priestess bond (directly violates Baseline-Lock; acts against mount-priestess)

**Delay/Reversal Factors:**
- Sleep Protection ritual = hold tier for 1 week
- Baseline-Lock Renewal = hold tier for 1 week
- Intensive Priestess-Work = roll back 1 tier (risky; may fail)
- Void-Binding Ritual = lock subject at current tier permanently (as prison, not cure)

# PART VII: ENCOUNTER FRAMEWORKS

## Teaching Scene Template

**Setup:**
- Location: Training grounds, private chamber, or outdoor Virtue-place
- Cast: One Domina + Arcana + Mercy (mount)
- Goal: Teach specific Virtue or ability; advance priestess relationship

**Structure:**

1. **Opening (Roleplay):** Domina explains today's lesson; establishes stakes or emotional context
2. **Teaching (Mechanical):** Priestess attempts Virtue-based task (ritual, meditation, combat form, etc.)
3. **Complication:** Something goes wrong or appears unexpectedly
4. **Resolution:** Priestess succeeds, partially succeeds, or fails; Void-sign appears if Mercy is corrupted

**Complication Table (d10):**
1. Mercy becomes distracted/disobedient (Void-contact)
2. Domina reveals unexpected truth (about Cathedral, teaching, Arcana)
3. Another priestess interrupts (social drama; alliance/rivalry)
4. Environmental hazard (sudden storm, structure fails, ground shakes)
5. Void entity briefly manifests (whispers, shadow, scent)
6. A Sister priestess appears injured/corrupted (crisis call)
7. Domina tests Arcana beyond lesson scope (hidden agenda)
8. Mercy has emotional response to teaching (fear, shame, defiance)
9. Magic fails unpredictably (Void-corruption of rituals)
10. Arcana reaches breakthrough; Virtue points awarded

**Reward:** 1-3 Virtue points (depending on success level); Relationship advancement with Domina

## Social Encounter Template

**Setup:**
- Location: Refectory, library, garden, or common space
- Cast: Arcana + 1-2 Sisters/NPCs + possible spectators
- Goal: Build relationship, gather information, navigate politics, or create conflict

**Structure:**

1. **Opening (Roleplay):** NPC initiates conversation; establishes emotional tone
2. **Dialogue (Player-Led):** Arcana responds; player has agency in direction
3. **Tension (Complication):** Emotional stakes or disagreement emerges
4. **Resolution:** Conversation concludes; relationship shifts or stasis

**Tension Table (d8):**
1. NPC reveals vulnerability (asks for help; confesses fear)
2. NPC makes difficult request (asks Arcana to keep secret; asks for compromise)
3. NPC hints at corruption (but doesn't directly say so)
4. NPC challenges Arcana's judgment (criticizes choices; questions loyalty)
5. Third party arrives; changes conversation dynamic
6. NPC reveals romantic interest (complicates relationship)
7. NPC offers forbidden knowledge (temptation; moral test)
8. Misunderstanding erupts; requires repair

**Reward:** +1 Relationship point with NPC; information; possible complication hook

## Exploration Encounter Template

**Setup:**
- Location: Abbey Vale, ruins, deep caves, or unmapped areas
- Cast: Arcana + Mercy + 1-2 Sisters (optional)
- Goal: Discover location, artifact, information, or threat

**Structure:**

1. **Navigation (Environment):** Describe location; priestesses move through space
2. **Discovery (Mechanical):** Priestesses find something (artifact, corpse, sign, entity)
3. **Complication (Threat):** Void-sign or entity appears; environmental hazard
4. **Resolution:** Priestesses secure discovery; flee; or are overwhelmed

**Discovery Table (d10):**
1. Corrupted creature (Void-touched animal or humanoid)
2. Artifact (magical item; possibly cursed)
3. Corpse (dead priestess or victim)
4. Shrine (hidden Void shrine; evidence of cult)
5. Ancient writing (lore about Elder, Cathedral, Void)
6. Void-touched location (area where corruption is strong)
7. NPC in need (captured victim; corrupted priestess)
8. Treasure (gold, valuables, ritual components)
9. Echo-creature (Temporal or Spirit entity)
10. Temporal distortion (fourth moment bleeds through)

**Complication Table (d8):**
1. Entity guards location (must fight or negotiate)
2. Location collapses (environmental hazard; escape urgency)
3. Void's power surges (Psyche drain increases; horror reveals)
4. Priestess becomes corrupted/tempted (moral choice)
5. Party is separated (spatial hazard; must regroup)
6. Artifact is trapped/cursed (activation causes problem)
7. Location is larger than expected (deeper mystery)
8. Second party arrives (faction members; allies or enemies)

**Reward:** Artifact; information; Virtue points; complication/hook for future sessions

## Void-Contact Scene Template

**Setup:**
- Location: Any; often in private moment or crisis
- Cast: Arcana + Mercy (or corrupted priestess + Void entity)
- Goal: Advance Mercy's corruption arc; show Void's seduction technique; create emotional stakes

**Structure:**

1. **Context (Roleplay):** Describe when/why Void-contact occurs
2. **Seduction (Narrative):** Void speaks directly; offers comfort/knowledge/power
3. **Test (Choice):** Subject must resist or surrender (mechanical or roleplay)
4. **Consequence (Mechanical):** Tier advancement or resistance; Psyche drain

**Void Offer Table (d8):**
1. "I can make the pain go away" (mercy; comfort; addiction vector)
2. "You're stronger than they are; you could rule" (power; ambition vector)
3. "I understand you; they never will" (belonging; loneliness vector)
4. "Together, we can be perfect" (fusion; transcendence vector)
5. "They're going to reject you anyway; join us first" (shame; preemptive surrender)
6. "This is your true nature; accept it" (identity; truth vector)
7. "I can save [loved one]; just let me in" (bargain; grief vector)
8. "Time is running out; choose now" (urgency; pressure)

**Resistance Test (Player Choice):**
- **Accept:** +1 Tier (Void contact fully succeeds)
- **Negotiate:** Tier unchanged; Void makes concession (temporary peace)
- **Resist (Difficulty DC = Tier + 3):** On success, hold tier; on failure, +1 tier anyway

**Outcome:** Tier advancement; Psyche drain; complication for future scenes

## Baseline-Lock Renewal Complications

**Monthly Renewal Required:** All priestesses must renew Baseline-Lock with mount at least monthly or bond weakens

**Complication Table (d12):**

1. Mount's will is resistant (bond is weakened by corruption or distance)
2. Void actively interferes with ceremony (+2 Psyche drain to attempt)
3. Location becomes hostile (weather, earthquake, enemy arrival)
4. Priestess has doubt/guilt (affects ceremony success; must pass ritual check)
5. Rope is damaged (needs repair or replacement before use)
6. Mount is injured or sick (affects Psyche pool; ceremony is harder)
7. Third party witnesses ceremony (social complication; exposure risk)
8. Void-entity appears and tempts during renewal (seduction attempt)
9. Ceremony works but requires extra Psyche cost (exhausting; difficult recovery)
10. Ceremony partially fails (bond held but weakened for next month)
11. Mount becomes temporarily fractured/confused (acts strangely post-ceremony)
12. Ceremony succeeds perfectly but reveals hidden truth (NPC corruption, hidden motivation)

**Renewal Mechanics:**

- **Standard:** 5 Psyche cost; successful ritual check (DC 10 + Mount's Corruption Tier)
- **If Tier II+:** +2 DC; +2 Psyche cost
- **If Tier III+:** +3 DC; +3 Psyche cost; high risk of failure
- **If Tier IV+:** +5 DC; +5 Psyche cost; often cannot be renewed (Void actively resists)

- Corruption Wave (spreads taint to nearby animals; +1 tier to any animal touched)

**Special Notes:** 
- Bound to Valley (can't leave valley without weakening severely; sanctuary in deep forests)
- Legacy Knowledge (remembers being sacred messenger; sometimes offers genuine prophecies alongside lies; tragic)

**Intelligence:** Cunning; understands priestess tactics; adapts

**Notes for GM:** Priestesses recognize it as the sacred Black Deer they once honored. Seeing it corrupted is deeply shameful/painful. Some may try to "save" it rather than destroy it—complication for the faction.

**Manifestation:** Perception breaks when viewing it; multiple forms simultaneously; doesn't obey physics; text describing it causes headaches; viewing it causes sanity checks

**Psyche Drain:** +6 per scene; every priestess in vicinity loses 1 Psyche/turn just from proximity

- Reality Breaking (warps causality, physics, logic)

**Intelligence:** Alien; incomprehensible
