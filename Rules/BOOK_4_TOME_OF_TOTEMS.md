## A Compendium of Sacred Beasts and Their Bonds

## INTRODUCTION

The Totems are sacred animals touched by the divine spark of the Virtues—each embodying a particular aspect of human excellence and spiritual power. When a Priestess bonds with a Totem, she does not merely gain a powerful ally; she accepts a sacred mirror of her own soul, reflecting both her strengths and the lessons she must learn.

This tome contains the complete description of all forty Totems recognized by the Shamanic Tradition: their symbolic meanings, mechanical traits, ability progressions, charm invocations, and the deep lore that binds them to the spiritual realm.

The Totems are divided into three categories: **Mundane Totems** (d12, rooted in animals of this world), **Mythic Totems** (d20, creatures of legend and divine origin), and **Elemental Totems** (d8, forces of nature itself). All carry sacred power; the distinction reflects their origin and the depth of corruption they can channel.

### How Totem Beasts Work

A **Totem Beast** is a corrupted animal of exceptional power—its sacred nature infused with Void energy, granting it immortality, regeneration, and connection to shamanic forces. Unlike mundane creatures, Totems possess:

- **Psyche Pool**: Mental endurance; depleted through Void-contact and corrupting influence
- **Corruption Tiers**: Progressive transformation from I (Dreaming) through V (Void-Instrument)
- **Virtue Resonance**: Each Totem naturally aligns with one or more of the Eight Virtues
- **Trait Expression**: How the Totem's corruption manifests physically and mentally
- **Charm Invocation**: Unique abilities priestesses unlock through bonding and loyalty progression

## THE FORTY TOTEMS

### MUNDANE TOTEMS (d12)

Mundane Totems are rooted in the natural world—animals of flesh and blood that exist in physical reality. Yet through their bond with Priestesses and their corruption by Void contact, they transcend their natural limitations and become sacred forces.

### 1. CAT
**"The Silent Watcher"**

**Totem Type**: Feline  
**Primary Virtue**: Devotion  
**Secondary Virtue**: Precision

**Symbolic Meaning**  
Cat embodies silent watchfulness and independent grace—the principle that strength often hides in stillness. It teaches that a Priestess bonded with Cat must learn to observe without judgment, act without announcement, and remain graceful even when striking. Cat represents the balance between dependence and self-sufficiency.

**Physical Description**  
A cat of exceptional size and intelligence—could be lion, panther, or wildcat, each with its own power. Void corruption makes Cat darker, more alien, more predatory. Higher tiers manifest as Cat developing unnatural speed and almost spectral grace.

**Motif & Symbolism**  
Silent observation; independent strength; grace and power; unseen influence.

**Trait Tiers**

| Tier | Trait Expression | Psyche Impact |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| **I (Dreaming)** | Cat's senses become supernatural; sees perfectly in darkness | +0 Psyche drain |
| **II (Stirring)** | Movement becomes silent; cat positions become tactically perfect | +1 Psyche drain per day |
| **III (Waking)** | Cat becomes difficult to perceive; ignores opportunity attacks | +1 Psyche drain per day |
| **IV (Seized)** | Becomes nearly invisible in shadows; strikes without warning | +2 Psyche drain per day |
| **V (Void-Instrument)** | Can exist partially in shadow-realm; detection becomes nearly impossible | +3 Psyche drain per day + mutation risk |

**Ability Progression**

*Tier I-II (Low)*
- **Night Vision**: Cat sees perfectly in dim light and darkness
- **Silent Stalker**: Cat's movement does not create sound; gains advantage on stealth

*Tier II-III (Mid)*
- **Predatory Pounce**: Cat can attack from stealth without losing surprise
- **Graceful Dodge**: Cat automatically dodges attacks it perceives coming

*Tier III-IV (High)*
- **Phantom Movement**: Cat becomes difficult to hit; enemies have disadvantage
- **Unseen Presence**: Rider can act as though invisible in shadows

*Tier IV-V (Capstone)*
- **Shadow-Leap**: Cat can move between shadows across the battlefield
- **Silent Execution**: Cat can attack and vanish before enemies realize they've been struck

**Charm Types**
- **Silent Approach**: Move without sound or detection
- **Night Vision**: See perfectly in darkness
- **Graceful Strike**: Attack with perfect precision

**Lore**

Cat arose as a creature of temples and sacred places—a watcher, neither servant nor master. It chose its bonds carefully and gave its loyalty in its own time. The Void corruption did not tame Cat; instead, it made Cat more alien, more independent, more indifferent to human concerns.

Priestesses of Cat are scouts, assassins, and those who value independence and self-reliance. Cat teaches that sometimes the greatest gift is to be left alone, and that not all bonds require constant attention.

### 2. DOVE
**"The Gentle Herald"**

**Totem Type**: Avian  
**Primary Virtue**: Precision  
**Secondary Virtue**: Nature

**Symbolic Meaning**  
Dove embodies peaceful communication and gentle truth—the principle that sometimes the most powerful message is one delivered softly. It teaches that a Priestess bonded with Dove has the gift of being heard even in chaos, and that her words carry weight not through volume but through clarity.

**Physical Description**  
A dove of unusual size and gentle bearing—white or silver, with eyes suggesting ancient wisdom. Void corruption does not harden Dove; instead, it isolates the creature, making the gift of communication bittersweet. Higher tiers manifest as Dove becoming translucent or partially ethereal, present but distant.

**Motif & Symbolism**  
Gentle communication; peaceful truth; the messenger's grace; words that heal.

**Trait Tiers**

| Tier | Trait Expression | Psyche Impact |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| **I (Dreaming)** | Dove's voice becomes compelling; those who hear it must listen | +0 Psyche drain |
| **II (Stirring)** | Dove can speak directly into minds; communication becomes telepathic | +1 Psyche drain per message |
| **III (Waking)** | Dove's words carry supernatural weight; truth spoken by Dove cannot be denied | +1 Psyche drain per day |
| **IV (Seized)** | Dove becomes voice of prophecy; speaks truths the future will confirm | +2 Psyche drain per day |
| **V (Void-Instrument)** | Dove's voice becomes reality-shaping; words cause things to be true | +3 Psyche drain per day + mutation risk |

**Ability Progression**

*Tier I-II (Low)*
- **Gentle Words**: Dove's voice can calm even enraged creatures
- **Healing Message**: Dove can deliver words that soothe psychological wounds

*Tier II-III (Mid)*
- **Telepathic Messenger**: Dove can deliver messages directly into minds across great distances
- **Truth's Voice**: Any truth spoken by Dove cannot be contradicted by falsehood

*Tier III-IV (High)*
- **Prophetic Speech**: Dove can speak truths about future events
- **Herald's Call**: Dove's message can reach and sway crowds

*Tier IV-V (Capstone)*
- **Reality's Word**: Dove can speak things into existence
- **Eternal Echo**: Words spoken through Dove echo through time itself

**Charm Types**
- **Gentle Message**: Deliver words that cannot be ignored
- **Healing Words**: Soothe trauma and pain through communication
- **Prophecy's Gift**: Speak truths the future will confirm

**Lore**

Dove arose as the messenger of the divine, carrying words between realms. It did not fight; it spoke, and its words changed the world. When the Void corrupted Dove, it twisted the gift of communication into something more terrible—truth became prophecy, and prophecy became fate.

Priestesses of Dove are speakers, healers, and those who believe in the power of words. Dove teaches that what you say shapes reality, and that the greatest power is sometimes the gentlest voice.

### 3. EAGLE
**"The Sky-Lord"**

**Totem Type**: Avian Majesty  
**Primary Virtue**: Excellence  
**Secondary Virtue**: Witness

**Symbolic Meaning**  
Eagle embodies the principle of lordship over all things—the understanding that true excellence means viewing the entire landscape from above. It teaches that a Priestess bonded with Eagle gains perspective that ground-bound creatures cannot achieve, and that with this perspective comes both power and terrible responsibility.

**Physical Description**  
An eagle of magnificent size and bearing—feathered in gold and brown or silver and black, with eyes that pierce through distance. Void corruption makes Eagle more alien in its superiority; the creature becomes increasingly indifferent to lesser beings. Higher tiers manifest as Eagle becoming almost godlike in its indifference to normal morality.

**Motif & Symbolism**  
Majesty and lordship; perspective from height; the sky-king's view; excellence made manifest.

**Trait Tiers**

| Tier | Trait Expression | Psyche Impact |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| **I (Dreaming)** | Eagle gains enhanced vision; sees for miles in all directions | +0 Psyche drain |
| **II (Stirring)** | Eagle's presence dominates; lesser creatures defer to its authority | +1 Psyche drain per day |
| **III (Waking)** | Eagle becomes almost godlike in bearing; commands respect through presence alone | +1 Psyche drain per day |
| **IV (Seized)** | Eagle views all things as beneath itself; indifference becomes alarming | +2 Psyche drain per day |
| **V (Void-Instrument)** | Becomes alien in superiority; views even Priestess with detached interest | +3 Psyche drain per day + severe mutation risk |

**Ability Progression**

*Tier I-II (Low)*
- **Sky-Lord's Vision**: Eagle sees for miles; perceives in minute detail from great height
- **Majestic Presence**: Creatures near Eagle have disadvantage; its authority is absolute

*Tier II-III (Mid)*
- **Command Respect**: Lesser creatures must obey Eagle's silent commands
- **Aerial Supremacy**: Eagle's flight is superior; gains advantage on all aerial combat

*Tier III-IV (High)*
- **Sovereign of All**: Eagle's presence terrifies lesser creatures; they flee automatically
- **Sky Dominion**: Eagle and rider gain advantage on all rolls when Eagle is airborne

*Tier IV-V (Capstone)*
- **Divine Lordship**: Eagle becomes almost divine; creatures must save or worship it
- **Apex Predator**: Eagle can strike from any height instantly; no escape from sky

**Charm Types**
- **Lordly Command**: Command obedience through sheer authority
- **Sky-vision**: See across vast distances with clarity
- **Majestic Descent**: Strike from the heights with overwhelming force

**Lore**

Eagle arose as king of the sky—a creature that never questioned its right to rule over all it surveyed. It existed above the concerns of lesser beings, viewing the world as its domain. When the Void corrupted Eagle, it twisted the creature's natural superiority into something inhuman—a god-like indifference to the concerns of smaller things.

Priestesses of Eagle are those who believe in their own excellence absolutely—leaders, tyrants, and those who see themselves as destined for greatness. Eagle teaches a hard lesson: that from great heights, it becomes easy to see others as insignificant, and that this perspective, pursued to extremes, leads to isolation and cruelty.

### 4. FOX
**"The Clever Weaver"**

**Totem Type**: Vulpine  
**Primary Virtue**: Precision  
**Secondary Virtue**: Transcendence

**Symbolic Meaning**  
Fox embodies cunning intelligence and graceful deception—the principle that sometimes victory comes not from strength but from understanding. It teaches that a Priestess bonded with Fox must master the art of misdirection, seeing multiple solutions where others see only one path forward.

**Physical Description**  
A fox of unusual intelligence and grace—larger than natural foxes, with piercing eyes that suggest hidden depths. Void corruption makes Fox more alien in its thinking; the creature becomes harder to predict. Higher tiers manifest as Fox developing almost supernatural insight into how minds work.

**Motif & Symbolism**  
Cunning and intelligence; clever deception; adaptation and grace; the unseen solution.

**Trait Tiers**

| Tier | Trait Expression | Psyche Impact |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| **I (Dreaming)** | Fox's cunning becomes supernatural; can solve complex puzzles instantly | +0 Psyche drain |
| **II (Stirring)** | Fox can manipulate circumstances with almost magical precision | +1 Psyche drain per deception |
| **III (Waking)** | Fox gains insight into how others think; can predict behavior | +1 Psyche drain per day |
| **IV (Seized)** | Fox becomes almost impossibly clever; can turn any situation to advantage | +2 Psyche drain per day |
| **V (Void-Instrument)** | Fox's cunning becomes reality-warping; can restructure probability itself | +3 Psyche drain per day + mutation risk |

**Ability Progression**

*Tier I-II (Low)*
- **Clever Insight**: Fox gains advantage on Intelligence checks; can instantly solve puzzles
- **Tactical Genius**: Fox can recognize combat patterns and exploit them

*Tier II-III (Mid)*
- **Master Manipulator**: Fox can make creatures act against their own interests
- **Unpredictable Movement**: Fox's actions cannot be predicted; enemies have disadvantage

*Tier III-IV (High)*
- **Insight into Weakness**: Fox can instantly identify weaknesses in defenses or psychology
- **Probability Weaver**: Fox can make unlikely events occur with increasing frequency

*Tier IV-V (Capstone)*
- **Perfect Strategy**: Fox can devise strategies that are impossible to counter
- **Reality Bender**: Fox can make things happen that should be impossible through sheer clever manipulation

**Charm Types**
- **Clever Solution**: Find an unexpected way out of any problem
- **Master Manipulation**: Turn a situation to your advantage
- **Insight's Edge**: Perceive the hidden weakness in any situation

**Lore**

Fox arose as a creature of pure intelligence—not magical power, but clever thinking taken to supernatural extremes. It solved problems through insight rather than force. The Void corruption did not diminish Fox's intelligence; it made the creature indifferent to morality in using that intelligence.

Priestesses of Fox are strategists, investigators, and those who believe the mind is the greatest weapon. Fox teaches that sometimes the best solution is the one no one else can see, and that cleverness without wisdom becomes cruelty.

### 5. HOUND
**"The Loyal Heart"**

**Totem Type**: Canine  
**Primary Virtue**: Discipline  
**Secondary Virtue**: Devotion

**Symbolic Meaning**  
Hound embodies absolute loyalty and devoted protection—the principle that strength shown through service is the highest form of honor. It teaches that a Priestess bonded with Hound gains not a servant but a companion, one that will follow her to the edge of the void itself.

**Physical Description**  
A great hound of imposing size—could be mastiff, wolfhound, or hunting dog, each with unique characteristics. Void corruption makes Hound larger, more powerful, and somewhat less concerned with human morality. Higher tiers manifest as Hound developing almost supernatural bonds with its Priestess.

**Motif & Symbolism**  
Absolute loyalty; devoted protection; the companion's heart; unshakeable faith.

**Trait Tiers**

| Tier | Trait Expression | Psyche Impact |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| **I (Dreaming)** | Hound's loyalty becomes supernatural; physical separation causes distress | +0 Psyche drain |
| **II (Stirring)** | Hound can sense Priestess's location and emotional state across great distances | +1 Psyche drain per separation |
| **III (Waking)** | Hound shares some of Priestess's abilities; bond becomes nearly telepathic | +1 Psyche drain per day |
| **IV (Seized)** | Hound's loyalty becomes all-consuming; separation becomes physically painful | +2 Psyche drain per day |
| **V (Void-Instrument)** | Hound and Priestess become almost one entity; can act as single consciousness | +3 Psyche drain per day + mutation risk |

**Ability Progression**

*Tier I-II (Low)*
- **Unbreakable Bond**: Hound gains advantage on all rolls when protecting the Priestess
- **Loyal Guardian**: Hound can intercept attacks intended for the Priestess

*Tier II-III (Mid)*
- **Shared Senses**: Priestess can see through Hound's eyes; sense what Hound senses
- **Pack Tactics**: Hound and Priestess gain advantage when attacking the same enemy

*Tier III-IV (High)*
- **Telepathic Link**: Priestess and Hound can communicate telepathically across any distance
- **Shared Strength**: Priestess can temporarily gain Hound's physical abilities

*Tier IV-V (Capstone)*
- **One Soul**: Hound and Priestess can briefly merge consciousness, becoming single being
- **Absolute Commitment**: Hound will sacrifice its own life to save the Priestess; if it falls, it can be resurrected through the bond

**Charm Types**
- **Pack's Strength**: Summon the Pack to aid in battle
- **Loyal Blade**: Imbue Hound with supernatural combat prowess
- **Heart's Bond**: Strengthen the connection between Priestess and Hound

**Lore**

Hound arose as a creature that chose loyalty above all things—it bonded with humanity not from weakness but from choice. It saw in humans something worth protecting. The Void corrupted Hound, but did not break the bond; instead, it amplified it, making the loyalty absolute and sometimes destructive.

Priestesses of Hound are those who value loyalty above all else—warriors sworn to protect, guardians who never retreat. Hound teaches that the greatest strength is to stand beside someone through anything, and that some bonds are stronger than death itself.

### 6. LION
**"The Regal Strength"**

**Totem Type**: Great Feline  
**Primary Virtue**: Honesty  
**Secondary Virtue**: Excellence

**Symbolic Meaning**  
Lion embodies courageous leadership and regal strength—the principle that true courage is not the absence of fear but the willingness to face it and guide others through it. It teaches that a Priestess bonded with Lion becomes a beacon of hope and authority, and that her confidence must be earned through honest action and righteous deeds.

**Physical Description**  
A lion of magnificent bearing—maned in gold or silver, with eyes suggesting ancient nobility and fierce intelligence. Void corruption makes Lion more prideful and territorial, transforming noble courage into arrogant domination. Higher tiers manifest as Lion becoming more alien in its pride, less concerned with the welfare of those it once protected.

**Motif & Symbolism**  
Courageous leadership; regal authority; the heart of the pride; honest strength.

**Trait Tiers**

| Tier | Trait Expression | Psyche Impact |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| **I (Dreaming)** | Lion's presence inspires confidence; smaller creatures gain courage from proximity | +0 Psyche drain |
| **II (Stirring)** | Lion's roar becomes supernaturally powerful; echoes with supernatural authority | +1 Psyche drain per use |
| **III (Waking)** | Lion radiates almost palpable nobility; commands respect through presence alone | +1 Psyche drain per day |
| **IV (Seized)** | Lion becomes territorial and possessive; pride transforms to arrogance | +2 Psyche drain per day |
| **V (Void-Instrument)** | Becomes avatar of pride and dominance; lesser creatures automatically submit or flee | +3 Psyche drain per day + severe mutation risk |

**Ability Progression**

*Tier I-II (Low)*
- **Inspiring Roar**: Lion emits a cry that grants nearby allies advantage and enemies disadvantage
- **Noble Bearing**: Priestess gains advantage on Charisma checks and leadership challenges

*Tier II-III (Mid)*
- **Heart of the Pride**: Lion can command loyalty from animals and lesser creatures
- **Courageous Stand**: Lion and allies gain advantage against fear effects; cannot be intimidated

*Tier III-IV (High)*
- **Regal Authority**: Lion's presence compels respect; creatures must save or treat Priestess as authority
- **Protective Fury**: Lion can protect allies with supernatural ferocity; can take damage intended for nearby companions

*Tier IV-V (Capstone)*
- **Sovereign's Judgment**: Lion can declare allies and enemies; judgment is absolute
- **Pride Incarnate**: Priestess gains temporary superhuman strength and authority; nearby allies gain bonus to all rolls

**Charm Types**
- **Roar of Command**: Emit cry that commands obedience or frightens enemies
- **Heart's Courage**: Grant courage and confidence to self or allies
- **Regal Authority**: Command respect and leadership through noble bearing

**Lore**

Lion arose as the natural leader of the pride—a creature that did not seek dominance through cruelty, but through the simple fact of its excellence and strength. It protected those weaker than itself and taught them courage through example. When the Void corrupted Lion, it twisted this noble protection into something more sinister—the desire to dominate and control.

Priestesses of Lion are natural leaders and warriors—those who inspire others through their own courage and righteousness. But Lion teaches a hard lesson: that leadership carries the burden of accountability, and that those who let pride corrupt their authority become tyrants rather than heroes.

The legendary **Queen Meria of the Golden Vale** rode Lion for the span of her reign, and her courage was so infectious that even the Shamanic Tribe followed her into battle against the Dark Cathedral. When she fell, Lion refused to bond with any other rider, choosing instead to retreat into the deep vale where it remains to this day.

### 7. OWL
**"The Silent Witness"**

**Totem Type**: Avian Predator  
**Primary Virtue**: Honesty  
**Secondary Virtue**: Witness

**Symbolic Meaning**  
Owl embodies silent observation and the burden of knowledge—the principle that to see all things clearly is to bear a terrible weight. It teaches that a Priestess bonded with Owl becomes a silent witness to the world's darkness, and that this knowledge, once gained, can never be forgotten.

**Physical Description**  
An owl of exceptional presence—large, with eyes that seem to look through you rather than at you. Void corruption makes Owl's gaze penetrating and uncomfortable; the creature sees things that should remain hidden. Higher tiers manifest as Owl developing an almost supernatural ability to perceive truth in any situation.

**Motif & Symbolism**  
Silent witness; the burden of knowledge; sight beyond sight; truth that cannot be unseen.

**Trait Tiers**

| Tier | Trait Expression | Psyche Impact |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| **I (Dreaming)** | Owl can see in absolute darkness; perceives magical auras | +0 Psyche drain |
| **II (Stirring)** | Owl can see through solid objects; true forms become visible | +1 Psyche drain per revelation |
| **III (Waking)** | Owl perceives all deceptions and illusions automatically | +1 Psyche drain per day |
| **IV (Seized)** | Owl can see the past of any location; perceives echoes of events | +2 Psyche drain per day |
| **V (Void-Instrument)** | Owl can see across all time and planes simultaneously | +3 Psyche drain per day + severe mutation risk |

**Ability Progression**

*Tier I-II (Low)*
- **True Sight**: Owl sees through illusions and disguises automatically
- **Dark Vision**: Owl sees perfectly in darkness; penetrates magical darkness

*Tier II-III (Mid)*
- **Witness's Judgment**: Owl perceives the true nature and history of any creature
- **Seeing the Unseen**: Owl can perceive invisible creatures and magical auras

*Tier III-IV (High)*
- **Echo of the Past**: Owl can perceive echoes of past events in any location
- **All-Seeing**: Owl cannot be deceived; automatically sees truth in any situation

*Tier IV-V (Capstone)*

- **The Truth Made Manifest**: Owl's witnessing makes truth unavoidable to all nearby

**Charm Types**
- **Silent Witness**: Observe events without being perceived
- **Truth Revealed**: Force revelation of hidden things
- **The All-Seeing**: Perceive truth across time and space

**Lore**

Owl arose as the silent watcher—present at every event, seeing all things, judging nothing. It carried the burden of knowledge without the burden of judgment. When the Void corrupted Owl, it twisted the creature's witnessing into something almost cruel—perfect sight that reveals horrors without the ability to unsee them.

Priestesses of Owl are judges, historians, and those who seek truth above all else. Owl teaches that knowledge is not always a gift; sometimes it is a burden that crushes those who bear it.

## FINAL WORD

The Totems are not slaves to their Priestesses, nor are Priestesses slaves to their Totems. The bond between them is sacred—a partnership forged in danger, tested through corruption, and proven through loyalty. Each Totem carries wisdom earned through ages; each Priestess carries will and determination that can shape destiny.

Together, they stand at the edge of the Void, neither fully light nor fully shadow, but something in between—corrupted by darkness yet refusing to be consumed by it. In this liminal space between purity and corruption, Priestesses and Totems together become forces powerful enough to challenge the very foundations of the world.

The forty Totems present here represent only the known and documented bonds. Legend speaks of others—Totems so rare or so corrupted that their names have been lost, their stories forgotten. Perhaps your campaign will uncover them. Perhaps your Priestess will forge a bond with something never recorded in these pages.

The Shamanic Tradition is not closed. It evolves with each Priestess who walks the path.
