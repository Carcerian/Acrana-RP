# CYCLOPEDIA OF ARTIFACTS
## A Compendium of Legendary Objects and Their Banes

---

## INTRODUCTION

While the Tome anchors spiritual power through totem bonds and the Grimoire grounds that power in claw and fang, the Cyclopedia of Artifacts extends the reach of magic into the material world itself. Artifacts are legendary objects imbued with supernatural power—weapons, talismans, vessels, and relics that carry the weight of story, divine blessing, or fell corruption.

This Cyclopedia contains one hundred artifacts divided into two balanced groups:
- **50 Virtuous & Thematic Artifacts:** Anchored to the Eight Virtues, designed to uplift, protect, and empower
- **50 Fell & Antithetical Artifacts:** Embodying opposition, corruption, and entropy; each serving as the nemesis of a Virtuous counterpart

Each artifact includes complete mechanical statting, attunement rituals, geas mechanics (resistable with penalties), unique banes with counter-Virtue workarounds, and lore tying artifacts to characters, locations, and campaign events.

## PART 2: ARTIFACT MECHANICS
### Core Systems for Activation, Attunement, and Banes

---

### HOW ARTIFACTS WORK

An artifact is a legendary object that requires **Attunement** to use its full power. Attunement involves a ritual, a cost (typically Psyche, gold, or a sacrifice), and a supernatural link between wielder and object.

**Key Principle:** Artifacts operate independently from totem bonding but can stack with totem effects and character abilities. A priestess can equip an artifact AND bond her totem—they don't interfere.

---

### ARTIFACT ATTRIBUTES

Every artifact has:

1. **Power Level (1-20):** Scale of overall power, determines artifact rarity, activation difficulty, and bane potency
2. **Virtue Alignment:** Single Virtue (8-16 pure artifacts) or Virtue combo (rest of 50 Virtuous)
3. **Origin:** Sacred, Divine/Supernatural, Fell/Dark, Saintly/Heroic/Infamous
4. **Activation Type:** Ritual (requires time/cost per use) or Always-On (passive benefit)
5. **Attunement Ritual:** Required ceremony, cost, and time to bond artifact to wielder
6. **Geas:** Compulsion, requirement, or restriction the artifact imposes (resistable)
7. **Unique Bane:** Specific weakness or nemesis artifact designed to counter it
8. **Creator/Bearer:** NPC or historical figure tied to artifact; grants lore hooks

---

### ATTUNEMENT SYSTEM

**Attunement Ritual (Universal Progression):**

All artifacts follow this progression:

**Step 1: Contact (1 hour, no cost)**
Creature must hold the artifact and meditate/commune with it for 1 hour. At end, must make Wisdom save (DC = 10 + artifact Power Level) to sense the artifact's nature and history.

**Step 2: Negotiation (Ritual, 1-4 hours)**
Creature must complete attunement ritual specific to artifact (varies by type; typically involves offerings, meditation, or combat trials). Cost is paid during this step.

**Step 3: Bond (Instant)**
On successful ritual completion, artifact bonds to creature. Creature gains all activated abilities and passives. Until attuned, artifact is inert (no abilities function).

---

### ATTUNEMENT COSTS

All artifacts have a cost to attune. Common costs include:

**Psyche Cost:** Reduce creature's Psyche pool by specified amount. Recovered through meditation/rest.
- Low-level (PL 1-5): 1d4 Psyche
- Mid-level (PL 6-12): 1d6 Psyche
- High-level (PL 13+): 1d8 Psyche

**Gold/Wealth Cost:** Payment in coin, gems, or valuables. Permanent.
- Low-level: 100-500 gold
- Mid-level: 500-5000 gold
- High-level: 5000+ gold (or priceless items)

**Sacrifice Cost:** Permanent loss or transformation. Most dangerous.
- Physical: Sacrifice body part (finger, eye, hair), suffers permanent disadvantage or pain
- Mental: Sacrifice memory or personality trait; gain Tainted condition
- Spiritual: Sacrifice a totem bond (rare, forbidden); loses all totem bonuses temporarily

**Shared Cost:** Multiple creatures can attune to single artifact. Each pays full cost. Artifact can serve multiple wielders but only one at a time can use activation abilities.

---

### GEAS SYSTEM

A **Geas** is a magical compulsion or restriction imposed by the artifact. Unlike attunement, which is consensual, a geas is a constraint that comes with using the artifact's power. A creature CAN resist a geas, but doing so imposes a penalty.

**Geas Mechanics:**

When a creature using an artifact is required to follow its geas (e.g., "protect the innocent"), they can:
- **Comply:** Follow the geas. No penalties.
- **Resist (Wisdom Save):** DC = 10 + artifact Power Level. On success, ignore the geas for 1 turn.
  - **Penalty on Failure:** Creature gains Tainted condition and loses 1d4 Psyche
  - **Cost on Success:** Creature suffers disadvantage on all saves and attacks for 1 minute (resisting the magical compulsion takes effort)

**Geas Examples:**

- **Sword of Honesty:** Wielder cannot lie and must speak truth when directly asked. Geas: "Speak only truth."
- **Cloak of Shadows:** Wearer becomes invisible in darkness. Geas: "Hide your true nature; reveal yourself only in light."
- **Ring of Ambition:** Grants power to pursue goals. Geas: "Advance your goals at any cost; sacrifice allies if necessary."

---

### RESISTANCE & VULNERABILITY

Artifacts can be **Resisted** when their effects target a creature or location:

**Resisting an Artifact Effect:**

When an artifact attempts to affect a creature or location, the target can make a Wisdom or Constitution save (varies by artifact). On success:
- Effect is reduced to half potency
- Condition doesn't apply (or is delayed 1 turn)
- Damage is negated

**Attunement Penalty for Resistance:**

If a creature actively resists an artifact's effect while attuned to it, they suffer:
- Disadvantage on the resistance save
- Automatic failure if they resist more than once per turn
- Tainted condition for 1 hour (cumulative if multiple resistances)

This represents the artifact fighting back when wielder tries to override its compulsion.

### ACTIVATION MECHANICS

**Ritual Activation (costs action + time):**
Creature must spend action + 1 minute ritual (typically meditation, chanting, or physical action) to activate artifact ability. Can be used once per short rest at low levels; more frequently at higher levels.

**At-Will Activation (costs action):**
Creature can activate artifact ability using action (costs no resource). Can be used multiple times per round.

**Always-On (passive):**
Artifact benefit is constant and requires no activation. Creature always benefits, even while sleeping.

**3/Turn (bonus action trigger):**
Creature can use artifact ability 3 times per round using bonus action (or reaction for some triggers).

**Unlimited (passive + reactive):**
Highest-tier artifacts grant multiple benefits simultaneously and can be triggered unlimited times.

## APPENDIX B: FINDING ARTIFACTS IN WORLD

### Artifact Locations by Power Level & Rarity

**Tier 1-2 Artifacts (Common, Uncommon):** 
- Found in adventurer shops, wealthy merchant caravans, minor temples
- NPCs can carry without difficulty; not legendary

**Tier 3-4 Artifacts (Rare, Very Rare):**
- Guarded in noble vaults, hidden in dungeons, possessed by rival priestesses
- Require quests or combat to obtain

**Tier 5 Artifacts (Legendary):**
- Held by major NPCs (high priestess, dragon lord, demon prince)
- Quests spanning multiple sessions; defended by monsters and wards

**Tier 6-7 Artifacts (Artifact Rarity, Divine/Fell):**
- Unique in world; may only be one in existence
- Defended by divine/fell forces; attainment requires epic quest or sacrifice
