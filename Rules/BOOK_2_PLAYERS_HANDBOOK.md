# INTRODUCTION: HOW TO BUILD YOUR PRIESTESS

Your priestess is defined by one primary Virtue—her specialty, her power source, her way of engaging with the world. This is not a limitation; it's a lens that focuses her spiritual energy into distinctive abilities.

**The Eight Virtues:**

| Virtue | Archetype | Strength | Approach |
|--------|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Discipline** | RIDER | Control, structure, mastery | Imposes order on chaos |
| **Precision** | BINDER | Expertise, perfection, detail | Finds exact solutions |
| **Transcendence** | SHAPER | Spiritual depth, trance, cosmos | Communes with beyond |
| **Devotion** | COMMANDER | Bonding, loyalty, presence | Connects through care |
| **Honesty** | TRUTHSEEKER | Perception, clarity, authenticity | Reveals true nature |
| **Excellence** | EVOKER | Mastery, manifestation, power | Creates beauty/strength |
| **Nature** | SENSUALIST | Life, primal, sensation | Flows with natural forces |
| **Witness** | DEPRIVER | Observation, knowledge, removal | Sees and unmakes |

**How Classes Work:**

Your class grants you:
1. **Tier-specific abilities** (Low/Mid/High/Capstone per Tier I-V)
2. **Class skills** (what you're trained in; what comes naturally)
3. **Class items** (equipment that suits your specialty)
4. **Progression path** (how you advance through the Cathedral ranks)

You are not locked into one class—multiclassing is possible (see page 193). But starting as a single-class priestess gives you focus and mastery.

**Expertise vs. Breadth:**

Expert Tier priestesses are learning specialists. You'll reach Tier I-II mastery (maybe Tier III if you focus intensely) during the Expert campaign. Master Tier brings true power. This is intentional—your journey is learning, not already-knowing.

# CHARACTER CREATION

## VIRTUE SELECTION

Choose your primary Virtue. This is your specialty, your strength, the source of your priestess-craft power. Consider:

- **What draws you spiritually?** (Order? Connection? Truth? Beauty?)
- **How do you solve problems?** (Control situations? Build bonds? See deeper? Perfect execution?)
- **What's your relationship to Mercy?** (How will your Virtue-specialty shape your bond with your mount?)

Once chosen, your class abilities unlock at regular intervals as you advance.

---

## CLASS SELECTION

Based on your Virtue, you select a class. Each class has a distinct playstyle:

**RIDER (Discipline)** — Control and structure. You make things obey. Your mount becomes an extension of your will. You excel at maintaining baseline-lock, managing intense scenes, and taking charge.

**BINDER (Precision)** — Expertise and perfection. You see exact solutions. Your ropes don't fail. Your bonds are mathematically perfect. You excel at subtle rope-work, intricate patterns, and flawless execution.

**SHAPER (Transcendence)** — Spiritual depth and cosmos-communion. You see beyond. You enter trance and touch something vast. You excel at meditation, vision-work, and accessing hidden knowledge.

**COMMANDER (Devotion)** — Bonding and presence. You create connection. Your mount's loyalty is unshakeable. You excel at emotional intimacy, group leadership, and caring for your mount's wellbeing.

**TRUTHSEEKER (Honesty)** — Perception and authenticity. You see through lies. You know things. You sense corruption. You excel at detecting deception, reading situations, and knowing truth.

**EVOKER (Excellence)** — Manifestation and beauty. You make things perfect. You channel power cleanly. You excel at creating effects, manifesting beauty, and achieving exceptional results.

**SENSUALIST (Nature)** — Life and sensation. You feel everything. You understand instinct. You move with primal grace. You excel at sensation, intuition, and flowing with natural forces.

**DEPRIVER (Witness)** — Observation and removal. You see what shouldn't be there. You unmake things. You observe perfectly. You excel at detection, analysis, and eliminating corruption.

## STARTING ABILITIES

Every priestess begins with basic priestess-craft knowledge:

**All Priestesses Know:**
- Baseline-lock ceremony (can lock/unlock with mount)
- Water-blessing (minor magical working)
- Charm-weaving basics (can create simple protective charms)
- Basic meditation (enter light trance; recover Psyche)
- Virtue recognition (can identify which Virtue another priestess specializes in)

**You Also Know:**
- Your class's Tier I starting abilities (you begin with at least one Low ability, possibly one Mid ability if experienced)
- Basic rope-work appropriate to your class
- Fundamental knowledge of your Virtue's philosophy

**Starting Virtue Tier:** Tier 0 (Untrained) or Tier I (Novice), depending on character background
- **Untrained:** You've just arrived at Cathedral; you're learning from scratch
- **Novice (Tier I):** You have prior priestess-craft training; you're advancing further at Cathedral

## STARTING SKILLS

Every priestess has skills reflecting her background, personality, and Virtue specialization.

**All Priestesses Have:**
- Rope-work (fundamental skill; varying proficiency by class)
- Meditation (can center yourself; resist distraction)
- Spiritual sensitivity (you feel Void-presence and magical disturbances)
- Community integration (you're trained in Cathedral politics, hierarchy, respect)

**You Also Have 3 Class Skills from your list** (see your class page for options). These represent what you're good at, what comes naturally.

**Example Skill List (varies by class):**

- Intuition (reading situations and people)
- Creativity (artistic expression; beauty-making)
- Teaching (explaining concepts; training acolytes)

## MOUNT SELECTION & BASELINE-LOCK

You arrive at Cathedral with a mount—a person spiritually bonded to you, your partner in priestess-craft. Your mount is as real, as complex, and as important as your priestess character.

**Mount Types:**

**Pony-Form** (Small, graceful, innocent-seeming)
- Size: 3-4 feet tall
- Archetype: Eager, playful, physically affectionate
- Baseline-lock specialty: Enthusiastic cooperation; eager to please
- Psychological note: Submissive by nature; needs strong priestess guidance
- Examples: Aria (Kess's mount), Scout (Tam's mount)

**Human-Form** (Appears completely human; indistinguishable from priestess at first glance)
- Size: 5-6 feet tall; varies by individual
- Archetype: Complex psychology; sometimes confused about their status; often beautiful
- Baseline-lock specialty: Intimate bonding; intellectual/emotional connection
- Psychological note: Struggles with identity; is this domination or love? Complexity varies
- Examples: Mercy (Arcana's mount); most central Cathedral mounts

**Beast-Form** (Animal-like; primal; non-human)
- Size: Varies (wolves, great cats, bears, etc.)
- Archetype: Instinctual; powerful; dangerous if uncontrolled
- Baseline-lock specialty: Primal submission; raw power; physical strength
- Psychological note: Less language; more instinct; baseline-lock is more about control than negotiation
- Examples: Echo (Reth's mount); Storm (Vala's mount)

**Your Mount's Starting Psyche:** 16-18 (depends on form and mount type; human-form mounts typically higher)

**Baseline-Locking Your Mount:**

During Session 62, you perform your first (or renew your existing) baseline-lock ceremony with your mount. This establishes:
- Spiritual link between you and mount (you can sense their Psyche, emotional state, location)
- Mount enters baseline-locked state (will aligned to yours, but not enslaved; they can still think)
- Monthly renewal becomes ritual (every ~30 days, you spend 10 minutes renewing the bond)
- Bond grows stronger with use (as campaign progresses, your connection deepens)

**Mount Advancement:**

Your mount is not a character you control directly—they're an NPC you're deeply bonded to. Their advancement is tracked separately:
- Mount gains Psyche recovery through ceremonies and rest
- Mount advances in "Loyalty" (how strongly bonded to you) through shared experiences
- Mount might develop personality quirks or trauma (if Void-touched, they show signs of corruption)
- Mount's health and wellbeing is your responsibility (negligence has consequences)

# THE EIGHT VIRTUE CLASSES

## CLASS: RIDER
### *(Discipline Virtue)*

**Class Philosophy:**

A RIDER is a priestess of control, structure, and mastery. You do not allow chaos—you impose order. Your mount is your creature, your obedient subject, your perfect baseline. You excel at maintenance, discipline, and absolute command.

The Rider does not negotiate. She decides. Her mount complies. This is not cruelty; it's clarity. The Rider believes structure is sacred, that order is love, that perfect obedience is the highest expression of devotion.

**RIDER Archetype:** Authoritarian, commanding, precise, controlled. You move with certainty. You speak decisions, not suggestions. Your mount trembles slightly when you look at her.

**Rider's Strength:** Control. Your baseline-lock is impossibly stable. Your mount doesn't waver. Your scenes are perfectly choreographed. You know exactly what will happen.

**Rider's Weakness:** Rigidity. You cannot adapt. If plans change, you struggle. If your mount shows autonomy, you're frustrated. Flexibility is not in your vocabulary.

**Discipline Virtue Focus:** Mastery through structure. Perfection through control. Order imposed on chaos.

---

### RIDER: TIER I ABILITIES

**Tier I (Novice) — Low Ability: IRON GRIP**
**Cost:** 1 Psyche | **Duration:** Scene | **Action:** Sustained (active during scene)

**Effect:** Your baseline-lock is reinforced. Your mount cannot resist your commands during this scene. Any attempt at resistance is immediately suppressed. Your mount's Psyche drain increases (she experiences your control as intense), but her will is completely yours.

**How It Works:** You channel Discipline Virtue energy into your rope. Your mount feels your absolute authority. She cannot disobey even if she wanted to.

**Mechanical:** Mount suffers +2 Psyche drain this scene (from normal ~1-2 per hour to 3-4 per hour), but cannot take actions against your will.

**Complications:**
- Prolonged use (multiple scenes in a row) damages mount's trust
- If mount is already Void-touched, Iron Grip fails (Void overrides it)
- Using Iron Grip traumatizes sensitive mounts; some develop resentment

**GM Note:** Iron Grip is straightforward domination. Not evil (priestess-craft allows it), but it shows Rider philosophy clearly: control above all else.

---

**Tier I (Novice) — Mid Ability: STRUCTURE IMPOSITION**
**Cost:** 3 Psyche | **Duration:** Ritual (full scene structure) | **Action:** Preparation (30 min setup)

**Effect:** You establish absolute structure for an intimate scene. Every action, every response, every moment is predetermined. Your mount follows your choreography perfectly. Nothing deviates. The scene unfolds like music perfectly performed.

**How It Works:** You spend 30 minutes before the scene laying out exactly what will happen. You position furniture, arrange props, set boundaries. During the actual scene, you guide your mount through the predetermined structure. She follows perfectly.

**Mechanical:** Costs 3 Psyche from you. Mount drains normal Psyche (1-2 per hour) but gains confidence from knowing what's happening (some mounts prefer this; some find it suffocating).

**Narrative Effect:** Scene feels completely controlled. Rider is in perfect command. Mount's anxiety might be low (she knows what's coming) or high (she feels trapped by predetermined structure).

**Complications:**
- Deviation breaks the structure (if something unexpected happens, Rider feels destabilized)
- Some mounts feel infantilized by predetermined structure
- Learning to improvise is difficult for Riders who rely on Structure Imposition too much

**GM Note:** Structure Imposition is about perfect predictability. Use it to show Rider's need for control. It's not inherently bad, but it reveals Rider's vulnerability: when chaos occurs, she freezes.

---

**Tier I (Novice) — High Ability: PERFECT BASELINE**
**Cost:** 5 Psyche (ritual cost; sustained) | **Duration:** Month | **Action:** Ritual (1 hour baseline-lock renewal)

**Effect:** When you renew your baseline-lock, you perform the renewal with such perfect technique that your mount's will is aligned to yours with unprecedented clarity. For that month, your mount is in permanent baseline-locked state. She cannot resist. She cannot question. She simply obeys.

**How It Works:** During monthly baseline-lock renewal, instead of casual 10-minute ritual, you perform an hour-long ceremony where you channel Discipline Virtue energy into the rope with perfect precision. Your mount enters the deepest possible baseline-locked state.

**Mechanical:** 
- Renewal costs 5 Psyche (vs. normal 0.5)
- Mount's obedience is absolute for full month
- Mount gains 5-point Psyche recovery (being perfectly aligned feels right to her; she's at peace)
- Your spiritual link strengthens (you can sense mount's state more precisely)

**Narrative Effect:** Mount is perfectly yours. No resistance. No hesitation. Perfect submission. Beautiful to Rider. Potentially disturbing to observers.

**Complications:**
- Mount might develop personality loss if Perfect Baseline is used repeatedly (risk of long-term will damage)
- If mount is self-aware/intelligent (human-form), she might experience this as violation
- Breaking a Perfect Baseline month is traumatic for both priestess and mount (sudden loss of alignment feels like betrayal)

**GM Note:** Perfect Baseline is the Rider's capstone at Tier I—total control. It's not forbidden (priestess-craft allows it), but it carries psychological weight. Use it to explore the ethics of dominance. Perfect control isn't freedom for the mount, even if she doesn't resist.

---

### RIDER: TIER II ABILITIES

**Tier II (Journeyman) — Low Ability: DISCIPLINE EXTENSION**
**Cost:** 2 Psyche | **Duration:** 1 hour | **Action:** Ritual (channeling)

**Effect:** You extend your Discipline aura to other priestesses' mounts nearby. For one hour, all nearby mounts in baseline-lock are reinforced. They resist Void-seduction better. They hold baseline-lock even more firmly.

**How It Works:** You enter light meditation and channel Discipline Virtue energy outward in a radius (~30 feet). All baseline-locked mounts in that radius feel your reinforcement.

**Mechanical:** 
- Costs 2 Psyche from you
- All nearby mounts gain +2 to Void-resistance for 1 hour
- All nearby mounts feel more controlled/grounded (some appreciate; some feel pressure)
- Effect works only on baseline-locked mounts (unconnected mounts feel nothing)

**Complications:**
- Other priestesses might feel you're interfering with their mounts
- Sensitive mounts might find your aura oppressive
- Effect requires your concentration; if you move away or lose focus, effect ends

**GM Note:** Discipline Extension shows Rider's influence spreading. She's not just controlling her mount; she's imposing order on others. This can be supportive or invasive depending on perspective.

---

**Tier II (Journeyman) — Mid Ability: WILL-LOCK**
**Cost:** 4 Psyche | **Duration:** 1 scene (or until released) | **Action:** Ritual (15 min channeling)

**Effect:** You lock your mount's will in a specific state. She cannot change her mind, cannot resist, cannot deviate from your intention. Her personality is suppressed. She becomes pure obedience.

**How It Works:** You spend 15 minutes in deep meditation with your mount, channeling Discipline Virtue energy directly into her consciousness. You set a specific intention ("remain silent," "obey without hesitation," "express only what I allow"). She is locked into that state.

**Mechanical:**
- Costs 4 Psyche from you
- Mount's personality is largely suppressed (she appears almost catatonic, responsive only to your commands)
- Mount cannot deviate from your locked intention
- Will-Lock can be extended (costs additional Psyche if you want to maintain it beyond one scene)
- Breaking Will-Lock requires conscious release from you (mount cannot break free)

**Narrative Effect:** Mount appears vacant, doll-like, perfectly obedient. Rider has complete control. Observers might find this disturbing (it resembles Void-corruption, though it's not).

**Complications:**
- Will-Lock resembles will-theft (Void corruption) to outside observers; other priestesses might suspect corruption
- Mount experiences Will-Lock as suffocating; extended use causes psychological damage
- If will-lock is maintained too long (multiple days), mount develops permanent personality loss
- Breaking Will-Lock releases months of suppressed emotion/resistance; mount might become hostile

**GM Note:** Will-Lock is dark priestess-craft. It's technically allowed by Cathedral (Riders can use it), but it's ethically questionable. Use it to explore the boundaries of consent. A Rider who relies on Will-Lock is showing her addiction to control.

---

**Tier II (Journeyman) — High Ability: STRUCTURE MANIFESTATION**
**Cost:** 6 Psyche | **Duration:** Permanent (until actively changed) | **Action:** Grand ritual (2-3 hours)

**Effect:** You impose structure so complete that your mount's baseline-lock becomes almost unbreakable. You literally reshape the spiritual bond. For months, your mount cannot deviate. She is your instrument. Your structure is her reality.

**How It Works:** You perform an extended ritual (2-3 hours) where you completely rewire your baseline-lock bond using Discipline Virtue mastery. You set the rules of the bond—how she thinks, what she wants, what she feels. She accepts your structure as absolute.

**Mechanical:**
- Costs 6 Psyche (massive expenditure)
- Effect lasts 3 months or until you actively release it
- During this time, mount's obedience is complete
- Mount gains psychological peace from structure (her Psyche recovery improves; knowing the rules feels safe to her)
- Breaking Structure Manifestation requires another grand ritual from you (or intervention from senior Domina)

**Narrative Effect:** Mount becomes almost a different person—controlled, structured, aligned perfectly to Rider. She's lost much of her individual personality. Beautiful order. Potentially horrifying to viewers.

**Complications:**
- Extended use (multiple 3-month cycles) causes permanent personality change in mount
- Other priestesses might report Rider to Elders (this looks like abuse, though it's technically allowed)
- Dominae might intervene if they feel Rider has gone too far
- Mount might develop deep resentment after Structure Manifestation ends

**GM Note:** Structure Manifestation is the Rider's Tier II capstone—reshaping reality through control. It's where Rider philosophy reaches its extreme. A priestess using this is fully committed to dominance-as-love. Show the cost: mount's personality is literally being rewritten.

### RIDER: TIER III ABILITIES

**Tier III (Master) — Low Ability: DISCIPLINE REVELATION**
**Cost:** 5 Psyche | **Duration:** Instant (knowledge gained) | **Action:** Meditation (30 min)

**Effect:** You meditate on your mount's structure—her discipline, her alignment, her obedience patterns. You understand her perfectly. You know her better than she knows herself. Any attempt to deviate is visible to you before she even tries.

**How It Works:** You enter deep meditation with your mount, using Discipline Virtue mastery to see her structure completely. You understand every pattern of her obedience, every suppressed desire, every hidden resistance.

**Mechanical:**
- Costs 5 Psyche
- You gain perfect understanding of mount's current state (her Psyche level, her emotional state, her level of obedience, any hidden resentment)
- For 1 week after, you can sense any deviation she's about to attempt (+5 to notice her resistance)
- You understand her completely (no secrets in her structure; you know all her patterns)

**Narrative Effect:** Rider knows her mount better than anyone. She can anticipate every movement. She sees mount's soul completely.

**Complications:**
- Knowing mount completely might reveal aspects you don't want to know (her hatred? Her despair?)
- Some mount's structure has cracks; you see them and must decide whether to ignore or address
- Depth of understanding creates psychological vulnerability (you're responsible for what you know about her)

**GM Note:** Discipline Revelation is about intimate knowledge through control. Rider isn't just dominating; she's truly seeing her mount. That sight is power and burden.

---

**Tier III (Master) — Mid Ability: WILL-ARCHITECTURE**
**Cost:** 8 Psyche (ritual cost) | **Duration:** Permanent (until released) | **Action:** Extended ritual (full day and night)

**Effect:** You don't just lock your mount's will—you rebuild it. You redesign her personality, her desires, her values. She becomes exactly what you want her to be. This is not temporary; this is remaking her as a person.

**How It Works:** You perform an extended ritual lasting a full day and night (24 hours). You channel Discipline Virtue energy at maximum power through your rope and into your mount's consciousness. You systematically rebuild her personality according to your design.

**Mechanical:**
- Costs 8 Psyche (extremely high)
- Mount's personality is permanently altered
- You can change specific aspects (make her more submissive, less questioning, more physically responsive, emotionally distant, etc.)
- Effect is very difficult to reverse (requires intervention from Keeper-level priestess)
- Mount might not recognize herself after (she's been remade)

**Narrative Effect:** Mount is literally a new person—designed by Rider. She has Rider's values, Rider's aesthetic, Rider's personality reflected back. She's beautiful and terrifying.

**Complications:**
- This is dangerously close to Void-corruption in impact (even though it's priestess-craft, not forbidden magic)
- Other priestesses will definitely report this to Elders (intervention likely)
- Mount's original self might be recoverable deep down; she might regain her original personality and remember the violation
- Using this on an intelligent, self-aware mount (human-form priestess or similar) is considered abuse even by Cathedral standards

**GM Note:** Will-Architecture is the point where Rider's philosophy becomes monstrous. A priestess using this is not just dominating; she's committing psychological violence. Use it as moral breaking point for character or for antagonist.

---

**Tier III (Master) — High Ability: ABSOLUTE DOMINION**
**Cost:** 10 Psyche (and part of your soul) | **Duration:** Until released or priestess dies | **Action:** Apocalyptic ritual (3 days)

**Effect:** You don't just control your mount. You make her yours so completely that she ceases to be a separate person. She becomes an extension of your will. You can sense through her eyes, think through her mind, move her body as if it's your own. She is you. You are her.

**How It Works:** You perform a 3-day ritual of absolute communion. You merge your consciousness with your mount's. The boundaries between you dissolve. You become one entity.

**Mechanical:**
- Costs 10 Psyche (massive expenditure; might take weeks to recover)
- Costs part of your Rider identity (you sacrifice some of your own autonomy to achieve this)
- For duration of Absolute Dominion (until actively released or priestess dies):
  - You can see through mount's eyes anytime you concentrate
  - You can move mount's body remotely (while her consciousness is suppressed)
  - Mount's Psyche is completely under your control (you can prevent her Psyche drain or accelerate it)
  - Mount ceases to be a person; she is your puppet

**Narrative Effect:** Rider and mount are no longer two beings. They're one consciousness in two bodies. Ultimate control. Ultimate violation.

**Complications:**
- Dominae will definitely intervene (this violates Cathedral's core consent principles)
- Using this makes Rider vulnerable to Void-seduction (she's already committed to violation; Void finds her)
- If released, mount is traumatized beyond recovery (she might be permanently broken)
- Using this more than once or twice is grounds for expulsion from Cathedral

**GM Note:** Absolute Dominion is the Rider's Tier III capstone and the point where her philosophy becomes clearly unethical. A priestess using this is choosing dominance over love, control over consent. This is endgame-content revelation of Rider's dark potential.

### RIDER: TIER IV-V CAPSTONE

**Tier IV-V (Avatar/Ascended) — CONTROL MANIFEST**

At Tier IV, the Rider has gone so far into dominance that she achieves something terrifying: she can control multiple mounts simultaneously, can reshape reality through pure force of will, can make her mount do things the mount didn't know was possible.

At Tier V, she becomes something beyond priestess—a tyrant, a living god of control. She can literally bind souls, create spiritual structures that persist beyond death, reshape the fabric of the Cathedral itself through sheer dominance.

This is endgame content. A Rider at Tier V is either the campaign's antagonist or a priestess who has chosen a path that leads toward darkness.

**Starting Equipment:**

- **Domina's Control Rope:** If a Domina of Discipline (Seraphine) favors you, she might lend you her personal teaching rope. It amplifies Discipline-based abilities. (+2 to control, rope-work, and will-locking; only usable with Domina's permission)

- **Binding Chain:** An alternative to rope—a chain woven with spiritual energy. Some Riders prefer it to rope. It doesn't break, can't be escaped, and causes psychological weight in those bound by it. (Purely flavor; functions same as rope but looks different)

## CLASS: BINDER
### *(Precision Virtue)*

**Class Philosophy:**

A BINDER is a priestess of exactitude, technique, and perfect execution. You understand rope the way mathematicians understand numbers—every knot has a purpose, every loop has a function, every connection is precise. Your baseline-lock is a masterwork of knot-craft. Your ropes sing with perfect tension.

The Binder does not improvise. She plans. She measures. She executes flawlessly. To a Binder, priestess-craft is not philosophy; it's engineering. Your rope is technology. Your mount is an instrument you've tuned perfectly.

**BINDER Archetype:** Meticulous, focused, technical, calculating. You examine rope for micro-variations in thread. You notice when your mount's baseline is off by a fraction. Your hands are stained with rope-fibers. Numbers and patterns consume your thoughts.

**Binder's Strength:** Precision. Your knots are perfect. Your rope-work never fails. Your baseline-lock is stable beyond measure. You know the technical mechanics of priestess-craft better than anyone.

**Binder's Weakness:** Rigidity in a different way than Rider. You're so focused on perfect technique that you miss the emotional reality of your mount. You optimize without considering cost.

**Precision Virtue Focus:** Perfect execution. Technical mastery. Knot-work as sacred art.

## PROGRESSION MECHANICS

### Virtue Points & Tier Advancement

**How You Gain Virtue Points:**

You earn Virtue Points by practicing your Virtue through activities:

| Activity | Points | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Training session with relevant Domina | 1-2 | Domina's teaching grants points |
| Using Virtue-based ability in practical scene | 1 | Each use of your class ability |
| Teaching another priestess your Virtue | 2 | Sharing knowledge multiplies understanding |
| Advancing another priestess in your Virtue | 2 | If you mentor someone to Tier up |
| Exploring location related to your Virtue | 1 | Finding Rope Shrine for Precision, Deep Forest for Nature, etc. |
| Breakthrough moment (GM-awarded) | 2-3 | When you have profound realization about your Virtue |
| Virtue Trial advancement (successful trial) | 5 | When Domina approves you for next Tier |

**Tier Advancement:**

| Tier | Name | Points Required | Casting Ability |
|------|------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 0 | Untrained | 0 | Can learn spells; modest ability |
| 1 | Novice | 5 | Can cast reliably; all Tier I abilities unlock |
| 2 | Journeyman | 15 total (10 new) | All Tier II abilities unlock; +2 effectiveness |
| 3 | Master | 30 total (15 new) | All Tier III abilities unlock; +3 effectiveness |
| 4 | Avatar | 50+ total (20+ new) | Tier IV capstone unlocks; cosmic-level power |
| 5 | Ascended | 100+ total (50+ new) | Tier V capstone unlocks; beyond-priestess power |

**Expert Tier Campaign Cap:** Most Expert Tier priestesses will reach Tier II or early Tier III during Sessions 62-90. Tier IV-V are Master Tier content (Sessions 91-120+).

### Multiclassing

You can learn abilities from multiple Virtues, but you never have two primary specialties. Here's how it works:

**Primary Class:** Your main Virtue specialty. You get full access to all Tier I-V abilities as you advance.

**Secondary Class (Optional):** A second Virtue you're developing. You can gain abilities from it, but you advance more slowly.

**How Secondary Classes Work:**
- You split your Virtue Points between primary and secondary (e.g., if you earn 3 points, you might put 2 in primary and 1 in secondary)
- Secondary class requires 1.5x as many points to advance (Tier 1 in secondary = 7-8 points instead of 5)
- You don't get capstone abilities in secondary class; you cap at Tier III
- Secondary class abilities are weaker versions of primary class (75% effectiveness)

**Example:** Priestess with primary Discipline (RIDER) might develop secondary Honesty (TRUTHSEEKER). She advances Rider quickly; she gains a few TRUTHSEEKER abilities for detection and truth-sensing, but they're not as powerful.

**Why Multiclass:**
- Flexibility (you're not locked into one approach)
- Depth (you understand multiple Virtues)
- Challenge (you level up slower but become more versatile)

**Dominae Approval:** Senior priestesses might encourage or discourage multiclassing depending on Cathedral politics and your individual arc.

### Mount Advancement

**Loyalty Progression:**

Your mount advances through Loyalty (how strongly bonded), not through Virtue Tiers.

| Loyalty Stage | How Achieved | Effect |
|---------------|-------------|--------|
| Bonded | First baseline-lock ceremony | Mount is connected to you; spiritual link active |
| Devoted | 1+ month of daily priestess-craft work | Mount trusts you; gains +1 Psyche recovery |
| Loyal | 3+ months of consistent bonding and care | Mount would die for you; +2 Psyche recovery; gain Mount Ability: Intuitive Response |
| Devoted-Beyond | 6+ months of intense intimacy (scenes, meditation, shared experiences) | Mount is your other half; +3 Psyche recovery; Mount Ability: Emotional Resonance |
| Soulbound | 1+ year of total devotion; major sacrifice from mount or priestess | Mount and priestess are spiritually merged; +5 Psyche recovery; Mount Ability: Consciousness Merging |

**Mount Abilities (By Loyalty Stage):**

- **Intuitive Response (Loyal):** Mount can sense your intentions before you speak them. +1 to mount's actions during scenes (she moves/responds naturally without explicit command).

- **Emotional Resonance (Devoted-Beyond):** Mount is so attuned to you that she can heal your emotional wounds. Once per week, mount can absorb 3-4 Psyche damage intended for you; she takes it instead.

- **Consciousness Merging (Soulbound):** For brief moments during deep scenes, you and mount share consciousness. You feel what she feels; she understands your intentions completely. No miscommunication possible.

### Rope & Bond Progression

**Rope Development:**

Your baseline-lock rope can grow stronger as your bond deepens:

| Rope Stage | How Achieved | Effects |
|------------|-------------|---------|
| Newly Woven | Initial baseline-lock ceremony | Functional rope; requires conscious focus to maintain baseline-lock |
| Familiar | 3+ months of use and monthly renewals | Rope bonds to your energy; baseline-lock is easier to maintain; rope glows faintly |
| Intimate | 6+ months of use; emotional connection deepened | Rope remembers your mount's responses; you can sense her state through rope alone; rope never tangles or breaks |
| Soulbound | 1+ year; deep spiritual connection established | Rope becomes almost alive; it acts sometimes independently to protect mount or support bond; can be felt from distance |

**Rope Powers (Soulbound stage):**

- **Self-Repair:** Rope never wears out; damages heal naturally
- **Distance Sensing:** You can feel your mount through rope even if separated (up to ~1 mile)
- **Emergency Response:** If mount is in serious danger, rope will instinctively tighten or loosen to protect her
- **Memory Holding:** Rope holds memories of your bond; can share visions of past scenes if partner touches it

### B: MOUNT TYPES & SPECIALTIES

**PONY-FORM MOUNTS**

Appearance: 3-4 feet tall, graceful, innocent-seeming
Example: Aria (Kess's mount)

Strengths:
- Physically agile (can move through spaces human-form mounts can't)
- Emotionally responsive (easy to read; expressive)
- Non-threatening appearance (people underestimate them)
- Natural submission (baseline-lock comes naturally to pony-form)

Weaknesses:
- Limited physical strength (can't do heavy work)
- Can't speak (limits intellectual exchange)
- Seen as childlike (some priestesses treat them as less serious)
- Easily traumatized (sensitive to harsh treatment)

Best For: Priestesses who value emotional connection, who like caregiving, who want non-threatening aesthetic

Corruption Risk: Moderate (Void can seduct through innocence)

---

**HUMAN-FORM MOUNTS**

Appearance: 5-6 feet tall; indistinguishable from priestess at first glance; varies by individual
Examples: Mercy (Arcana's), most central Cathedral mounts

Strengths:
- Can communicate (full language exchange)
- Can understand complex instructions
- Can do sophisticated work
- Intimate connection possible (shared humanity)

Weaknesses:
- Complex psychology (harder to manage)
- Questions authority (more likely to resist)
- Struggles with identity (is this love or possession?)
- More emotionally demanding

Best For: Priestesses who want intellectual engagement, who value communication, who can handle complexity

Corruption Risk: High (human-form mounts are more interesting to Void; more sophisticated seduction possible)

---

**BEAST-FORM MOUNTS**

Appearance: Animal-like (wolves, great cats, bears, etc.); primal; non-human
Examples: Echo (Reth's), Storm (Vala's)

Strengths:
- Powerful (can do heavy lifting, physical protection)
- Instinctual (obeys primal impulses; less complex psychology)
- Independent (doesn't require constant communication)
- Physically dangerous (intimidating presence)

Weaknesses:
- Limited language (no complex discussion possible)
- Primal emotions (harder to control when enraged or frightened)
- Cannot do fine work (too large/clawed)
- Requires space (can't fit in small chambers; needs to roam)

Best For: Priestesses who value physical strength, who prefer instinct over intellect, who can handle powerful animals

Corruption Risk: Low (Void finds beast-form less interesting; simpler consciousness easier to protect)
