## THE EIGHT VIRTUES AT A GLANCE

### TIER PROGRESSION MECHANICS (ALL SCHOOLS)

### Core Spell Resolution (d20 System, scaled from Rolemaster d100)
**Base Mechanics:**
- **Spell Roll:** d20 + Arcana's Cha Modifier + Spell Level = Total Attack Roll
- **Target MAC:** 10 + Mercy's Int Modifier (typically 10-12, unchanging)
- **Success Threshold:** Attack Roll ≥ Target MAC = Spell Effect Lands
- **Critical Success (NAT 20):** Spell effect maximized, no resistance possible
- **Critical Failure (NAT 1):** Spell fizzles; Arcana may retry or select different approach

**Example Resolution:**
- Arcana casts Rope Binding (3rd-level spell): d20 + 5 (Cha mod) + 3 (spell level) = 14
- Mercy's MAC: 10 + 1 (Int mod) = 11
- 14 ≥ 11 = Success. Rope binding lands. Mercy bound for duration.

### Psyche Pool (Mercy's Mental Endurance)
**What It Is:** Mercy's mental-resistance reserve. Like HP, but for psychological-intensity.  
**Starting Pool:** Mercy's Wisdom Score (typically 16-18 = 16-18 points per full recovery)  
**Drain Rate:** Varies by spell intensity (Tier 1: 1-2/hr | Tier 5: 9-15/hr)  
**Recovery:** Downtime between sessions. Intense work requires preparation & aftercare.  
**Exhaustion State:** If Psyche drops to 0, Mercy enters dissociation/subspace-loss. Scene pauses for grounding.

### MAB (Mental Attack Bonus)
**Formula:** d20 + Arcana's Cha Modifier + Spell Level  
**Cha Modifier:** Arcana's Charisma stat determines base-bonus (typically +5 to +8)  
**Spell Level:** 1st-level spells (+1), 3rd (+3), 5th (+5), 7th (+7), 9th (+9)  
**Result:** Total attack roll compared against Mercy's MAC.

### MAC (Mental Armor Class)
**Formula:** 10 + Mercy's Int Modifier  
**Int Modifier:** Mercy's Intelligence stat determines defense (typically -1 to 0 = MAC 9-10)  
**Static Defense:** MAC does not change per spell. Low Int is architectural feature—priestess techniques land reliably.  
**Resistance Mechanics:** Wis saves optional (d20 + Wis mod) to resist *duration* extension, not initial landing.

### Optional: Contested Resolution (Mercy Resists)
**When Applicable:** Mercy actively resists priestess-spell (rare; typically only Tier 4-5 work)  
**Mercy's Counter-Roll:** d20 + Wis Modifier (Psyche grounding-attempt)  
**Resolution:** If Mercy's roll ≥ Arcana's MAB, effect duration halved or intensity reduced  
**Psyche Cost to Resist:** Mercy spends 2-3 Psyche points per resistance-attempt (mental effort)  
**Note:** Green-consent means Mercy typically doesn't resist; contested rolls emerge only in roleplay-conflict scenarios

### Duration Scaling
**Short Spells (Tier 1-2):** Minutes (5-60 min typical)  
**Medium Spells (Tier 3-4):** Hours (1-6 hrs typical)  
**Eternal Spells (Tier 5):** Permanent-state (no time-limit; effect persists indefinitely or until priestess-cancels)

**Psyche Pool:** Mercy's mental endurance (like HP). Drained by intense scenes. Recovered through downtime/aftercare.  
**MAB (Mental Attack Bonus):** Arcana's Cha + Spell Level (1st-9th)  
**MAC (Mental Armor Class):** 10 + Mercy's Int Modifier (typically 10-12, unchanging)  
**Resolution:** d20 + Cha vs. MAC when contested. Automatic success vs. Mercy's typically low defense.

**Progression Trigger:** 3 consecutive successful Tier sessions → Tier escalates  
**Capstone Achievement (Tier 5):** Complete rewriting of aspect; Mercy's being permanently transformed

## MECHANICAL RESOLUTION: SPELL-CASTING RULES

### Standard Spell Resolution (Cooperative Play)
**Priestess Casts Spell:**
1. Arcana declares spell-name, spell-level, intended-effect
2. Arcana rolls: d20 + Cha Mod + Spell Level
3. Compare roll to Mercy's MAC (10 + Int Mod)
4. **Roll ≥ MAC:** Spell lands. Duration/effect applies. Psyche Pool drain begins.
5. **Roll < MAC:** Spell fizzles or is resisted. Arcana may retry or switch spells.

**Example (Rope Binding, 3rd-level):**
- Arcana's Cha Mod: +6 | Spell Level: +3 | d20 roll: 8
- Total MAB: 8 + 6 + 3 = **17**
- Mercy's MAC: 10 + 0 (Int Mod) = **10**
- 17 ≥ 10 = **Success.** Rope binding lands. 1-2 Psyche drain/hour begins.

### Contested Resolution (Mercy Resists)
**When Used:** Mercy actively opposes priestess-spell (roleplay-conflict, not Green-consent default)

**Mercy's Resistance Roll:**
1. Mercy rolls: d20 + Wis Modifier (Psyche grounding-attempt)
2. Compare to Arcana's MAB
3. **Mercy's Roll ≥ Arcana's MAB:** Effect duration halved; intensity reduced
4. **Mercy's Roll < Arcana's MAB:** Effect lands as full-power; Mercy's resistance failed

**Psyche Cost to Resist:** Mercy spends 2-3 Psyche points per resistance-attempt (mental effort of fighting priestess-will)

**Example (Transcendence Spell, Mercy Resists):**
- Arcana's MAB: 16 (d20 roll 8 + Cha 6 + Spell 5)
- Mercy's Resistance: d20 roll 12 + Wis Mod 4 = **16**
- 16 ≥ 16 = **Tie = Mercy Succeeds.** Duration halved (45 min instead of 90). Mercy spends 3 Psyche.

### Critical Success (NAT 20)
**Effect:** Priestess's spell is maximized; no resistance possible; duration extended; Psyche drain increased by 50%

**Example (Correction Sequence, Nat 20):**
- Arcana rolls 20 + 6 (Cha) + 5 (spell level) = **31** (max roll)
- Mercy's MAC: 10
- Result: Correction lands with maximum intensity. Duration doubled. Psyche cost: 9 instead of 6.

### Critical Failure (NAT 1)
**Effect:** Priestess's spell fizzles completely. Effect does not land. Mercy is unaffected. Arcana must wait 1 round (real-time ~5 min roleplay) before retrying.

**Example (Command Voice, Nat 1):**
- Arcana rolls 1 + 6 (Cha) + 3 (spell level) = **10**
- Mercy's MAC: 10
- Tie on crit-fail = **Fizzle.** Command doesn't land. Mercy ignores instruction. Arcana must retry or switch approach.

### Psyche Pool Exhaustion
**When Psyche = 0:** Mercy enters dissociation-state. Scene pauses for grounding. Options:
- **Immediate Grounding:** 5-10 min real-time conversation, reconnection to body/Arcana
- **Extended Aftercare:** Formal aftercare ritual before resuming (if resuming at all)
- **Scene Conclusion:** End session, deep aftercare, full recovery before next work

**Prevention:** Priestess monitors Psyche during scene. If approaching exhaustion, Arcana may reduce intensity, extend duration, or conclude gracefully.

### Duration Mechanics
**Spell Duration Types:**
- **Concentration:** Spell lasts only while priestess actively maintains focus (e.g., holding rope-tension)
- **Fixed Duration:** Spell lasts specific time regardless of priestess attention (e.g., "30 minutes" = auto-ends)
- **Eternal:** Spell lasts indefinitely until priestess-cancels or Mercy's Psyche fully recovers (Tier 5 only)

**Extending Duration:** Priestess may spend additional Psyche (equal to 1 hour's drain-cost) to extend duration 1 additional hour

**Dice Use:** Optional safety mechanism, not required for normal play. Supports both cooperative-narrative and contested-resolution. Most scenes run narratively; dice emerge only for uncertainty/conflict/stakes-clarification.
