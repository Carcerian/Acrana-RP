# DIVINE INFLUENCE FRAMEWORK
## How Pantheon Powers Manifest in Creatures & Campaign

This bestiary is designed to support **progressive divine influence** in your campaign:

**Stage 1 (Levels 1-5):** Subtle omens—animals displaying unusual behavior, minor elemental anomalies, occasional celestial sightings
**Stage 2 (Levels 6-10):** Obvious allegiances—creatures serving specific deities, clear pantheon representation, factions emerging
**Stage 3 (Levels 11-15):** Active intervention—priestess factions, divine minions, pantheon conflicts affecting the world
**Stage 4 (Levels 16-20):** Divine war—factions battling openly, deities meddling directly, reality warping
**Stage 5 (Levels 21-40):** Cosmological shift—parties become agents of gods, ascending to divine power themselves

Each creature entry now includes **Divine Affiliation** to guide when/how they appear based on campaign stage.
