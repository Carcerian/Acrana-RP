## LADY EMBERA
**Title:** The Flame Princess, She Who Burns, Mistress of Immolation  
**Spheres of Influence:** Fire, passion, destruction, renewal through flame, forge magic  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral | **Pantheon:** Elemental  
**CR:** 6 | **Hit Points:** 52 | **Armor Class:** 15

### Description
Embera manifests as a towering inferno given humanoid shape—flames dance perpetually across her form in shades of gold, crimson, and white-hot intensity. Her voice crackles like a roaring bonfire. She embodies passion, destruction, and the transformative power of fire. Priestesses who bond with Embera gain the ability to walk through flame unburned and speak with fire elementals.

### Attributes
Str 16 (+3) | Dex 14 (+2) | Con 16 (+3) | Int 13 (+1) | Wis 12 (+1) | Cha 15 (+2)

### Traits
- **Fire Immunity:** Immune to fire damage; healed by fire
- **Immolation Aura:** Any creature within 15 ft of Embera takes 1d6 fire damage
- **Legendary Resilience:** Advantage on saves vs. spells and magical effects
- **Flame Trail:** Leaves harmless flame trail as she flies

### Actions
- **Flameburst Scimitar:** +5 to hit, 2d6+3 slashing + 1d6 fire damage
- **Inferno Jet (Recharge 5-6):** +5 to hit (range 60 ft), 2d8+3 fire damage; target Kindled on failed DC 14 save
- **Pyroclastic Eruption (Recharge 5-6):** 20-ft radius, creatures save (DC 14) or take 3d6 fire and Prone

### Legendary Actions (2/turn)
- Flameburst Attack
- Inferno Jet (costs 2 actions)
- Fly (move up to half flying speed)

### Powers & Abilities
- **Forge of Creation:** Can craft legendary fire-touched weapons and armor
- **Wildfire Command:** Summons wildfires and controls flame within 1 mile
- **Rebirth Protocol:** Resurrects fallen allies through ritual flame (once per cycle)
- **Immolation Touch:** Marks priestesses with sacred flame; grants fire immunity and +1d6 fire damage on attacks

### Artifacts
- **Flameheart Sword:** +3 legendary weapon, deals 2d8+3 fire damage, leaves persistent flame trails
- **Ember Crown:** +3 AC, immunity to fire, wearer radiates warmth within 30 ft
- **Pyrekeeper's Tome:** Records all flame magic; grants fire spellcasting at will

### Minions (1d4+2)
- **Ifrit Warriors** (CR 3-4 each) — Fire elemental lord servants
- **Salamander Court** (CR 3 each) — Flame aura, regeneration
- **Pyroclastic Elementals** (CR 2 each) — Lesser fire servants
- **Flame Imps** (CR 1/4 each) — Scouting minions

### Followers & Cults
- **Priestesses of the Sacred Flame:** Ascetics who walk through fire, test their resolve through immolation
- **Forge Masters:** Artificers who craft legendary weapons blessed by Embera
- **Wildfire Druids:** Those who see destruction as necessary renewal
- **Pyromancers:** Mages who channel pure elemental fire magic

### Rules & Doctrines
1. **Passion Feeds the Flame:** Embrace emotion and intensity; fear dulls the fire
2. **Forge Your Own Path:** Create through destruction; old things must burn for new growth
3. **The Eternal Burning:** Never let the flame within diminish; the cold death awaits the powerless
4. **Fire Tests All:** Prove your worth through trials of heat and endurance
5. **Destruction is Rebirth:** Cleansing flame purifies all corruption

# PANTHEON RULES FOR INTERACTION

## Domains of Power
Each pantheon controls specific spheres and cannot fully interfere outside their domain without ritual or war.

## Hierarchy & Respect
Smaller entities show fealty to larger ones; direct challenge to supremacy results in planar conflict.

## Priestess Bonding
Each priestess can bond to ONE supreme being; bonding grants powers but also restrictions and obligations.

## Divine Conflict
When pantheons clash, the material world suffers; priestesses are caught between warring factions.

## Ascension Possible
Legendary priestesses can ascend to demi-godhood, challenging existing pantheon members.
