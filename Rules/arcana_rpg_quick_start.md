# ARCANA RPG: QUICK START GUIDE
## Get Playing in 30 Minutes

If you don't have time to read everything, start here.

---

## THE CONCEPT IN 60 SECONDS

You are a **Priestess** who bonds with a magical beast called a **Totem**. Your Totem gives you:
- **Virtue Bonus:** A stat boost (+1, +2, or +3 to one or more Virtues)
- **Animal Traits:** Speed, strength, perception, regeneration, etc.
- **Natural Weapons:** Fangs, claws, horns, tails, breath attacks, venom
- **Charms:** 3 unique magical abilities per totem (usable 3 times per short rest)

Together, you and your Totem fight enemies, complete quests, and pursue your goals.

---

## CHARACTER CREATION (15 MINUTES)

### Step 1: Choose Your Totem (5 minutes)

Pick one Totem from the **TOME OF TOTEMS** quick-view tables:
- **Mundane d12 (beginner):** Cat, Dove, Eagle, Fox, Hound, Lion, Owl, Panther, Pony, Raven, Tiger, Wolf
- **Mythic d20 (intermediate):** Alicorn, Chimera, Dragon, Griffon, Hellhorse, Hydra, Kirin, Kraken, Lamia, Manticore, Nightmare, Pegasus, Phoenix, Qilin, Sphinx, Starmare, Unicorn, Wyvern
- **Elemental d8 (advanced):** Flame, Land, Moon, Sea, Sky, Spirit, Sun, Void

**For Arcana:** Choose a +3 Virtue specialist (Dragon, Tiger, Phoenix, Unicorn, Manticore, Sphinx, Kirin, Honesty)
**For Mercy:** Choose a +2/+1 Virtue dual (Alicorn, Griffon, Hydra, Lamia, Wyvern, etc.)

### Step 2: Understand Your Virtue Bonus (5 minutes)

Your totem grants:
- **+3 bonus:** You're a specialist in one Virtue (highest power, narrow focus)
- **+2/+1 bonus:** You're balanced in two Virtues (flexible, mid-power)
- **+1/+1/+1 bonus (Void only):** You're versatile in all Virtues (maximum flexibility, lower peak power)

This bonus applies to saves and checks related to your Virtue. Example:
- Dragon (Discipline +3): You gain +2 (at Bond Rating 2) to Strength saves, resisting being Knocked Prone, and mental fortitude

### Step 3: Bond with Your Totem (5 minutes)

Perform the bonding ritual:
1. **Contact:** Spend 30 minutes with your Totem (meditation, communion)
2. **Negotiation:** Your Totem may require proof of worthiness
3. **Ritual:** Perform a ritual specific to your Totem (varies: blood-letting, sacrifice, magic)

You gain **Bond Rating 1** and become linked to your Totem.

## YOUR TOTEM'S ABILITIES

### Traits (You'll Reference These in Combat)

Your Totem has animal traits that grant bonuses:
- **Speed:** Extra movement (Swift trait: +10 feet at BR 1)
- **Defense:** Extra AC and hit points (Tough trait: +1 AC at BR 1)
- **Senses:** Better perception (Keen Senses trait: +1 to Perception at BR 1)
- **Combat:** Extra damage and attack rolls (Lethal trait: +1d4 bonus damage at BR 1)

**Traits scale with Bond Rating:** BR 1 = base, BR 2 = enhanced, BR 3 = capstone.

### Natural Weapons (You'll Use These in Combat)

Your Totem has natural weapons:
- **Fangs/Bite:** 1d6 piercing damage (scales to 1d8 at BR 2, 1d10 at BR 3+)
- **Claws/Rake:** 1d4-1d6 slashing damage (can use multiple claws per turn)
- **Tail:** 1d4-1d6 damage (varies by tail type: blunt, spiked, whipping, etc.)
- **Breath:** Fire, frost, lightning, poison, or void energy (30-foot cone, recharge 1d2)
- **Horn:** 1d6 piercing damage + Knock Prone on save failure

**Weapons scale with Corruption Tier:** Tier I = base, Tier II-V = progressively more damage.

### Charms (You'll Use These Strategically)

Your Totem has 3 unique charms. Example (Dragon Totem):
- **Burning Regeneration:** Dragon regains 1d10 HP and gains fire immunity for 1 round
- **Dragon's Sense:** You and Dragon can communicate telepathically; you sense danger within 1 mile
- **Infernal Strike:** Dragon's next attack deals +1d8 fire damage and Ignites enemies on hit

You can invoke charms **3 times per short rest** (total, across all three charms).

## YOUR PRIESTESS

You have a Priestess character who:
- **Wears armor** (leather, chain, plate — your choice)
- **Carries weapons** (sword, bow, staff, etc. — or can fight unarmed)
- **Casts spells** (if your class supports it)
- **Shares Virtue bonuses with Totem** (you gain +1 to related saves/checks at BR 1)

**Important:** Your Priestess is NOT as powerful as your Totem. The Totem is the heavy hitter. Your Priestess supports, strategizes, and coordinates.

## COMBAT BASICS

### Your Turn (Simple Version)

1. **Action:** Move and attack (you and your Totem each get an action)
   - Priestess: Use weapon attack, cast spell, or invoke charm
   - Totem: Use natural weapon (fangs, claws, breath, etc.) or invoke charm
2. **Bonus Action:** Use a quick ability (your Priestess can invoke charm; Totem might use secondary ability)
3. **Movement:** Move up to your speed (typically 30 feet for Priestess, 30-60+ for Totem)
4. **Reaction:** (Save for your next turn to react to enemy actions)

**Example Combat Turn:**
- Priestess moves 20 feet closer to enemy
- Totem moves 40 feet and uses Bite attack (+4 to hit, 1d8 damage)
- Priestess uses bonus action to invoke "Burning Regeneration" charm (Totem heals 1d10)
- Done!

### Combat Example: Dragon Totem vs. Dire Wolf

**Your Turn:**
- **Dragon (Totem) attacks:** Bite: +6 to hit (Strength modifier +2, Lethal trait +2, armor bonus +2), deals 1d8 + 2 + 1d8 = 13 average damage
- **If hits:** Dire Wolf is Grappled (can't escape without escaping grapple check)
- **Priestess bonus action:** Invoke charm "Burning Regeneration" — Dragon heals 1d10 and gains fire immunity

**Dire Wolf's Turn:**
- **Dire Wolf attacks:** Bite: +4 to hit, deals 1d8 damage
- If hits Grappled Dragon: The Grapple isn't broken; combat continues

**Your next turn:**
- **Dragon uses bonus action to drag Grappled Dire Wolf:** Movement with Grapple; move 20 feet while holding Wolf
- **Priestess attacks Wolf:** Your rapier +4 to hit, deals 1d8 + 2 = 5 average damage
- **Done!**

## PSYCHE & CORRUPTION

Your Totem corrupts over time as you use its power. Corruption progresses through **Tiers (I-V)**.

### How It Works

Each time you use your Totem's most powerful abilities (high-tier charms, legendary attacks), you drain **Psyche** (mental/spiritual energy).
- **Tier I (starting):** Low drain (1d4 Psyche per day of heavy use)
- **Tier II:** Moderate drain (1d6 Psyche per day)
- **Tier III:** Significant drain (1d8 Psyche per day)
- **Tier IV:** Serious drain (1d10 Psyche per day)
- **Tier V:** Extreme drain; Totem is barely sane; Psyche recovers extremely slowly

### The Temptation

**Higher tiers = more powerful abilities and weapon damage.** You will be tempted to corrupt your Totem further.

**Cost of Tier V:** Your Totem becomes a Mental Chimera (carries consumed identities; barely rational but immensely powerful).

## ARTIFACTS

You may find legendary items called **Artifacts** (from the CYCLOPEDIA OF ARTIFACTS).

### How Artifacts Work

1. **Find or quest for artifact**
2. **Perform Attunement ritual** (ritual specific to artifact; costs gold, Psyche, or sacrifice)
3. **Gain artifact abilities** (can use artifact's powers in combat)
4. **Accept artifact's Geas** (magical compulsion or restriction; resistable but costs Psyche to resist)

### Example Artifact: Blade of Duelist

- **Attunement Cost:** 300 gold + 1d4 Psyche (ritual involves 8 hours of combat practice)
- **Geas:** "Never use this blade against the weak or defenseless; reserve its power for worthy opponents"
- **Abilities:**
  - **Perfect Balance:** You gain advantage on attack rolls with this blade; critical hit range expands to 19-20
  - **Riposte:** When attacked and attack misses, you can make free counterattack
  - **Masterwork:** Once per turn, you can make one attack guaranteed to hit critical

In combat, artifact abilities stack with your Priestess abilities and Totem abilities.

## FIRST COMBAT SCENARIO (20 MINUTES)

**The Setup:** You encounter a **Dire Wolf** (CR 1) in a forest.

**Your Stats (Dragon Totem, BR 1):**
- **Dragon AC:** 14 (natural scales)
- **Dragon HP:** 20 (medium creature)
- **Dragon traits:** Lethal (+1 to attacks, +1d4 damage), Tough (+1 AC), Regeneration (regain 5 HP/round)
- **Dragon bite:** +4 to hit, 1d8 + 2 piercing damage

**Dire Wolf Stats (CR 1):**
- **Dire Wolf AC:** 13
- **Dire Wolf HP:** 18
- **Dire Wolf bite:** +4 to hit, 1d8 + 2 piercing damage

**Round 1:**
- **Initiative:** Both roll Dexterity. (Let's say you go first.)
- **Your turn:** Dragon moves 30 feet toward wolf and bites. Roll: 16 + 4 = 20 (hit!). Damage: 5 + 2 + 3 (lethal bonus) = 10 damage. Wolf HP: 18 → 8.
- **Wolf's turn:** Wolf bites back. Roll: 14 + 4 = 18 (hit!). Damage: 6 + 2 = 8 damage. Dragon HP: 20 → 12.

**Round 2:**
- **Your turn:** Priestess invokes charm "Burning Regeneration" (bonus action). Dragon heals 1d10 = 8 HP. Dragon HP: 12 → 20. Dragon also bites again. Roll: 18 + 4 = 22 (critical!). Critical hit damage: 1d8 + 1d8 + 1d4 = 20 damage. Wolf HP: 8 → dead.
- **Combat ends!** You win!

**Takeaway:** Your Dragon Totem dealt most of the damage. Your Priestess healed at the right moment. Together, you defeated the wolf.

## PROGRESSION

As you bond with your Totem and survive dangers, you progress:

### Bond Rating (1-3)
- **Bond 1 (start):** +1 shared Virtue bonus; traits at level 1; weapons at tier 1
- **Bond 2 (mid-game):** +2 shared Virtue bonus (or +1/+1 for duals); traits enhanced; weapons stronger
- **Bond 3 (late-game):** +3 shared Virtue bonus (or +2/+1 for duals); traits peak; weapons strongest

### Corruption Tier (I-V)
- **Tier I (start):** Base abilities
- **Tier II:** Weapon damage increases; new tactical options unlock
- **Tier III:** Charms grow more powerful; Psyche drain noticeable
- **Tier IV:** Reality-warping abilities; Totem barely sane
- **Tier V:** Legendary power; Totem is fully Corrupted Mental Chimera

## QUICK HELP (Common Questions)

**Q: Can I use my Priestess and Totem abilities in the same turn?**  
A: Yes! Priestess gets one action, Totem gets one action. Use them independently or together.

**Q: What happens if my Totem dies?**  
A: Your Totem can respawn/return if you perform a resurrection ritual (varies by totem). Alternatively, you can bond a new Totem.

**Q: Can I bond more than one Totem?**  
A: Yes! At high levels, you can bond a second (even third) Totem. Each requires its own Bond Rating progression.

**Q: How do artifacts interact with my Totem?**  
A: Artifacts are separate from Totem bonuses. They stack. Example: Blade of Duelist (artifact) + Dragon Totem (natural weapons) = both usable in same combat, bonuses combine.

**Q: What if I don't like my Totem choice?**  
A: After playtesting, you can switch Totems! No mechanical penalty for refocusing your bond.

**Q: Can I refuse a Geas?**  
A: Yes! You can resist a geas by making a Wisdom save. If you fail, you lose 1d4 Psyche and gain Tainted condition. Resisting has a cost.
