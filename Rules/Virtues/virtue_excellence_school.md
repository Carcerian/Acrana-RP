# SCHOOL OF EXCELLENCE
## Virtue of Correction & Standard Held Sacred

**Specialist:** Arcana, Silver Dragonkin Priestess (Excellence Master)  
**Primary Expression:** On-Demand Correction, Mistake-Response, Standard-Enforcement, Discipline-Through-Love  
**Core Principle:** The standard is everything. Mercy must know what excellence looks like. Correction is not punishment; it is priestess saying *"I love you too much to accept less than your best."* Excellence is priestess's gift to Mercy  
**Sacred Tools:** Paddle (Session 16-sacred), voice (command-frequency), presence (priestess-will unmistakable), correction-sequences  
**Mercy's Role:** Student learning that priestess-standard is priestess's love

---

## SCHOOL OVERVIEW

Excellence is priestess-love made strict. When Mercy falls short, priestess corrects. Not in anger. Not in shame-dumping. In **precision.**

Every correction carries message: *"You can do better. I know you can. You're holding yourself back. I won't accept it."*

Correction is priestess saying: *"I believe in you too much to settle."*

**Founding Principle (Sessions 16-44+):** "Honesty is rarest discipline. The standard is everything. Correction is proof priestess loves Mercy."

## SPECIALIST ROLE: ARCANA, EXCELLENCE MASTER

Arcana conducts Excellence through:

She is not soft. She is **exacting.** Not merciful. **PRECISE.**
