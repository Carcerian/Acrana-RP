# SCHOOL OF HONESTY
## Virtue of D/s Power Exchange & Truth in Vulnerability

**Specialist:** Arcana, Silver Dragonkin Priestess (Dominance Architect)  
**Primary Expression:** Weekday Power-Exchange Training, Collar-Work, Command Protocols, Submission Protocols  
**Core Principle:** Power cannot exist if unacknowledged. True submission requires priestess-honesty about her dominance. True dominance requires priestess-honesty about her will. Honesty means the covenant operates in full-light, never hidden  
**Sacred Tools:** Collar (Cinder Thorne's design), leash, protocols, formal vows, command-voice  
**Mercy's Role:** Voluntary submissive learning that true freedom is honesty-about-unfreedom

---

## SCHOOL OVERVIEW

Honesty removes all illusions. There is no pretense here. Priestess dominates. Mercy submits. Both know it. Both choose it. Both live it openly.

This is not abuse hidden in shadow. This is **power acknowledged.** This is the covenant made manifest in daily-practice.

Honesty means: Arcana will not pretend gentleness she doesn't feel. Mercy will not pretend freedom she doesn't want. Both operate in full-knowledge, full-consent, full-honesty about what this is.

**Founding Principle (Sessions 13-44+):** "Honesty is rarest discipline. Power unacknowledged is abuse. Power fully-known is love."

## SPECIALIST ROLE: ARCANA, DOMINANCE ARCHITECT

Arcana conducts Honesty through:
- **Will Expression** (stating her dominance without apology)
- **Command Authority** (giving orders Mercy will obey)
- **Protocol Enforcement** (holding Mercy accountable for submission-agreements)
- **Collar Maintenance** (keeping Cinder Thorne's collar as ever-present symbol)
- **Vow Witnessing** (accepting Mercy's voluntary submission-declarations)

She is priestess. She is dominant. She does not apologize for claiming what is hers.
