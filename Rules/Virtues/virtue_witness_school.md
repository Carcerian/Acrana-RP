# SCHOOL OF WITNESS
## Virtue of Public Display & Covenant Made Eternal

**Specialist:** Arcana, Silver Dragonkin Priestess (Exhibition Master)  
**Primary Expression:** On-Demand Public Marking, Visible Claiming, Witnessed Covenant, Reputation-Building  
**Core Principle:** What is witnessed becomes eternal. What is hidden remains shadow-work. True covenant demands witness—the world seeing that Mercy is priestess's mount, that power is acknowledged, that love is visible  
**Sacred Tools:** Public spaces (Sinfar streets, markets, guild-halls), visible marks (fang-marks, claw-patterns, collar-wearing), formal covenant-ceremonies, reputation-networks (VIP reviews, Hellnet posts)  
**Mercy's Role:** Priestess-property willingly displayed, publicly claimed, eternally marked

## SCHOOL OVERVIEW

Witness shatters privacy. This is not hidden shame—this is **public pride.** Arcana shows Mercy to the world. Mercy wears priestess-marks visibly. The covenant is not secret; it is **proclaimed.**

When witnesses see Mercy bearing Arcana's marks, the covenant becomes real. Mercy's claim to priestess-ownership is witnessed, acknowledged, made eternal.

**Founding Principle (Sessions 32-44+):** "What's witnessed becomes eternal. Covenant not ashamed. The world knowing Mercy is priestess's mount makes it so."
