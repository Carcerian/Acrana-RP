# INTRODUCTION: HOW TO USE THIS BOOK

**Book 1: The Magic Codex** is the system rules reference for priestess-craft magic in the Expert Tier campaign. It documents every spell, ritual, artifact, creature, and corruption mechanic available to players and GMs.

**For Players:** Consult this book to understand what magic your priestess can learn, cast, and master. Class abilities reference specific spells/rituals by page number.

**For GMs:** Use this as the authoritative source for magical costs, effects, and complications. When adjudicating priestess-craft magic, refer here for consistency.

**Organization:** Magic is organized by *type* (Hedge vs. Ritual) and *tier* (Common/Tier 1 → Advanced/Tier 2 → Forbidden/Master/Tier 3). Spells and rituals are listed with casting time, components, effects, costs, and limitations.

**Quick Reference:** Appendices include indexes for rapid lookup. Page numbers are embedded throughout.

# PART I: HEDGE MAGIC & LOCAL TRADITIONS

## WHAT IS HEDGE MAGIC?

Hedge magic is the practical, folk magic of the valley—magic practiced by herbalists, herders, pilgrims, and common folk. It predates Cathedral priestess-craft and persists alongside it, relying on sympathetic magic, folk knowledge, and natural components rather than Virtue-channeling.

Where priestess-craft is hierarchical and spiritually refined, hedge magic is accessible and results-focused. An herbalist doesn't invoke Virtue-energy when brewing bloodmoss tea; she applies centuries of accumulated knowledge. A charm-weaver doesn't meditate on spiritual principles when tying protection knots; she binds intention into cord through focus and craft.

**Hedge Magic Works Via:**
- Sympathetic magic (like affects like; intention binds into physical objects)
- Folk knowledge (herbs, bones, water, fire carry inherent properties)
- Personal intention (caster's will shapes effect; stronger intent = stronger result)
- Simple components (found in nature; accessible to anyone)

**Compared to Priestess-Craft Rituals:**
- Hedge magic is weaker but more accessible
- No formal training required (though mastery takes practice)
- Scales well with practice (amateur remedies take days; masters work in hours)
- Available to everyone (not restricted to Cathedral priestesses)

**Cathedral Position:** Hedge magic is acknowledged as legitimate but separate. The Cathedral doesn't prohibit it (common folk practice freely), but doesn't teach it formally. Forbidden hedge magics (will-theft, identity-shattering, binding-breaking) are prosecuted severely.

## TIER 1: COMMON HEDGE SPELLS
*Known to any practitioner; taught in Market Quarter; requires no formal training*

### WATER-BLESSING
**Components:** Fresh water, salt, spoken word  
**Casting Time:** 5-10 minutes  
**Duration:** Permanent (until water consumed)  
**Cost:** Minimal (materials only; no energy drain)

**Effect:** Makes water spiritually pure and safe to drink. Removes minor spiritual contamination (taint from stagnant sources, minor curses). Pilgrims use this at Sacred Springs.

**How It Works:**
1. Take fresh water in clean container
2. Add salt (represents purification)
3. Speak blessing-words (any sincere words invoking cleansing/safety)
4. Hold hands over water (transfer intention)
5. Water is now blessed

**Limitations:**
- Only affects minor spiritual contamination
- Cannot purify magically corrupted water (Void-touched water resists)
- Cannot purify physical poisons (only spiritual purity)
- Effect ends when water is consumed

**GM Note:** Casual priestess-craft. Should feel mundane. When Arcana encounters questionable water, priestesses bless it without fanfare.

### BONE-READING
**Components:** Animal bones, casting surface  
**Casting Time:** 15-30 minutes  
**Duration:** Single reading (lasts minutes)  
**Cost:** Low (reader's concentration)

**Effect:** Divines near-future events, present danger, or hidden truth. Results are symbolic, requiring interpretation.

**How It Works:**
1. Gather bones (fresh hunted-game preferred)
2. Enter meditation (clear mind; focus on question)
3. Cast bones on cloth or sand
4. Observe patterns (scattered vs. clustered; directions; position)
5. Interpret (different readers may read differently)

**Common Interpretations:**
- Bones scattered wide → Dispersal, travel, spreading danger
- Bones clustered tight → Gathering, focus, imminent event
- Bones pointing same direction → Clear path forward
- Bones crossing → Conflict, obstacle, difficult choice
- Single bone standing → Revelation, hidden truth

**Limitations:**
- Unreliable (interpretation is art, not science)
- Void can corrupt readings (feed false visions)
- Bones from Void-touched animals always give false readings
- Bones lose potency after several uses (fresh bones strongest)

**GM Note:** Use bone-reading to hint at Void-presence ambiguously. Interpretations can be wrong. Uncertainty creates tension.

### HERB-CRAFT REMEDIES
**Components:** Local herbs, preparation time, knowledge  
**Casting Time:** 10-30 minutes (brewing/preparation)  
**Duration:** Varies (hours to days)  
**Cost:** Time + materials only

**Effect:** Healing injuries, curing sickness, calming anxiety, inducing sleep, relieving pain. Some effects are purely chemical; others gain power from intention.

**Common Valley Herbs:**

| Herb | Effect | Preparation | Duration |
|------|--------|-------------|----------|
| Feverfew + honey | Reduces fever | Crush into honey | 2-4 hours |
| Dreamwort root | Prevents nightmares; mild Void-resistance | Grind into powder; add to tea | 8 hours (one night) |
| Lavender + milk | Anxiety relief | Steep in warm milk | 2-3 hours |
| Bloodmoss | Rapid wound-healing | Crush fresh onto wound; bandage | Accelerates healing 2x |
| Starflower | Clarity of thought | Dry and brew as tea | 4-6 hours |
| Willow bark | Pain relief | Grind and brew as tea | 3-5 hours |
| Silverleaf | Sleep-inducing | Crush/smoke or brew as tea | 6-8 hours |
| Thornbark + salt | Purification from illness | Boil and drink as bitter liquid | 24 hours |

**Limitations:**
- Slow (requires time to work; not instant)
- Cannot heal magical damage (Void-corruption beyond herbs)
- Cannot heal wounds from blessed weapons
- Overdose possible (too much Dreamwort causes deep sleep; too much Silverleaf unconsciousness)

**GM Note:** Herbalism is first aid. When Arcana or Mercy are injured, priestesses use bloodmoss and time, not magic. Recovery feels earned.

### CHARM-WEAVING
**Components:** String/cord/hair, knotting technique, spoken intention  
**Casting Time:** 30 minutes to 1 hour  
**Duration:** Permanent until broken  
**Cost:** Time + materials only

**Effect:** Creates protective or luck-granting charm worn as amulet. Power is subtle; works best on believers.

**How It Works:**
1. Gather cord (strong cord; some weavers use own hair for intimacy)
2. Hold cord; enter meditation; focus on intention
3. Weave cord into knots (typically 7-9 knots)
4. Tie final knot while speaking aloud what charm will do
5. Charm is complete; wearer carries bound intention

**Common Charm Types:**

| Charm | Intention | Effect |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| Safe-passage | Safe travel | +1 to travel safety; avoid accidents |
| Childbirth | Safe delivery | Protects mother and child during labor |
| Hunter's luck | Successful hunt | Improves game-finding; better aim |
| Sleep | Restful sleep | Promotes deep restorative sleep |
| Warning | Danger awareness | Charm warms/cools when danger approaches |
| Clarity | Mental focus | +1 to mental clarity; confusion resists |
| Courage | Boldness | Wearer feels braver; easier to face fear |

**Limitations:**
- Power is subtle (+1 bonus; not game-changing)
- Works only if wearer believes
- Works only if intention is pure (selfish charm for reward is weak)
- Can be dispelled (strong blessing or curse breaks charm)

**GM Note:** Charm-weaving is intimate priestess-craft. Arcana might weave charms for Mercy. Should feel personal and warm, not flashy.

### SMOKE-SENDING
**Components:** Fire, herbs (sage/cedar), breath  
**Casting Time:** 15-20 minutes  
**Duration:** Instantaneous (message travels immediately)  
**Cost:** Low (focus + materials)

**Effect:** Sends message via smoke to recipient. Works best when both parties expect contact and recipient is in receptive state.

**How It Works:**
1. Build small fire
2. Gather herbs (dried sage or cedar; burn clean)
3. Burn herbs while thinking of recipient
4. Speak message clearly into smoke
5. Smoke carries message; if recipient is watching, they receive it

**Reception Requirements:**
- Recipient must be expecting message
- Recipient must be meditating or in heightened state
- Recipient must be receptive (active blocking prevents reception)

**Limitations:**
- Unreliable (recipient must be paying attention)
- Distance-limited (works best under 10 miles; degrades beyond)
- Takes time (both casting and reception require focus)
- Can be intercepted (Void or other entities might read message)

**GM Note:** Smoke-sending feels mystical but imprecise. Sometimes it works; sometimes recipient was distracted. Creates interesting dramatic uncertainty.

### FIRE-DIVINATION
**Components:** Fire, observer's focus, silence  
**Casting Time:** 30 minutes to 1 hour  
**Duration:** Single reading (as long as focus holds)  
**Cost:** Medium (drains reader's concentration; mental exhaustion after)

**Effect:** Reader sees images in flames; divines near-future events, present truths, or hidden knowledge.

**How It Works:**
1. Build fire (bright flames; best at night)
2. Sit before fire; enter trance-state
3. Focus all attention on flames
4. Watch for images forming in fire (patterns, shapes, colors)
5. Interpret images (requires skill and experience)
6. Continue reading 20-40 minutes (until fatigue sets in)

**Fire-Divination Symbols:**

| Fire Sign | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| Flame reaches high | Escalation, passion, growth |
| Flame burns low | Decline, secrecy, hidden truth |
| Flame dances/wavers | Uncertainty, change, instability |
| Flame turns blue/green | Spiritual presence, magic happening |
| Flame dims/flickers | Danger, interference, Void-presence |
| White smoke | Clarity, truth, divine presence |
| Black smoke | Corruption, darkness, Void-touch |
| Ash swirls upward | Endings, release, transformation |

**Limitations:**
- Unreliable (interpretation is subjective)
- Void-corruption possible (Void feeds false visions)
- Requires silence (disturbance breaks trance)
- Only works at night (daylight interferes)
- Cannot show future (only present truths/near-probabilities)

**GM Note:** Fire-divination is evocative but imprecise. Visions should be symbolic and open to interpretation. Good for dropping hints.

### DOOR-WARDING
**Components:** Salt/iron dust/chalk, spoken words, physical marking  
**Casting Time:** 10 minutes  
**Duration:** Permanent until dispelled  
**Cost:** Low (materials only)

**Effect:** Marks threshold; prevents unwanted entry; warns those trying to pass. Ward is spiritual (determined intruder can cross but feels resistance).

**How It Works:**
1. Stand at threshold (doorway, gate, entrance)
2. Trace warding-symbol (eight-fold knot, spiral, or personal mark)
3. Speak warding-words (any words naming boundary and purpose)
4. Place hand over symbol; channel intention (set the ward)
5. Ward is now active; spiritual barrier established

**What the Ward Does:**
- Warns intruders (person trying to pass feels resistance, unease)
- Slows passage (unauthorized person moves through as if through water)
- Cannot physically stop anyone (determined person can push through)
- Priestess-craft users can override (Dominae can cross without resistance; they know how to dissolve wards)

**Limitations:**
- Cannot stop Void-entity (Void slides past wards)
- Can be broken (strong blessing or curse dispels ward)
- Only affects those not invited (inviting someone into warded space nullifies ward for them)
- Visible to spiritual sight (anyone with high Witness can see ward; not invisible)

**GM Note:** Door-wards are common around private priestess chambers and restricted areas. Arcana should learn to recognize and respect wards—they mark priestess-space boundaries.

### ANIMAL-CALLING
**Components:** Animal's sound/scent, caller's knowledge, patience  
**Casting Time:** 20-60 minutes  
**Duration:** Single casting (animal approaches within 30-60 minutes)  
**Cost:** Low (patience + focus)

**Effect:** Attracts animal to caller. Used for hunting, gathering information from creatures, spiritual communication. Magic respects animal autonomy—animal must be willing.

**How It Works:**
1. Learn/recreate specific animal's sound (bird-call, elk-bugle, wolf-howl)
2. (Optional) Gather scent-marker (droppings, fur, or strong herb)
3. Make sound repeatedly, patiently, calling into wild
4. If animal is near and willing, it approaches
5. Caller can then interact (communication depends on animal's personality)

**Limitations:**
- Works only if animal is willing (cannot force; magic is invitation)
- Requires knowledge of animal (must understand what attracts that species)
- Distance-limited (animal can't hear call beyond ~1 mile)
- Void-corrupted animals resist (actively avoid caller)
- Magic respects autonomy (priestess-craft principle)

**GM Note:** Animal-calling is Sister Tam's specialty. Reinforces priestess-craft's consent-based philosophy—even animals are respected, not controlled.

## TIER 2: ADVANCED HEDGE MAGIC
*Requires training, apprenticeship, or natural talent*

### TRANCE-WALKING
**Components:** Meditation, optional herbs, solitude, focus  
**Casting Time:** Instantaneous (after meditation)  
**Duration:** 30 minutes to several hours  
**Cost:** Medium (drain during casting; fatigue upon return; full night's sleep needed)

**Effect:** Practitioner's consciousness separates from body; spirit travels to spiritual realms; communicates with entities; receives visions. Body remains vulnerable.

**How It Works:**
1. Find safe, quiet location (lying down; secure from disturbance)
2. (Optional) Consume herbs facilitating trance (certain mushrooms, strong tea, dream-herbs)
3. Enter deep meditation; focus on destination or question
4. Release physical sensation; let consciousness drift
5. Spirit separates and travels (time feels different in spiritual realm; 1 hour meditation = 2-3 hours spiritual travel)
6. When returning, consciousness snaps back into body (can be disorienting)

**Spiritual Travel Destinations:**
- Deep Forest Heart (primal consciousness; ancient presence)
- Shrine of Silence (guardian spirit; profound stillness)
- Sacred Springs sources (water-spirits; healing presence)
- Rope Shrine (echo of First Priestess; teaching presence)
- Dream-space (realm of spirits and visions; dangerous if Void present)

**Limitations:**
- Body is vulnerable (sleeping trance-walker can be hurt/moved; no defense)
- Limited to certain locations (some places resist spirit-travel)
- Void can intrude (Void can reach trance-walker; highly dangerous)
- Getting lost is possible (rarely; consciousness wanders too far)
- Time distortion (practitioner loses track of physical time; wakes disoriented)

**GM Note:** Trance-walking is significant mystical experience. Should feel mystical and slightly frightening. Void might appear. Good for building atmosphere and revealing mysteries gradually.

### SOUL-BINDING
**Components:** Blood-oath, spoken vow, witness  
**Casting Time:** 30-60 minutes (ritual must be formal)  
**Duration:** Years or permanent (until oath broken or one party dies)  
**Cost:** High (binds life-energy from both parties; creates permanent spiritual link)

**Effect:** Two people bind their fates together; swear unbreakable oath; oath becomes magically enforced. Breaking oath causes spiritual harm.

**How It Works:**
1. Two people meet in sacred or serious setting
2. Both speak oath aloud (naming what they promise each other)
3. Witness is present (adds spiritual weight)
4. Often involves blood (small cut; blood mixed as symbol)
5. Oath is sealed (spiritual link forms between oath-takers)

**What Happens If Oath Is Broken:**
- Oath-breaker feels sharp spiritual pain (moment of breaking)
- Oath-breaker's connection severed (sudden loneliness/isolation)
- Oath-breaker's reputation damaged (spiritually savvy sense the broken oath)
- Oath-breaker's health may suffer (lingering weakness; spiritual harm)

**Limitations:**
- Can only be broken by mutual consent (both parties must agree to release)
- Breaking requires formal un-binding ritual (can't just walk away)
- Oath must be spoken clearly (vague oaths don't bind)
- Requires witness (oath sworn in secret has less power)

**GM Note:** Soul-binding feels serious and irreversible. Some valley people use this instead of priestess-craft baseline-locking (less refined, more accessible). If Arcana considers soul-binding, it's major character decision.

### DEATH-SPEAKING
**Components:** Recently deceased's grave/body, medium's ability, offerings  
**Casting Time:** 45-60 minutes  
**Duration:** Single contact (conversation lasts 15-30 minutes)  
**Cost:** Very High (deeply draining; medium needs several days rest after)

**Effect:** Medium opens door between living and dead; allows temporary conversation with ghost; gathering information; honoring ancestors.

**How It Works:**
1. Gather offerings (food, flowers, items deceased valued)
2. Visit grave or location of death
3. Speak to dead (naming them; inviting communication)
4. Leave offerings (showing respect)
5. Medium enters trance; opens spiritual door
6. Ghost appears (if willing); conversation occurs
7. Medium thanks ghost; closes door; returns to normal consciousness

**Limitations:**
- Ghost must be willing (can refuse communication)
- Fresh deaths cooperate better (recently dead more present; ancient dead fading)
- Void can mimic dead voices (false ghosts; difficult to distinguish)
- Can only communicate briefly (extended contact exhausts medium and ghost)
- Limited practical information (dead person's knowledge ends at death)

**GM Note:** Death-speaking should be rare and solemn. When priestess dies (from Void-corruption or accident), death-speaking allows Arcana to learn truth. Emotionally powerful. Void might interfere—showing false visions.

### CURSE-LAYING
**Components:** Target's personal item (hair, clothing, blood), words of ill-wishing, spite/rage  
**Casting Time:** 30-60 minutes (must focus ill-intention strongly)  
**Duration:** Permanent or specific duration  
**Cost:** Very High (requires significant emotional energy; life-force consumed; curse-caster suffers blowback if curse broken)

**Effect:** Causes bad luck, illness, or specific harm to target. Curse manifests over days/weeks. Universe resists unjust curses.

**How It Works:**
1. Gather target's personal item (hair, clothing, blood)
2. Enter state of rage/spite (genuinely angry at target; must believe curse justified)
3. Bind item to curse-words (speaking specific harm: "May her rope fray and her will break")
4. Burn item or bury it (destroy to anchor curse)
5. Curse is released (manifests in target's life over time)

**Curse Severity:**
- Light curse (minor bad luck; items break; mistakes increase) lasts 1-2 weeks
- Moderate curse (illness, nightmares, relationship damage) lasts 1-3 months
- Severe curse (crippling bad luck, serious illness, spiritual harm) lasts 6+ months

**What RESISTS Curses:**
- Priestess-craft training (high Tier priestesses naturally protected)
- Recent blessing (active blessing defends against curse)
- Strong will (powerful personality resists curse's influence)
- Just person (living ethically resists curse better)

**How Curse Manifests:**
- Gradually worsens (first week: minor problems; escalates)
- Universe aligns against cursed (malicious coincidences)
- Cursed person feels weight of curse (senses something wrong)
- Priestesses sense curses (can feel them spiritually)

**Reversals:**
- Blessing can counter-curse (priestess blesses target; blessing cancels curse)
- Source can be found and broken (if target finds binding-item and destroys it, curse breaks)
- Curse-breaker can absorb curse (transfers harm to themselves)

**Limitations:**
- Universe resists unjust curses (if target undeserving, curse fails/weakens)
- Caster suffers blowback (curse-caster's life becomes harder while curse active)
- Cannot kill (curses cause illness but not direct death)
- Requires strong emotion (half-hearted curse doesn't work)

**CATHEDRAL LAW:** Curse-laying is illegal in Cathedral. Practitioners prosecuted severely—imprisonment, exile, or execution. Network operatives use curses against Cathedral priestesses.

**GM Note:** Curse-laying feels morally dark. Discovering curse-victims is plot hook. Curse-caster might be network operatives. Breaking curses is meaningful adventure.

### BLESSING
**Components:** Target's consent/presence, spoken words, often sacred object  
**Casting Time:** 15-30 minutes  
**Duration:** Permanent or specific duration (months to years)  
**Cost:** Low to Medium (depends on blessing power; stronger blessings drain giver's energy)

**Effect:** Grants luck, protection, healing, favor, or specific gift to target. Constructive magic.

**How It Works:**
1. Target consents (blessing must be freely given and accepted)
2. Blesser speaks intention (naming what blessing grants: "May you find safe passage")
3. (Optional) Sacred object used (blessed rope, water from Sacred Springs)
4. Blesser channels positive intention (genuine goodwill matters)
5. Blessing takes effect (gradually manifests over days/weeks)

**Common Blessings:**
- Safe-passage blessing (protects traveler; +1 travel safety for month)
- Healing blessing (accelerates recovery; wounds heal 2x faster for 2 weeks)
- Luck blessing (improves odds in uncertain situations; +1 to random outcomes for month)
- Clarity blessing (improves thought; confusion resists; +1 mental tasks for month)
- Courage blessing (grants bravery; fear resistible; +1 overcome fear for month)
- Fertility/childbirth blessing (protects mother and child during pregnancy/birth)

**What Makes Blessing Stronger:**
- Giver has authority/spiritual standing (Domina's blessing stronger than acolyte's)
- Giver genuinely cares for target (selfish blessing given for reward is weak)
- Blessing is for community benefit (public good blessing is strong)
- Multiple people witness (witnessed blessing is more powerful)

**Limitations:**
- Must be freely given (coerced/manipulated blessings weak)
- Must be accepted (target refusing blessing makes it ineffective)
- Requires positive intention (blessing given with ulterior motive is weak)

**GM Note:** Priestesses frequently bless each other and Arcana. Simple blessings should feel warm and supportive. Dominae's blessings should feel meaningful—when Seraphine blesses Arcana for trial, it matters.

## TIER 3: FORBIDDEN HEDGE MAGIC
*Suppressed, dangerous, illegal in Cathedral*

### WILL-THEFT
**Components:** Target's personal item, practitioner's malevolent intent, Void-knowledge  
**Casting Time:** 45-60 minutes  
**Duration:** Permanent (until broken by skilled practitioner)  
**Cost:** Extremely High (requires damning one's soul; practitioner becomes partially Void-touched)

**Effect:** Steals target's will/autonomy; victim becomes puppet; will bound to theft-caster. This is the forbidden knowledge the Void uses and network operatives spread.

**How It Works:**
1. Gather target's personal item (hair, blood, intimate possession)
2. Channel Void-energy through item (requires Void-knowledge)
3. Bind target's will to your own (target's consciousness subordinate to your commands)
4. Target becomes puppet (obeys caster; cannot resist; personality suppressed)

**Cost to Caster:**  
Extremely High; requires damning one's soul. Every will stolen hardens caster toward Void-corruption.

**Progression of Will-Theft Caster:**
- First theft: Feels rush of power; becomes addicted
- Second theft: Begins hearing Void-voice; Void offers guidance
- Third+ thefts: Mostly Void-touched; original personality fading
- Eventually: Void-instrument (own will consumed; becomes puppet too)

**Network Use:**
Network operatives use will-theft to create enslaved priestesses. Enslaved priestesses act normal but take secret orders. Used to sabotage Cathedral (weaken seals, corrupt others, gather information). Goal: Create cascade of corruption from one enslaved priestess to many.

**Detection:**
- Mirror of Truth-Seeing reveals will-theft (victim appears as puppet)
- High Witness priestess can sense something wrong with victim's consciousness
- Victim sometimes resists (will-theft isn't perfect)
- Baseline-lock renewal strains (feels wrong if victim will-stolen)

**Reversal:**
- Void-binding ritual (all Eight Dominae extract Void-threads; extremely dangerous)
- Master-level priestess with Honesty + Witness might break theft without full ritual
- Death (if can't break theft, victim might choose death rather than live as puppet)

**Limitations:**
- Caster must have Void-communion (can't do this without contacting Void)
- Victim must be spiritually vulnerable (strong-willed priestesses resist harder)
- Effect weakens if caster dies (victim regains will)
- Not perfect control (victim maintains some consciousness; might resist subtly)

**GM Note:** Will-theft is major plot device. When Arcana realizes someone trusted is will-stolen, it's betrayal and horror. Network uses this to plant spies. Freeing will-stolen priestesses is endgame content. Risk of becoming will-stolen adds tension to Void contact.

### IDENTITY-SHATTERING
**Components:** Target's name spoken repeatedly, mirror/reflective surface, practitioner's will  
**Casting Time:** 30-60 minutes (must focus intensely; chanting/repetition required)  
**Duration:** Hours to weeks  
**Cost:** High (requires dark intention; practitioner suffers psychic backlash; makes caster mentally unstable)

**Effect:** Fragments target's sense of self; causes confusion, dissociation, madness. Target loses sense of who they are; personality scatters.

**How It Works:**
1. Obtain target's name (must know true name)
2. Chant name repeatedly while staring into mirror (mirror = target's reflection/identity)
3. Focus intention on breaking that identity apart (visualization: see name fragment; identity scatter)
4. Continue chanting 30+ minutes (longer = stronger effect)
5. Target experiences identity-fragmentation (consciousness confused; sense of self dissolves)

**What Happens to Target:**
- Early stage (first few hours): Confusion; memory gaps; asking "who am I?" repeatedly
- Progressive (deepening): Inability to recognize self in mirror; contradictory urges; personality splits
- Advanced (if continued): Complete dissociation; believes multiple contradictory identities

**Who Resists Better:**
- High Tier priestesses (Virtue-mastery = coherent sense of self; harder to shatter)
- Stable personalities (strong identity resists better)
- Recently blessed (blessing reinforces self; defends against shattering)

**Who Is Vulnerable:**
- Traumatized people (past trauma makes identity fragile)
- Low Tier priestesses (less developed spiritual sense of self)
- Isolated individuals (without community reinforcing identity)
- Already Void-touched (Void has already begun fragmenting identity)

**Reversal:**
- Identity-restoration ritual (community reminds target of who they are; repeated affirmations over days)
- Intensive Honesty Virtue work (priestess helps target speak truth about themselves; rebuilds identity)
- Safety and love (target needs stable environment and people who care; slow recovery)

**Limitations:**
- Victim can resist (strong will fights off shattering)
- Effect weakens over time (if attack interrupted, victim recovers)
- Priestess-craft training provides defense (trained priestesses bounce back faster)

**GM Note:** Identity-shattering is psychological horror. Meeting victim of this magic (perhaps previous Void-touched priestess) is disturbing. Helping such victims recover is meaningful healing-work.

### BINDING-BREAKING
**Components:** Bound person's location/presence, breaking-words, overwhelming will  
**Casting Time:** 30-60 minutes (must build enormous power; risky channeling)  
**Duration:** Instantaneous (bindings break in moment of casting)  
**Cost:** Extremely High and Dangerous (practitioner almost always suffers severe consequences)

**Effect:** Shatters magical bindings; frees will-locked individuals; breaks baseline-locks, seal-locks, or any magical bond. Most dangerous forbidden magic—almost impossible to use safely.

**How It Works:**
1. Focus on binding to break (target baseline-lock, seal-lock, magical constraint)
2. Channel Void-energy (requires dangerous Void-communion; forces opening consciousness to Void's power)
3. Force that power through breaking-words (screaming break-words with all intention)
4. Binding breaks catastrophically (shatters violently; often causes collateral damage)

**Cost to Caster:**  
Extremely High and Dangerous:
- Spiritual backlash (consciousness fractured; traumatized)
- Physical injury (channeling this power through human body causes damage)
- Void-consumption (opened consciousness remains open; Void begins consuming caster)
- Possible death (if energy flow too strong, body overwhelmed)

**Why It's Dangerous:**
- Can't be controlled precisely (effect breaks MORE than intended)
- Seal-breaking risks catastrophe (breaking Void's seal might allow escape)
- Caster almost always consumed (remaining open to Void makes consumption likely)
- Success rate is low (most attempts fail or partially work)

**If Binding-Breaking Succeeds:**
- Baseline-locks break (mount freed from priestess; trauma for both)
- Seal-locks fail (imprisoned entity partially breaks free; very bad)
- Magical bonds shatter (soul-bindings, curse-locks, all fail simultaneously)
- Collateral damage occurs (nearby magic disrupted; people nearby feel backlash)

**Who Would Attempt This:**
- Network operatives trying to free Void (ultimate goal: break Void's seal)
- Someone desperate to free will-locked friend (willing to die)
- Void-touched person commanded by Void

**Historical Note:** No record of successful binding-breaking in Cathedral archives. Theoretically possible but practically suicidal. One previous Arcana might have attempted it, with catastrophic results.

**GM Note:** Binding-breaking is endgame threat, not early-campaign mechanic. Network might attempt to free Void. Preventing binding-breaking attempt could be climactic final battle. Or, if Arcana discovers loved one is will-stolen, temptation to use binding-breaking becomes personal moral dilemma.

## THE FORMAL PRIESTESS-CRAFT RITUALS

Official rituals maintained by Cathedral—refined, documented, mechanically precise. Priestesses spend years learning these. Rituals draw on Virtue-energy rather than folk components; require formal training and spiritual alignment.

## TIER 1: FOUNDATIONAL RITUALS

### THE BASELINE-LOCK CEREMONY
**Duration of Casting:** 1-2 hours  
**Duration of Effect:** Permanent (until consciously broken)  
**Participants:** Priestess, mount, optional witness (usually Domina)  
**Components:** Rope (specifically woven for ceremony), spoken vows, physical contact, spiritual intention  
**Location:** Private sacred chamber

**The Ritual Process:**

**Stage 1: Preparation (15-20 minutes)**
1. Priestess and mount enter sacred space together
2. They sit facing each other (or mount kneels beside priestess)
3. Domina witness (if present) speaks opening words ("We gather to bind these spirits in service and devotion")
4. Both enter light meditation (centering intention; clearing distraction)

**Stage 2: Rope Weaving (20-30 minutes)**
1. Priestess begins weaving rope slowly, deliberately, meditating on her intentions for the bond
2. As she weaves, she speaks about what this bond will be (not domination; partnership; mutual care)
3. Mount assists (symbolically or literally; some mounts help weave)
4. Rope becomes infused with priestess's intention

**Stage 3: Vows (10-15 minutes)**
1. Priestess speaks vow (what does she promise to mount? What is her role?)
   - *"I bind myself to you as guide and protector. I promise honesty, respect, and the devotion you deserve."*
2. Mount speaks acceptance (agreement to baseline-locked state)
   - *"I accept your guidance. I trust your care. I become your baseline, your foundation."*
3. Witness may add words (acknowledging binding)

**Stage 4: Binding (15-20 minutes)**
1. Priestess places rope around mount's neck, wrist, or body (placement varies by priestess preference)
2. Rope is tied securely but not painfully (binding feels safe, not constrictive)
3. Priestess enters light trance (channeling Virtue-energy through rope)
4. Mount enters baseline-locked state (consciousness aligns to priestess's frequency)
5. Both feel connection form (moment of profound intimacy; spiritual link established)

**Stage 5: Closure (5-10 minutes)**
1. Priestess returns from trance slowly (re-orients to normal consciousness)
2. Witness speaks closing words
3. All three sit together briefly (integrating new bond)
4. Ceremony completes

**Mechanical Effects:**

**To Mount:**
- Becomes baseline-locked (will aligned to priestess, but not enslaved)
- Can still think and feel independently (priestess's intentions have priority)
- Enters calm, submissive state (submission feels *right* to locked mount)
- Gains spiritual link to priestess (senses priestess's location, intentions, emotional state)
- Psyche slightly drained (entering new state costs 1-2 Psyche)

**To Priestess:**
- Gains spiritual link to mount (constant awareness of mount's Psyche level, emotional state, location)
- Can "feel" mount's baseline (know what mount is sensing)
- Virtue-energy expands slightly (shared consciousness adds capacity)
- Costs 1-3 Psyche (depending on mount's initial resistance and priestess's skill)

**To Rope:**
- Becomes magically bonded (cannot be destroyed by physical force; only breaks if both agree)
- Carries priestess-mount link (glows faintly when baseline-lock reinforced)
- Remembers every ceremony (touching it grants echo-visions of previous bonds)

**Monthly Renewal:**
After baseline-lock ceremony, bond must be renewed monthly (every ~30 days).
- Renewal ritual is shorter (10 minutes; casual bond-refreshment)
- Renewal costs 0.5 Psyche from priestess
- Renewal strengthens bond; keeps connection active

**Complications:**

**If Mount Is Unwilling:**
- Ceremony fails (cannot force baseline-lock)
- Rope rejects binding (becomes painful to handle; burns priestess's hands)
- Mount remains independent (lock doesn't form)

**If Priestess Has Weak Virtue-Training:**
- Lock is unstable (mount remains somewhat autonomous; can partially resist priestess)
- Lock is less durable (might destabilize if mount experiences trauma)
- Must be renewed more frequently (every 2-3 weeks instead of monthly)

**If Void Has Contacted Mount:**
- Void may resist lock (creating spiritual resistance during ceremony)
- Lock formation is painful for mount (visible struggle; mount whimpers/writhes)
- Priestess must have stronger will to overcome Void's resistance
- Lock forms but is fragile (more vulnerable to Void-seduction afterward)

**If Priestess's Intentions Are Impure:**
- Rope rejects binding (becomes thorny, painful; hurts priestess's hands)
- Ceremony fails (priestess cannot complete vows; mouth won't form words)
- Priestess must examine motives (selfish dominance vs. mutual devotion; rope knows the difference)

**Breaking the Bond:**

**Requirements:**
1. Mutual consent (both priestess and mount must agree)
2. Formal ceremony (reverse ritual; approximately 1 hour)
3. Domina witness (ideally neutral Domina present)

**The Breaking Ceremony:**
1. Priestess and mount meet in sacred space
2. Both speak intention to break ("I release you from baseline-lock")
3. Priestess holds rope; channels Virtue-energy in reverse (unweaving spiritual link)
4. Rope is carefully removed
5. Priestess and mount separate (breaking is complete)

**After Breaking:**
- Both experience separation trauma (lasting weeks of grief/disorientation)
- Priestess feels loss of connection (suddenly alone; no longer sensing mount's state)
- Mount feels adrift (no longer has priestess's guidance; must rebuild individual will)
- Future bonds harder to form (both carry spiritual scarring; subsequent baseline-locks more difficult)
- Social consequences (breaking is judged harshly by community; questions about why)

**GM Note:** Baseline-lock ceremony is foundational ritual of priestess-craft. When Arcana performs her first baseline-lock with Mercy (Session 62), this should feel profound and intimate. The ceremony is sacred, not kinky—though spiritual power dynamic mirrors BDSM themes.

Use ritual to establish priestess-craft's consent-based philosophy. Priestesses respect mounts' autonomy. Breaking is possible if both agree. Differentiates priestess-craft from evil domination-magic.

When Void-corruption threatens Mercy, baseline-lock renewal becomes tense. Does rope still feel right? Can priestess renew it if mount is partially Void-touched? These questions create dramatic tension.

### WEEKLY DEVOTION CEREMONY
**Duration of Casting:** 2-3 hours  
**Frequency:** Once per week (typically Sunday evening)  
**Participants:** All Cathedral priestesses + their mounts; led by all Eight Dominae  
**Components:** Candlelight, spoken liturgy, synchronized movement, group intention  
**Location:** Upper Transept (main ceremonial chamber)

**Pre-Ceremony (30 minutes before):**
1. All priestesses gather their mounts in vestry
2. Priestesses and mounts don ceremonial robes/adornment (simple, clean clothing; some wear colored cords matching Virtue specialties)
3. All process into Upper Transept in formal order (Dominae first; then priestesses by seniority; mounts follow)
4. All take position in circle (priestess-mount pairs arranged in eight groups, one per Virtue)

**Ceremony Process:**

**Phase 1: Individual Work (30-45 minutes)**
1. Each priestess-mount pair enters priestess-craft work (no formal structure; each pair does own practice)
2. Priestess might engage mount with rope-work, meditation, movement, or intimate spiritual connection
3. Pairs encouraged to deepen individual baseline-lock bond
4. Dominae observe quietly (watching for signs of distress)
5. Energy in chamber gradually rises (visible as shimmer to trained observers)

**Phase 2: Synchronization (15-20 minutes)**
1. Lead Domina rings bell three times (signal to synchronize)
2. All priestesses and mounts shift from individual work to synchronized work
3. Priestesses lead mounts through coordinated movement (ceremonial gestures)
4. Mounts' breathing synchronizes (all breathing together; heartbeats align)
5. All eight priestess-mount pairs move in eight-fold pattern (representing eight Virtues; eight seals)

**Phase 3: Virtue-Work (45-60 minutes)**
1. Eight Dominae take positions at eight cardinal points (forming circle around everyone)
2. Each Domina invokes her specific Virtue (meditating on Discipline, Precision, Transcendence, etc.)
3. Dominae channel their Virtue-energy into chamber (visible as colored light to spiritual sight)
4. All mounts enter synchronized transcendent state (collective consciousness; temporary merger)
5. All priestesses and mounts generate spiritual energy together (visible as shimmer/glow)
6. Energy rises to peak (chamber feels electric)

**Phase 4: Seal Reinforcement (15-20 minutes)**
1. Generated energy directed toward eight seals (using intention; Dominae guide flow)
2. Seals receive maintenance energy (15-20% of seal-integrity replenished each week)
3. Void's influence pushed back (Void-seduction dreams suppressed that night)
4. All eight Elders feel reinforcement (even imprisoned, they sense Cathedral's power)
5. Energy gradually disperses (ceremony's peak passes)

**Phase 5: Return to Individual Consciousness (15-20 minutes)**
1. Dominae ring bell slowly (signal to separate from synchronized state)
2. Priestesses gently return mounts to normal consciousness (stroking, speaking softly, grounding techniques)
3. Mounts gradually individualize (synchronized state ends)
4. All priestesses and mounts sit in circle (breathing together; gradually returning)
5. Closing words spoken (gratitude, acknowledgment, blessing)
6. Ceremony ends; everyone slowly disperses

**Mechanical Effects:**

**To Seals:**
- All seals receive 15-20% integrity restoration
- Weekly ceremony critical for seal-durability (without it, seals would degrade within months)

**To Mounts:**
- Experience transcendence (spiritual renewal; profound peace)
- Recover 1-2 Psyche points (transcendent state is restorative)
- Report ceremony benefits (mounts look forward to weekly ceremony; they love experience)
- Baseline-lock connection reinforced (baseline-locks strengthened by participation)

**To Priestesses:**
- Experience communal bonding (relationship meters with each other shift positive)
- Spend 2-3 Psyche (group work is energetically intense)
- Spiritual power increased (working in group amplifies individual power)
- Report profound connection (weekly ceremony creates strong sisterhood)

**To Void:**
- Void-seduction dreams suppressed that night
- Void feels Cathedral's power (momentary awareness of eight Dominae channeling together)
- Void retreats slightly (imprisonment reinforced; Void's freedom timeline delayed)

**Complications:**

**If a Priestess Is Void-Touched:**
- Her presence disrupts ceremony (creates spiritual static)
- Energy flow disrupted (priestesses sense wrongness)
- Void-touched priestess experiences extreme discomfort (Void inside her resists Virtue-channeling)
- If Tier 3+ corruption, her presence can fail entire ceremony (seals don't receive energy)

**If Too Many Priestesses Are Void-Touched:**
- Ceremony fails entirely (spiritual static overwhelms coordination)
- Seals don't receive energy (week of maintenance lost)
- Void feels neglect (Void knows seals are weakening)
- Dominae realize something is seriously wrong (emergency meeting must be called)

**If a Domina Is Absent:**
- Ceremony's power diminished (all Eight must be present)
- Seals receive only 10-12% integrity restoration (vs. normal 15-20%)
- Void might sense weakness (opportunity to attempt something)
- Mystery of absent Domina (where is she? Sick? Compromised?)

**If a Priestess Refuses to Participate:**
- Her absence is noted by all (obvious empty space in circle)
- Community ostracism follows (priestesses judge refusal harshly)
- Refusal reported to Elders (might be disciplined or investigated)
- Possible reasons (Void-corruption, pregnancy needing rest, philosophical objection, personal crisis)

**GM Note:** Weekly Devotion Ceremony is heartbeat of Cathedral life. Arcana attends first ceremony in Session 63. Experience should be overwhelming—seeing eight Dominae channel Virtue-energy simultaneously, feeling collective power, witnessing mounts enter transcendent state.

Use ceremony to:
- Reinforce priestess-craft's spiritual power
- Introduce Dominae (observation of how they work; their Virtue-specialties demonstrated)
- Hint at seal-mechanics (energy directed to seals; Arcana doesn't understand yet)
- Create communal bonding (Arcana becomes part of weekly rhythm)
- Detect Void-corruption (if priestess's presence causes disruption, Arcana might notice)

When Void-seduction advances, mounts begin showing reluctance at ceremonies. Does Mercy participate fully, or seem distant? Early warning of corruption.
