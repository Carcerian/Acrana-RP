### LADY ZEPHYRA — The Storm Princess [CR 7]
**Hit Points:** 55  
**Armor Class:** 14 (Natural Armor)  
**Speed:** Fly 120 ft (hover)

**Attributes:**
- STR 14 (+2) | DEX 17 (+3) | CON 15 (+2) | INT 15 (+2) | WIS 13 (+1) | CHA 16 (+3)
- **Saves:** DEX +5, CHA +5
- **Skills:** Perception +4, Arcana +5
- **Senses:** Truesight 120 ft, passive Perception 14

**Resistances:** Lightning damage (immunity)  
**Damage Immunities:** Lightning  
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened

**Traits:**
- **Legendary Resistance (3/Day):** If Zephyra fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
- **Stormsurge Aura:** Any creature within 15 ft of Zephyra has disadvantage on ranged attack rolls.
- **Flyby Attack:** Zephyra does not provoke opportunity attacks when flying out of an enemy's reach.
- **Tempest Form:** Zephyra can float and hover; leaves visible wind trail when moving.

**Actions:**
- **Multiattack:** Zephyra makes three attacks: two with Stormreaver Spear and one with Tempest Blast.
- **Stormreaver Spear:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 10 ft, one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d6+2) piercing damage + 7 (2d6) lightning damage.
- **Chain Lightning (Recharge 5-6):** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +5 to hit, range 60 ft, one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d8+2) lightning damage. The lightning arcs to 1d4 other creatures within 30 ft of the first target.
- **Tempest Blast (Recharge 5-6):** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14. Each creature within 15 ft must make a save or take 14 (2d6+2) lightning damage and be knocked prone.

**Legendary Actions (3/turn):**
- **Spear Attack:** Zephyra makes one Stormreaver Spear attack.
- **Move:** Zephyra moves up to her flying speed.
- **Chain Lightning (Costs 2 Actions):** Zephyra uses Chain Lightning if available.

**Lair Actions (If in Storm):**
- **Summon Winds (Costs 1 Action):** Creates difficult terrain in 30-ft radius centered on Zephyra.
- **Lightning Strike:** Calls lightning on one creature within 60 ft (target makes DC 14 DEX save or takes 11 (2d6+2) lightning damage).

### LILITH — The Infernal Queen [CR 9]
**Hit Points:** 88  
**Armor Class:** 16 (Natural Armor, Infernal Beauty)  
**Speed:** 30 ft, Fly 60 ft

**Attributes:**
- STR 16 (+3) | DEX 17 (+3) | CON 16 (+3) | INT 15 (+2) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 18 (+4)
- **Saves:** DEX +6, WIS +5, CHA +7
- **Skills:** Deception +7, Insight +5, Perception +5, Persuasion +7
- **Senses:** Truesight 120 ft, passive Perception 15

**Resistances:** Fire, psychic  
**Damage Immunities:** Fire, poison  
**Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, poisoned

**Traits:**
- **Legendary Resistance (3/Day):** If Lilith fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
- **Infernal Beauty:** Humanoids that can see Lilith have disadvantage on saving throws against her charm spells and abilities.
- **Seduction Aura:** Any humanoid within 30 ft that can see Lilith must succeed on a DC 15 CHA save or be charmed.
- **Freedom of Movement:** Lilith ignores difficult terrain and any spell or effect that reduces its speed. Oppressive force, such as magical restraints, do not affect it. If Lilith is restrained and escapes, it teleports away.

**Actions:**
- **Multiattack:** Lilith makes three attacks: two with Infernal Blade and one with Psychic Lash.
- **Infernal Blade:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8+3) slashing damage + 7 (2d6) fire damage.
- **Psychic Lash (Recharge 5-6):** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +6 to hit, range 60 ft, one target. *Hit:* 16 (3d6+4) psychic damage.
- **Charm (Recharge 5-6):** One humanoid within 30 ft that Lilith can see must succeed on a DC 15 CHA save or be charmed by Lilith until the end of Lilith's next turn. If Lilith or its allies are fighting the charmed creature, it has advantage on the save.

**Legendary Actions (3/turn):**
- **Blade Attack:** Lilith makes one Infernal Blade attack.
- **Move:** Lilith moves up to her speed.
- **Psychic Lash (Costs 2 Actions):** Lilith uses Psychic Lash if available.
- **Cast a Spell (Costs 2 Actions):** Lilith casts a spell using a spell slot if available (typically charm-based spells).

**Lair Actions (In Her Infernal Palace):**
- **Teleport:** Lilith magically teleports up to 60 ft to an unoccupied space in the lair.
- **Mirror Image:** Creates illusory duplicates of itself (AC 14, 22 HP each).

- **Scourge Attack:** Asmodea makes one Infernal Scourge attack.
- **Move:** Asmodea moves up to her speed.
- **Spell (Costs 2 Actions):** Asmodea casts a spell using a spell slot.
- **Disintegration Ray (Costs 2 Actions):** Asmodea uses Disintegration Ray if available.

**Lair Actions (In Her Infernal Forge):**
- **Summon Pit Fiends (Costs 2 Actions):** Summons 1d3 Pit Fiends (CR 11) as allies.
- **Contract Manifestation:** Creates binding magical contract in 60-ft radius; creatures must succeed on DC 18 WIS save or be bound by terms Asmodea sets.
