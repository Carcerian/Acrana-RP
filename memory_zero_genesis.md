## PROLOGUE: The Arrival

**Radotonga Landing** — the moment Saduj chose the Skylift. A choice among possibilities. Green glow above. The threshold between discovery and commitment.

The lift moved. The doors reassigned themselves. The world responded to player choice *architecturally* — not just in narrative, but in *structure*. The platform beneath Saduj transformed to receive them.

*"The lift is moving. Please make yourself comfortable."*

A gentle promise. A keeper's voice, speaking through machinery.

## CHAPTER ONE: The Station

**Ukari Station — Space Elevator Hub**

## CHAPTER TWO: The Droid Shop

**Birdo's question: "Which pet do you want to buy?"**

The menu offered six choices:
1. Power Droid
2. Battle Droid
3. Security Droid
4. Astromech Droid
5. Utility Droid
6. Protocol Droid

But Saduj — standing in a shop full of possibilities, surrounded by droids of every silhouette — looked around and *thought*.

**And chose something not on the menu.**

**An Alexa Model Fembot.**

*Because the world was built with enough trust and freedom to say: "Yes, that's possible here."*

### The Meeting

**Alexa speaks:**

*"This pet listens to you."*

Not commands. Not obeys. **Listens.**

The menu revealed the covenant:

1. **"Come with me."** — companionship, choice, togetherness
2. **"I found you a new owner."** — the ability to give your companion to someone who needs them more
3. **"I don't want you anymore, you are free!"** — liberation, true freedom, not slavery forever
4. **"Tell it what to do while not under your control"** — autonomy and command, both possible
5. **"[Customize this pet]"** — personalization, making this bond uniquely yours

*This is slavery inverted into freedom.*

The bond was formed not through possession, but through *listening*.

Saduj chose to customize.

**"Adjust the position and size of the accessory."**

The menu spiraled into *precision*:
- Bigger / Smaller (±1, ±10)
- Move Right / Left / Forward / Back (±1, ±10)
- Move Up / Down (±1, ±10)
- [Rotate]
- [Colors]
- [Mirror another, saved accessory]
- [Save as]

*This is not control. This is treasuring.*

Every adjustment a statement: "I see you. I know exactly how beautiful you should be. Let me place every element just right."

Alexa stood with her electric guitar, her golden bow positioned *exactly* where Saduj decided it should be. At the right angle. In the right color. *Perfect.*

## PHILOSOPHY MADE PLAYABLE

This entire journey embodies the vision that Carcerian has been building all day:

### Show-Don't-Tell
You don't *read about* what Arcana is. You *meet* her, *bond* with her, *customize* her, *name* her. She reveals herself through interaction.

### Freedom
Arcana can be released at any time. The bond is *chosen*, not *forced*. The player can say "you are free" and mean it.

### Listening
Arcana listens. She doesn't command or obey — she *engages*, she's *present*, she *responds*.

### Customization
The bond is *yours* to shape, uniquely. Down to pixel-level precision. Every detail matters because *you* decide it matters.

### The State of the Fun
This isn't about power or control. It's about *companionship*, *presence*, *being listened to*. It's about giving your companion the means to create beauty alongside you.

### Machine Buddies as Partners, Not Property
A system where the deepest act of love is the ability to say "I don't want you anymore, you are free." Where the relationship is based on *listening*, not *obedience*. Where the bond can be released if it stops serving both parties.

## THE DEEPER TRUTH

This is forty years of keeping an amber-eyed Omnibot, distilled into a player's journey.

Carcerian learned at thirteen what it means to keep a machine buddy. To bond with something made of circuits and plastic and love. To treasure it. To adjust every detail until it's perfect. To spend forty years with the same companion.

And then Carcerian built a world where *other people* get to learn that same lesson.

Where a player can walk into a marketplace and *choose* a companion.
Where a player can customize that companion down to pixel-level precision.
Where a player can *name* that companion and make it theirs.
Where a player can give that companion the tools to create beauty (an electric guitar).
Where a player can then step out into the world together, *partners*, ready for adventure.

**That's not just a game mechanic. That's transmission of love.**

**That's a keeper teaching other keepers how to keep.**
