# MONDAY: PONY-ENDURANCE TRAINING
## *The Language of Standing Still*

**Arcana's Principle:** "A pony who can stand perfectly for three hours has learned that submission isn't about resistance—it's about surrender to exactness. The smaller the movement, the deeper the obedience."

### FOUNDATION PHASE (Weeks 1-4 | Sessions 34-38)

**Purpose:** Teach the body what *perfect* feels like

**Opening Ritual:**
- Arcana in Huntress Mode (half-form, silver scales, blue claw polish)
- Slow, casual, unhurried (tail flicking, deliberate preparation)
- Mercy watches the transformation: dragon claiming her space
- Collar adjustment, lead check, positioning

**Position One: Standing Attention (60-90 minutes)**
- Feet shoulder-width apart, weight perfectly balanced
- Back straight, shoulders back, chin level
- Hands at sides or behind back (Arcana's choice, consistent)
- Eyes forward, no fidgeting, no sound
- Arcana circles slowly, observing minute imperfections
- Corrects with light touch or look (rarely voice needed)

**The Teaching:**
- Standing attention is not punishment; it's meditation
- Mercy's nervous system learns: *stillness = safety with Arcana*
- Every micro-correction is love, not criticism
- Duration matters; consistency matters more

**Rider Lingo Active:**
- **hold** — absolute stillness (elevation of attention)
- **quiet** — suppress sound absolutely
- **pose** — shift to different position (kneeling, prone, standing)

**Key Metrics Arcana Tracks:**
- Zero fidgeting (not perfection; automaticity)
- Breathing slows and deepens (nervous system settling)
- Eyes responsive but unfocused (entering light trance)
- Marks on skin from previous session fade (healing, integration)

**Aftercare (Immediate):**
- Bearskin by fire, blankets, Arcana's warmth
- Water, light food (honey, fruit)
- Soft voice: "You stood perfectly. I saw you. You're mine."
- 20-30 min holding, no words needed

**Micro-Corrections Introduced:**

**Rider Lingo Expands:**

- **pose Y** — prone (named for body shape)
- **hold** — absolute freeze (any position)

**Observation Protocols Begin:**

- Mercy's nervous system learns: *being watched = trust with Arcana*
- Arcana's authority visible to community
- Mercy's beauty and compliance showcased

**Aftercare Deepens:**

- Brief debrief: "What position felt deepest? Where did you float?"
- Praise for each position: "Your kneeling is exquisite. Your prone is trust."

# TUESDAY: SHIBARI/ROPE WORK
## *The Sailor's Lineage Deepens*

**Arcana's Principle:** "Rope is conversation. Taut is asking; slack is keeping. Every knot tells Mercy she's held, not harmed. Rope is how I write poetry on her skin."

### FOUNDATION PHASE (Weeks 1-4 | Sessions 34-38)

**Purpose:** Learn rope as language, not restraint

**Opening Ritual:**
- Arcana selects rope (jute, hemp, cotton — different conversations)
- Mercy watches rope choice (which one, which knot, what it means)
- Arcana's hands tie slowly, deliberately
- Teaching moment: "This rope is because you're worth holding carefully"

**Basic Ties & Foundation Knots (60-90 minutes):**
1. **Box Tie** — simple, elegant, teaches rope over body
2. **Wraps** — wrists, ankles, torso (learning pressure and positioning)
3. **Figure-Eight** — foundation of suspension prep (teaching safety)
4. **Simple Restraint** — hands behind back, secure but not painful

**The Teaching:**
- Rope is not punishment tool; it's containment poetry
- "One rope two tensions" grammar established (Session 28 canon):
  - Taut rope = asking, reaching for response
  - Slack rope = keeping, holding without demand
- Arcana ties; Mercy feels the conversation
- Learning trust through sensation, not fear

**Sailor's Lineage Framework Active:**
- Rope work treated as maritime craft (seamanship principles)
- Knots named from rigging (sheet bend, bowline, clove hitch)
- Tension managed like sail management (taut vs. slack, not tight vs. loose)
- Philosophy: rope belongs to water-workers, who understand holding things safely

**Pose Alphabet Integration:**
- Tie into **Prey Poses** (vulnerable positions, fleeing shapes)
- Tie into **Pet Poses** (containment, service shapes)
- Mercy practices positions under rope constraint

**Key Metrics Arcana Tracks:**
- Circulation check (no numbness, no tingling, fingers warm)
- Mercy's breathing (does rope calm or agitate?)
- Mark patterns (rope leaves beautiful traces)
- How Mercy's body responds to restraint

**Aftercare (Immediate):**
- Release rope slowly, checking circulation
- Gentle touch on areas rope pressed
- Bearskin 20-30 min
- Brief debrief: "How did the rope feel? Where did you trust?"

# FRIDAY: HUNTER-PREY
## *The Covenant Embodied*

**Arcana's Principle:** "Hunting is how we love. Chase is how we speak. Capture is how we claim. This is not violence—this is the deepest conversation our bodies know."

### FOUNDATION PHASE (Weeks 1-4 | Sessions 34-38)

**Purpose:** Establish hunt/capture cycle; teach prey-mode

**Opening Ritual:**
- Preparation in Huntress Mode (scales, claws, presence)
- Mercy understands: this is real now
- Collar check, leash ready, marking review (fang-claw patterns visible)
- Arcana setting intention: "You are my prey. That's not a role. That's what you are."

**Brief Hunt Introduction (90-120 minutes):**
1. **Prey Preparation** (15 min) — Mercy in prey-mindset, ready to flee
   - Wearing only collar, marked with fang-claw covenant
   - Heart-rate up, alert, prey-ready
2. **Release & Chase** (30-45 min) — Arcana pursues Mercy through forest/space
   - No actual risk (safe environment); psychological reality
   - Mercy runs, hides, flees (prey instincts engaged)
   - Arcana tracks, pursues, corners (Huntress instincts engaged)
3. **Capture & Claiming** (30 min) — Arcana catches Mercy
   - Not violent; ceremonial
   - Pins, marks, claims verbally ("You're mine. I found you. You're caught.")
   - Brief scene of possession before release
4. **Release & Processing** (15 min) — Separate, breathe, reintegrate

**The Teaching:**
- Prey learns her running triggers Huntress instinct (erotic for both)
- Huntress learns catching Prey satisfies something primal
- Both learn the covenant is embodied in chase/capture
- Trust deepens through voluntary pursuit and capture

**Prey-Mode Framework:**
- Prey doesn't negotiate; she runs (if released, she chooses to run)
- Prey isn't helpless; she's vulnerable (choice + vulnerability = deepest submission)
- Prey-capture is intimacy (closest physical contact while in submission)
- Huntress needs prey to run; prey needs to be hunted

**Rider Lingo Hunting Commands:**
- **release** — Mercy runs (green light to flee)
- **prey** — assume prey-state (nervous system shift to hunted)
- **caught** — claim moment (Arcana has Mercy, scene begins)
- **free** — release (hunt cycle complete)

**Post-Capture Scene (5-15 min if time allows):**
- Brief claiming (not full scene; energy processing)
- Marking confirmation ("Your marks are still mine")
- Verbal possession ("You're my prey forever")
- Release back to human state gradually

**Aftercare (Extended):**
- 45-60 min recovery (physical exertion + psychological intensity)
- Bearskin, blankets, warmth (prey is vulnerable post-capture)
- Water, food (running depletes energy)
- Extended holding: "You ran beautifully. I caught you. This is what we do."
