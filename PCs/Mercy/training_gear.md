## SECTION VIII: SAFETY & QUALITY STANDARDS

**All gear should meet these criteria:**
- Body-safe materials (silicone, leather, stainless steel)
- No sharp edges or defects
- Properly fitted to Mercy's body
- Tested for durability before use
- Maintained and cleaned regularly
- Stored safely between sessions

**For Electrical Equipment (TENS Unit):**
- Professional safety manual included
- Low-intensity settings only
- Conductive pads rated for skin contact
- Clear off-switch and emergency stop
- Testing on Huntress's own arm first (never on prey until verified safe)

**For Restraint Equipment:**
- Inspect for wear before each session
- Ensure proper blood circulation (not cutting off)
- Always have quick-release available
- Know signs of nerve damage (numbness, tingling)
- Ice/heat application available for swelling
