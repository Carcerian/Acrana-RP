### Field Notes on the Islands, Towns, and Territories of the Wandering World
*As recorded by Arcana, Silver Dragonkin Loremistress — fair copy by Sage*
*Compiled July 5, 2026 — Volume the First; the world is larger than any single volume*

## PREFATORY NOTE ON NAMES
*(A loremistress begins with etymology, or she begins wrong.)*

Three names, easily confused by travelers and deliberately confused by tavern storytellers:

- **SINFERA** — the *planet*. The world entire, with its seas, archipelagos, and impossible neighbors.
- **SINIFER** — the *city*, and the archipelago that bears its name: the great starter city where new arrivals first make landfall, seat of the founding steward Cire.
- **SINFAR** — the *plane* itself, the encompassing reality in which Sinfera turns. *(The scribes of the outermost archive note this is also what the world is called "from outside" — a distinction I record without pretending to fully understand it. Some doors are labeled on both sides.)*

Thus: a traveler stands in Sinifer, on Sinfera, within Sinfar. Any bard who rhymes all three in one verse is showing off.

## I. THE SHAPE OF THE WORLD — Four Archipelagos

Sinfera is not one land but a **federation of islands**: four great archipelagos, each of many isles, each isle stewarded by a different builder-hand with its own vision, lore, and weather — yet all woven into one navigable world. One sails from a sci-fi station to a nightmare coast to a paradise beach without ever leaving the same sea.

- **SINIFER** — the founding archipelago; the starter city and the world's front door
- **THE OUTER ISLES** — the proving grounds: leveling, hunting, and crafting territories spanning the full breadth of an adventurer's career
- **DREAD** — the far dark: epic-tier raid country, where the fortieth-level go to be humbled. Its isles include faithful renderings of storied cities of other worlds — Waterdeep among them, and beneath it the black market port of Skullport
- **ARCHTERRE** — the fourth sister, of similar breadth

Passage between all of it is kept by **navigator NPCs** at every dock — sixteen destinations to a page, pages beyond counting. *(Sage's marginal note: Her Loremistress attempted to read the entire navigator menu on her first voyage. The navigator eventually sat down.)*

## II. THE AEON EMPIRE — An Overland of Every Genre

A full campaign continent, and the finest demonstration of Sinfera's governing principle: *every register of story, one map.*

**Nim** — the royal capital. A cathedral-spired keep ringed by townhouses and crimson autumn foliage — regal and faintly ominous at once, as courts should be.

**Stonehold** — the dwarf fortress. A squat walled gate dwarfed by the mountain behind it, flanked by carved ancestors. The overland shows only the front door; the true city is in the rock.

**Hightree** — home of elves, fey, and hin together beneath one great conifer rising from a walled bailey in a mountain pass. The tree *is* the keep.

**Winterking Village** — ice-crystal monoliths around a frost-rimed hall; gateway to the frozen reaches.

**Champion City** — the tonal swing: a walled compound of towers, machinery, and crashed-tech clutter. Heroes and villains, chrome and sorcery — the Citadel of Steel keeps a *hangar*. I have not yet performed there. I suspect the acoustics are electric.

**Crater Town** — a settlement spiraling down into a crater with something green glowing at the bottom, gating outward to wasteland hunting zones. The locals' relationship with the object at the crater's heart is, I am told, *devotional*. I have filed this under Religions, Inadvisable.

**Tower of Wor** — arena and fighting pit. Where the punchy people are.

**Snowbunny Base Camp & Ski Lodge** — the Bunny Guild's mountain resort, and proof the guild's hospitality reaches every climate.

**Ambrosia** — the empire's gracious front door: smoke rising from town, an angel statue by the shore, and four entries — the west woods, the old quarter, the harbor, and the beach. Four entrances, four moods of arrival. Generous design; I arrived by the beach, which explains everything that followed.

## III. AMBROSIA BEACH — A Border Realm of Elysium
*(My home territory; forgive the length. Loremistresses annotate what they love.)*

Formally: **Elysium — Bunnitopia Prime**, a border realm where Elysium's goodness-and-rest meets the Twin Paradises' virtuous industry. The Bunny Guild's whole theology — *work hard, play hard* — is literal planar geography here: labor and leisure as mirrored layers of one paradise.

**The sunlit layer:**
- **Bunny Borough** — the residential heart of the warren
- **The Guest House** — my Bastion; Saduj's owned-but-open house, between the Baths and Mermaid Cove *(see the Bastion Writeup, this same library)*
- **The Watchtower** — the beach's defensive tower, sundeck above — and within it, **D'Laura's library**: the guild's institutional memory, shelved with a sea view. The best office on the coast, held by the historian I most intend to befriend
- **The Lore Tower** — the Bardic College; my academic home, Julia's workshop, and the theatre where I auditioned into the Advanced tier
- **The Teahouse, the Spa, the Baths** — civilization, measured properly
- **The Palmetto Casino** — with its Siren and Sultan's suites
- **The Love Shack** — upstairs *and* down; I catalogue, I do not judge
- **Mermaid Cove** — as advertised
- **The blessed falls** — singing at all hours
- **The hanging gardens** — where I witnessed Dyna's vows
- **The Harbor** — home of the **Rosewood** restaurant (fine dining; the seared course is worth the reservation)
- **The Royal Museum & Sinfar Historical Archives** — halls of Greyhawk, Athas, Sosaria, and the curious "Third Rock" wing; the Sword of Mercy under glass; a gift shop of genuine menace *(see: Cataberry Seed Packet)*
- **The tropical grotto** — private. Some entries the gazetteer keeps brief.

**The depths beneath — the Sapphire Caves:**
A full leveled descent under paradise: Crab Caves (novice) → Pirate Caves → Shark waters → Deep Ruins → and then the trinity of **Apex, Descent, and Nadir**. A beach with a properly named *Nadir* beneath it. Leisure above, deeps below — the Twin Paradises doctrine expressed vertically.

**The uneasy border — the Dreamlands:**
Down the coast, dream-geography touches the waking shore: **Innsmouth**, **R'lyeh**, the **Scorching Sands**, the **Dark Woods**. Sun, sand, and R'lyeh within walking distance. I keep my performances on *this* side of that border and recommend all bards do likewise; some audiences should not be woken with song.

**Above — Bunnitopia:**
The guild's home shard on the Elysium border, with its interworld gate. The warren keeps real estate in paradise, earned the bunny way: joyfully, and with chores.

**Adjacent estates — Mercuria:**
My treasure's demesne: **Arboria**, **Botania**, the **Manor** (with cellar), and **Opal Keep**. A fallen Bliss Angel with an unfallen address — a whole district of paradise's edge. The gardens alone deserve their own volume.

## IV. THE AMAZON TERRITORIES

**Amazon Isle (Rexuria)** — a linked isle of the "lost world" kind: primitive, proud, and populated by megafauna. *Dinosaurs*, recorded here without exaggeration. The Rexuria amazon village holds it under sister-law — sisterhood by blood and by covenant both, with founding lineages (Irae's line among the leadership) still walking its paths. **All women welcome; no males.** My expedition, with Mercy as guide and voucher, is planned. My intention to politely interview a triceratops about local history has been noted by my household with concern.

**The Ambrosia Enclave** — the amazons' *other* answer: a classical polis in the Athenian style, crowned by a **colossus of Artemis wielding a flaming bow** that polices the sea lane against piracy. Where Rexuria survives its world, the enclave commands its coast. Males admitted on leashes only. *(I qualify for entry at both territories and am, by prior arrangement, already compliant with the enclave's dress code — a symmetry I intend to mention aloud, repeatedly.)* An ode to the colossus is in composition, to be performed from fireproof distance.

## V. TERRITORIES OF NOTE, FARTHER AFIELD

**Radotonga Island** — the sci-fi register at full volume: a magic-siphon station of strange machinery and stranger custodians. The steward's own island, and it shows the same hand as half the marvels in this gazetteer.

**The Steampunk City** — brass, steam, and civilized appetites; the Rosewood evening began a chapter of my own story here. Its full survey awaits a return visit.

**The Hellfire Stacks** — the Hellfire Guild's territory: architecture built around desire. Noted on the atlas, unvisited. Some chapters are for later volumes.

**Tenebrae & the Antediluvian Depths** — where law is built around death; the dark mirror to the warren's joy. The name alone is worth three lectures.

**Charon's Pier** — the ferry point whose name tells you everything about the passenger manifest.

**The Fashion Quarter** *(distributed)* — Felidea's Fashions, Merryway's, the Vogue houses, and **EM Fashion Design** (Eleecil's house, one of the four founding pillars of Sinferan fashion). A loremistress notes where the artistry lives; a lady notes where the wardrobe flowers come from.

## VI. HOW THE WORLD HOLDS TOGETHER
*(A closing note for future scribes.)*

Sinfera should not work. Fallout craters beside elf-trees; R'lyeh down the beach from a spa; amazons, angels, and automatons sharing one sea. Yet it coheres — because each island is one steward's honest vision, and the sea between them is the agreement. The world's unity is not sameness but *navigability*: everything is somewhere, everywhere is reachable, and every dock has someone who knows the way.

A federation of dreams with a common harbor. I have catalogued kingdoms with less sensible constitutions.
